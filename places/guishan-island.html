歷史 History

從漁村到生態島

From Fishing Village to Ecological Park

十九世紀的移民、一九七七年的遷村與二〇〇〇年的重新開放
Settlers of the 1800s, the 1977 relocation and the reopening in 2000

旁白全文 Full narration

宜蘭外海的龜山島，如今沒有常住居民。一百多年間，這裡卻是一座漁村。清道光年間的文獻，已記載島上有人居住。十九世紀中葉起，福建移民陸續在此落腳。聚落集中在龜尾的湖畔。石砌的屋舍覆著茅草，一間緊挨著一間。島民捕魚維生，也在山坡種地瓜與花生。水泥房屋到一九五〇年代才出現。全盛時期，島上住了七百多人。飲水靠山泉，夜裡多半點煤油燈。

湖邊曾有島上唯一的學校，龜山國小。一九七四年颱風接連來襲，小島一度斷了補給。同年，宜蘭縣政府提出遷村。一九七七年，全島居民遷往對岸的大溪。新家名為仁澤新村，島嶼此後列為管制區。遷村隔年，行政區「龜山里」遭到裁撤。島民奔走多年，二〇〇一年終於復里。一九九四年，宜蘭縣政府舉辦返鄉活動。離島多年的居民，得以再次踏上故鄉。

出發的烏石港，曾是蘭陽第一大港。一八七八年洪水帶來泥沙，舊港逐漸淤塞。港口在一九九一年重建。如今登島的船班由這裡出發。一九九九年底，行政院同意有條件開放。二〇〇〇年，龜山島正式開放觀光。同年，龜山島納入國家風景區，定位為海上生態公園。開放之初，每天只收二百五十人。名額逐步放寬，目前每日上限一千八百人。每週三保留給學校與學術單位。

通往最高點的步道，共一千七百零六階。每天限一百人，而且只在上午開放。二〇二四年康芮颱風重創設施，島嶼提前封閉。隔年三月一日才重新開放。百年漁村，如今只在開放季節迎接訪客。對岸的大溪，仍有一個以龜山島為名的社區。

Guishan Island, off the Yilan coast, has no permanent residents today. Yet for more than a century it was a fishing village. Qing records from the Daoguang era already mention people living here. From the mid-1800s, settlers from Fujian made the island their home. The village clustered beside the lake at the turtle's tail. Stone houses with thatched roofs stood packed closely together. Islanders fished for a living and grew sweet potatoes and peanuts on the slopes. Concrete houses did not appear until the 1950s. At its peak, more than 700 people lived on the island. Water came from springs, and most homes were lit by kerosene lamps.

By the lake stood Guishan Elementary, the island's only school. In 1974 a run of typhoons cut the island off, and supplies ran low. That same year the Yilan County Government proposed relocating the residents. In 1977 all the residents moved across the water to Daxi. Their new home was Renze New Village; the island became a restricted zone. A year after the move, Guishan Borough was abolished as an administrative unit. After years of campaigning, the residents won it back in 2001. In 1994 the county government organised a homecoming visit. Islanders who had been away for years set foot on their home again.

Wushi Harbor, the departure point, was once the largest port in Lanyang. Silt from a flood in 1878 began to choke the old harbor. The harbor was rebuilt in 1991. Boats to the island now leave from here. In late 1999 the Executive Yuan approved a partial reopening. In 2000 the island formally opened to visitors. That year it joined the national scenic area as a marine ecological park. At first, only 250 visitors a day were admitted. The quota was raised in stages and now stands at 1,800 a day. Wednesdays are reserved for schools and academic groups.

The trail to the highest point has 1,706 stone steps. It is limited to 100 people a day, in the morning only. Typhoon Kong-rey badly damaged facilities in 2024, and the island closed early. It reopened on 1 March the following year. A village for a century, it now receives visitors only in season. Across the water in Daxi, a community still carries the island's name.

人文 Culture

海平線上的故鄉

Home on the Horizon

宜蘭的精神地標、島民的媽祖與大溪的漁港
Yilan's landmark, the islanders' Mazu and the fishing harbor at Daxi

從宜蘭的海岸望出去，海上總有一座龜山島。公共電視的報導稱它是宜蘭的精神地標。外澳的海灘正對著島嶼。「龜山朝日」是龜山八景之一。沿著頭城海岸往北，島的輪廓不斷變化。從大溪一帶看，東端像一顆龜頭。換到羅東、五結的方向，它卻像個三角形。這種變化，被稱為「龜山轉頭」。山頂罩著雲時，人們說是「神龜戴帽」。在地的說法是，這代表天氣要變壞了。

登島與賞鯨的船，都從烏石港出發。登島的遊客，都要有專業解說員陪同。上岸後不遠，就是島上唯一的信仰中心。它原名拱蘭宮，建於清光緒十七年。島民靠海維生，廟裡後來改奉媽祖。遷村時，神像隨著居民渡海到大溪。留在島上的廟，後來改奉觀世音菩薩。一九九九年，廟名改為普陀巖。每年五月，島民後代會回島祭祖。媽祖神像也一同回到原來的廟。

語言學者發現，島民的閩南語有自己的特色。它保留了較多宜蘭腔的老特徵。島民幾乎全靠漁業維生。離島後多數落腳大溪，也有人去了南方澳、花蓮與基隆。大溪漁港鄰近龜山島漁場，魚獲種類多。下午兩三點漁船回港，魚貨就地喊價拍賣。二〇〇五年，社區成立了漁村文化館。館內展示早年島上的漁具與生活器物。長輩用「打寸子」這類童玩，留住島上的記憶。社區的門牌上，還印著龜山島的輪廓。

二〇一七年起，蘭陽博物館與文化館合作整理館藏。長輩對著老物件說故事，剪成影片〈龜山島來的人〉。火車穿出草嶺隧道，龜山島就出現在海上。北關的觀海平台，也是眺望島嶼的地點。島上已沒有居民，記憶仍在對岸延續。龜山島依舊守在宜蘭的海平線上。

From the Yilan coast, Guishan Island is always there on the sea. Taiwan's Public Television Service has called it Yilan's spiritual landmark. The beach at Wai'ao looks straight out at the island. Sunrise over the island is one of the "Eight Views of Guishan". Heading north along the Toucheng coast, the island's outline keeps changing. Seen from around Daxi, its eastern end looks like a turtle's head. From the direction of Luodong and Wujie it looks triangular instead. The change is known as "the turtle turning its head". When cloud sits on the summit, people say the turtle is wearing a hat. Local belief holds that bad weather is on the way.

Boats for landing and for whale watching all leave from Wushi Harbor. Visitors who land must be accompanied by a trained guide. Not far from the landing stands the island's only place of worship. First called Gonglan Temple, it was built in 1891, in the Guangxu reign. As the islanders lived from the sea, the temple came to enshrine Mazu. When the village moved, her statue crossed to Daxi with the residents. The temple left on the island was later dedicated to Guanyin. In 1999 it was renamed Putuoyan. Every May, descendants of the islanders return to honour their ancestors. The Mazu statue travels back with them to its original temple.

Linguists have found the islanders' Taiwanese Hokkien to be distinctive. It keeps more of the older features of the Yilan accent. Almost all the islanders made their living from fishing. Most resettled in Daxi; others went to Nanfang'ao, Hualien and Keelung. Daxi Fishing Harbor lies near the Guishan fishing grounds and lands a varied catch. Boats return around two or three in the afternoon, and the fish are auctioned on the quay. In 2005 the community opened a fishing-village culture hall. It displays fishing gear and household objects from life on the island. Elders keep island memories alive through old games such as "da cun zi". House number plates in the community carry the outline of the island.

From 2017 the Lanyang Museum worked with the culture hall to sort its collection. Elders' stories about the old objects became a film, "People from Guishan Island". As trains emerge from the Caoling Tunnel, the island appears out at sea. The viewing platform at Beiguan is another place to look across to it. The island has no residents now, but its memory is kept alive across the water. And the island still sits on Yilan's horizon.

自然 Nature

火山島上的森林與海

Forest and Sea on a Volcanic Island

原生蒲葵林、島上的動物與熱泉邊的怪方蟹
Native fan palms, island wildlife and the crab of the hot vents

太平洋上的龜山島，每年冬季封島三個月。東北季風帶來風浪，島上生態也趁此休養。山頂一帶仍是低海拔的原生森林。島上可見姑婆芋、五節芒與相思樹。島上有一片原生的蒲葵林。官方介紹它是全臺唯一天然生長的蒲葵林。毛柿步道旁，有樹徑超過八十公分的老樹。島民把這些毛柿視為神木。龜尾湖畔長著海檬果。海岸一帶則有馬鞍藤與蔓荊。

四月下旬，島上的百合陸續開花。島上的一草一木，都不能採摘帶走。調查記錄到兩棲類三種、蜥蜴五種、蛇類六種。登島須知提醒遊客留意毒蛇與蜂。鳥類有二十五種，其中十六種是留鳥。蝴蝶則記錄到大約一百零四種。二〇二五年開島之際，島上種下毛柿、蒲葵和樹杞。它們是保育類狐蝠的食源與棲息樹種。周邊海域淺，魚群聚集，是臺灣三大漁場之一。這裡記錄過十七種以上的鯨豚。

常見的是飛旋海豚、瓶鼻海豚、小虎鯨與偽虎鯨。四月到九月下旬，是賞鯨豚的季節。龜首附近的海底，熱泉持續湧出。泉口周圍住著烏龜怪方蟹。二〇一六年地震與颱風引發崩塌，堵住許多泉口。研究發現，泉口邊的生物挺過了水質劇變。陡峭的岩壁上，也有適應峭壁的植物。周圍海域則記錄到大約六十種珊瑚。每年三月開島前，都要先淨灘。二〇二五年的淨灘，清出將近三公噸垃圾。

漂上岸的垃圾裡，常見漁業浮球與塑膠桶。遊客產生的垃圾，則全數隨船運回本島。島上禁止狩獵、生火、餵食與放生。未經許可，也不能攜入動植物。森林、礫灘、湖泊與熱泉，擠在不到三平方公里的島上。管制登島人數，正是為了保護這裡的自然生態。

Guishan Island, in the Pacific, is closed for three months every winter. The northeast monsoon brings rough seas, and the island's ecosystems get a rest. Around the summit the slopes are still covered in native lowland forest. Giant taro, silvergrass and Taiwan acacia grow on the island. The island has a stand of native Chinese fan palms. Official guides describe it as the only naturally growing one in Taiwan. Beside the Maoshi Trail stand old trees more than 80 cm across. Islanders regard these ebony trees as sacred. Sea mango trees grow beside Guiwei Lake. Beach morning glory and beach vitex grow along the shore.

In the second half of April, the island's lilies come into bloom. Nothing that grows here may be picked or taken away. Surveys have recorded three amphibian, five lizard and six snake species. Visitor rules warn people to watch for venomous snakes and bees. Twenty-five bird species are listed, sixteen of them resident. Around 104 species of butterfly have been recorded. Around the 2025 reopening, ebony, fan palm and Ardisia saplings were planted here. They are food and roosting trees for the protected flying fox. The surrounding sea is shallow and full of fish, one of Taiwan's three major fishing grounds. More than 17 species of whales and dolphins have been recorded here.

Spinner and bottlenose dolphins, pygmy killer whales and false killer whales are the regulars. The watching season runs from April to late September. On the seabed near the turtle's head, hot springs keep pouring out. Around the vents lives a crab, Xenograpsus testudinatus. In 2016 an earthquake and a typhoon set off landslides that blocked many vents. Studies found that vent-side species coped with the abrupt change in water chemistry. Even the sheer rock faces carry plants adapted to cliffs. In the surrounding waters about 60 species of coral have been recorded. Each March, before the island opens, the beach is cleaned. The 2025 clean-up removed nearly three tonnes of rubbish.

Fishing floats and plastic drums are common among what washes ashore. Rubbish from visitors all goes back to the mainland by boat. Hunting, fires, feeding wildlife and releasing animals are all banned. No animals or plants may be brought in without permission. Forest, gravel beach, lake and hot vents share less than three square kilometres. Visitor numbers are limited precisely to protect the island's natural ecology.

地理 Geography

還在冒煙的火山島

A Volcano Still Breathing

龜首、龜甲、龜尾，與海底的熱泉
Head, shell and tail of the turtle, and the hot vents beneath the sea

在 YouTube 觀看 Watch on YouTube在模擬器飛這段 Fly this tour

旁白全文 Full narration

頭城海岸以東約十公里，一座火山島浮出海面。它為什麼像烏龜，又為什麼還在冒煙？島嶼東西長約三點三公里，南北約一點七公里。面積二點八五平方公里，海岸線約十公里。它是一座層狀火山露出海面的頂部。岩石以安山岩質的熔岩流與火山碎屑為主。這裡位在沖繩海槽的西南端。海槽的尖端越過島嶼，延伸進蘭陽平原。中央的龜甲是主體，最高點海拔三百九十八公尺。加上山頂三公尺高的建物，得名四〇一高地。

龜甲原是一座圓錐形的火山。南半部崩落入海，留下陡直的斷崖。北側與西北側，則是熔岩流形成的扇狀坡面。東端的龜首，是另一座橢圓形的火山錐。岩質脆弱，颱風與地震時容易崩塌。二〇一五年四月，龜首在地震後崩落一角。二〇二四年四月三日的強震，岩壁再次崩塌。龜首周圍的海底，有數十個熱泉噴口。它們位在水下五到三十公尺，水溫可達攝氏一百度左右。

噴出的硫磺與氣泡，把海水染成乳白與淺藍。這片海因此被稱為「牛奶海」。龜首的岩縫間，硫氣孔仍在噴氣。二〇二一年十一月，這裡曾冒出明顯的煙柱。過去七千年，這座火山至少噴發過四次。清代文獻與一八五三年的航海紀錄，都留下相關描述。往西是低平的龜尾。一道長約一公里的安山岩礫灘，伸向海中。礫灘的位置，隨冬夏的風向南北擺動。這就是八景裡的「靈龜擺尾」。

礫灘圍住的龜尾湖，長約四百公尺、寬約二百公尺。它原是淡水湖，引入海水後成了半鹹水湖。對岸的北關，海岸是單面山與豆腐岩地形。烏石港以南，海岸則轉為沖積平原。龜首、龜甲與龜尾，分別是火山錐、崩塌的山體與礫灘。海底的熱泉說明，這座火山仍在活動。

About ten kilometres east of the Toucheng coast, a volcanic island rises from the sea. Why does it look like a turtle, and why is it still steaming? The island is about 3.3 km from east to west and 1.7 km from north to south. It covers 2.85 square kilometres, with roughly 10 km of coastline. What stands above the water is the top of a stratovolcano. Its rock is mainly andesitic lava flows and volcanic debris. The island sits at the southwestern end of the Okinawa Trough. The tip of the trough runs past it and on into the Lanyang Plain. The central "shell" is the main body, peaking at 398 metres. A three-metre structure on top gives it the name 401 Highland.

The shell was once a cone-shaped volcano. Its southern half collapsed into the sea, leaving sheer cliffs. To the north and northwest, lava flows left broad, fan-shaped slopes. The "head" at the eastern end is a separate, oval volcanic cone. Its rock is weak and prone to collapse in typhoons and earthquakes. In April 2015 part of the head fell away after a series of earthquakes. The strong earthquake of 3 April 2024 brought down more of the cliff. On the seabed around the head are dozens of hydrothermal vents. They lie 5 to 30 metres down, and the water emerges at around 100°C.

Sulphur and gas bubbles from the vents turn the sea milky white and pale blue. The patch of water is nicknamed the "Milky Sea". Fumaroles still vent from cracks in the rock of the head. In November 2021 a conspicuous plume of gas rose here. The volcano has erupted at least four times in the past 7,000 years. Qing-era accounts and a ship's report from 1853 both describe activity here. To the west lies the low, flat "tail". A beach of andesite gravel about a kilometre long reaches out into the sea. The gravel spit shifts north and south with the winter and summer winds. This is the scene known as "the turtle wagging its tail".

Enclosed by the gravel, Guiwei Lake is about 400 m long and 200 m wide. Once fresh water, it turned brackish after seawater was let in. Across the water at Beiguan, the coast is one of cuestas and "tofu" rock. South of Wushi Harbor the shore becomes alluvial plain. Head, shell and tail are, in turn, a volcanic cone, a collapsed mountain and a gravel spit. The vents on the seabed show that this volcano is still active.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

龜山島須事先向東北角及宜蘭海岸國家風景區管理處申請才能登島，島上另有不開放的管制區；官方登島規定未列出無人機專門條文。實際飛行前請用民航局「Drone Map」App 查詢並依規定申請。

Landing on Guishan Island requires prior application to the Northeast and Yilan Coast National Scenic Area Administration, and parts of the island are closed zones; the official landing rules contain no drone-specific clause. Before any real flight, check the Civil Aviation Administration "Drone Map" app and apply as required.
