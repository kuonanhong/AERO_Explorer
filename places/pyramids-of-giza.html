歷史 History

第四王朝的一百年

A Century of the Fourth Dynasty

古夫、卡夫拉、孟卡拉三位法老，與蓋了三千八百年最高紀錄的金字塔
Khufu, Khafre, Menkaure, and a building that stayed the tallest for 3,800 years

這裡是埃及吉薩，。開羅市中心西南方約十三公里。三座大金字塔與人面獅身像，。都建於古埃及第四王朝。大約是公元前二六〇〇到二五〇〇年，。古王國時期。最大的是古夫金字塔，。約建於公元前二五八〇到二五六〇年。它原本高一百四十六點六公尺，。是三千八百多年間世界上最高的人造建築。

卡夫拉的金字塔在它西南方幾百公尺，。高度略矮一些。再往西南是孟卡拉金字塔，。約在公元前二五一〇年完成。東側的人面獅身像，。一般認為是卡夫拉在位時刻成的。誰蓋了金字塔？希羅多德在公元前四五〇年聽說是四十萬人、二十年。墓葬證據則顯示，。大約一萬名工人輪班三個月，一座要約三十年。

考古學家馬克・萊納的團隊，。在卡夫拉與孟卡拉金字塔東南方找到工人村。吉薩是整片金字塔區最北的一段；。往南還有薩卡拉與達舒爾。1979年，從吉薩到達舒爾的金字塔區，。連同孟菲斯列入世界遺產。四千五百年前的一個王朝，。在沙漠邊緣留下了這片地景。

This is Giza in Egypt, about 13 kilometres south-west of central Cairo. The three great pyramids and the Sphinx all date from the Fourth Dynasty. That is roughly 2600 to 2500 BC, in the Old Kingdom. The largest is the Great Pyramid of Khufu, built around 2580 to 2560 BC. It once stood 146.6 metres tall, the tallest human-made structure for more than 3,800 years.

Khafre's pyramid stands a few hundred metres to the south-west, slightly smaller. Farther south-west is Menkaure's pyramid, completed around 2510 BC. On the east side, the Sphinx is thought to date from Khafre's reign. Who built them? Herodotus, visiting in 450 BC, was told 400,000 men had worked for 20 years. Tomb evidence points instead to about 10,000 labourers in three-month shifts, about 30 years per pyramid.

Mark Lehner's team found the workers' village south-east of the Khafre and Menkaure complexes. Giza is the northernmost part of the pyramid fields; Saqqara and Dahshur lie to the south. In 1979 the pyramid fields from Giza to Dahshur, with Memphis, became World Heritage. A dynasty of four and a half thousand years ago left this landscape on the edge of the desert.

人文 Culture

給來世的船與王后

Boats and Queens for the Afterlife

王后金字塔、太陽船與人面獅身像的鼻子
Queens' pyramids, Khufu's boat and the Sphinx's missing nose

金字塔是法老的陵墓，。周圍還有一整套為來世準備的設施。古夫金字塔旁有三座王后金字塔，。編號G1-a、G1-b、G1-c。還有三個船坑；。南側兩個坑裡，發現時船還完整。修復後的古夫之船，。如今在大埃及博物館展出。卡夫拉的金字塔有五個船坑，。還有一座附屬金字塔。

孟卡拉金字塔旁，。也有三座王后金字塔。人面獅身像長七十三公尺、高二十公尺，。從岩盤直接刻出來。它面朝正東，。臉孔一般認為是卡夫拉。鼻子是被人用棍棒或鑿子刻意去掉的，。不是拿破崙軍隊的砲彈打掉的。拿破崙之前的畫作就已經沒有鼻子，。十五世紀的史學家馬克里齊也提過。

三座金字塔的四邊，。都精準對齊東西南北。古夫金字塔各邊的平均偏差，。只有約三分三十八秒角，約十分之一度。往東北十多公里，。就是開羅市中心。城市與陵墓，。如今只隔著幾公里。

The pyramids are royal tombs, surrounded by a whole set of buildings for the afterlife. Beside Khufu's pyramid are three queens' pyramids, numbered G1-a, G1-b and G1-c. There are also three boat pits; the two on the south side held intact ships when excavated. The restored Khufu ship is now in the Grand Egyptian Museum. Khafre's pyramid has five boat pits and a subsidiary pyramid.

Menkaure's pyramid, too, has three queens' pyramids beside it. The Sphinx is 73 metres long and 20 metres high, carved out of the bedrock. It faces due east; its face appears to represent Khafre. Its nose was deliberately removed with rods or chisels, not shot off by Napoleon's troops. Artwork from before Napoleon already shows it missing, and the 15th-century historian al-Maqrizi mentioned it.

The sides of all three pyramids are aligned to the cardinal directions with great precision. The Great Pyramid's sides deviate on average by only 3 minutes 38 seconds of arc, about a tenth of a degree. A little over ten kilometres to the north-east is central Cairo. The modern city and the tombs are now only a few kilometres apart.

自然 Nature

沙漠邊緣的高原

A Plateau on the Edge of the Desert

尼羅河、石灰岩高原與從南方延伸過來的金字塔群
The Nile, a limestone plateau, and pyramid fields stretching south

金字塔蓋在吉薩高原上，。離尼羅河約九公里。往西是沙漠，往東是河谷與城市。高原的岩層是石灰岩，。大部分石塊就地取自穆卡塔姆層。古夫金字塔外層原本包著。圖拉出產的細白石灰岩。中世紀時外殼大多被拆走，。底部還留著一些。

卡夫拉金字塔的頂端，。還保留著原本的外殼石。孟卡拉金字塔則已完全看不到原本的外殼。沿著沙漠邊緣往南，。金字塔一路延伸到薩卡拉。左塞爾金字塔在薩卡拉，。離吉薩約十五公里。東邊的河谷裡，。是古都孟菲斯。

金字塔都蓋在河谷西側的沙漠邊上，。都城則在河谷裡。再往南是達舒爾，。有紅色金字塔與彎曲金字塔。世界遺產的範圍有一萬六千公頃，。從吉薩一直延伸到達舒爾。綠色的河谷與黃色的沙漠，。界線就是金字塔所在的地方。

The pyramids stand on the Giza plateau, about nine kilometres west of the Nile. West lies the desert; east, the river valley and the city. The plateau is limestone; most of the pyramid stone came from the underlying Mokattam Formation. The Great Pyramid was once cased in fine white limestone from Tura. Most of the casing was stripped in the Middle Ages; some casing stones remain at the base.

Khafre's pyramid still keeps some of its casing stones at the apex. Menkaure's pyramid is the only one seen today without its original casing. Along the edge of the desert to the south, the pyramids continue to Saqqara. The Pyramid of Djoser stands at Saqqara, about fifteen kilometres from Giza. In the valley to the east lay the ancient capital, Memphis.

The pyramids were built on the desert edge west of the valley; the capital lay in the valley itself. Farther south is Dahshur, with the Red Pyramid and the Bent Pyramid. The World Heritage property covers 16,000 hectares, from Giza all the way to Dahshur. Green valley and yellow desert: the pyramids stand right on the line between them.

地理 Geography

兩百三十萬塊石頭

Two Point Three Million Blocks

古夫金字塔的尺寸、石材，與九百公里外運來的花崗岩
The Great Pyramid's measurements, its stone, and granite from 900 km away

這部影片用數字來看古夫金字塔。底座是正方形，。每邊約二百三十點三公尺。原高一百四十六點六公尺，。現在約一百三十八點五公尺。估計用了兩百三十萬塊大石，。總重約六百萬公噸。工期大約二十六年。

外殼最底層的石塊高一點四九公尺，。上層每塊約五百公斤。國王墓室的花崗岩，。來自九百多公里外的亞斯文。最大的花崗岩塊重二十五到八十公噸，。架在墓室與減壓室的頂上。內部的大走廊高八點六公尺，。長四十六點六八公尺。卡夫拉金字塔的高度與體積，。都比古夫金字塔略小一些。

孟卡拉金字塔的規模就小得多。人面獅身像寬十九公尺，。後來又用石灰岩塊補砌修復。整個高原在開羅西南約十三公里，。尼羅河以西約九公里。兩百三十萬塊石頭疊成的形狀，。從空中看，仍然是一個完美的正方形。

This film looks at the Great Pyramid through its numbers. Its base is a square about 230.3 metres on each side. It was 146.6 metres high; today it is about 138.5 metres. It is estimated at 2.3 million large blocks, weighing about 6 million tonnes in all. Construction took about 26 years.

The lowest casing course is 1.49 metres tall; upper casing stones weigh around 500 kilograms. The granite of the King's Chamber came from Aswan, more than 900 kilometres south. The largest granite blocks weigh 25 to 80 tonnes and roof the King's Chamber and the relieving chambers. Inside, the Grand Gallery is 8.6 metres high and 46.68 metres long. Khafre's pyramid is slightly smaller in height and volume than its neighbour.

Menkaure's pyramid is relatively modest in size. The Sphinx is 19 metres wide; it was later restored with layers of limestone blocks. The whole plateau lies about 13 kilometres south-west of central Cairo and 9 kilometres west of the Nile. From the air, a shape stacked from 2.3 million stones still reads as a square.
