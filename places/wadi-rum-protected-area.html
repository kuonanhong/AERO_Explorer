歷史 History

沙漠裡的一萬兩千年

Twelve Thousand Years in the Desert

兩萬五千幅岩畫、納巴泰神殿、勞倫斯與阿拉伯起義，到2011年的世界遺產

這裡是約旦南部的瓦地倫，。靠近沙烏地阿拉伯邊界，在阿卡巴東邊大約六十公里。它也叫「月亮谷」；。「瓦地倫」在阿拉伯文裡，是「細沙之谷」的意思。人類在這裡生活，至少已經一萬兩千年；。留下了兩萬五千幅岩畫，和兩萬條銘文。一百五十四處考古遺址，從新石器時代到納巴泰時期；。銘文用了四種文字，包括薩姆德文、納巴泰文和阿拉伯文。這些岩畫與銘文，。記錄了早期字母的發展。

青銅時代之後，氣候變得更乾，。人們用集水設施，在沙漠裡繼續生活。納巴泰神殿在1933年被發現，。1997年，一支法國團隊完成了發掘。艾因沙拉萊泉邊，有納巴泰人的銘文；。T・E・勞倫斯，曾經描寫過這道泉水。1917到1918年的阿拉伯起義期間，。勞倫斯好幾次經過瓦地倫。一座原名「馬茲馬爾山」的岩峰，。在1980年代，以他的書改名為「智慧七柱」。

1949年，謝赫・哈姆丹帶著測量員登上倫山；。1952年11月，兩位女性完成了歐洲人的首次登頂。薩拉比亞貝都因人在1980年前後來到這裡，。今天村子裡有學校，也有沙漠巡邏隊的總部。南邊的烏姆達米山，是約旦的最高點；。最早找到它的，是薩拉比亞人迪法拉・阿提格。1997年，這裡成為保護區，2002年擴大；。2011年，列入世界遺產，是自然與文化的雙重遺產。從新石器時代的獵人，到今天的貝都因人，。一萬兩千年來，人們都在這片沙漠裡留下了名字。

This is Wadi Rum in southern Jordan, near the Saudi border, about 60 km east of Aqaba. It is also called the Valley of the Moon; the Arabic name means "valley of light, airborne sand". People have lived here for at least 12,000 years, leaving 25,000 rock carvings and 20,000 inscriptions. Its 154 archaeological sites run from the Neolithic to the Nabataean period; the inscriptions use four scripts, including Thamudic, Nabataean and Arabic. These carvings and inscriptions record the early development of the alphabet.

After the Bronze Age the climate grew drier, and people used water catchment systems to live on in the desert. The Nabataean temple was discovered in 1933, and a French team completed its excavation in 1997. The Ain Shalaaleh spring carries Nabataean inscriptions; T. E. Lawrence described it. During the Arab Revolt of 1917–18 Lawrence passed through Wadi Rum several times. A rock formation once called Jabal al-Mazmar was renamed the Seven Pillars of Wisdom in the 1980s, after Lawrence's book.

In 1949 Sheikh Hamdan guided surveyors to the summit of Jabal Rum; in November 1952 Charmian Longstaff and Sylvia Branford made the first recorded European ascent. The Zalabieh Bedouin arrived around 1980; today the village has schools and the Desert Patrol headquarters. Jabal Umm ad Dami to the south is Jordan's highest point; it was first located by Difallah Ateeg of the Zalabia. The protected area was set up in 1997 and extended in 2002; in 2011 it became a mixed natural and cultural World Heritage site. From Neolithic hunters to the Bedouin of today, people have left their names in this desert for twelve thousand years.

人文 Culture

電影、攀岩與沙漠營地

Films, Climbs and Desert Camps

《阿拉伯的勞倫斯》帶起的觀光、火星電影外景、貝都因攀岩路線與沙漠營地
The tourism Lawrence of Arabia began, a stand-in for Mars, Bedouin climbing routes and more and more camps

1962年的電影《阿拉伯的勞倫斯》，在這裡拍攝沙漠場景，。帶起了約旦的觀光業。這裡常被用來拍以火星為背景的科幻電影，。包括《絕地救援》。《星際大戰外傳：俠盜一號》也在瓦地倫拍攝；。約旦電影委員會因此在2017年得到國際外景協會的獎。2017年，有十六萬兩千名遊客來到瓦地倫。貝都因人世世代代攀爬這些砂岩，。他們的「貝都因之路」，後來被攀岩者一條條記錄下來。

1984年，英國攀岩者在這裡開出第一批有紀錄的路線；。1987年，第一本攀岩指南出版。倫山上大約有十條攀爬路線，。最受歡迎的是「薩姆德路線」和「謝赫哈姆丹路線」。2000年的「聖戰」，是瓦地倫第一條全程打釘的路線，。長四百五十公尺。東南邊的布爾達石橋，估計高三十五公尺，。跨過一道深溝，人可以走過它的頂端。上去的路很難走，有陡坡和兩段暴露的煙囪地形；。路線沒有標示，建議請貝都因嚮導帶路。

烏姆弗魯斯石橋是一座獨立的石拱，。估計高十五公尺，可以從斜坡爬上頂端。2023年12月的調查，有二百二十九座營運中的沙漠營地；。2019年，估計有大約四百座沒有執照。2011年有五百到一千輛四輪傳動車在沙漠裡跑；。2019年的非正式估計，是一千到一千五百輛。保護區有八十多名員工，很多是當地的貝都因人；。由阿卡巴經濟特區管理局經營。電影、攀岩與營地，。讓這片沙漠成了全世界的舞台。

The desert scenes of Lawrence of Arabia (1962), filmed here, kick-started Jordan's tourism industry. It is often used for science-fiction films set on Mars, among them The Martian. Rogue One was also filmed in Wadi Rum, and in 2017 Jordan's Royal Film Commission won an LMGI award for it. In 2017, 162,000 tourists visited Wadi Rum. The Bedouin have climbed this sandstone for generations, and modern climbers have documented their "Bedouin Roads".

The first recorded rock climbs were made in 1984 by English climbers; the first climbing guidebook came out in 1987. About ten scrambling routes cross Jabal Rum; the most popular are the Thamudic and Sheikh Hamdan's routes. Guerre Sainte, climbed in 2000, was the first fully bolted route in Wadi Rum, 450 metres long. To the southeast, the Rock Bridge of Burdah, about 35 metres high, spans a deep gully, and you can walk across its top. The hike is hard, with steep sections and two exposed chimneys; the routes are unmarked, so Bedouin guides are recommended.

The Rock Bridge of Um Fruth stands on its own, about 15 metres high, with its top reached by slabs. A December 2023 baseline counted 229 active desert camps; in 2019 about 400 were estimated to be unlicensed. Some 500 to 1,000 four-wheel-drive vehicles were operating in 2011; informal reports suggest 1,000 to 1,500 in 2019. The protected area has more than 80 staff, many of them local Bedouin; it is run by the Aqaba Special Economic Zone Authority. Films, climbs and camps have made this desert a stage for the world.

瓦地倫一年的雨量，只有五十到一百毫米，。雨都在冬天落下。雨多半來自雷雨和山洪；。嚴重的乾旱，越來越常發生。這裡記錄到一百八十三種植物、二十六種哺乳動物，。三十四種爬蟲類，和一百一十九種鳥。一百一十九種鳥裡，。三十四種在這裡繁殖，八十五種是過境的候鳥。倫山的山區住著努比亞羱羊，。是易危的物種。

2005年，在阿布達比環境署協助下，。阿拉伯大羚羊重新被引進這裡。這裡也記錄到獰貓、灰狼和條紋鬣狗；。沙貓和阿拉伯豹，不久之前也還在這一帶出沒。這一帶看得到黑鵰，。還有中東特有的西奈朱雀和休氏林鴞。沙丘植被佔了這裡大約六成，。以白梭梭為主，大多在北部、東部和中部。西邊和南邊是礫石荒漠，。岩屑坡上長著金合歡。

在峭壁的頂上，。還留著稀疏的腓尼基刺柏林。2000年以後，駱駝越來越多，。放牧的壓力，在局部地區變得嚴重。世界自然保護聯盟2025年的評估：。前景「良好，但有些問題要注意」。緩衝區正在擴大兩倍以上，。到一千三百四十一平方公里。在一年不到一百毫米的雨裡，。羱羊、羚羊和刺柏，仍然找到了活下去的方法。

Wadi Rum receives only 50 to 100 mm of rain a year, falling in winter. Most of it comes from thunderstorms and flash floods, and serious droughts have become frequent. Some 183 plant species, 26 mammals, 34 reptiles and 119 bird species have been recorded here. Of the 119 birds, 34 breed here and 85 are migrants. Nubian ibex live in the Rum mountains, a species listed as Vulnerable.

In 2005 Arabian oryx were reintroduced here, with help from the Abu Dhabi environment agency. Caracal, grey wolf and striped hyena have been recorded; sand cat and Arabian leopard were here not long ago. Verreaux's eagles are seen in the area, along with the Sinai rosefinch and Hume's tawny owl of the Middle East. Sand-dune vegetation covers about 60% of the site, dominated by white saxaul, mostly in the north, east and centre. Stony desert lies to the west and south, and acacias grow on the rocky slopes.

On the cliff tops a sparse woodland of relict Phoenician juniper survives. Camel numbers have risen since 2000, and grazing is becoming locally serious. IUCN's 2025 assessment rated the outlook as "good, with some concerns". The buffer zone is being enlarged by more than 200%, to 1,341 square kilometres. With less than 100 mm of rain a year, ibex, oryx and junipers still find a way to live.

地理 Geography

Half a Billion Years of Sandstone

Sandstone 500 million years old, the Arabian Shield, honeycomb weathering, rock bridges and Jordan’s summit

瓦地倫面積大約七百二十平方公里，。是約旦最大的乾谷。山壁是倫群的石英砂岩，。在五億四千萬到四億七千萬年前沉積下來。底下是阿拉伯地盾前寒武紀的基盤，。由深成岩和變質岩組成。寒武紀的薩拉布砂岩，是四十到六十公尺高的基座；。上面的烏姆伊什林砂岩，形成了垂直的峭壁。烏姆伊什林砂岩富含鐵質、很耐侵蝕，。奧陶紀的迪西砂岩，蓋在山頂。

地下水積在砂岩和花崗岩的交界，。在山的東坡湧出成泉。這一帶到今天還在抬升，。大約每一千年七十毫米。風雕出了蜂窩狀的風化孔、天然石橋，。還有新月形沙丘和爬升沙丘。周圍的山，大多高出地面約四百公尺，。底部是花崗岩或紫色砂岩，山頂是白色砂岩。倫山海拔一千七百三十四公尺，。是約旦第二高的山。

倫村南邊大約三十公里的烏姆達米山，。海拔一千八百五十四公尺，是約旦的最高點。它就在沙烏地阿拉伯邊界附近，。位在世界遺產的緩衝區裡。七月平均最高溫三十四點六度，。一月平均最低溫四點六度。五億年的砂岩，加上風與水，。雕出了這座月亮般的山谷。

Wadi Rum covers about 720 square kilometres, the largest wadi in Jordan. The cliffs are Rum Group quartz sandstones, laid down 540 to 470 million years ago. Beneath lies the Precambrian basement of the Arabian Shield, made of plutonic and metamorphic rock. The Lower Cambrian Salab sandstone forms a 40 to 60 metre plinth; the Umm Ishrin sandstone above it forms the sheer cliffs. The Umm Ishrin sandstone is iron-rich and resists erosion, and the Ordovician Disi sandstone caps the buttes.

Groundwater gathers where the sandstone meets the granite and emerges as springs on the eastern slopes. The area is still rising, at about 70 mm every thousand years. The wind has carved honeycomb weathering and natural bridges, along with barchan and climbing dunes. Most of the surrounding mountains rise about 400 metres, on bases of granite or purple sandstone with white sandstone summits. Jabal Rum rises to 1,734 metres, Jordan's second-highest peak.

Jabal Umm ad Dami, about 30 km south of Rum village, reaches 1,854 metres, Jordan's highest point. It stands near the border with Saudi Arabia, within the World Heritage buffer zone. In July the mean daily maximum is 34.6 °C; in January the mean minimum is 4.6 °C. Half a billion years of sandstone, worked by wind and water, have carved this valley of the moon.

Jordan: all drones must be registered with the Civil Aviation Regulatory Commission (CARC), and flights need Ministry of Interior approval, applied for through CARC at least 10 working days ahead; stay below 400 ft and within 500 m of the approved location at the approved times; operators must be at least 21 and insured. Amendments in force from 2025 simplify some licensing. Check the current rules with CARC before any real flight.
