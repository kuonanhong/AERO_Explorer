歷史 History

草山、硫磺與行館

Grass Mountain, Sulfur and a Guesthouse

從採硫的山，到離城市最近的國家公園
From a sulfur mountain to the national park nearest the city

臺北盆地北邊的這片山，。以前叫做「草山」。相傳官府為了防人盜採硫磺而放火燒山，。山上只剩草，不長樹。硫磺在明、清兩代，。就有人開採與買賣。十八世紀，漢人開始移入這片山區。十九世紀末，。這一帶出現了溫泉旅館與俱樂部。

1937年12月27日，。日本在這裡設立大屯國立公園。日治時期，擎天崗被劃為大嶺峠牧場，。用來種茶與放牛。戰後，政府在擎天崗與冷水坑一帶。設立陽明山牧場。1950年，為了紀念明代學者王陽明，。「草山」改名「陽明山」。中山樓，過去是國民大會開會的地方。

陽明書屋原名中興賓館，1969到1970年興建，。是蔣中正在臺灣唯一親自選定興建的行館。1962年開始規劃國家公園，。最初的範圍有兩萬八千四百公頃。1985年9月16日，陽明山國家公園成立，。面積約一萬一千三百公頃。2020年6月，這裡獲認證為世界第一座「都會寧靜公園」。

These hills on the northern edge of the Taipei Basin used to be called Grass Mountain. Officials are said to have burned the slopes to deter sulfur thieves, leaving grass but no trees. Sulfur was mined and traded here during the Ming and Qing dynasties. In the eighteenth century Han Chinese began to settle these hills. By the late nineteenth century hot-spring clubs and resorts had appeared in the area.

On 27 December 1937 Japan established Daiton National Park here. Under Japanese rule Qingtiangang was made the Dalingzhi Ranch, for growing tea and grazing cattle. After the war the government set up the Yangmingshan Ranch around Qingtiangang and Lengshuikeng. In 1950, in honour of the Ming scholar Wang Yangming, Grass Mountain was renamed Yangmingshan. The Chung-Shan Building was once the meeting place of the National Assembly.

Yangming Shuwu, first called the Zhongxing Guesthouse, was built in 1969 and 1970, the only residence in Taiwan whose site Chiang Kai-shek chose himself. Planning for a national park began in 1962, with an initial area of 28,400 hectares. On 16 September 1985 Yangmingshan National Park was established, covering about 11,300 hectares. In June 2020 it was certified as the world's first Urban Quiet Park.

人文 Culture

城市的後山

The City's Back Mountain

牧場、行館與走得到的火山
A ranch, a guesthouse and volcanoes within walking reach

陽明山，是距離都會核心最近的國家公園。範圍橫跨臺北市的北投、士林，。和新北市的萬里、金山、三芝、淡水。陽明公園距離市區大約十七公里。1950年改名之後，。政府設立陽明山管理局，開闢了這座公園。陽明書屋過去用來接待國內外貴賓，。也是夏天避暑的地方。

現在它是國家公園的人文史蹟，。也是環境教育的場地。竹子湖海拔約650到670公尺，。在公園的核心區。北有小觀音山、東有七星山、西有大屯山，。往南則敞開，俯瞰臺北盆地。往東越過七星山，。是擎天崗草原。擎天崗的牧場後來由臺北市農會經營，。牛隻屬於北投、士林與金山的農民。

草原上現在還有牛，。公園提醒遊客要保持安全距離。夢幻湖有觀景臺與步道，。路程不長，適合親子健行。擎天崗，是公園裡最受歡迎的健行地點。春有花季、夏有山嵐濃霧，。臺北人的四季，在這座山上分得清楚。

Yangmingshan is the national park closest to the city's core. It spans Beitou and Shilin in Taipei and Wanli, Jinshan, Sanzhi and Tamsui in New Taipei. Yangming Park is about 17 kilometres from the city centre. After the renaming of 1950, a Yangmingshan administration was set up and laid out this park. Yangming Shuwu once received guests from home and abroad and served as a summer retreat.

Today it is one of the park's historic sites and a place for environmental education. Zhuzihu, at about 650 to 670 metres, lies in the park's core area. Xiaoguanyin to the north, Qixing to the east, Datun to the west; southward it opens over the Taipei Basin. East across Qixing Mountain lies the grassland of Qingtiangang. The ranch at Qingtiangang was later run by the Taipei City Farmers' Association; the herds belonged to farmers of Beitou, Shilin and Jinshan.

Cattle still roam the grassland, and the park asks visitors to keep a safe distance. Menghuan Pond has a viewing deck and a short trail, an easy walk for families. Qingtiangang is the park's most popular hiking destination. Flowers in spring, mist and fog in summer: on this mountain Taipei's seasons are easy to tell apart.

自然 Nature

芒草、水韭與一年兩百天的雨

Grassland, Quillwort and Two Hundred Days of Rain

雲霧裡的火山，長著什麼、住著什麼
What grows and lives on volcanoes wrapped in cloud

陽明山國家公園的海拔，。從兩百公尺到一千一百二十公尺。鞍部氣象站的年雨量約四千七百公釐，。一年有兩百零五天下雨。年平均相對濕度將近九成，。全年日照只有九百五十多個小時。七星山東南側的夢幻湖，。是國家級重要濕地。湖裡長著臺灣水韭，。一種只在臺灣才有的水生蕨類。

湖面常因雲霧飄過。而顯得朦朧。公園裡記錄到1,360種維管束植物。擎天崗是起伏平緩的草原，。草地之間夾著低矮的灌木。這裡記錄到168種蝴蝶。與122種鳥。在火山噴氣口附近，。火山氣體讓植物無法生長。

園區裡的小油坑，海拔約805公尺，。有噴氣孔與硫磺結晶。春天是公園的花季，。櫻花是其中一景。這裡的最低溫紀錄，。是零下3.7度。雨、霧與火山的熱，。一起決定了這片山上長什麼。

Yangmingshan National Park rises from 200 to 1,120 metres above the sea. The Anbu weather station records about 4,700 millimetres of rain a year, falling on 205 days. Relative humidity averages nearly 90 per cent, and the sun shines for only some 950 hours a year. Menghuan Pond, on the south-east side of Qixing, is a wetland of national importance. In it grows the Taiwan quillwort, an aquatic fern found only in Taiwan.

Drifting cloud and mist often veil the water. The park has recorded 1,360 species of vascular plants. Qingtiangang is gently rolling grassland dotted with low shrubs. The park lists 168 butterfly species and 122 kinds of birds. Close to the volcanic vents, the gases kill the vegetation.

Xiaoyoukeng, at about 805 metres, has fumaroles and sulfur crystals. Spring is the flower season in the park, with cherry blossom among its sights. The lowest temperature recorded here is minus 3.7°C. Rain, mist and volcanic heat together decide what grows on these hills.

地理 Geography

臺北頭頂上的火山群

The Volcanoes above Taipei

大屯火山群：二十多座火山，與還在冒氣的地底
The Tatun group: twenty volcanoes and a ground that still steams

大屯火山群在臺北市北邊約十五公里，。有二十座左右的火山。它位在琉球島弧的西端，。是板塊隱沒帶上的火山。火山活動主要發生在280萬到20萬年前；。岩石以安山岩質為主。七星山海拔1,120公尺，。是臺北市的最高點。它大約七十萬年前開始噴發；。山頂原有火山口，侵蝕後留下七座小峰。

南側的紗帽山是一座圓形的熔岩穹丘，。看起來像一頂烏紗帽。紗帽山與七股山，。都是七星山的寄生火山。冷水坑曾是火山爆裂口，。泉源附近的岩石因地熱而變成黏土。擎天崗，是竹篙山熔岩形成的階地。竹子湖原本是三十五萬年前火山噴發造成的堰塞湖，。後來缺口被侵蝕，湖水流光。

大屯山，是火山群裡第三大的山體。2019到2022年的觀測顯示，。地底的岩漿熱液系統仍在釋放氣體。研究認為，七星山、大油坑與磺嘴山，。是未來可能噴發的地點。中央研究院在公園裡設有大屯火山觀測站，。持續監測這些火山。

The Tatun Volcanic Group lies about 15 kilometres north of Taipei and has some twenty volcanoes. It stands at the western end of the Ryukyu Arc, above a subduction zone. Most of its eruptions came between 2.8 and 0.2 million years ago; the rock is mainly andesitic. Qixing Mountain, 1,120 metres, is the highest point of Taipei City. It began erupting about 700,000 years ago; its summit crater has eroded into seven small peaks.

Shamao Mountain to the south is a round lava dome shaped like an official's gauze cap. Shamao and Qigu are both parasitic volcanoes of Qixing. Lengshuikeng was once an explosion crater; near its springs, heat has turned the rock to clay. Qingtiangang is a terrace formed by lava from Zhugao Mountain. Zhuzihu was a lake dammed by an eruption 350,000 years ago; erosion breached it and the water drained away.

Datun Mountain is the third largest edifice in the group. Observations from 2019 to 2022 show a magmatic-hydrothermal system still degassing below. Studies name Qixing, Dayoukeng and Huangzui as the places where a future eruption could occur. Academia Sinica keeps the Tatun volcano observatory in the park to monitor these volcanoes.
