歷史 History

祖靈之山

Mountain of the Ancestors

這裡是婆羅洲北部、馬來西亞沙巴的京那巴魯山，。海拔四千零九十五公尺，是馬來西亞和海上東南亞的最高峰。1997年用衛星重新測量，。比原本公布的四千一百零一公尺，矮了六公尺。「京那巴魯」可能來自「阿基・納巴魯」，。意思是「納巴魯祖父」，守護這座山的靈。卡達山杜順族的利灣人，叫它「偉大之名」，。每年都有朝山的儀式。1851年3月，英國人休・羅，。跟著吉奧村的杜順族嚮導拉邁英，爬上了山頂高原。

他沒有登上最高點，。但最高峰，後來還是以他命名為「羅氏峰」。1888年，動物學家約翰・懷海德，。第一個登上了最高點。1910年2月，植物學家莉蓮・吉布斯，。成為第一位登頂的女性，也是第一位登頂的植物學家。1961和1964年，英國皇家學會的考察隊來到這裡；。1964年，京那巴魯公園成立，是馬來西亞最早的國家公園之一。山腳下的昆達山戰爭紀念園，建於1962年，。紀念從山打根到拉瑙的三次死亡行軍。

兩千四百二十八名澳洲和英國戰俘死在戰俘營與行軍途中；。大約兩千五百名戰俘裡，只有六名澳洲人活了下來。西邊的首府原名「傑瑟頓」，1899年建立；。1967年12月22日，以這座山改名為「哥打京那巴魯」。2000年12月，京那巴魯公園列入世界遺產，。是馬來西亞的第一個世界遺產。2015年6月5日早上七點十五分，一場地震引發山崩，。十八名登山客和嚮導罹難，「驢耳峰」嚴重受損。對許多山下的人來說，。這是一座有守護靈的聖山。

This is Mount Kinabalu in Sabah, Malaysia, in northern Borneo: at 4,095 metres the highest peak in Malaysia and Maritime Southeast Asia. A satellite re-survey in 1997 found it 6 metres lower than the published 4,101 metres. Kinabalu probably comes from Aki Nabalu, "Grandfather Nabalu", the mountain's guardian spirit. The Liwan Kadazandusun call it Gayo Ngaran, "The Great Name", and make a pilgrimage to it every year. In March 1851 Hugh Low reached the summit plateau with the Dusun guide Lemaing of Kampung Kiau.

He did not reach the highest point, but the highest peak was later named Low's Peak after him. In 1888 the zoologist John Whitehead became the first to reach the highest point. In February 1910 Lilian Gibbs became the first woman and the first botanist to reach the summit. Royal Society expeditions came in 1961 and 1964; in 1964 Kinabalu Park was founded, one of Malaysia's first national parks. The Kundasang War Memorial below the mountain was opened in 1962 to remember the three death marches from Sandakan to Ranau.

It names 2,428 Australian and British prisoners who died in the camp and on the marches; of about 2,500 prisoners, only six Australians survived. The capital to the west, founded as Jesselton in 1899, was renamed Kota Kinabalu after the mountain on 22 December 1967. In December 2000 Kinabalu Park became World Heritage, Malaysia's first World Heritage Site. At 7:15 am on 5 June 2015 an earthquake and the landslides it set off killed 18 hikers and guides and badly damaged Donkey Ear's Peak. To many people below, this is a sacred mountain with a guardian spirit.

爬京那巴魯山，不需要登山裝備，。但一定要有合格的嚮導陪同。步道從海拔一千八百六十六公尺的廷波洪門出發，。離公園總部五點五公里。多數人走三到六小時，到海拔3,270公尺的拉班拉打山屋過夜；。挑夫背著補給上山，有時超過三十五公斤。最後兩公里，走在光禿的花崗岩上，。要花兩到四個小時。只有住在山屋的人，才能拿到登頂許可；。2025年的評估說，每天的上限是一百八十八人。

1987年起，這裡舉辦京那巴魯山登山賽跑，。1988年成為國際賽事。2010年，公園有六十一萬多名訪客，。其中四萬七千六百一十三人是登山客。2022年有五十二萬九千多人，2023年是三十二萬四千多人；。2016年前後，山上開了兩條岩壁攀登路線。公園總部海拔一千五百六十三公尺，離亞庇約九十公里；。公園由沙巴公園局管理。昆達山海拔一千九百公尺，。被稱為馬來西亞最高的聚落，2023年約有一萬人。

它以蔬菜市集聞名，木頭攤位沿著大路排開；。買家從沙巴、砂拉越和汶萊來批貨。紀念園有四座花園，代表死者的家鄉；。英國玫瑰園的黑色大理石上，刻著六百四十一個名字。婆羅洲花園，紀念幫助過戰俘的沙巴原住民，。也種著稀有的羅斯柴爾德拖鞋蘭。1945年的轟炸後，傑瑟頓只剩三棟建築；。2020年，亞庇市區有五十萬零四百二十一人。嚮導、挑夫與山下的居民，。讓每一位登山客，都能走到這座山的頂端。

Climbing Kinabalu needs no mountaineering gear, but an accredited guide is mandatory. The trail starts at Timpohon Gate, at 1,866 metres, 5.5 km from park headquarters. Most climbers take 3 to 6 hours to reach the Laban Rata resthouse at 3,270 metres; porters carry the supplies, sometimes over 35 kg. The final 2 km cross bare granite and take 2 to 4 hours. Summit permits go only to climbers staying in the mountain huts; the 2025 assessment gives a cap of 188 climbers a day.

The Mount Kinabalu Climbathon has been run since 1987 and became international in 1988. In 2010 the park had 611,624 visitors, of whom 47,613 were climbers. Visitors numbered 529,963 in 2022 and 324,757 in 2023; two via ferrata routes were opened on the mountain around 2016. Park headquarters stands at 1,563 metres, about 90 km from Kota Kinabalu; the park is run by Sabah Parks. Kundasang, at 1,900 metres, is called the highest settlement in Malaysia; about 10,000 people lived there in 2023.

It is known for its vegetable market of wooden stalls along the main road, with bulk buyers from Sabah, Sarawak and Brunei. The memorial has four gardens for the homelands of the dead; the English Rose Garden lists 641 British servicemen on black marble. The Borneo Garden honours the people of Sabah who helped the prisoners, and grows rare orchids such as Rothschild's slipper orchid. After the bombing of 1945 only three buildings were left standing in Jesselton; in 2020 the city had 500,421 people. Guides, porters and the people who live below the mountain help every climber on the way to its summit.

Kinabalu Park holds 5,000 to 6,000 species of vascular plants, at least half of all Borneo's plant species. There are about 1,000 orchid species, and over 600 ferns, fifty of them found nowhere else. Thirteen species of Nepenthes grow on the mountain, five found only here; Nepenthes rajah has the largest pitchers in the world. The flowers of Rafflesia keithii can reach 94 cm across. There are 78 species of fig, 14 of them endemic, and 24 rhododendrons, five found only here.

Lowland forest grows up to about 1,500 metres, and montane rain forest from about 1,400 to 2,900 metres. Sub-alpine scrub grows from 2,600 to 3,200 metres; above 3,500 metres it is too harsh for trees. Some 326 bird species have been recorded in the park, and 24 live mainly on the mountain. The Kinabalu serpent eagle is critically endangered. There are about a hundred mammal species, including an estimated 25 to 120 Bornean orangutans.

At least 26 endemic land snails live in the summit zone, and the park is home to the Kinabalu giant earthworm. Drought and fire are the main threats from climate change; traditional cropping has encroached on 5 to 15% of the site. A dandelion-like weed, Hypochaeris radicata, is widespread and rated a high threat. A study linked traffic noise near the main access roads to effects on bird populations. From lowland rainforest to the rocks of the summit, one mountain holds half of Borneo's plants.

京那巴魯山的突出度，也是四千零九十五公尺，。在全世界排第二十。它是地球上第三高的島嶼山峰，。離它最近的更高山峰，在兩千五百一十三公里外。山的核心，是一塊花崗閃長岩的深成岩體，。侵入始新世到中新世的地層裡。這些花崗岩大約在一千萬年前冷卻變硬，。直到今天，山還以每年大約五毫米的速度長高。大約十萬年前的更新世，。冰河曾經覆蓋這座山。

北側的羅氏峽谷，深一千八百公尺；。1998年，才有一支二十七人的隊伍，第一次完整下到谷底。2022年10月，京那巴魯山的新近紀花崗岩，。列入國際地質科學聯合會的地質遺產名單。山頂在十二月到一月，氣溫是零下四度到八度，。六月到九月是三度到十二度。山頂曾在1975、1993和2022年下雪；。海拔三千七百公尺以上，地面經常結霜。公園總部一年的雨量，大約兩千七百毫米；。總部在海拔一千五百六十三公尺。

公園面積七百五十四平方公里，。從低地龍腦香林到山頂，跨越四個氣候帶。從海邊的亞庇看過去，。京那巴魯山在東北東方大約五十公里處。2015年的地震之後，登頂步道改了路線；。梅西勞步道，從那時起一直關閉。冷卻的岩漿、冰河與地震，。一起雕出了這座還在長高的山。

Kinabalu's prominence is also 4,095 metres, 20th in the world. It is the third-highest island peak on Earth, and the nearest higher peak is 2,513 km away. Its core is a granodiorite pluton that pushed into Eocene to Miocene strata. The granite cooled and hardened about 10 million years ago, and the mountain is still rising about 5 mm a year. About 100,000 years ago, in the Pleistocene, glaciers covered the mountain.

Low's Gully on the north side is 1,800 metres deep; in 1998 a 27-strong team made the first complete descent. In October 2022 Mount Kinabalu's Neogene granite was listed by the IUGS as a geological heritage site. Summit temperatures range from −4 to 8 °C in December and January, and from 3 to 12 °C from June to September. Snow was recorded at the summit in 1975, 1993 and 2022; above 3,700 metres there is persistent ground frost. Park headquarters receives about 2,700 mm of rain a year; it stands at 1,563 metres.

The park covers 754 square kilometres and spans four climate zones, from lowland dipterocarp forest to the summit. From Kota Kinabalu on the coast, the mountain lies about 50 km to the east-northeast. After the 2015 earthquake the summit trail was re-routed, and the Mesilau trail has been closed ever since. Cooled magma, glaciers and earthquakes have shaped a mountain that is still growing.
