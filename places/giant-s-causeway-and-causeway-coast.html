歷史 History

巨人、學者與無敵艦隊

Giants, Scholars and the Armada

Finn's legend, the Royal Society in 1693, an Armada wreck, a hydro-electric tramway and World Heritage in 1986

這裡是北愛爾蘭安特里姆郡的巨人堤道，。大約四萬根玄武岩柱，緊緊排在一起。傳說愛爾蘭巨人芬恩，造了這條堤道，。要跨過北海峽，去和蘇格蘭巨人貝南多納決鬥。另一個版本說，芬恩的妻子把他扮成嬰兒；。貝南多納嚇得逃回去，一路把堤道毀掉。海峽對岸，蘇格蘭斯塔法島的芬加爾洞，。也有一模一樣的玄武岩柱。1692年，德里主教威廉・金來到這裡；。隔年，布爾克利爵士向英國皇家學會發表了論文。

1739年，畫家蘇珊娜・德魯里畫下這裡的水彩，。1743年，這些畫被刻成了版畫。西邊的鄧路斯城堡，最早建於十三世紀，。1513年的文獻，記載它屬於麥奎蘭家族。1584年，麥克唐奈家族的索利・博伊，。奪下了這座城堡。1588年10月26日，西班牙無敵艦隊的赫羅納號，。在暴風雨中撞上礁石，船上一千三百多人，只有九人生還。船上的大砲，被裝到鄧路斯城堡的門樓；。貨物賣掉的錢，拿來整修城堡。

1967到1968年，潛水員撈起當時最大的一批無敵艦隊寶藏；。今天收藏在貝爾法斯特的阿爾斯特博物館。1883年，從波特拉什到布希米爾斯的電車通車，。1887年延伸到巨人堤道。它被稱為全世界第一條水力發電的電車，。一直開到1949年9月30日。1961年，國民信託買下這裡；。1986年列入世界遺產，1987年成為國家自然保護區。從巨人的傳說，到學者的論文，。三百多年來，人們一直來到這片岩柱前。

This is the Giant's Causeway in County Antrim, Northern Ireland: about 40,000 interlocking basalt columns. Legend says the Irish giant Fionn mac Cumhaill built it across the North Channel to fight the Scottish giant Benandonner. In another version Fionn's wife disguises him as a baby, and Benandonner flees, tearing up the causeway behind him. Across the sea, Fingal's Cave on the Scottish isle of Staffa has identical basalt columns. In 1692 Bishop William King of Derry visited; the next year Sir Richard Bulkeley presented a paper to the Royal Society.

In 1739 Susanna Drury painted watercolours of the Causeway, engraved in 1743. Dunluce Castle to the west was first built in the 13th century; records of 1513 place it in McQuillan hands. In 1584 Sorley Boy MacDonnell took the castle. On 26 October 1588 the Armada galleass Girona was wrecked in a storm; of more than 1,300 aboard, nine survived. The Girona's guns were mounted in Dunluce's gatehouses, and its cargo was sold to pay for restoring the castle.

In 1967–68 divers raised the largest Armada treasure then known from the wreck; it is on show at the Ulster Museum in Belfast. In 1883 a tramway opened from Portrush to Bushmills, and in 1887 it was extended to the Giant's Causeway. It is described as the world's first tramway powered by hydro-electricity, and ran until 30 September 1949. The National Trust acquired the site in 1961; it became World Heritage in 1986 and a national nature reserve in 1987. From the giants' legend to the scholars' papers, people have been coming to these columns for more than 300 years.

人文 Culture

堤道上的故事

Stories on the Causeway

近百萬名訪客、巨人的靴子與管風琴、失落的小鎮、《權力遊戲》與漁夫的索橋
Nearly a million visitors, the Giant's Boot and Organ, a lost town, Game of Thrones and a rope bridge

2019年，大約九十九萬八千人來到巨人堤道；。疫情期間少了九成五，2023年回到六十六萬三千人。走進堤道本身不用錢，。遊客中心則要收費。1986年的遊客中心，在2000年燒毀；。新的遊客中心，到2012年才啟用。岩石有許多名字：巨人的靴子、巨人的眼睛、。牧羊人的階梯、蜂巢、巨人的豎琴、駱駝背。「巨人的管風琴」大約有六十根岩柱，。高十二公尺。

十八世紀的導遊，還講過另一個故事：。芬恩造這條堤道，是為了愛情。1608年，城堡旁蓋起了一座小鎮，。街道排成棋盤格，房子裡可能還有室內廁所。這座小鎮在1641年的叛亂中被夷平；。2011年的考古發掘時，九成五還埋在地下。地方傳說，1639年一個暴風雨的夜裡，。城堡的廚房掉進了海裡。影集《權力遊戲》用它的外景，。拍出葛雷喬伊家族的派克城。

布希米爾斯的名字，來自布希河，。和十七世紀初蓋在河邊的一座大水車磨坊。2002年起，三公里多的窄軌蒸汽小火車，。沿著舊電車的路線，從村子開到巨人堤道。東邊的卡里克-a-里德索橋，長二十公尺，。離下面的岩石有三十公尺高。一般認為，捕鮭魚的漁夫三百五十多年來都在這裡搭橋；。2018年，有四十八萬五千多人走過它。巨人、城堡、沉船與漁夫，。都把故事留在了這段海岸上。

About 998,000 people visited the Giant's Causeway in 2019; numbers fell 95% in the pandemic and reached 663,000 in 2023. Access to the Causeway itself is free; the visitor centre charges a fee. The 1986 visitor centre burned down in 2000; a new one opened only in 2012. The rocks have names: the Giant's Boot, the Giant's Eyes, the Shepherd's Steps, the Honeycomb, the Giant's Harp and the Camel's Hump. The Giant's Organ has about sixty columns, 12 metres high.

Guides in the 1700s told another story: that Finn built the Causeway for love. In 1608 a town was built beside the castle, with a grid of streets and possibly indoor toilets. The town was razed in the rebellion of 1641; at the 2011 excavations about 95% of it was still undiscovered. Local tales say that one stormy night in 1639 the castle's kitchens fell into the sea. Game of Thrones used its exterior for Pyke, seat of House Greyjoy.

Bushmills takes its name from the River Bush and a large watermill built there in the early 17th century. Since 2002 a narrow-gauge steam train has run just over 3 km along the old tramway route from the village to the Causeway. To the east, the Carrick-a-Rede rope bridge is 20 metres long and 30 metres above the rocks below. Salmon fishermen are thought to have built bridges here for over 350 years; in 2018 more than 485,000 people crossed it. Giants, castles, shipwrecks and fishermen have all left their stories on this coast.

自然 Nature

海崖上的鳥與花

Birds and Flowers of the Cliffs

200 plant species, 80 kinds of bird, seals and otters, a protected snail and declining razorbills

巨人堤道的海崖上，大約長著兩百種植物，。包括紅列當、海鐵角蕨與蛙蘭。還有兔腳三葉草、春綿棗兒，。和海羊茅。這裡有五十種留鳥，和三十種過境的鳥，。包括刀嘴海雀、暴風海燕與遊隼。海鳥還有暴雪鸌、鸕鷀、歐鸕鷀，。紅腳鷸和海鴉。海裡住著灰海豹和歐亞水獺，。瓶鼻海豚也會游過這片海岸。

小小的窄口螺旋蝸牛，。受到歐盟棲地指令的保護。2011年10月，這裡還發現了。一群疊層石，由微生物一層層堆起。東邊的羊島鳥類保護區，。刀嘴海雀的數量，正在減少。1960年代，這裡的漁夫一天能捕到近三百條鮭魚；。到2002年，整季只捕到兩百五十條。1987年，海岸成為國家自然保護區；。1989年，堤道上方的台地列為傑出自然美景區。

世界自然保護聯盟2025年的評估：。前景「良好，但有些問題要注意」。氣候變遷可能加劇海岸侵蝕，。崩塌與落石的風險，也在升高。有些遊客把硬幣塞進岩縫，。也被列為需要注意的破壞。周圍的景觀，法律保護比較弱；。離岸風電與石油探勘，被列為潛在的威脅。海鳥、海豹與岩縫裡的小花，。和這些岩柱一起，面對北大西洋的風浪。

About 200 plant species grow on the Causeway's cliffs, including red broomrape, sea spleenwort and frog orchid. There are also hare's-foot trefoil, vernal squill and sea fescue. Fifty resident and thirty migrant bird species are found here, including razorbills, European storm petrels and peregrine falcons. Other seabirds include fulmars, cormorants, shags, redshanks and guillemots. Grey seals and European otters live in these waters, and bottlenose dolphins pass along the coast.

The tiny narrow-mouthed whorl snail is protected under the EU Habitats Directive. In October 2011 a colony of stromatolites, layered structures built by microbes, was reported here. At Sheep Island special protection area to the east razorbill numbers are declining. In the 1960s fishermen here caught almost 300 salmon a day; by 2002 they caught 250 in the whole season. The coast became a national nature reserve in 1987; the plateau above was made an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty in 1989.

IUCN's 2025 assessment rated the outlook as "good, with some concerns". Climate change may increase coastal erosion, and the risk of slides and rockfalls is rising. Some visitors wedge coins into the cracks of the stones, a kind of vandalism that is a concern. The surrounding landscape has weaker legal protection, and offshore wind and petroleum exploration are potential threats. Seabirds, seals and the small flowers in the cracks face the North Atlantic together with the columns.

地理 Geography

六千萬年前的熔岩

Lava from Sixty Million Years Ago

古新世的噴發、紅土夾層、九道熔岩流、緩慢冷卻出的六角柱，與火山頸變成的小島
Paleocene eruptions, a laterite layer, nine lava flows, slow-cooled columns and a volcanic-plug island

巨人堤道形成在五千萬到六千萬年前的古新世，。當時愛爾蘭還和北美洲連在一起。非常稀的玄武岩熔岩，穿過白堊層湧出，。堆成了一片火山高原。最下面的下玄武岩組，大約六千二百六十萬年前，。最厚可達五百三十一公尺。上面是一層約三十公尺厚的紅土，。記錄了火山長期停歇、岩石深度風化的年代。再上面的上玄武岩組，約五千九百六十萬年前，。最厚三百四十六公尺。

形成岩柱的「堤道拉斑玄武岩」，。是九道熔岩流，積在一處窪地，最厚三十公尺。這麼厚的熔岩，冷卻得很慢，。才能裂出近乎完美的岩柱。熔岩冷卻時收縮、裂開，。岩柱的粗細，主要取決於冷卻的速度。大多數岩柱是六角形，。也有四邊、五邊、七邊和八邊的。岩柱平均直徑約四十五公分，最高大約十二公尺；。海崖上的熔岩，有些地方厚達二十八公尺。

下層整齊的岩柱叫做「柱列」，。上層破碎的玄武岩，叫做「柱頂層」。卡里克-a-里德島，是北愛爾蘭最好的火山頸，。海浪侵蝕，露出了古老火山的通道。靠海的一面是堅硬的輝綠岩，後面是比較軟的凝灰岩；。周圍的玄武岩海崖下，墊著白堊。世界遺產的範圍只有七十公頃，。堤道海岸的傑出自然美景區，長約二十九公里。三百年來，地質學家在這裡讀著熔岩的紀錄，。巨人的堤道，其實是一部地球的歷史。

The Causeway formed 50 to 60 million years ago, in the Paleocene, when Ireland was still attached to North America. Highly fluid basalt lava pushed up through beds of chalk and built a volcanic plateau. The Lower Basalt Formation, about 62.6 million years old, is up to 531 metres thick. Above it lies a laterite about 30 metres thick, recording a long pause in the eruptions and deep weathering. The Upper Basalt Formation above, about 59.6 million years old, is up to 346 metres thick.

The Causeway Tholeiite that forms the columns is nine lava flows that ponded in a hollow, up to 30 metres thick. Lava that thick cooled slowly, which allowed nearly perfect columns to crack apart. As the lava cooled it contracted and cracked; the size of the columns depended mainly on how fast it cooled. Most of the columns are hexagonal; some have four, five, seven or eight sides. The columns average 45 cm across and reach about 12 metres; the lava in the cliffs is 28 metres thick in places.

The neat lower columns are called the colonnade, and the broken basalt above them the entablature. Carrickarede island is the best volcanic plug in Northern Ireland: the sea has worn away the rock to expose an old volcanic neck. Hard dolerite faces the sea, with softer tuff behind it; the basalt cliffs around it sit on chalk. The World Heritage site covers just 70 hectares; the Causeway Coast Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty runs for about 29 km. For three hundred years geologists have read the lava here; the giant's causeway is really a history of the Earth.

UK Drone Code (Northern Ireland): never more than 120 m from the closest point of the surface; keep 50 m horizontally from uninvolved people and never fly over crowds; stay 150 m from residential, commercial, industrial and recreational areas (drones under 250 g and C0 class excepted); do not enter airport flight restriction zones without permission. The Giant's Causeway and Carrick-a-Rede are cared for by the National Trust, which does not permit unauthorised drone operation on its land. Check the current rules before any real flight.
