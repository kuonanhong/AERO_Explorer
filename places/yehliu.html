歷史 History

魔鬼岬變成地質公園

From Devil's Cape to Geopark

一座偏僻漁村的岬角如何被看見
How a remote fishing-village headland came into public view

旁白全文 Full narration

新北市萬里區，野柳岬伸進東海。這趟飛行，看一座漁村岬角怎麼被世人認識。荷蘭人繪的古地圖，把這裡標為 Punto Diablos。意思是魔鬼岬，西班牙航海者因暗礁與急流而取名。居民不懂西班牙語，Diablos 口耳相傳成了 ia-liu。寫成漢字，就是今天的野柳。園方記述，這裡的墾殖已有三百年，從林姓家族開始。港灣裡的野柳漁港，日治時期就已建港。一九六三年，電影《海灣風雲》讓這座偏僻漁村有了名氣。隔年，攝影家黃則修以野柳為題辦展。

媒體報導之後，野柳對外開放，設立風景區。起初由萬里的地方公所管理。一九六四年三月十八日，一名來自香港的臺大僑生在岸邊落海。漁民林添禎聽見呼救，躍入海中。他沒能把人救起，自己也沒有回來。園區海邊立有他的銅像，紀念這次捨身救人。同一片岩岸上的女王頭，後來成為野柳最為人知的象徵。往岬角末端飛，高處的野柳燈塔建於一九六七年。附近海域暗礁多，它補上富貴角與基隆之間的導航空隙。

塔身做成燭臺造型，配合野柳的地景設計。底下設有桌椅，供走到岬角盡頭的人休息。一九七〇年，野柳列為省屬風景特定區。一九七八年改由臺北縣接管，設管理所。一九八三年成立遊客服務中心。之後納入北海岸及觀音山國家風景區。入口旁的野柳海洋世界，一九八〇年開幕。觀光署稱它是國內第一座海洋動物表演館。二〇〇六年起，園區委託民間公司營運。名稱也改為野柳地質公園。

二〇二一年一月，新北市政府公告這裡為地方級自然地景。依據的是文化資產保存法，類別是地質公園。一九六〇年代還是偏僻漁村的岬角，如今是一座地質公園。名字裡的魔鬼岬，還留在野柳兩個字的發音裡。

Yehliu Cape reaches into the East China Sea from Wanli District, New Taipei. This flight asks how a fishing-village headland became known to the world. An old Dutch-drawn map labels this headland Punto Diablos. Devil's Cape: Spanish sailors named it for its reefs and dangerous currents. Villagers who knew no Spanish passed the word on as ia-liu. Written in Chinese characters, it became Yehliu. The park's account dates settlement here back 300 years, starting with the Lin family. Yehliu Fishing Harbour in the bay was already built in the Japanese era. In 1963 the film 海灣風雲 made the remote fishing village widely known. The next year photographer Huang Tse-hsiu held an exhibition devoted to Yehliu.

After press coverage Yehliu opened to visitors and became a scenic area. At first it was run by the local Wanli township office. On 18 March 1964 a National Taiwan University student from Hong Kong slipped into the sea here. Fisherman Lin Tien-chen heard the cries and jumped in. He could not save the student, and he did not come back himself. A bronze statue on the park's shore commemorates his sacrifice. On the same shore stands the Queen's Head, which became Yehliu's best-known emblem. Toward the tip, Yehliu Lighthouse on the high ground dates from 1967. Amid reefs, it fills the gap between the lights of Fugui Cape and Keelung.

Its body is shaped like a candlestick to match Yehliu's landscape. Tables and seats beneath it give walkers a rest at the end of the cape. In 1970 Yehliu was listed as a provincial special scenic area. In 1978 Taipei County took over and set up a management office. A visitor service centre followed in 1983. Later the cape joined the North Coast and Guanyinshan National Scenic Area. Yehliu Ocean World beside the entrance opened in 1980. The Tourism Administration calls it Taiwan's first marine animal show hall. Since 2006 a private company has operated the park under contract. The name changed to Yehliu Geopark.

In January 2021 New Taipei City designated it a local-level natural landscape. The basis is the Cultural Heritage Preservation Act; the category is geopark. A headland that was a remote fishing village in the 1960s is now a geopark. The old name, Devil's Cape, survives in the sound of the word Yehliu.

人文 Culture

岬角下的漁村

The Fishing Village below the Cape

漁港、保安宮與元宵跳港的神轎
A harbour, a temple and sedan chairs that leap into the sea

野柳岬的根部，有一座被岬角護著的漁港。這一趟看的是住在岬角下的人，怎麼和海相處。東北側的岬角擋下強勁的東北季風，港灣成了天然良港。北方的低溫海流與黑潮在外海交會，漁場豐富。在地人把野柳分成三個小聚落。港西路一帶舊稱野柳邊，港東路一帶叫中社。第三個聚落在東邊的東澳，又叫後沃。龜吼路開通以前，東澳人得繞經野柳才能出入。東澳緊鄰駱駝山，漁港規模小。停的多是沿岸作業的小型漁船，也有海釣船。

回到野柳漁港，這是萬里停泊漁船最多的港。漁法有棒受網、一支釣、延繩釣和曳繩釣四種。漁汛期從每年三月到十月。小卷、魩仔魚、鯖魚、白帶魚和螃蟹，都是主要漁獲。港邊的保安宮，主祀開漳聖王。相傳一八二〇年，外海漂來一艘無人的船。船上有十六根福杉、一尊神像和一對青斗石墩。居民用海邊的咾咕石砌牆，以福杉為樑，蓋起小廟。廟在一九六〇年改建，最近一次翻修於二〇〇六年底完工。

每年元宵，保安宮舉行神明淨港。信眾扛著神轎躍入漁港，游到對岸上岸。出巡的有開漳聖王、媽祖、周倉將軍和土地公。每頂轎由五名信徒扛抬，祈求海不生波、合境平安。上岸之後還要過火，赤腳踏過炭火堆。二〇〇八年，這項儀式登錄為民俗類文化資產。岬角第一區的海蝕洞，地方上稱媽祖洞。相傳清嘉慶年間，村民在洞裡發現漂來的媽祖神像。神像後來迎到西邊的金山，供奉在今天的慈護宮。每年農曆四月十六日退潮時，二媽回到這座洞。

南邊是龜吼，萬里另一處漁港所在。萬里的花蟹、三點蟹和石蟳，以萬里蟹為名行銷。漁村裡的百年老屋瑪鋉居，曾是野柳最早的雜貨店。瑪鋉是萬里舊名，園方說明源自巴賽（Vasai）族的語音。岬角擋風，海流帶來魚群，廟會把神請進海裡。野柳的地景之外，還有一座仍在作業的漁村。

At the root of Yehliu Cape lies a fishing harbour sheltered by the headland. This flight looks at how the people below the cape live with the sea. The cape to the northeast blocks the strong northeast monsoon, making a natural harbour. Cold currents from the north meet the Kuroshio offshore, creating rich fishing grounds. Locals divide Yehliu into three small settlements. The Gangxi Road side was known as 野柳邊 (Yeliubian), the Gangdong Road side as 中社 (Zhongshe). The third settlement is Dong'ao to the east, also called Houwo. Before Guihou Road opened, its people had to pass through Yehliu to get in and out. Dong'ao sits beside Luotuo (Camel) Hill, with a small harbour of its own. It holds mostly small inshore boats, along with sea-angling boats.

Back at Yehliu Harbour, the port with the most fishing boats in Wanli. Four methods are used: stick-held dip nets, pole and line, longlines and trolling. The fishing season runs from March to October each year. Squid, whitebait, mackerel, hairtail and crabs are among the main catches. Bao'an Temple by the harbour is dedicated to Kaizhang Shengwang. Tradition says that in 1820 an unmanned boat drifted in from the open sea. Aboard were sixteen fir timbers, a statue of the deity and a pair of stone pedestals. Villagers built a small shrine with coral-stone walls and the fir as beams. The temple was rebuilt in 1960; its latest renovation was completed at the end of 2006.

Every Lantern Festival the temple holds the Cleansing of the Harbour. Bearers leap into the harbour with the sedan chairs and swim to the far side. Kaizhang Shengwang, Mazu, General Zhou Cang and the Earth God all take part. Five bearers carry each chair, praying for calm seas and a safe community. After landing comes fire-walking: barefoot across a bed of charcoal. In 2008 the rite was registered as a folk-custom cultural asset. A sea cave in the park's first zone is known locally as Mazu Cave. Tradition says villagers found a drifting Mazu statue there in the Jiaqing era. The statue was later taken west to Jinshan, to what is now Cihu Temple. Each year at low tide on the 16th of the fourth lunar month, the Second Mazu returns to the cave.

To the south is Guihou, site of another of Wanli's fishing harbours. Wanli's flower crabs, three-spot crabs and stone crabs are marketed as Wanli Crab. Malian House, a century-old home in the village, was Yehliu's first general store. Malian, Wanli's old name, comes from the speech of the Basay (Vasai) people, the park says. The cape blocks the wind, currents bring the fish, and a festival carries the gods into the sea. Beyond the famous rocks, Yehliu is still a working fishing village.

候鳥的第一站

First Stop for Migrating Birds

岬角上的鳥、濱海植物與潮間帶
Birds, coastal plants and the intertidal zone of the cape

對南下的候鳥來說，野柳岬是抵達臺灣的第一站。這一趟看的是岬角上的鳥、植物和潮間帶生物。春天北返時，這裡又是離開臺灣前的最後一站。園方資料說，每年春秋兩季鳥類數量最多。臺灣約有六百種鳥，其中三百多種曾在野柳出現。當中有六十種屬於保育類。二〇〇〇年，國際鳥盟把野柳列入臺灣的重要野鳥棲地。全臺當時共列了五十三處。岬角上的灌叢和潮間帶，供應鳥類食物與棲所。園方列出的過境鳥有魚鷹、戴勝、紫綬帶和黃眉黃鶲。

冬候鳥名單上有遊隼、藍磯鶇和藍尾鴝。留鳥則有黑鳶、岩鷺、小白鷺、白頭翁和綠繡眼。依二〇〇〇到二〇一〇年的紀錄，過境期約有一百七十種。冬季約六十種，夏季約五十種。陸地上有五百多種植物，八成以上是原生種。春天開花的有鐵炮百合、月桃和厚葉石斑木。夏天是林投、海檬果和濱薊，秋天有金花石蒜。冬天可看大葉山欖、黃心柿和海桐。貼近浪花的岩臺上，長著石板菜和脈茸草。陡峭海崖上是林投和稜果榕，園方也復育鐵炮百合。

往下看是海蝕平臺。退潮後，壺穴積水成為潮池。裡面有海藻、蟹類，還有海葵與梅氏長海膽。園方記錄的還有石蓴、石花菜、寄居蟹和眼斑海兔。帆水母和織錦芋螺有毒，園方提醒不可碰觸。園方說，岬角位在黑潮、東海與南海三個海洋生態系交會處。海流帶來浮游生物和養分，撐起多樣的食物鏈。岬角周圍的海域，調查記錄了一百零六種石珊瑚。另有三十種八放珊瑚，無脊椎動物超過一百六十種。岬角較外側的第三區，是生態保育的範圍。只在四到六月和九月，以預約導覽的方式開放。

園區規定不能撿拾貝殼、不能戲水，也不能觸摸岩石。一道狹長的岩岬，是候鳥的驛站，也是潮間帶生物的家。賞鳥的季節，是每年三到五月和十到十二月。

For birds flying south, Yehliu Cape is a first stop on reaching Taiwan. This flight looks at the cape's birds, plants and intertidal life. In spring, heading north, it is the last stop before leaving Taiwan. The park notes that bird numbers peak every spring and autumn. Of roughly 600 bird species in Taiwan, more than 300 have been recorded at Yehliu. Sixty of them are protected species. In 2000 BirdLife International listed Yehliu as an Important Bird Area. It was one of 53 such areas named in Taiwan. Shrubland and the intertidal zone on the cape offer birds food and shelter. Passage migrants on the park's list include osprey, hoopoe, paradise flycatcher and narcissus flycatcher.

Winter visitors on the list include peregrine falcon, blue rock thrush and red-flanked bluetail. Residents include black kite, Pacific reef heron, little egret, bulbul and white-eye. Records from 2000 to 2010 show about 170 species in the passage seasons. About 60 are present in winter and about 50 in summer. More than 500 land plants grow here, over 80 percent of them native. Spring brings the flowers of Easter lily, shell ginger and Yeddo hawthorn. Summer has screw pine, sea mango and coastal thistle; autumn, golden spider lily. Winter is the season for Formosan nato tree, sea ebony and pittosporum. On wave-cut platforms near the spray grow stonecrop and other low herbs. Screw pine and figs hold the steep cliffs; the park is also restoring Easter lilies.

Below lies the wave-cut platform; at low tide its potholes hold tide pools. They shelter seaweeds and crabs, along with sea anemones and rock-boring urchins. The park's records also list sea lettuce, agar weed, hermit crabs and sea hares. It warns that sail jellyfish and textile cone snails are toxic and must not be touched. The park places the cape where Kuroshio, East China Sea and South China Sea ecosystems meet. Currents bring plankton and nutrients that support varied food chains. Surveys of the waters around the cape have recorded 106 species of stony coral. There are also 30 octocoral species and more than 160 kinds of invertebrates. The third zone, farther out on the cape, is kept as an ecological conservation area. It opens only by reserved guided tour, from April to June and in September.

Park rules forbid collecting shells, playing in the water and touching the rocks. One narrow rocky cape serves as a waystation for migrants and a home for intertidal life. The birding seasons are March to May and October to December.

一道傾斜的砂岩

A Tilted Slab of Sandstone

單面山、結核與差異侵蝕刻出的岬角
A cuesta, concretions and differential erosion shape the cape

旁白全文 Full narration

野柳岬長約一千七百公尺，最寬處只有兩百五十公尺。它為什麼這麼窄、這麼斜，岩石又為什麼長成這樣。岬角是大屯山系伸入海中的餘脈。岩層屬於大寮層，約兩千萬年前在海裡堆積。約六百萬年前的蓬萊造山運動，把岩層抬升並推斜。推測直到數萬年前，岬角才逐漸露出海面。整座岬角是一座單面山，岩層向東南傾斜約二十度。北面是陡崖，南面是緩坡。從西邊的金山方向看，岬角像一隻潛入海中的巨龜。所以又叫野柳龜，末端的山頭當地稱龜頭山。

厚層砂岩堅硬，南側卻是較軟弱的砂頁岩。崁腳斷層從南邊經過，留下破碎帶。砂岩成岩時，鈣和鐵質沉澱，結成堅硬的結核。結核比周圍耐蝕，底下的岩柱被削細，就成了蕈狀岩。園區約有一百八十座蕈狀岩。它們依序經歷無頸、粗頸、細頸，最後斷頸。女王頭的輪廓出現在一九六二到六三年間。頂部結核沿節理崩落一塊，才有了這個側臉。北觀處的量測：一九九四年頸圍約兩百二十公分。二〇二四年十月實測，剩下一百一十六點五九公分。

北觀處表示，本體沒有做任何人為處置。學者每年定期監測，並以三維雷射掃描記錄外形。往入口方向是第一區，以燭臺石為代表。圓球狀的結核像燭火，海水沿邊緣迴流，蝕出環狀溝槽。薑石是露出地表的硬結核，紋路和色澤都像老薑。壺穴則是海水帶著石粒，在凹處打轉磨出來的。兩組近乎垂直的節理把砂岩切成方格，就是豆腐岩。海浪沿節理侵蝕加寬，也刻出一道道海蝕溝。海崖腳下是寬平的海蝕平臺。這是一段受侵蝕而不斷後退的海岸。

砂岩裡還有海膽化石，以及生物鑽洞留下的生痕化石。東邊海面上的基隆嶼是火山島，由火成岩構成。野柳則是沉積岩，兩者的來歷完全不同。堅硬的砂岩被抬起、推斜，再交給海浪和季風雕刻。女王頭的頸子仍在變細，這片地景還沒有定稿。

Yehliu Cape is about 1,700 metres long and only 250 metres across at its widest. Why is it so narrow and so tilted, and why do its rocks look the way they do? The cape is a spur of the Datun mountain system running out to sea. Its rock belongs to the Daliao Formation, laid down in the sea about 20 million years ago. About six million years ago the Penglai orogeny lifted and tilted the strata. The cape is thought to have emerged from the sea only tens of thousands of years ago. The whole cape is a cuesta: its beds dip about 20 degrees to the southeast. The north face is a cliff; the south side is a gentle slope. Seen from the Jinshan side to the west, the cape resembles a giant turtle diving into the sea. Hence the nickname Yehliu Turtle; locals call the hill at its end Guitoushan, Turtle Head.

The thick sandstone is hard, but softer sandstone and shale lie on the south side. The Kanjiao Fault passes to the south, leaving a zone of broken rock. As the sandstone hardened, calcium and iron minerals formed tough concretions. They resist erosion; the softer column beneath is whittled away, leaving a mushroom rock. The park has about 180 mushroom rocks. They pass through stages: no neck, thick neck, thin neck and finally a broken neck. The Queen's Head took its profile between 1962 and 1963. Part of the concretion on top broke off along a joint, leaving this silhouette. The scenic area office measured the neck at about 220 cm around in 1994. By October 2024 it was down to 116.59 cm.

The office says no artificial treatment has been applied to the rock itself. Scholars monitor it every year and record its shape with 3D laser scans. Toward the entrance is the first zone, known for its candle rocks. A round concretion forms the flame; swirling seawater cuts a ring groove around it. Ginger rocks are exposed hard concretions with the texture and colour of old ginger. Potholes are ground out where seawater swirls pebbles in a hollow. Two nearly perpendicular sets of joints cut the sandstone into blocks: tofu rocks. Waves widen the joints and also carve long sea-erosion trenches. At the foot of the sea cliffs lie broad wave-cut platforms. This is an eroding, retreating coast.

The sandstone also holds sea-urchin fossils and the burrow traces of ancient animals. Keelung Islet on the eastern horizon is a volcanic island of igneous rock. Yehliu is sedimentary rock; the two have entirely different origins. Hard sandstone was lifted and tilted, then handed to the waves and monsoon winds to carve. The Queen's neck is still thinning; this landscape is not finished.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

野柳地質公園園區禁止使用無人機（空拍機）；如需空拍，須事先向交通部民航局及北海岸及觀音山國家風景區管理處申請。未經申請飛行，可依民用航空法處罰。

Drones are prohibited inside Yehliu Geopark; aerial filming requires prior application to the Civil Aviation Administration and the North Coast and Guanyinshan National Scenic Area Administration. Unauthorised flights can be fined under the Civil Aviation Act.
