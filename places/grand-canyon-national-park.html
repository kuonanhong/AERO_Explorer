歷史 History

從家園到國家公園

From Homeland to National Park

1540年的西班牙人、1869年的鮑威爾，到1919年的國家公園
Spanish explorers in 1540, Powell in 1869, a national park in 1919

數千年來，大峽谷一直是原住民的家園。普韋布洛人把這裡視為神聖的地方。1540年，西班牙人加西亞·洛佩斯·德·卡德納斯來到這裡。他是已知第一位看見大峽谷的歐洲人。1869年，約翰·衛斯理·鮑威爾率隊乘船順流而下。這是第一次有紀錄的科羅拉多河峽谷航行。在峽谷底的河段，激流一個接一個。鮑威爾的探險隊有人中途離開，再也沒有回來。1893年，班傑明·哈里森總統把這一帶劃為森林保留區。保護大峽谷的工作，從這時開始。

1905年，鐵路公司在南緣蓋了艾爾托瓦飯店。鐵路把遊客直接送到峽谷邊。老羅斯福總統在1906年設立大峽谷狩獵保護區。1908年1月11日，再宣布成立大峽谷國家紀念區。1919年2月26日，威爾遜總統簽署法案。大峽谷正式成為國家公園。1932年，建築師瑪麗·柯爾特設計了沙漠景觀瞭望塔。塔高約21公尺，塔頂是南緣的最高點。谷底的幻影牧場，是峽谷內唯一的住宿點。徒步或騎騾子，要花大半天才能下到這裡。

北緣每年12月1日到5月15日因積雪關閉。大約九成的遊客，都去比較方便的南緣。1979年，大峽谷國家公園列入世界遺產。公園面積約4,926平方公里。2024年，公園的休閒遊客超過490萬人次。一個世紀前的鐵路觀光，變成了全世界的旅行目的地。公園與三個原住民部族的保留地相鄰。西邊的哈瓦蘇峽谷，是哈瓦蘇派族的土地。從原住民的聖地、探險家的航程，到國家公園，。大峽谷的歷史，比人類的紀錄更深，也比國界更早。

For thousands of years the canyon has been home to Native peoples. Pueblo people regarded it as a holy place. In 1540 the Spaniard García López de Cárdenas arrived here. He was the first known European to see the canyon. In 1869 John Wesley Powell led boats down the river. It was the first recorded passage through the canyon on the Colorado. On the canyon-floor reaches, rapids follow one after another. Some of Powell's men left the expedition partway and never returned. In 1893 President Benjamin Harrison made the area a forest reserve. Protection of the canyon had begun.

In 1905 the Santa Fe Railway built El Tovar Hotel on the South Rim. The railway carried visitors right to the edge. Theodore Roosevelt created the Grand Canyon Game Preserve in 1906, then proclaimed Grand Canyon National Monument on 11 January 1908. On 26 February 1919 President Woodrow Wilson signed the act. The Grand Canyon became a national park. In 1932 the architect Mary Colter designed the Desert View Watchtower. At 70 feet, its top is the highest point on the South Rim. Phantom Ranch on the canyon floor is the only lodging inside the canyon. Walkers and mule riders take most of a day to get down.

Snow closes the North Rim from 1 December to 15 May each year. About 90 per cent of visitors go to the more accessible South Rim. In 1979 Grand Canyon National Park joined the World Heritage List. The park covers about 4,926 square kilometres. In 2024 the park received more than 4.9 million recreational visits. Railway tourism a century ago became a destination for the world. The park shares boundaries with three federally recognised tribes. To the west, Havasu Canyon is the land of the Havasupai. From Native holy place to explorers' route to national park, the canyon's story runs deeper than any record people kept.

人文 Culture

十一個部族的峽谷

A Canyon of Eleven Nations

原住民的家園，與二十世紀初的鐵路觀光
Native homelands, and the railway tourism of the early 1900s

大峽谷自古就是原住民的家園。國家公園管理局列出十一個與這裡有歷史連結的部族。哈瓦蘇派族、華拉派族和霍皮族都在其中。還有納瓦荷族、祖尼族、亞瓦派－阿帕契族和多個派尤特部族。哈瓦蘇派族至今住在西側的哈瓦蘇峽谷裡。藍綠色的溪水，是他們名字的由來：「藍綠水之民」。小科羅拉多河峽谷，對霍皮族有特別的意義。這片土地，承載著部族起源的故事。南緣東段的圖薩揚遺址，是古普韋布洛人的村落。大約八百年前，這裡住著種玉米、豆子和南瓜的農人。

峽谷裡和邊緣，留有數千處古代遺址。人們在這裡生活，比任何文字紀錄都早。沙漠景觀瞭望塔的設計，參考了古普韋布洛人的石塔。塔內有霍皮族畫家佛瑞德·卡波提的壁畫。塔建於1932年，設計者是建築師瑪麗·柯爾特。她為南緣設計了多座融入地景的建築。二十世紀初，鐵路把遊客帶到南緣。1905年開幕的艾爾托瓦飯店，就是為鐵路旅客蓋的。南緣的大峽谷村，至今仍保留當年的旅館與車站。峽谷觀光，就從這裡開始。

飯店旁的霍皮之家，1905年由柯爾特設計，仿照霍皮族的石屋。1914年，她又在崖邊蓋了觀景工作室。光明天使步道從村子下切到谷底。騎騾子下峽谷，是延續了一百多年的旅行方式。谷底的幻影牧場，是峽谷內唯一的住宿點。北凱巴布步道從這裡過河，連到北緣。北緣比較安靜，冬天會因積雪關閉。那裡也提供騎騾下切峽谷的行程。許多遊客在南緣的觀景點，等待日落。岩壁的顏色，會隨著陽光一分一秒改變。

今天，大峽谷國家公園管理局與各部族合作，。讓部族的聲音，出現在公園的解說裡。對十一個部族來說，這裡是家園與聖地；。對世界各地的旅人，這裡是一生要看一次的風景。

The canyon has been home to Native people since time immemorial. The Park Service lists 11 tribes with historic ties to these lands. They include the Havasupai, Hualapai and Hopi, the Navajo, Zuni, Yavapai-Apache and several Paiute bands. The Havasupai still live in Havasu Canyon to the west. Their name means people of the blue-green water. The Little Colorado gorge holds special meaning for the Hopi. These lands carry the stories of where peoples began. Tusayan Ruin, near the east end of the South Rim, was an Ancestral Puebloan village. Some 800 years ago farmers here grew corn, beans and squash.

Thousands of ancient sites lie in and around the canyon. People lived here long before any written record. The Desert View Watchtower was modelled on the stone towers of Ancestral Puebloans. Inside are murals by the Hopi painter Fred Kabotie. It was built in 1932 to a design by the architect Mary Colter, who shaped several South Rim buildings to blend into the land. In the early 1900s the railway brought visitors to the South Rim. El Tovar Hotel opened in 1905 for its passengers. Grand Canyon Village on the South Rim keeps its early hotels and station. Canyon tourism began here.

Hopi House beside the hotel, designed by Colter in 1905, echoes Hopi stone dwellings. In 1914 she added the Lookout Studio on the cliff edge. The Bright Angel Trail drops from the village to the canyon floor. Riding a mule into the canyon is a way of travel more than a century old. Phantom Ranch on the floor is the only lodging inside the canyon. From here the North Kaibab Trail crosses the river to the North Rim. The North Rim is quieter, and snow closes it in winter. Mule trips several thousand feet into the canyon start there too. Many visitors wait for sunset at the South Rim viewpoints. The colours of the walls change by the minute with the light.

Today the park works with the tribes so that their voices are part of how the canyon is explained. For 11 tribes this is homeland and holy ground; for travellers from everywhere, a view to see once in a lifetime.

自然 Nature

從沙漠到森林

From Desert to Forest

從谷底到北緣，一天之內經過好幾種氣候
From river to North Rim, several climates in a single day

谷底海拔約八百公尺，夏天十分炎熱。這裡是沙漠的環境。科羅拉多河是峽谷裡最重要的水源。河岸邊的植物，靠著河水生長。從谷底往上爬，每上升一段，氣溫就降一些。植物也從沙漠灌叢，換成矮林。南緣海拔兩千一百公尺左右，長著矮松和杜松。夏天常有午後雷雨。北緣比南緣高約三百公尺，降雨和降雪都比較多。這裡長著高大的西黃松與雲杉、冷杉。

帝國角一帶，是北緣最高的地方。從谷底到這裡，就像從墨西哥走到加拿大。積雪讓北緣從12月到隔年5月中關閉。冬天的北緣，只屬於野生動物。北緣森林裡，住著白尾巴的凱巴布松鼠。南緣的近親艾伯特松鼠，尾巴卻是灰色的。峽谷把同一種松鼠的族群隔開。兩邊各自演化出不同的模樣。1963年，上游的格倫峽谷水壩開始蓄水。從水庫深處放出的河水，變得清澈而冰冷。

西側的哈瓦蘇峽谷，溪水呈現藍綠色。水中溶解的碳酸鈣，讓溪床堆積出一層層石灰華。瀑布與水潭，是峽谷沙漠裡的綠洲。這裡是哈瓦蘇派族的土地，造訪須事先取得許可。峽谷上空，可以看到體型巨大的加州神鷲。1996年起，牠們被重新野放到大峽谷附近。神鷲曾在野外瀕臨滅絕。如今，牠們又能乘著峽谷的上升氣流盤旋。小科羅拉多河的水，在某些季節也是藍綠色的。水的顏色，同樣來自溶解的礦物質。

一條河，在乾燥的高原中央，切出一道生命線。動植物沿著水，分布在峽谷的深處。從谷底的沙漠，到北緣的森林，。大峽谷把好幾種氣候，疊在同一個地方。

The canyon floor, about 800 metres up, is fiercely hot in summer. It is desert country. The Colorado is the canyon's great source of water. Plants along its banks live on the river. Climb from the floor and the air cools with every step up. Desert scrub gives way to low woodland. The South Rim, around 2,100 metres, grows pinyon pine and juniper. Summer afternoons often bring thunderstorms. The North Rim, about 300 metres higher, gets more rain and snow. Tall ponderosa pine, spruce and fir grow here.

Point Imperial is the North Rim's high point. Going from the floor to here is like travelling from Mexico to Canada. Snow closes the North Rim from December to mid-May. In winter the North Rim belongs to the wildlife. The North Rim forest is home to the white-tailed Kaibab squirrel. Its South Rim cousin, Abert's squirrel, has a grey tail. The canyon split one squirrel population in two, and each side has taken on a look of its own. In 1963 Glen Canyon Dam upstream began holding back the river. Water released from deep in its reservoir now runs clear and cold.

To the west, Havasu Creek runs blue-green. Calcium carbonate in its water builds terraces of travertine. Falls and pools make an oasis in the canyon desert. This is Havasupai land, and visits need a permit in advance. Above the canyon you may see the huge California condor. Since 1996 condors have been released again near the Grand Canyon. The condor once came close to vanishing from the wild. Now it rides the canyon's thermals again. The Little Colorado also runs blue-green in some seasons, its colour again coming from dissolved minerals.

One river cuts a lifeline through the dry plateau. Plants and animals follow the water deep into the canyon. From desert on the floor to forest on the North Rim, the Grand Canyon stacks several climates in a single place.

地理 Geography

Two Billion Years, Cut by One River

長446公里、深1,857公尺，科羅拉多河五、六百萬年的作品
446 km long and 1,857 m deep: the work of the Colorado in five or six million years

美國亞利桑那州的大峽谷，長446公里。寬度從6.4到29公里不等。最深的地方超過一英里，約1,857公尺。谷底海拔大約只有八百公尺。切出這座峽谷的，是谷底的科羅拉多河。它大約在五百萬到六百萬年前，確立了現在的河道。河流一面向下切，高原一面緩緩抬升。兩股力量加在一起，峽谷愈切愈深。峽谷壁上露出將近二十億年的地球歷史。每一層顏色，代表一段不同的環境。

谷底最古老的岩石，是深色的變質岩。它們在將近二十億年前，形成於地底深處。往上是一層層的砂岩、頁岩和石灰岩。它們曾是海底、海岸和沙漠。最上面的凱巴布石灰岩，約有兩億七千萬年。它就是我們腳下的峽谷邊緣。峽谷寬闊的地方，是河流和支流長期侵蝕的結果。雨水、霜凍與崩塌，讓谷壁不斷後退。北緣比南緣高出約三百公尺。地層朝南微微傾斜，所以北緣的雨水多半流進峽谷。

帝國角是北緣最高的觀景點。從這裡看得到峽谷東段的開闊景色。小科羅拉多河在這裡匯入科羅拉多河。兩條河的峽谷，在此交會。南緣東端的沙漠景觀，峽谷突然轉向。河流在這裡從南北走向，轉成東西走向。皇家角從北緣伸進峽谷。峽谷內聳立的山頭，是還沒被侵蝕掉的高原殘塊。從正上方看，河道在岩層之間彎彎曲曲。這條河仍在繼續切割。

二十億年的岩層，被一條河在幾百萬年間切開。大峽谷，是一本攤開的地球歷史書。

Arizona's Grand Canyon is 446 kilometres long. It is between 6.4 and 29 kilometres wide. At its deepest it is over a mile deep, about 1,857 metres. The floor lies only about 800 metres above sea level. The river at the bottom, the Colorado, cut this canyon. It settled into its present course about five to six million years ago. As the river cut down, the plateau slowly rose. Together the two forces drove the canyon ever deeper. The walls expose nearly two billion years of the Earth's history. Each band of colour records a different world.

The oldest rocks, at the bottom, are dark metamorphic rocks. They formed deep underground nearly two billion years ago. Above them lie layer on layer of sandstone, shale and limestone, laid down as sea floors, shorelines and deserts. The topmost layer, the Kaibab Limestone, is about 270 million years old. It forms the rim beneath our feet. Where the canyon is wide, the river and its side streams have worked longest. Rain, frost and rockfall keep pushing the walls back. The North Rim stands about 300 metres higher than the South Rim. The layers tilt gently south, so North Rim rain drains into the canyon.

Point Imperial is the highest viewpoint on the North Rim. From here the eastern canyon opens wide. Here the Little Colorado joins the Colorado. Two canyons meet. At Desert View, the east end of the South Rim, the canyon turns. The river swings from a north–south course to east–west. Cape Royal reaches out from the North Rim into the canyon. The buttes inside it are pieces of plateau not yet worn away. From straight above, the river winds between the layers. It is still cutting today.

Two billion years of rock, opened by one river in a few million years. The Grand Canyon is a history of the Earth laid open.

Since 2014 (Policy Memorandum 14-05) the US National Park Service has prohibited launching, landing or operating uncrewed aircraft on the lands and waters it administers, Grand Canyon National Park included; special uses need a park permit.
