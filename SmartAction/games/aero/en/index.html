FLY AT YOUR OWN PACE

Free browser drone simulator

Free browser game for unmanned air, land and sea vehicles, with automatic hardware tiers, keyboard and dual sticks, first- and third-person views, nearby places and multiple languages. Google imagery services can be configured.

How do I control it on a phone or computer?

On a computer, use W/S/A/D and the arrow keys. On phones and tablets, use the on-screen dual sticks or directional pads.

Is this a real airspace map?

Simulated scenery is not surveyed terrain. Google surface imagery requires an API key and area coverage; underwater ecology is simulated.
