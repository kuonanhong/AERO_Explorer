歷史 History

黃金山城的起落

Rise and Fall of a Gold Town

從 1893 年的金脈露頭，到 1971 年封坑與之後的重生
From the 1893 gold outcrop to the 1971 closure, and what came after

旁白全文 Full narration

新北市瑞芳區的山坡上，房屋層層疊疊，這裡是九份。一座山城如何因黃金而興起，又因黃金而沉寂？清末修築基隆到臺北的鐵路時，工人在基隆河裡發現砂金。淘金的人沿著河谷，一路往上游尋找源頭。1893 年，溯溪而上的淘金客在九份山頂發現小金瓜露頭。隔年，東邊的金瓜石也找到大金瓜礦脈。1896 年，日本的藤田組取得瑞芳礦山的礦權。1914 年，藤田組把整座礦山租給顏雲年經營。顏雲年把礦區分成許多小區，再分租給其他業者與個人。採金人每天繳交牌費入坑，淘到的金子歸自己。

1920 年，經營礦山的公司更名為臺陽礦業株式會社。九份長期採承包制，與直營的金瓜石不同。依總督府的統計，九份一帶在 1917 年有 7,191 人。到 1939 年增加為 18,080 人，是統計表中的最高數字。日治時期，九份有「小上海」、「不夜城」之稱。當時流傳一句話：上品送金九，次品輸臺北。山城西南側的五番坑，坑口開鑿於 1927 年。礦土曾用高空輸送管，送到山下的搗礦場搗碎。1931 到 1937 年，產金政策與交通建設帶來第二次產金高峰。1937 年，可通行汽車的自動車路完工，就是今天的汽車路。

1940 年代末，金價不再上漲，富礦脈也已降到海平面以下。臺灣的金礦業從此走向衰退。1950 年代末，臺陽公司曾提議結束這座礦山。因為仍有一萬多人依靠礦業生活，經營勉力延續下去。1971 年，礦山全面停採，如今坑口都已封閉。多數礦工轉往煤礦或其他行業，人口大量外流。豎崎路上的昇平戲院，也在 1986 年結束營業。封坑之後，山城沉寂了十多年。1989 年，在此取景的電影《悲情城市》讓九份重新被看見。1990 年代初，遊客開始湧入這座舊礦城。

2004 年，東邊的金瓜石成立黃金博物館，保存礦業遺址。2011 年，整修後的昇平戲院重新開放，由博物館管理。坑口封了，街道與階梯仍是礦業年代留下的輪廓。從空中看，九份的山坡上寫著一段採金史。

On a hillside in Ruifang District, New Taipei, houses stack up the slope: this is Jiufen. How did gold build this town, and what happened when the mining stopped? In the late Qing era, workers building the Keelung-Taipei railway found gold dust in the Keelung River. Prospectors then worked their way upstream, looking for the source. In 1893 prospectors coming up the valley found the Xiaojingua gold outcrop on the hilltop at Jiufen. A year later the Dajingua vein was found at Jinguashi, just to the east. In 1896 the Japanese firm Fujita-gumi obtained the mining rights to the Ruifang mine. In 1914 Fujita leased the whole mine to the local businessman Yan Yun-nian. Yan divided the lease into many small plots and sublet them to other firms and individuals. Diggers paid a daily fee to enter, and kept the gold they found.

In 1920 the company running the mine was renamed Taiyang Mining. Jiufen kept its contract system for decades, unlike directly run Jinguashi. Colonial government statistics count 7,191 people in the Jiufen area in 1917. By 1939 the figure was 18,080, the highest in the table. In the Japanese era Jiufen was nicknamed Little Shanghai and the city that never sleeps. A saying of the time: the best goods go to the gold towns, the second best to Taipei. Wufankeng, the No. 5 adit on the town's southwest side, was opened in 1927. Ore was once carried by an overhead conveyor to a stamp mill down the slope. From 1931 to 1937, gold policy and new transport links brought a second production peak. In 1937 the motor road was completed; it is today's Qiche Road.

By the late 1940s the gold price had stopped rising and the rich veins lay below sea level. Gold mining across Taiwan went into decline. In the late 1950s Taiyang proposed closing the mine. With more than ten thousand people still depending on it, the work was kept going. In 1971 all mining stopped; every adit has since been sealed. Most miners moved on to coal mines or other trades, and people left the hill. The Shengping Theater on Shuqi Road closed in 1986. After the mines shut, the town stayed quiet for well over a decade. In 1989 the film A City of Sadness, shot here, brought Jiufen back into view. By the early 1990s visitors were arriving in the old mining town.

In 2004 the Gold Museum opened in Jinguashi to the east, preserving the mining sites. In 2011 the restored Shengping Theater reopened under the museum's management. The adits are sealed, but the streets and stairways keep the outline the mining years gave them. Seen from the air, a history of gold mining is written across Jiufen's slope.

人文 Culture

石階、茶樓與紅燈籠

Stone Steps, Teahouses and Red Lanterns

沿著「丰」字形的老街，看礦城留下的生活風景
Daily life along a street grid shaped like the character 丰

九份老街沿著山坡往上爬，窄巷、石階與屋頂擠在一起。這座舊礦城的日常，就藏在街道的形狀裡。從上方看，九份的道路排成一個「丰」字。直的一豎是豎崎路，橫向有基山街、輕便路與汽車路。基山街是最熱鬧的商店街，街寬只有大約 2 公尺。過去入夜後人潮聚集，這裡又被稱為「暗街」。街上賣的是芋圓、草仔粿和紅糟肉圓。稱為穿屋巷的小巷，從屋子之間穿過，連接各條街道。豎崎路全程以石階築成，一共三百多階。兩側多是紅磚與木造的老屋，一路通向山下的汽車路。

基山街上的翁山英故居，是石造混合木構的老屋。屋子前段一層、後段兩層，如今是九份茶坊。九份第一家藝文茶樓開張後，同業相繼跟進。這個礦業聚落裡，陸續出現二十幾家茶樓。豎崎路與輕便路交會的地方，是昇平戲院。1916 年，舊市場旁先有一座木造戲臺，以演歌仔戲為主。木造戲臺在 1927 年倒塌，1930 年代才建起戲院。它的規模，在日治時期的臺灣數一數二。1989 年，侯孝賢導演的《悲情城市》在九份取景。這部片獲得威尼斯影展金獅獎，是臺灣電影首次得到此獎。

停業後的戲院，也成為《多桑》等電影的場景。現在館內重現五、六〇年代老戲院的樣子。這裡多雨多風，老屋因此蓋成斜屋頂。屋頂鋪上塗了柏油的油毛氈，稱為黑油皮屋頂，如今多已改成水泥。老街頂端的開成殿建於 1957 年，主祀玉皇大帝。廟前平臺位在九份的至高點，可以望見深澳漁港。汽車路上的九份派出所旁，是九份遊客中心。館內展示礦石、採礦工具與輕便車的模型。入夜後，豎崎路上懸掛的紅燈籠依序亮起。2023 年，九份首度舉辦紅燈籠祭，之後持續辦理。

燈區從基山街、豎崎路，一路延伸到五番坑。封閉的坑口前，現在是一座社區小公園。石階、茶樓、戲院與燈籠，都順著同一面山坡排開。九份的人文風景，是礦城生活留下來的日常。

Jiufen Old Street climbs the slope in a tangle of lanes, stone steps and rooftops. The everyday life of this former mining town is held in the shape of its streets. From above, Jiufen's streets resemble the Chinese character 丰. The upright stroke is Shuqi Road; across it run Jishan Street, Qingbian Road and Qiche Road. Jishan Street is the busiest shopping lane, only about two metres wide. Crowded after dark in earlier days, it was also known as the Dark Street. Stalls here sell taro balls, herbal rice cakes and red-yeast meatballs. Small passages called chuanwu lanes cut between the houses to link the streets. Shuqi Road is built of stone steps all the way, more than three hundred of them. Red-brick and timber houses line both sides down toward the motor road below.

The former Weng Shan-ying residence on Jishan Street is an old house of stone and timber. One storey at the front and two behind, it is now the Jiufen Teahouse. After Jiufen's first arts teahouse opened, others followed. More than twenty teahouses eventually appeared in the old mining settlement. Where Shuqi Road meets Qingbian Road stands the Shengping Theater. It began in 1916 as a wooden stage beside the old market, mainly for Taiwanese opera. The wooden stage collapsed in 1927, and a full theater was built in the 1930s. It ranked among the largest theaters in Taiwan under Japanese rule. In 1989 director Hou Hsiao-hsien shot A City of Sadness in Jiufen. It won the Golden Lion at Venice, the first Taiwanese film to take the prize.

After it closed, the theater served as a set for films such as A Borrowed Life. Inside, it now recreates a cinema of the 1950s and 60s. Rain and wind are frequent here, so the older houses were given pitched roofs. They were covered with tarred felt, the black-skin roofs; most are concrete now. Kaicheng Temple at the top of the old street was built in 1957 and is dedicated to the Jade Emperor. Its forecourt is the highest point in Jiufen, with a view to Shen'ao fishing harbour. Beside the police station on Qiche Road is the Jiufen Visitor Center. It displays ore samples, mining tools and a model of the old push-car. After dark the red lanterns hung along Shuqi Road light up one after another. In 2023 Jiufen held its first Red Lantern Festival, and it has continued since.

The lantern displays run from Jishan Street and Shuqi Road as far as Wufankeng. In front of the sealed mine adit there is now a small community park. Steps, teahouses, theater and lanterns are all laid out along the same slope. Jiufen's cultural landscape is the daily life a mining town left behind.

自然 Nature

風、雨與芒草的山

A Mountain of Wind, Rain and Silvergrass

在海風與礦水之間，看東北角山海的生命力
What lives on a sea-blown, mine-marked mountain coast

九份旁的基隆山面向東海，終年迎著海風。在風、雨和礦水之間，這片山海長出了什麼？基隆山名列臺灣小百岳。側看像橫臥的孕婦，所以又叫大肚美人山。登山口在九份老街口外約 350 公尺的路旁。步道全長約 0.89 公里，全程是階梯，爬升 267 公尺。山上岩層堅硬，終年又有海風吹拂。東北季風帶來含鹽的雨，樹木稀疏，芒草成片生長。坡面上以芒草和其他草本植物為主。整條步道幾乎沒有樹蔭。

步道途中設有涼亭，海風直接吹上山坡。官方建議上山時帶著防風衣物、飲水與雨具。天氣多變的時候，山頂會被一頂「斗笠雲」罩住。山頂視野約十公里，東北方是鼻頭角燈塔與陰陽海。西北方可見基隆嶼與和平島，西邊的山巒之後是臺北 101。多山、多風、多雨，是瑞芳山城的特色。這些雨水，也是附近溪流與海灣顏色的起點。天氣好的時候，從九份高處可以望見深澳漁港。日落時分，是這個平臺景色最受稱道的時刻。

金瓜石的本山經過露天開採，岩層大片裸露。礦山的石縫裡，伏石蕨密密地冒出來。黃金瀑布的水來自礦區，是酸性水，酸鹼值約 2.6。水中含有重金屬，植物很難在這樣的水邊生長。水裡的鐵氧化菌，會加快鐵質沉澱成鏽的速度。溪水流入濂洞灣，海面分成黃褐與湛藍兩種顏色。黃金博物館的出版品指出，這並不是工業污染造成的。西邊的深澳漁港，周圍是成片的礁岩海岸。這裡是磯釣的好地點，也以休閒海釣聞名。

港裡的漁獲以小卷、透抽和白帶魚為主。船隻也前往基隆嶼與北方三島一帶的海域。從港邊的觀景步道回望，可以遠眺山坡上的九份。從海面到山頂不到六百公尺，環境卻一路不同。強風、鹽雨與礦水，決定了這片山海能長出什麼。

Keelung Mountain, beside Jiufen, faces the East China Sea and takes the sea wind all year. Between wind, rain and mine water, what manages to grow on this coast? Keelung Mountain is one of Taiwan's Xiaobaiyue, the hundred minor peaks. Seen side-on it resembles a reclining pregnant woman, hence the name Big-Belly Beauty Mountain. The trailhead is beside the road about 350 metres from the entrance to Jiufen Old Street. The path is about 0.89 km long, steps all the way, climbing 267 metres. The rock here is hard, and the sea wind blows all year. The northeast monsoon brings salt-laden rain, so trees are sparse and silvergrass grows in thickets. Silvergrass and other herbaceous plants dominate the slopes. The trail has almost no shade from trees.

Pavilions along the path offer a rest, and the sea wind blows straight onto the slope. The official advice is to carry windproof clothing, water and rain gear. When the weather turns, a cap cloud known as a bamboo-hat cloud can settle on the summit. The summit view reaches about ten kilometres: Bitou Cape lighthouse and the Yinyang Sea to the northeast. Keelung Islet and Heping Island lie to the northwest; beyond the ranges to the west is Taipei 101. Hilly, windy and wet: that is the character of Ruifang's hill towns. The same rain is where the colours of the nearby stream and bay begin. In fine weather the high ground of Jiufen looks out to Shen'ao fishing harbour. The view at sunset is what this forecourt is best known for.

At Benshan in Jinguashi, open-cut mining left broad faces of bare rock. In crevices around the mines, the rock-hugging fern known as fushi-jue sprouts thickly. The water of the Golden Waterfall drains from the mines and is acidic, with a pH of about 2.6. It carries heavy metals, and plants struggle to grow beside water like this. Iron-oxidising bacteria in the water speed up the settling of iron into rust. Where the stream enters Liandong Bay, the sea divides into yellow-brown and deep blue. A Gold Museum publication notes that this is not the result of industrial pollution. Around Shen'ao fishing harbour to the west, the coast is a broad apron of rock. It is a good place for rock fishing and is well known for recreational sea angling.

Catches landed here are mainly squid, known locally as xiaojuan and touchou, and hairtail. Boats also head for the waters around Keelung Islet and the three northern islets. From the harbour's viewing walkway, Jiufen can be seen far off on its hillside. From the sea to the summit is less than six hundred metres, yet conditions differ all the way up. Wind, salt rain and mine water decide what can live on this stretch of coast.

地理 Geography

山、海與礦脈之間

Between Mountain, Sea and Ore

從基隆山到陰陽海，讀水金九的地形與地質
Landforms and geology from Keelung Mountain to the Yinyang Sea

旁白全文 Full narration

九份位在臺灣東北部，整個聚落坐落在山坡上。一邊靠山，一邊望海，這樣的位置是怎麼形成的？山下的瑞芳車站設於 1919 年，當時宜蘭線通車到這裡。它是前往水湳洞、金瓜石與九份的轉運站。九份北側的基隆山，標高 588 公尺。它是九份與金瓜石兩個聚落之間的界山。從海上望去，山形像一只雞籠，所以舊稱雞籠山。它緊鄰東海，早年是航海人辨認方位的地標。基隆山是錐形的死火山，屬於基隆火山群。火山群的岩石定年，大約落在 80 萬到 170 萬年前。

山體東西約 1.9 公里，南北約 1.6 公里。這個火山群擁有臺灣最大的金銅礦床。山城的橫向街道沿著山腰展開，汽車路就是縣道 102 號。輕便路原本是通往五番坑的臺車道。直向的豎崎路順著坡面上下，所以整條都是石階。從基山街口的觀景臺，可以俯瞰北海岸。往東越過稜線，下一個山谷裡是金瓜石。金瓜石北邊的海岸是水湳洞，三地合稱「水金九」。金瓜石的本山，山頭原本海拔 660 公尺。經過百年開採，山頂降低了 106 公尺。

礦脈從地表一路延伸到海平面以下 130 公尺。露天開採讓岩層清楚出露，成了野外的地質教室。礦床來自地下熱水的礦化作用，岩石也因此矽化。矽化岩抗風化，形成茶壺山這類突出的山頭，標高 602 公尺。這一帶多雨，礦石裡的硫化鐵礦物隨雨水進入水流。水流經過的地方，鐵氧化沉澱，積成厚厚的金黃色鐵鏽。臨海山坡上的十三層遺址，是 1933 年興建的選煉廠。它提煉金、銀、銅礦，1987 年結束運作。金瓜石山區富含黃鐵礦，風化後的鐵離子隨水入海。生成的氫氧化鐵吸附泥沙，海灣呈黃褐色，與藍色外海並列。

這片海灣本名濂洞灣，岸邊的聚落叫水湳洞。地名來自巨岩旁的海蝕洞，與溪水形成的濕地。西邊的深澳岬角舊名番仔澳，港區周圍是礁岩海岸。從九份的山坡上，可以望見這座漁港。火山岩體、熱水礦脈、陡坡與海灣，都擠在幾公里之內。九份的位置，是地質與採礦共同決定的。

Jiufen lies in northeastern Taiwan, the whole settlement set on a hillside. Mountain on one side, sea on the other: what produced a site like this? Ruifang Station in the valley opened in 1919, when the Yilan Line reached this point. It is the transfer point for Shuinandong, Jinguashi and Jiufen. Keelung Mountain, on the north side of Jiufen, rises to 588 metres. It is the boundary peak between the settlements of Jiufen and Jinguashi. From the sea its outline resembles a chicken coop, hence the old name Jilong Shan. Standing beside the East China Sea, it was a landmark for early navigators. Keelung Mountain is an extinct cone-shaped volcano in the Chilung Volcanic Group. Rock samples from the group have been dated to roughly 0.8 to 1.7 million years ago.

The mountain measures about 1.9 km east to west and 1.6 km north to south. This volcanic group holds the largest gold and copper deposits in Taiwan. The town's cross streets run along the hillside; Qiche Road is County Road 102. Qingbian Road began as a push-car track leading to the Wufankeng adit. Shuqi Road runs straight up and down the slope, so it is steps for its whole length. From the lookout at its Jishan Street junction the north coast is in view. East across the ridge, the next valley holds Jinguashi. On the coast north of it is Shuinandong; together the three are called Shui-Jin-Jiu. Benshan, the main ore hill at Jinguashi, once stood 660 metres high. A century of mining lowered its summit by 106 metres.

The ore body runs from the surface to 130 metres below sea level. Open-cut mining left the rock layers clearly exposed, an outdoor geology classroom. The ore formed through hydrothermal activity, which also silicified the surrounding rock. Silicified rock resists weathering and stands out as knobs such as Teapot Mountain, 602 metres. Rain is frequent here, and it carries iron sulphide minerals from the ore into the stream. Along its course the iron oxidises and settles as a thick layer of golden rust. On the slope above the sea, the Remains of the 13 Levels is an ore refinery built in 1933. It processed gold, silver and copper ore and stopped operating in 1987. The Jinguashi hills are rich in pyrite; weathering releases iron ions that wash to the sea. They form iron hydroxide that binds silt, turning the bay yellow-brown beside the blue open sea.

The bay is properly called Liandong Bay; the settlement on its shore is Shuinandong. The name comes from a sea cave beside a great rock and the marshy ground a stream left there. To the west, the Shen'ao headland was once called Fanzi'ao; its harbour is ringed by rocky shore. Jiufen's slope looks out toward this fishing port. Volcanic rock, hydrothermal veins, steep slopes and a bay all lie within a few kilometres. Geology and mining together decided where Jiufen stands.
