歷史 History

世界最古老的議會之一

One of the Oldest Parliaments

930年成立的議會、1000年的信仰抉擇，與1944年的共和國
An assembly founded in 930, a choice of faith in 1000, and a republic in 1944

西元930年，冰島人在這裡成立全國議會「阿爾庭」。每年夏天，各地的人來到這片原野開會。法律石是議會的中心，人們在這裡發表演說。議會選出的「宣法人」任期三年，負責主持會議。宣法人要在法律石上，背誦全國的法律。在還沒有成文法典的時代，法律靠記憶流傳。宣法人三年任期內，每年背誦三分之一的法律。三年下來，全國的法律就被完整念過一遍。阿爾曼納峽谷的岩壁，像一道天然的屏障與音響牆。議會就在峽谷邊的原野上舉行。

約在西元1000年，議會決定接受基督教。這個決定，避免了一場內部的分裂。這裡除了一座農莊和後來的兩座教堂，。並沒有其他永久的建築。參加議會的人，住在臨時搭建的棚屋裡。斧頭河的水，供應了聚會期間的需要。議會在這裡一直開會到1798年。之後才遷到雷克雅維克。十九世紀中葉，議會在雷克雅維克恢復運作。它一直延續到今天，是冰島的國會。

1930年，議會成立一千週年，這裡成為國家公園。它是冰島第一座國家公園。1944年6月17日，冰島在這裡宣布成立共和國。國家的重大時刻，總是回到辛格韋德利。2004年，辛格韋德利列入世界遺產。它以文化遺產的身分登錄，見證了一個民族的自治傳統。在兩塊大陸裂開的地方，。冰島人建立了屬於自己的議會與國家。

In 930 the Icelanders founded their national assembly, the Althing, here. Every summer people came from all over the island to meet on these plains. The Law Rock was the focus of the assembly, a natural platform for speeches. The lawspeaker, elected for three years, presided over the meeting. The lawspeaker recited the law of the land from the Law Rock. Before written codes, law lived in memory. Over a three-year term the lawspeaker recited a third of the law each year, so that in three years the whole law was heard. The wall of Almannagjá made a natural backdrop and sounding board, and the assembly met on the plain beside it.

Around the year 1000 the Althing chose to accept Christianity, a decision that averted a split within the country. Apart from a farm and, later, two churches, there were no permanent buildings here. Those attending lived in temporary booths, and the Öxará supplied water while the assembly lasted. The Althing met here until 1798, after which it moved to Reykjavík. In the mid-19th century the Althing was revived in Reykjavík, and it continues today as Iceland's parliament.

In 1930, the Althing's thousandth anniversary, Þingvellir became a national park, Iceland's first. On 17 June 1944 the Republic of Iceland was proclaimed here. The nation returns to Þingvellir for its great moments. In 2004 Þingvellir joined the World Heritage List as a cultural site, a witness to a people's tradition of self-government. Where two continents pull apart, the Icelanders built their own assembly, and their own nation.

人文 Culture

露天的議會

An Open-Air Assembly

法律石、棚屋、宣法人，與冰島人心中的聖地
The Law Rock, the booths, the lawspeaker, and a place Icelanders hold dear

走進阿爾曼納峽谷，一邊是岩壁，一邊是開闊的原野。這裡曾是冰島全國的聚會場。議會開會時，人們在法律石前聚集。宣法人在這裡宣讀法律，也宣布判決。議會期間，原野上搭滿了臨時棚屋。各地來的人在這裡交換消息、做買賣、尋找婚配。教堂與農莊，是原野上少數的建築。今天的白色小教堂，仍是國家公園的地標。冰島的國家大事，常選在這裡舉行。1944年的建國，就在辛格韋德利宣布。

國家公園成立於1930年，紀念議會的一千年。這片原野，被視為冰島民族的起點。絲浮拉裂縫，是世界知名的潛水地點。潛水客在兩個板塊之間的清水裡漂浮。能見度約一百公尺，像是在空氣裡游泳。這片水，在地下旅行了約三十年才到達這裡。辛格瓦德拉湖畔，散布著度假小屋。湖是冰島人休閒、釣魚的地方。斧頭河瀑布是遊客最常停留的地方之一。水聲在斷層崖之間迴盪。

教堂旁的農舍，今天是冰島總理的夏季官邸。國家的領導人，仍然回到這片原野。辛格韋德利與蓋錫爾間歇泉、黃金瀑布，合稱「黃金圈」。它是冰島最熱門的旅遊路線之一。2004年，辛格韋德利以文化遺產列入世界遺產。UNESCO肯定它作為議會發源地的意義。法律在這裡被背誦、國家在這裡誕生，。辛格韋德利是冰島人心中的聖地。

Walk into Almannagjá: rock wall on one side, open plain on the other. This was once the meeting place of all Iceland. During the assembly people gathered before the Law Rock, where the lawspeaker proclaimed the law. While it lasted, the plain filled with temporary booths. People traded news and goods here, and sometimes found spouses. The church and farm were among the few buildings on the plain. Today's small church is still a landmark of the park. Iceland often marks its great events here; the republic itself was proclaimed at Þingvellir in 1944.

The park was founded in 1930 to mark a thousand years of the Althing. This plain is seen as the birthplace of the Icelandic nation. The Silfra fissure is a world-famous dive site. Divers float in clear water between two plates. With visibility around 100 metres, it feels like swimming in air. The water took some 30 years underground to arrive. Summer houses dot the shores of Þingvallavatn. The lake is a place for Icelanders to rest and fish. Öxarárfoss is one of the places visitors linger longest. The sound of water echoes between the fault walls.

The farmhouse by the church is now the Prime Minister's summer residence. The nation's leaders still come back to this plain. With Geysir and Gullfoss, Þingvellir makes up the Golden Circle, one of Iceland's most popular routes. In 2004 Þingvellir was inscribed as a cultural World Heritage site, recognised by UNESCO as the cradle of the Althing. Law was recited here and a nation was born here: Þingvellir is the place Icelanders hold dearest.

自然 Nature

冰與火之間的湖

A Lake Between Ice and Fire

冰河過濾的泉水、地熱，與北極紅點鮭
Glacier-filtered springs, geothermal heat, and Arctic charr

辛格瓦德拉湖是冰島最大的天然湖。它的水，大部分來自地下湧出的泉水。泉水源頭是北方的朗格冰河。融水在熔岩裡流動約三十年，被過濾得非常清澈。湖裡的北極紅點鮭，演化出好幾種不同的型態。它們吃不同的食物，住在湖的不同深度。一座湖裡，就能看到物種如何分化。科學家把這裡當作研究演化的天然實驗室。西南方的亨吉德火山，地底熱能豐富。溫泉與蒸氣，從地面不斷冒出。

這裡的地熱電廠，為雷克雅維克供應熱水與電力。冰島人把火山的熱，變成日常生活的一部分。裂谷的岩壁上，長著苔蘚與矮小的植物。冰島的植物要適應短暫的夏天與寒冷的冬天。裂谷東側的山，是另一個板塊的邊緣。整個谷底，約有五公里寬。斯傑爾德布雷達的熔岩，鋪滿了谷地的北方。熔岩上慢慢長出了苔原與低矮的樺樹林。斧頭河的水，最後流入湖中。河與湖，連接著這片裂谷裡的生命。

朗格冰河是冰島第二大冰河，位在湖的北方。它把融水，透過地下慢慢送進湖裡。熔岩上的苔蘚長得很慢。一旦被踩壞，要很多年才能恢復，請走在步道上。從正上方看，裂縫一道道平行排列。大地被拉開的方向，清楚地寫在地面上。冰河的水、火山的熱、裂開的大地，。辛格韋德利的生命，生長在冰與火之間。

Þingvallavatn is the largest natural lake in Iceland, fed mostly by springs welling up from underground. The springs begin at the Langjökull glacier to the north. Meltwater spends about 30 years in the lava and emerges crystal clear. The lake's Arctic charr have evolved into several distinct forms, eating different food at different depths. In one lake you can watch a species diverge. Scientists treat it as a natural laboratory of evolution. South-west, the Hengill volcano holds abundant heat underground, with hot springs and steam rising from the ground.

Its geothermal plants send hot water and electricity to Reykjavík. Icelanders have made the volcano's heat part of daily life. Moss and low plants cling to the walls of the rift. Iceland's plants must cope with short summers and cold winters. The mountains on the eastern side mark the edge of the other plate. The whole valley floor is some 5 kilometres wide. Lava from Skjaldbreiður blankets the north of the valley, slowly colonised by moss heath and low birch woodland. The Öxará finally flows into the lake, linking life across the rift.

Langjökull, Iceland's second-largest glacier, lies north of the lake, sending its meltwater slowly underground into it. Moss on the lava grows very slowly. Once trampled it takes years to recover, so keep to the paths. From straight above, the fissures run in parallel lines. The direction the land is being pulled is written on the ground. Water from the glacier, heat from the volcano, ground that splits apart: life at Þingvellir grows between ice and fire.

地理 Geography

裂開的大地

Where the Earth Splits

北美與歐亞板塊之間的裂谷、裂縫中的泉水，與冰島最大的湖
A rift between two plates, spring water in the cracks, and Iceland's largest lake

冰島的辛格韋德利，位在一條裂谷裡。這條裂谷，是大西洋中洋脊露出地面的一段。裂谷的一邊是北美板塊，另一邊是歐亞板塊。兩塊板塊正在慢慢分開。西側的阿爾曼納峽谷，是這一帶最大的斷層。它看起來像一道真正的峽谷。斷層的西壁高聳，東側的地面則往下沉陷。大地拉開時，中間的地塊就跟著下陷。斧頭河在這裡從斷層的崖頂落下，形成斧頭河瀑布。河水沿著峽谷，流向辛格瓦德拉湖。

湖的北端有一道裂縫，叫絲浮拉。它最寬約十公尺，最深約六十公尺。裂縫裡的水，來自朗格冰河。融水滲進多孔的熔岩，在地下流動約三十年才湧出。經過熔岩過濾，水的能見度約有一百公尺。絲浮拉的意思是「銀」，形容它的清澈。這一帶約五公里寬，有大約一百道裂縫。絲浮拉每年加寬約一到五公釐。辛格瓦德拉湖面積84平方公里，是冰島最大的天然湖。平均深34公尺，最深114公尺。

湖水從南端的索格河流出。湖本身，就躺在下陷的裂谷裡。湖的西南方是亨吉德火山。它一帶的地熱，供應首都雷克雅維克的熱水與電力。東北方的斯傑爾德布雷達，是一座盾狀火山。「盾狀火山」這個名稱，就是從它而來。這一帶的熔岩，大約在九千年前流成。之後，大地就一直被拉開、下陷。兩塊大陸之間的裂谷、冰河水注滿的裂縫、一座大湖，。辛格韋德利是地球正在生長的地方。

Iceland's Þingvellir lies in a rift valley, a stretch of the Mid-Atlantic Ridge exposed on land. On one side is the North American plate; on the other, the Eurasian. The two are slowly drawing apart. On the west, Almannagjá is the largest fault in the area, a veritable canyon. Its western wall stands high while the ground to the east has dropped. As the land pulls apart, the block in between sinks. Here the Öxará river spills over the fault as the Öxarárfoss. The water then runs along the rift to the lake.

At the north end of the lake lies a fissure called Silfra, up to 10 metres wide and 60 metres deep. Its water comes from the Langjökull glacier. Meltwater seeps into porous lava and travels underground for about 30 years. Filtered by the lava, the water gives visibility of about 100 metres. Silfra means silver, for its clarity. About a hundred fissures cross a zone some 5 kilometres wide here. Silfra widens by roughly 1 to 5 millimetres a year. Þingvallavatn covers 84 square kilometres, the largest natural lake in Iceland. It averages 34 metres deep and reaches 114.

The lake drains south through the river Sog. It lies in the sunken floor of the rift itself. South-west of the lake rises the Hengill volcano, whose geothermal heat supplies Reykjavík with hot water and power. To the north-east, Skjaldbreiður is a shield volcano; the very term "shield volcano" comes from its name. The lava here poured out about 9,000 years ago. Ever since, the ground has been pulled apart and has sunk. A rift between continents, cracks filled with glacier water, a great lake: Þingvellir is a place where the Earth is still growing.
