歷史 History

大天使的山

The Archangel's Mount

708年的異象、諾曼人的修道院、百年戰爭的圍城、監獄歲月，到1979年的世界遺產
A vision in 708, a Norman abbey, Hundred Years' War sieges, a prison, and World Heritage in 1979

這裡是法國諾曼第的聖米歇爾山，。離海岸大約一公里的潮汐島。它原本叫做「通布山」；。相傳708年，大天使米迦勒向一位主教顯現。他是阿夫朗什的主教奧貝爾，。大天使要他在山上蓋一座小禮拜堂。傳說他到第三晚才照做，大天使用手指按出了凹痕；。那顆頭骨，今天還收藏在阿夫朗什的聖熱爾韋大教堂。867年的貢比涅條約，把這裡交給布列塔尼人；。933年，它才確定成為諾曼第的一部分。

貝葉掛毯的第16、17景，。畫到了這座山，和附近的流沙。966年，本篤會修士來到山上；。十一世紀，沃爾皮亞諾的威廉，在四座地下室上蓋起教堂。1204年一場大火之後，法王腓力二世出資重建；。十三世紀，蓋起了哥德式的「奇蹟樓」。百年戰爭時，英軍在1423年進駐北邊的通布蘭島，。把它當成圍攻聖米歇爾山的基地。1423到1424年，1433到1434年，兩次圍城都失敗；。1434年6月17日，英軍丟下了攻城的大砲。

法國大革命之後，修道院變成監獄，一直到1863年，。大約關過一萬四千人。作家雨果從1836年起，為它奔走；。1874年，聖米歇爾山列為歷史古蹟，開始修復。1879年，一條堤道把山和陸地連起來；。1901到1938年，還有電車開到山腳。1944年7月31日，巴頓將軍的部隊抵達阿夫朗什，。帶來了著名的「阿夫朗什突破」。1966年，修道院慶祝建立一千年；。1979年，聖米歇爾山和它的海灣，列入世界遺產。

This is Mont-Saint-Michel in Normandy, France, a tidal island about a kilometre off the coast. It was once called Mont Tombe; legend says that in 708 the Archangel Michael appeared to a bishop. He was Aubert, Bishop of Avranches, and the archangel asked him to build an oratory on the mount. The story says he acted only on the third night, after Michael pressed a finger into his skull; the dented skull is kept in the Saint-Gervais basilica in Avranches. The Treaty of Compiègne of 867 gave the mount to the Bretons; in 933 it became Norman for good.

Scenes 16 and 17 of the Bayeux Tapestry show the mount and the quicksand nearby. In 966 Benedictine monks settled on the mount; in the 11th century William of Volpiano raised a church on four crypts. After a fire in 1204, King Philip Augustus paid for rebuilding; in the 13th century the Gothic Merveille was built. In the Hundred Years' War the English took the islet of Tombelaine in 1423 and used it as a base against the mount. The sieges of 1423–24 and 1433–34 both failed; on 17 June 1434 the English abandoned their bombards.

After the French Revolution the abbey became a prison until 1863, holding about 14,000 prisoners. From 1836 the writer Victor Hugo campaigned for it; in 1874 it became a historic monument and restoration began. In 1879 a causeway joined the mount to the mainland; from 1901 to 1938 a tramway ran out to its foot. On 31 July 1944 General Patton's troops reached Avranches, leading to the Avranches Breakthrough. In 1966 the abbey marked its thousandth year; in 1979 Mont-Saint-Michel and its bay became World Heritage.

人文 Culture

朝聖、手抄本與鹽沼羊

Pilgrims, Manuscripts and Salt-Meadow Lamb

二十三位居民與每年兩百五十萬訪客、雨果的奔走、阿夫朗什的手抄本，與鹽沼上的羊
23 residents and 2.5 million visitors a year, manuscripts in Avranches, and sheep on the salt marshes

山上的居民，2023年只有二十三人；。每年來訪的遊客，卻有大約兩百五十萬人。山上有六十多處受保護的建築，。還有五十家商店、四座博物館和一百三十二間客房。1979年列入世界遺產時，它因為景觀之美，。和在基督教傳統裡的地位而受到肯定。2007年，世界遺產的範圍擴大；。今天的緩衝區有六千五百五十公頃。阿夫朗什的「手抄本博物館」，。收藏聖米歇爾山從八世紀到十五世紀的手抄本。

法國大革命時，這些手抄本被送到阿夫朗什保管；。展出的書卷，每三個月更換一次。1172年5月21日，英王亨利二世。為了貝克特大主教遇害，在阿夫朗什公開懺悔。作家雨果寫過這裡的潮水，。也替這座山的保存四處奔走。今天，停車場搬到兩公里半外的陸地上，。免費接駁車把遊客送到離山四百公尺的地方。最後一段路，走在一座細長的橋上，。橋由建築師迪特瑪・費希廷格設計，2014年啟用。

這道擋潮閘出自建築師呂克・韋茲曼，。2010年6月起，開放給民眾參觀。海灣邊的鹽沼上放牧著羊群，。牠們的肉，就是「鹽沼羊」。平均每天有一千五百五十輛車來到這裡；。受保護的列級景區，有一萬六千八百多公頃。潮汐係數超過一百一十的大潮時，山會變回島幾個小時；。這樣的景象，曾經消失了一百三十多年。朝聖者、修士、囚犯與遊客，。一千三百年來，都走向同一座山。

In 2023 just 23 people lived on the mount, yet about 2.5 million visitors come every year. The mount has more than sixty protected buildings, along with 50 shops, four museums and 132 hotel rooms. When it was listed in 1979 it was recognised for its beauty and its importance to the Christian tradition. The World Heritage listing was extended in 2007; its buffer zone today covers 6,550 hectares. The Scriptorial museum in Avranches holds manuscripts of Mont-Saint-Michel from the 8th to the 15th centuries.

During the French Revolution the manuscripts were taken to Avranches; the volumes on show change every three months. On 21 May 1172 King Henry II of England did penance at Avranches for the murder of Thomas Becket. Victor Hugo wrote about the tides here and campaigned to save the mount. Today the car park is 2.5 km away on the mainland, and free shuttles drop visitors 400 metres from the mount. The last stretch is a slender bridge-walkway by the architect Dietmar Feichtinger, opened in 2014.

The dam was designed by the architect Luc Weizmann and opened to the public in June 2010. Sheep graze the salt marshes around the bay, giving the salt-meadow lamb of the region. On average 1,550 vehicles a day come here; the protected landscape covers 16,825 hectares. When the tide coefficient tops 110 the mount is an island for a few hours, a sight that had not been seen for more than 130 years. Pilgrims, monks, prisoners and visitors have walked towards the same mount for thirteen centuries.

自然 Nature

潮間帶的生命

Life Between the Tides

十萬隻冬候鳥、海豹與海豚、歐洲數一數二的鹽沼，與通布蘭島的鳥類保護區
100,000 wintering waterbirds, seals and dolphins, vast salt marshes and a bird reserve on Tombelaine

1994年，聖米歇爾灣成為國際重要濕地，。登錄的範圍有四萬七千八百公頃。每年冬天，這裡有十萬隻以上的水鳥；。紅腹濱鷸和黑腹濱鷸，都超過全球族群的百分之一。瀕危的水棲葦鶯在遷徙途中停靠，。每年有幾十隻被監測記錄。巴利阿里鸌在換羽的季節，。也會來到這片海灣。北邊的通布蘭島，1985年起是鳥類保護區；。1933年，國家把整座島買了下來。

十一世紀，隱修者曾住在島上；。1137年，這裡建了一座小修道院。灣裡住著灰海豹，。港海豹也在這裡繁殖。灣裡也有瓶鼻海豚，。和港灣鼠海豚。這裡的鹽沼，是歐洲面積最大的鹽沼之一，。長著二十二種受保護的植物。海蟲築成的「蜂巢礁」，。是歐洲最大的這類礁體。

歐洲鰻和斑鰩，。也在這片灣裡生活。海灣北岸，卡羅勒到尚普的海崖上，。記錄到四百八十種植物。為了讓塞呂訥河恢復暢通，。河上兩座水力發電壩的拆除計畫已經展開。海灣周圍大約五萬公頃，。大多是海域，都列入歐盟的「自然2000」保護網。潮水一退一進，。把食物送給這裡數不清的鳥、魚和海豹。

In 1994 the bay became a Ramsar wetland of international importance, covering 47,800 hectares. Each winter more than 100,000 waterbirds gather here; red knot and dunlin exceed 1% of their populations. The aquatic warbler stops here on migration, with several dozen monitored every year. Balearic shearwaters come to the bay during their moulting season. Tombelaine to the north has been a bird reserve since 1985; the state bought the islet in 1933.

In the 11th century hermits lived on the islet; in 1137 a priory was founded here. Grey seals live in the bay, and harbour seals breed here. The bay is also home to bottlenose dolphins and harbour porpoises. The bay's salt marshes are among the largest in Europe and hold 22 protected plant species. Reefs built by honeycomb worms, called hermelles, are the largest of their kind in Europe.

The European eel and the brown skate also live in the bay. On the cliffs of Carolles and Champeaux on the north shore 480 plant species have been recorded. To restore the Sélune's ecological continuity, a project to level two hydroelectric dams on the river is under way. About 50,000 hectares around the bay, mostly sea, are part of the EU's Natura 2000 network. With every ebb and flow the tide feeds countless birds, fish and seals.

地理 Geography

奔馬般的潮水

Tides Like a Galloping Horse

歐洲第二大的潮差、退到十公里外的海、湧潮、花崗岩，與讓山變回島的擋潮閘
Europe's second-largest tidal range, a sea that retreats 10 km, a tidal bore, granite and a dam

聖米歇爾灣大約五百平方公里，。潮差是歐洲第二大。平均潮差十到十一公尺，。春分、秋分前後的大潮，可以到十六公尺。退潮時，海水可以退到十公里外，。露出兩百多平方公里的潮間帶。雨果說，潮水來時「快得像奔馳的馬」；。實際測到的最快速度，大約每小時六點一公里。最大的潮水，會在河口形成湧潮，。沿著河道往上游推進好幾公里。

庫埃農、塞和塞呂訥三條河，流進這片海灣；。阿夫朗什就在塞河與塞呂訥河匯流的岩丘上。阿夫朗什的海拔，從七公尺到一百一十一公尺，。鎮上看得到整片海灣和聖米歇爾山。聖米歇爾山是一塊花崗岩，。大約五億兩千五百萬年前形成。它高九十二公尺，面積大約七公頃，。繞山一圈，大約九百六十公尺。三公里外的通布蘭島，也是花崗岩，。高四十五公尺，長兩百五十、寬一百五十公尺。

每年有將近一百萬立方公尺的泥沙，。沉積在這片海灣裡。專家警告，不採取行動的話，。到2040年，山的四周會變成一片鹽沼。2009年啟用的擋潮閘有八道閘門，。每道寬九公尺、高七點四公尺，重三十噸。它可以存下一百二十萬立方公尺的海水，。再放出來，把河口的泥沙沖向大海。整個工程花了一億八千多萬歐元；。大潮的時候，聖米歇爾山又成了一座島。

The bay covers about 500 square kilometres and has the second-largest tidal range in Europe. The average range is 10 to 11 metres, reaching 16 metres in the equinox tides. At low tide the sea can withdraw up to 10 km, baring more than 200 square kilometres of flats. Hugo said the tide comes in "at the speed of a galloping horse"; the fastest observed speed is about 6.1 km/h. The highest tides can raise a tidal bore that runs many kilometres up the rivers.

Three rivers, the Couesnon, Sée and Sélune, flow into the bay; Avranches stands on a rock where the Sée and Sélune meet. Avranches rises from 7 to 111 metres above sea level, with a view over the whole bay to Mont-Saint-Michel. Mont-Saint-Michel is a block of granite formed about 525 million years ago. It rises 92 metres and covers about 7 hectares, with a circumference of about 960 metres. Tombelaine, about 3 km away, is granite too: 45 metres high, 250 metres long and 150 wide.

Each year nearly a million cubic metres of sediment settle in the bay. Experts warned that without action the mount would be ringed by salt marsh by 2040. The dam, in service since 2009, has eight gates, each 9 metres wide, 7.4 metres high and weighing 30 tonnes. It can hold up to 1.2 million cubic metres of seawater and release it to flush silt from the estuary out to sea. The whole project cost about 184 million euros; at the highest tides Mont-Saint-Michel is an island once more.
