歷史 History

通往世界之巔的路

The Way to the Top of the World

從十六世紀的雪巴人遷徙、1953年首登，到1976年的國家公園
From the Sherpa migration of the 1500s and the first ascent of 1953 to a national park in 1976

尼泊爾東北部的昆布地區，是雪巴人的家鄉。南崎市集，被稱為「雪巴人的首都」。雪巴人的祖先，在十六世紀中葉從西藏的康區翻山而來。帶領他們的，是一位喇嘛。十九世紀中葉，馬鈴薯傳進這裡，。讓高海拔的村落能住進更多人。天波切寺建於1916年，屬寧瑪派。1934年的大地震把它震毀，後來又重建起來。1989年1月19日，電線走火又燒毀了寺院。靠著各地的捐款重建，1993年重新開光。

1953年5月29日上午11點半，。希拉里與丹增·諾蓋站上了聖母峰頂。他們從昆布冰河往上，穿過昆布冰瀑，。再登上洛子峰的山壁，抵達南坳。他們在峰頂停留了大約十五分鐘。丹增·諾蓋，就出生在這一帶的塔美村附近。1964年，希拉里監督在盧卡拉修建了一條跑道。這塊地是他向當地雪巴人買下的，花了2,650美元。跑道只有527公尺長，坡度11.7%。2008年，機場改名為「丹增—希拉里機場」。

1976年7月19日，薩加瑪塔國家公園正式成立，。面積1,148平方公里。1979年，國家公園列入世界遺產名錄。2002年，又劃設了275平方公里的緩衝區。2015年4月25日的大地震，引發普莫里峰雪崩，。雪崩衝過南側基地營，至少19人罹難。2020年，中國與尼泊爾公布新的峰高：8,848.86公尺。2022年，因冰河變薄，當局宣布基地營要往下移。從一個遷徙民族的山谷，到全世界登山者的目的地，。昆布的歷史，一直和這座山綁在一起。

The Khumbu region of north-eastern Nepal is the homeland of the Sherpa people. Namche Bazaar is known as the Sherpa capital. The Sherpas' ancestors crossed the Himalaya from Kham in Tibet in the mid-16th century, led by a lama. In the mid-19th century the potato arrived here, allowing denser settlement in the high villages. Tengboche Monastery was founded in 1916, in the Nyingma tradition. The great earthquake of 1934 destroyed it, and it was rebuilt. On 19 January 1989 an electrical fire burned it down again. Rebuilt with donations from around the world, it was consecrated in 1993.

At 11:30 in the morning on 29 May 1953, Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay stood on the summit of Everest. They climbed up the Khumbu Glacier and through the Khumbu Icefall, then up the Lhotse face to the South Col. They stayed about fifteen minutes at the top. Tenzing Norgay was born near Thame, a village in this region. In 1964 Hillary oversaw the building of an airstrip at Lukla. He bought the land from local Sherpas for 2,650 US dollars. The runway is only 527 metres long, with a gradient of 11.7%. In 2008 the airport was renamed Tenzing–Hillary Airport.

Sagarmatha National Park was formally established on 19 July 1976, covering 1,148 square kilometres. In 1979 the park was inscribed on the World Heritage List. In 2002 a buffer zone of 275 square kilometres was added. The earthquake of 25 April 2015 set off an avalanche from Pumori that swept through South Base Camp, killing at least 19 people. In 2020 China and Nepal announced a new height: 8,848.86 metres. In 2022, as the glacier thinned, officials announced the camp would move lower. From the valley of a people on the move to a goal for climbers from everywhere, the history of the Khumbu has always been tied to this mountain.

人文 Culture

雪巴人的山谷

Valleys of the Sherpa

市集、寺院、學校，以及登山者與挑夫的步道
Markets, monasteries, a school, and the trail of climbers and porters

多數人從盧卡拉開始走進昆布。往天波切沒有公路，只能步行。步道沿著杜德科西河谷往北，。每年約有四萬人走到聖母峰基地營。南崎市集海拔3,440公尺，是昆布的貿易中心。每週六早上，鎮中心有市集。十九世紀，雪巴人用犛牛馱貨翻越山口，。在西藏與印度次大陸之間的商路上做中間人。1995年，塔美—南崎水力電廠開始供電。鎮上的燈火，來自山谷裡的水。

南崎北邊的山坡上是昆瓊村，海拔3,790公尺。1961年，希拉里的喜馬拉雅信託在這裡蓋了學校。學校從兩間教室開始，現在有超過三百五十名學生。村裡的寺院，還收藏著傳說中的雪人頭皮。天波切寺坐落在杜德科西河與因札河交會的山脊上。這座寺院是昆布地區的信仰中心。每年藏曆十月，約在十到十一月，。寺院會舉行為期十九天的瑪尼仁度節。從寺院望出去，聖母峰、洛子峰與阿瑪達布朗峰一字排開。1953年的探險隊長亨特，稱這裡是世上最美的地方之一。

阿瑪達布朗峰的名字，在雪巴語裡是「母親的護身盒」。兩側長長的山脊，像母親張開保護的雙臂。山壁上懸著的冰河，。就像雪巴婦女傳統的護身墜飾。卡拉帕塔是許多健行者的終點。從這裡，能最近距離地看見聖母峰。二十世紀初起，雪巴人就是登山隊的好手。今天，觀光與健行已是雪巴人主要的生計。市集、學校、寺院和步道，串起了這幾道山谷。登山者來了又走，雪巴人一直住在這裡。

Most people enter the Khumbu on foot from Lukla. There is no road to Tengboche; it is reached only on foot. The trail runs north up the Dudh Kosi valley, and about 40,000 people a year walk on to Everest Base Camp. Namche Bazaar, at 3,440 metres, is the trading centre of the Khumbu. Every Saturday morning a market is held in the town centre. In the 19th century Sherpas took yaks over the high pass, acting as go-betweens on the trade routes between Tibet and the Indian subcontinent. In 1995 the Thame–Namche hydropower plant began to supply power. The town's lights come from the water of the valley.

Khumjung, at 3,790 metres, lies on the slopes north of Namche. In 1961 Hillary's Himalayan Trust built a school there. It began with two classrooms and now has more than 350 students. The village monastery keeps what is said to be a yeti's scalp. Tengboche Monastery sits on a ridge where the Dudh Kosi and the Imja Khola meet. It is a spiritual centre of the Khumbu. In the tenth month of the Tibetan calendar, around October and November, the monastery holds Mani Rimdu, a festival lasting nineteen days. From the monastery, Everest, Lhotse and Ama Dablam stand in a row. John Hunt, the 1953 leader, called it one of the most beautiful places in the world.

In the Sherpa language Ama Dablam means 'mother's charm box'. Its long side ridges are like a mother's protective arms. The glacier hanging on its face resembles the traditional pendant worn by Sherpa women. Kala Patthar is the end point for many trekkers. From here they get the closest accessible view of Everest. By the early 20th century Sherpas were known as skilled mountaineers. Today tourism and trekking dominate the Sherpa economy. Markets, schools, monasteries and trails link these valleys together. Climbers come and go; the Sherpas have always lived here.

自然 Nature

從松林到冰河

From Pine Forest to Glacier

五個生物氣候帶、雪豹與小貓熊，以及一座越長越大的冰河湖
Five climate belts, snow leopards and red pandas, and a glacial lake that keeps growing

薩加瑪塔國家公園，從溫帶一路延伸到終年積雪的冰雪帶，。一共跨越五個生物氣候帶。河谷兩側的森林，主要有松、鐵杉、冷杉、杜松與樺樹。櫟樹林從前分布更廣，現在只剩零星幾片。春天，天波切四周的山坡開滿杜鵑花。樺樹與杜鵑混生的林子，是亞高山帶的特色。大約三千八百到四千公尺，就是森林的上緣。再往上，只剩杜松與杜鵑的低矮灌叢。國家公園裡記錄到雪豹、麝香鹿與小貓熊。喜馬拉雅塔爾羊與鬣羚，也在陡坡上活動。

這裡記錄過194種鳥類。高山草甸，可能是瀕危的林沙錐繁殖的地方。海拔四千五百到五千五百公尺，是高山草原與矮灌木。再往上，只有貼地生長的墊狀植物。國家公園有69%的土地，在五千公尺以上，。幾乎是一片裸露的岩石與冰雪。坤布冰河長約十七公里，從海拔七千六百公尺流下。它被稱為世界上最高的冰河。伊姆札湖海拔約五千公尺，在冰河末端形成。1962年的地圖上，它還只是幾個小水池。

到了七〇年代，水池連成一座湖。今天湖面約1.3平方公里，最深處超過九十公尺。湖水被一道冰磧擋住，潰決時可能釀成洪水。後來在湖邊挖了出水道，排出超過四百萬立方公尺的水。基地營就搭在坤布冰河上，。冰河正在快速融化、變薄。從開滿杜鵑的森林，到冰河與冰湖，。一座國家公園裡，裝著從溫帶到極地的風景。

Sagarmatha National Park runs from the lower temperate zone to the nival zone of permanent snow, spanning five bioclimatic zones. The forests on the valley sides are mainly pine, hemlock, fir, juniper and birch. Oak was once more widespread; only small patches remain. In spring the hillsides around Tengboche are covered in flowering rhododendrons. Birch and rhododendron forest is typical of the subalpine belt. The tree line lies at about 3,800 to 4,000 metres. Above it, only low scrub of juniper and rhododendron remains. Snow leopards, musk deer and red pandas are recorded in the park. Himalayan tahr and serow also move about the steep slopes.

194 bird species have been recorded here. The alpine meadows may be a breeding ground for the threatened wood snipe. From about 4,500 to 5,500 metres lie alpine grassland and dwarf shrubs. Higher still, only cushion plants grow, pressed to the ground. 69% of the park lies above 5,000 metres, almost all of it bare rock, snow and ice. The Khumbu Glacier is about 17 km long, flowing down from 7,600 metres. It is called the world's highest glacier. Imja Tsho, at about 5,000 metres, formed at the snout of the glacier. On the map of 1962 it was only a few small ponds.

By the 1970s the ponds had merged into one lake. Today it covers about 1.3 square kilometres and is over 90 metres deep at most. The lake is held back by a moraine and could burst in a flood. An outlet was later built, draining more than four million cubic metres of water. The base camp stands on the Khumbu Glacier itself, and the glacier is melting and thinning fast. From rhododendron forest to glaciers and glacial lakes, one national park holds scenery from the temperate zone to the polar.

地理 Geography

世界最高的地方

The Highest Place on Earth

八千公尺高峰、四億七千萬年前的海底石灰岩，以及仍在上升的山脈
Eight-thousanders, seabed limestone 470 million years old, and mountains still rising

聖母峰海拔8,848.86公尺，是地球最高的山。尼泊爾人稱它薩加瑪塔，國家公園也以此為名。這個高度，是中國與尼泊爾在2020年共同公布的。兩國分別在2019與2020年，用衛星定位重新測量。山頂的岩石，是灰色的石灰岩，。大約形成於四億七千萬年前的奧陶紀。石灰岩裡有三葉蟲、腕足類與海百合的碎片。它原本沉積在印度大陸北邊的淺海陸棚上。印度板塊向北撞上歐亞板塊，把海底的地層推上高空。據估計，聖母峰每一百年仍上升約十公分。

國家公園集水區的四周，有二十五座以上超過六千公尺的山峰。洛子峰8,501公尺，卓奧友峰8,153公尺。昆布冰瀑在西冰斗的出口，。是南坳路線上第一道主要難關。南側基地營海拔5,364公尺，。下方的格拉克榭海拔5,163公尺，是最早的基地營。在大部分的山谷裡，聖母峰峰頂被努子峰擋住。卡拉帕塔位在普莫里峰的南稜上，是少數看得見的地方。往南，這些山谷匯入杜德科西河谷。國家公園的緩衝區，沿著河谷一路延伸到盧卡拉。

天波切海拔約3,867公尺，南崎3,440公尺，。而盧卡拉的機場，在2,846公尺。南崎在盧卡拉北方約十三公里，。是登山者適應高度、通往高喜馬拉雅的門戶。從盧卡拉到聖母峰頂，水平距離不到四十公里，。高度卻相差六千公尺。海底的石灰岩、仍在推擠的板塊、從冰河流下的大河：。世界之巔，至今還在慢慢長高。

At 8,848.86 metres, Mount Everest is the highest mountain on Earth. In Nepal it is called Sagarmatha, and the park bears that name. China and Nepal announced this height jointly in 2020, after surveys by Nepal in 2019 and China in 2020 using satellite positioning. The rock at the summit is a grey limestone, laid down about 470 million years ago in the Ordovician period. It holds fragments of trilobites, brachiopods and crinoids. It was first deposited on the shallow shelf at India's northern edge. India pushed north into Eurasia, thrusting the seabed layers up into the sky. By one estimate Everest still rises about 10 cm a century.

More than 25 peaks over 6,000 metres ring the park's catchment. Lhotse rises to 8,501 metres and Cho Oyu to 8,153. The Khumbu Icefall lies at the mouth of the Western Cwm, the first major obstacle on the South Col route. South Base Camp stands at 5,364 metres; Gorak Shep below, at 5,163 metres, was the original base camp. From most of the valley, Nuptse hides the summit of Everest. Kala Patthar, on the south ridge of Pumori, is one of the few places it shows. To the south these valleys open into the Dudh Kosi valley. The park's buffer zone runs down that valley as far as Lukla.

Tengboche lies at about 3,867 metres and Namche at 3,440; the airstrip at Lukla is at 2,846. Namche lies about 13 km north of Lukla, a gateway to the high Himalaya where trekkers acclimatise. From Lukla to the top of Everest is under 40 km in a straight line, yet the height difference is 6,000 metres. Seabed limestone, plates still pushing, great rivers born of glaciers: the top of the world is still slowly growing.

Flying a drone in Sagarmatha National Park requires registration with the Civil Aviation Authority of Nepal, approval letters from the Department of National Parks and Wildlife Conservation and the District Administration Office in Salleri, Solukhumbu, and finally a permit from the park.
