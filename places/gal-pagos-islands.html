歷史 History

Pirates, Whalers and Darwin

An accidental discovery in 1535, the Beagle in 1835, a national park in 1959 and World Heritage in 1978

加拉巴哥群島，在南美洲大陸西方約九百公里的海上，。橫跨赤道兩側。1535年3月10日，巴拿馬主教貝蘭加的船，。被海流帶到這裡，意外發現了群島。1570年，群島第一次出現在奧特柳斯與麥卡托的地圖上。後來，海盜和英國船長也陸續來到。1684年，考利畫出了群島的地圖；。1793年，科爾內特的海圖又加上了更多名字。1818到1820年，捕鯨船在這一帶發現大量抹香鯨，。捕鯨業因此興盛起來。

1832年2月12日，厄瓜多正式把群島納入領土。這座港鎮，是群島上最古老的聚落。1835年9月15日，英國的小獵犬號抵達群島，。年輕的達爾文就在船上。達爾文在這裡待了大約五個星期。巴托洛梅島的名字，來自小獵犬號上的軍官蘇利文。港鎮的名字，紀念1916年第一位來訪的。厄瓜多總統巴克里索。1959年，國家公園成立，涵蓋群島97.5%的陸地。同一年，達爾文基金會在聯合國教科文組織支持下成立。

1964年，查爾斯·達爾文研究站在聖克魯斯島正式啟用。1978年，群島成為第一批世界遺產之一。1986年，周圍七萬平方公里的海域成為海洋保護區；。2001年，海洋保護區也列入世界遺產。2007年，群島被列入瀕危世界遺產名單，。2010年7月才移除。2012年6月，平塔島最後一隻巨龜「孤獨喬治」死去，。沒有留下後代。2022年，新的赫曼達海洋保護區又加入了六萬平方公里，。受保護的海域增加到十九萬八千平方公里。

從海盜與捕鯨船的補給站，。到研究演化的天然實驗室，這是群島走過的路。

The Galápagos Islands lie some 900 km west of mainland South America, straddling the equator. On 10 March 1535 the ship of Bishop Tomás de Berlanga drifted here on the currents and found the islands by accident. In 1570 the islands first appeared on maps by Ortelius and Mercator. Pirates and English captains followed. In 1684 William Cowley mapped the islands; in 1793 James Colnett's chart added more names. In 1818–1820 whalers found sperm whales in great numbers here, and whaling boomed.

On 12 February 1832 Ecuador formally annexed the islands. This harbour town is the oldest settlement in the archipelago. On 15 September 1835 HMS Beagle reached the islands, with the young Charles Darwin aboard. Darwin spent about five weeks exploring the islands. Bartolomé Island is named after Bartholomew Sulivan, an officer on the Beagle. The town is named after Alfredo Baquerizo, the first Ecuadorian president to visit, in 1916. In 1959 the national park was created, covering 97.5% of the land. That year the Charles Darwin Foundation was founded under the auspices of UNESCO.

In 1964 the Charles Darwin Research Station was dedicated on Santa Cruz. In 1978 the islands were among the very first World Heritage Sites. In 1986 the surrounding 70,000 km² of ocean became a marine reserve; in 2001 the marine reserve joined the World Heritage Site. In 2007 UNESCO put the islands on its List of World Heritage in Danger; they were removed in July 2010. In June 2012 Lonesome George, the last known Pinta tortoise, died without leaving any offspring. In 2022 the new Hermandad Marine Reserve added 60,000 km², bringing the protected sea to 198,000 km².

From a supply stop for pirates and whalers to a natural laboratory of evolution: that is the road these islands have taken.

2022年的普查，群島有兩萬八千五百八十三位居民，。比2010年增加了大約百分之二十一。最大的城鎮，是聖克魯斯島上的阿約拉港。達爾文研究站就在港邊的學院灣旁。首府是聖克里斯托巴爾島上的巴克里索莫雷諾港，。2022年約有七千兩百九十人。鎮上有一小片海灘，專門留給海獅；。牠們也會躺在船上，甚至占據公園的長椅。鎮外約一點六公里的軍艦鳥山，。可以看到兩種黑色的軍艦鳥。

第三大的聚落，是伊莎貝拉島的比亞米爾港，。島上約兩千兩百位居民，大多住在這裡。十八世紀的捕鯨人，在弗洛雷阿納島放了一個木桶當郵筒，。讓寂寞的水手把信寄回家。今天的遊客，還會從桶裡找出寄往家鄉附近的信，。親手帶回去送，不用郵資。新冠疫情前，觀光大約占群島經濟的八成。2023年有三十二萬九千多名遊客，2024年約二十七萬九千人。群島有一百一十六處遊客點，。五十四處在陸地上，六十二處是潛水或浮潛點。

到巴托洛梅島的觀景台，要爬三百二十六級階梯。島上的尖塔岩，是群島最常被拍攝的景色之一。有個傳說，說1940年代美軍試射武器，。把尖塔岩炸成了現在的形狀；但這只是傳說。研究站有一百多位科學家、教育人員、志工與學生，。2002年獲得國際宇宙獎。依2026年的國家公園新規定，。在保護區裡禁止以休閒或個人目的飛無人機。群島上的人，和海獅、軍艦鳥住在一起，。也一起守著這片保護區。

The 2022 census counted 28,583 people in the islands, about 21% more than in 2010. The largest town is Puerto Ayora on Santa Cruz. The Darwin research station stands beside its Academy Bay. The capital is Puerto Baquerizo Moreno on San Cristóbal, home to about 7,290 people in 2022. A small town beach is reserved for the sea lions alone; they also lounge on moored boats and take over park benches. Frigatebird Hill, about 1.6 km outside town, is known for two species of black frigatebird.

The third settlement is Puerto Villamil on Isabela, home to most of the island's roughly 2,200 people. In the 18th century whalers set up a barrel on Floreana as a post box, so that lonely sailors could send letters home. Visitors today still pick out letters bound for places near their homes and deliver them by hand, with no postage. Before COVID-19 tourism made up nearly 80% of the economy. There were over 329,000 visitors in 2023 and about 279,000 in 2024. There are 116 visitor sites: 54 on land and 62 for diving or snorkelling.

Reaching the viewpoint on Bartolomé means climbing 326 steps. Its Pinnacle Rock is among the most photographed views in the islands. A legend says American servicemen shaped Pinnacle Rock in weapons tests in the 1940s; it is only a legend. The station has a team of over a hundred scientists, educators, volunteers and students, and won the International Cosmos Prize in 2002. Under the national park rules issued in 2026, flying drones for recreation or private use is banned in the protected areas. The people of the islands live alongside sea lions and frigatebirds, and look after this protected place together.

加拉巴哥的生物，啟發了達爾文的天擇演化理論。許多物種，世界上其他地方都沒有。原生的維管束植物約有五百種，。其中大約一百八十種是特有種。海裡記錄到兩千九百零九種生物，。其中百分之十八點二是特有種。尖塔岩後面的洞穴裡，。有一小群加拉巴哥企鵝在這裡繁殖。群島的海鬣蜥，是世界上唯一的一種海洋蜥蜴。原生的陸地哺乳類有十二種，十一種是特有種。

粉紅陸鬣蜥只住在沃爾夫火山的山坡上。1986年被巡守員發現，2009年確認是獨立的物種。2015年火山噴發時，熔岩流向東與東南，。避開了鬣蜥居住的北坡和西坡。埃斯帕諾拉島，是波紋信天翁唯一的繁殖地，。族群約兩萬五千到三萬隻。牠是唯一在熱帶繁殖的信天翁，。翼展可達兩公尺半，可以活到四十歲。1960年代，島上只剩下十四隻巨龜：。十二隻母龜和兩隻公龜。

1963到1974年，牠們被帶到聖克魯斯島繁殖；。1977年，又有一隻公龜從聖地牙哥動物園回來。之後有超過一千五百隻小巨龜被送回島上；。1978年，島上的山羊也被清除。內格拉火山的南坡與東南坡，。住著瀕危的內格拉山巨龜。群島已記錄到一千五百七十五種外來物種，。其中五十九種被認為高度入侵。從企鵝、信天翁到巨龜，。這些只在這裡的生命，靠人們細心地守護。

The wildlife of the Galápagos inspired Darwin's theory of evolution by natural selection. Many of its species are found nowhere else. There are about 500 native vascular plant species, some 180 of them endemic. 2,909 marine species have been recorded, 18.2% of them endemic. In a cave behind Pinnacle Rock a small colony of Galápagos penguins breeds. The marine iguana is the only one of its kind in the world. Of the 12 native land mammals, 11 are endemic.

The pink land iguana lives only on the slopes of Wolf Volcano. Rangers found it in 1986, and it was recognised as a species in 2009. When the volcano erupted in 2015, the lava ran east and south-east, away from the northern and western slopes where the iguanas live. Española is the only breeding ground of the waved albatross, a population of 25,000 to 30,000 birds. It is the only albatross that breeds in the tropics, with wings up to 2.5 metres across, and it can live to 40. By the 1960s only 14 giant tortoises were left on the island: 12 females and 2 males.

From 1963 to 1974 they were taken to Santa Cruz for breeding; in 1977 a third male came back from the San Diego Zoo. Since then more than 1,500 young tortoises have been returned to the island; in 1978 its goats were removed. On the southern and south-eastern slopes of Sierra Negra lives the endangered Sierra Negra giant tortoise. 1,575 introduced species have been recorded in the islands, 59 of them considered highly invasive. From penguins and albatrosses to giant tortoises, life found only here depends on careful protection.

群島的陸地約七千八百八十平方公里，。散布在四萬五千平方公里的海面上。一共有十八座主要島嶼、三座較小的島，。和一百零七塊岩礁與小島。最大的伊莎貝拉島，。占了群島將近四分之三的陸地。伊莎貝拉北端的沃爾夫火山，就在赤道上，。是群島的最高點，大約一千七百公尺。伊莎貝拉島由六座火山連在一起形成；。沃爾夫在2022年1月又噴發了一次。

內格拉火山口長約九點三公里、寬七點二公里，。是加拉巴哥所有火山中最大的火山口。它也是伊莎貝拉最淺的火山口，只有約一百公尺深。1992到1998年間，火山口的地面升高了兩百四十公分。它在2005年與2018年都曾噴發；。1961到2011年間，九座活火山一共噴發了二十四次。群島坐在納斯卡板塊上，板塊在一個固定的熱點上方移動，。火山活動至少已經持續了兩千萬年。所以島嶼由西向東越來越老：伊莎貝拉約六十五萬年，。聖克魯斯約一百七十萬年，聖克里斯托巴爾約三百二十萬年。

最南邊的埃斯帕諾拉島，大約四百萬年，。是最古老的島之一，面積約六十平方公里。巴托洛梅島只有一點二平方公里，最高一百一十四公尺。島的東邊是熔岩濺落錐，西邊是侵蝕過的凝灰岩丘。冷的洪堡洋流流過群島，。每隔三到七年，聖嬰現象會帶來暖水與大雨。六月到十一月是涼爽多霧的「毛毛雨季」，約二十二度；。十二月到五月是暖季，約二十五度。熱點、板塊與洋流，。造出了這片還在生長的群島。

The islands hold some 7,880 km² of land spread over 45,000 km² of ocean. There are 18 main islands, 3 smaller ones and 107 rocks and islets. Isabela, the largest island, makes up nearly three-quarters of the land. Wolf Volcano, at the northern end of Isabela, sits on the equator and is the highest point of the islands, about 1,700 metres. Isabela was formed by six volcanoes merging; Wolf erupted again in January 2022.

The Sierra Negra caldera is about 9.3 km by 7.2 km, the largest of all the Galápagos volcanoes. It is also the shallowest on Isabela, only about 100 metres deep. Between 1992 and 1998 its floor rose by 240 cm. It erupted in 2005 and again in 2018; between 1961 and 2011 nine active volcanoes erupted 24 times. The islands ride the Nazca Plate as it moves over a fixed hotspot; volcanism has gone on here for at least 20 million years. So the islands get older from west to east: Isabela about 0.65 million years, Santa Cruz 1.7 million and San Cristóbal 3.2 million.

Española, the southernmost, is about 4 million years old, one of the oldest islands, with an area of about 60 km². Bartolomé covers only 1.2 km² and rises to 114 metres. Splatter cones lie to its east, an eroded tuff cone to its west. The cold Humboldt Current flows past the islands, and every 3 to 7 years El Niño brings warm water and heavy rain. June to November is the cool, misty garúa season, around 22 °C; December to May is the warm season, around 25 °C. A hotspot, a moving plate and ocean currents have built these islands, which are still growing.

Under the 2026 resolution of the Galápagos National Park Directorate (MAE-PNG/DIR-2026-0006-RM, published 6 March 2026), recreational or private drone use is banned outright in the protected areas; only scientific research, rescue, environmental management and some approved filming can be authorised by the park, operators need Ecuadorian civil aviation (DGAC) certification, and the ceiling is 122 m.
