歷史 History

伊察人的城市

The City of the Itza

從古典期晚期到後古典期早期，奇琴伊察的興起與兩次「發現」
The rise of Chichen Itza, from the Late Classic to the early Postclassic

這裡是墨西哥尤卡坦半島北部的奇琴伊察，一座瑪雅古城。城市從古典期晚期，大約公元六〇〇年起，。一直使用到約一二〇〇年。約在八〇〇年，伊察人來到這裡；。「伊察」是一個族群與家族的名稱。九〇〇到一〇五〇年是全盛期，。它控制著半島北部與中部。遺址最早的象形文字日期相當於八三二年，。最晚的是九九八年。

建築混合了瑪雅風格與墨西哥中部的風格，。也有普克與切內斯樣式。戰士神廟的格局，。和托爾特克首都圖拉的B神廟相似。今天多數學者認為，那是文化交流，。不是一場移民或征服。1904到1910年，美國人愛德華・湯普森。在北邊的聖井打撈文物。卡內基研究院在1925到1928年，。挖掘並修復了戰士神廟。

1988年，奇琴伊察列入世界遺產。2007年，城堡金字塔在全球票選中。名列「世界新七大奇蹟」。遺址是聯邦財產，由國家人類學與歷史研究院管理；。2010年州政府買下主要古蹟下方的土地。一座城市的興衰，。大約橫跨六百年。

This is Chichen Itza, in the north of Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula: a Maya city. It was occupied from the Late Classic, around AD 600, into the early Postclassic, around 1200. The Itza arrived around 800; the name refers to an ethnic and lineage group. Its peak came between 900 and 1050, as a regional capital ruling north and central Yucatán. The earliest hieroglyphic date here equals AD 832; the last known date is from 998.

The buildings mix Maya styles with those of central Mexico, plus Puuc and Chenes forms. The Temple of the Warriors complex resembles Temple B at Tula, the Toltec capital. Most scholars now see this as cultural diffusion, not migration or conquest. From 1904 to 1910 Edward Thompson dredged the Sacred Cenote to the north. The Carnegie Institution excavated and restored the Temple of the Warriors from 1925 to 1928.

In 1988 Chichen Itza was inscribed as World Heritage. In 2007 El Castillo was named one of the New 7 Wonders after a worldwide vote. The ruins are federal property, run by INAH; in 2010 the state of Yucatán bought the land beneath the main monuments. The city's rise and decline spans some six centuries.

人文 Culture

球場、天文台與聖井

A Ball Court, an Observatory and a Sacred Well

奇琴伊察的公共建築，以及它們留下的問題
Chichen Itza's public buildings, and the questions they leave

旁白全文 Full narration

奇琴伊察有一整片公共建築，。從空中看最清楚。大球場長一百六十八公尺、寬七十公尺，。是中美洲保存最好的球場。兩側平行的平台各長九十五公尺，。牆高八公尺。城堡金字塔正中，。春分秋分前後會出現「蛇影」。傍晚的三角形陰影落在北側階梯的欄杆上，。像一條蛇往下爬。

不過這個光影會持續好幾個星期，。學者認為不太可能是刻意用來標記日期。戰士神廟前排滿雕著戰士的石柱，。階梯頂端有一尊查克莫爾像。蝸牛塔是一座圓形建築，。裡面有螺旋石階，約建於九〇六年。它可能是天文台：二十九種天象裡，。有二十種能在建築中找到對應的視線。瑪雅人知道金星週期約五八四天，。五個週期正好等於八個太陽年。

頭骨台上的頭骨是直立排列的，。不同於高原上的橫向排法。北邊的聖井直徑約六十公尺，。井壁下方約二十七公尺才是水面。井裡撈出黃金、玉、陶器、香料與人骨；。是否全是人祭，至今仍有爭議。2017年有兩百六十萬名遊客來到這裡。

Chichen Itza has a whole precinct of public buildings, easiest to read from the air. The Great Ball Court is 168 by 70 metres, the largest and best preserved in ancient Mesoamerica. Its parallel platforms are each 95 metres long, with walls 8 metres high. At the centre stands El Castillo; around the equinoxes a "serpent" appears on it. Late-afternoon triangles of shadow fall on the northern balustrade like a serpent descending the stairs.

But the effect lasts several weeks, so scholars think it unlikely to have been built to mark dates. The Temple of the Warriors is fronted by carved warrior columns; a Chac Mool figure sits at the top of the stairs. El Caracol is a round building with a spiral stair inside, dated to around AD 906. It may have been an observatory: of 29 astronomical events, sight lines for 20 can be found in the structure. The Maya knew Venus repeats every 584 days, and that five Venus cycles equal eight solar years.

On the Tzompantli, the skull platform, skulls were set vertically, unlike the horizontal racks of the highlands. To the north, the Sacred Cenote is about 60 metres across, its water some 27 metres below the rim. Gold, jade, pottery, incense and human remains came out of it; whether all were sacrifices is still debated. In 2017, 2.6 million visitors came here.

自然 Nature

沒有河的半島

A Peninsula Without Rivers

石灰岩、天然井與地底河，奇琴伊察為什麼建在這裡
Limestone, cenotes and underground rivers: why the city stands here

旁白全文 Full narration

從空中看尤卡坦半島北部，。一片平坦的綠色。這裡是石灰岩喀斯特地形，。地面上沒有河流或溪流。內陸的河流，都在地底下流動。人們取水靠的是天然井，。也就是石灰岩塌陷後露出地下水的井洞。奇琴伊察一帶有四個看得到的天然井，。終年有水，吸引人在此定居。

北邊的聖井，。和城市以一條石砌大道相連。2015年，科學家判定。庫庫爾坎神廟底下還藏著一個天然井。考古學家至今沒有親眼見過它。往東南約三公里，。是伊基爾天然井。井口往下，可以看到地下水面。

再往東，是巴蘭坎切洞穴。這是一處瑪雅人使用過的洞穴遺址。城市往四面八方鋪出八十多條石砌大道。在沒有河的土地上，。城市跟著水井而生。

From the air, northern Yucatán is a flat expanse of green. This is limestone karst country, with no rivers or streams on the surface. Inland, the rivers run underground. People drew water from cenotes: sinkholes where the limestone has given way to the water table. Four visible cenotes here gave water all year and made the site attractive for settlement.

The Sacred Cenote to the north is joined to the city by a raised causeway. In 2015 scientists determined that a hidden cenote lies beneath the Temple of Kukulcán. Archaeologists have never seen it. About three kilometres south-east is the Ik Kil cenote. Through the opening the groundwater comes into view.

Farther east lies the cave of Balankanché. It is a cave site used by the Maya. More than 80 causeways, sacbeob, spread from the city in every direction. In a land without rivers, the city grew where the water was.

地理 Geography

九層平台與四道階梯

Nine Terraces and Four Stairways

庫庫爾坎神廟、大道與天然井的尺寸
The measurements of El Castillo, the causeway and the cenote

旁白全文 Full narration

這部影片用尺寸來看奇琴伊察。城堡金字塔約三十公尺高，。頂上還有一座六公尺的神廟。它由九層方形平台疊成，。每層約二點五七公尺高。底座每邊約五十五點三公尺，。四面各有一道階梯。階梯的坡度是四十五度。

大球場一百六十八乘七十公尺，。球場牆高八公尺。一號大道從城堡金字塔往北，通往聖井。大道長約二百七十公尺，。平均寬約九公尺。聖井直徑約六十公尺，。垂直的井壁往下約二十七公尺。湯普森1909年曾潛入井中，。說底部有一層約五公尺厚的藍色顏料。

從上面看，主要建築都散布在一大片。平坦的廣場與林地之間。往西南約二十公里，。是另一座瑪雅遺址亞舒納。半島上的瑪雅城市，。散布在同一片平坦的石灰岩台地上。九層平台、四道階梯、一條大道與一口井，。組成了這座城市的骨架。

This film reads Chichen Itza through its measurements. El Castillo is about 30 metres high, with a 6-metre temple on top. It is built of nine square terraces, each about 2.57 metres high. Its base measures about 55.3 metres a side, with a stairway on each of the four faces. The stairways climb at 45 degrees.

The Great Ball Court measures 168 by 70 metres; its walls are 8 metres high. Sacbe Number One runs north from El Castillo to the Sacred Cenote. It is about 270 metres long and averages 9 metres wide. The Sacred Cenote is about 60 metres across, with sheer walls dropping some 27 metres. Thompson dived into it in 1909 and reported a layer of blue pigment about 5 metres thick.

From above, the main buildings are scattered across flat plazas and woodland. About twenty kilometres south-west is another Maya site, Yaxuná. The peninsula's Maya cities are spread across the same flat limestone platform. Nine terraces, four stairways, a causeway and a well make up the skeleton of the city.
