歷史 History

仙跡、燈杆與八拱橋

Immortals, a Light and Eight Arches

從退潮涉水到跨海步橋，一座離岸島的百年紀事
From wading at low tide to a footbridge: a century on an offshore island

旁白全文 Full narration

臺東成功鎮東北方約三公里，岬角外有一座小島。人們怎麼走到島上？這趟飛行從這個問題開始。島上有三塊巨大的岩石。傳說八仙中的呂洞賓、李鐵拐、何仙姑曾登臨此島。島上的仙劍峽、合歡洞，也都附會著三仙的故事。這裡舊稱釣魚台。1944年的一張地圖，則把它標作 SANSENDAI。陸地在岬角收尾，對面就是小島。過去沒有橋，遊客只能趁退潮涉水過去。

1987年，跨海步橋完工。八座拱連成近四百公尺，把岬角和小島接在一起。橋身起伏像波浪。官方的形容是：宛如一條巨龍伏臥在海上。建橋是為了讓當地阿美族人登島採集海產，也讓遊客上橋觀景。島上還有更早的建設。1915年，日治時期在這裡設立一座白色燈杆。燈杆高7公尺，立在第二岩峰呂洞賓岩上。燈火高出高潮面61.5公尺，是東海岸最早的燈台。

1999年改用太陽能充電蓄電池，如今無人看管。西南方的成功鎮，舊名麻荖漏。1921年日人改稱新港，戰後才改為成功。成功漁港至今又叫新港漁港。從港口往東北望，三仙台就在海面盡頭。回到橋上。鋼構橋身長年受鹽害侵蝕。2021年，橋梁完成一次耐震補強。2022年9月18日大地震後，檢測發現橋身位移、橋墩裂損。同年12月，管理處宣布封橋。

2024年2月有條件重新開放。風力達十級就預先封閉，橋上同時最多容納800人。2026年5月起全面整修：防蝕塗裝、地磚、護欄與橋墩補強。施工期間封橋，預計2027年底完工。陸側的遊客中心重新建置，2016年10月啟用。整個景區由東部海岸國家風景區管理處管理。傳說、燈杆、步橋，三個年代疊在同一座小島上。天色暗下來，島上的燈依舊按時亮起。

About 3 km northeast of Chenggong, Taitung, a small island lies off a headland. How have people reached it? This flight starts from that question. Three huge rocks stand on the island. Legend says three of the Eight Immortals, Lü Dongbin, Li Tieguai and He Xiangu, once landed here. Features on the island such as Xianjian Gorge and Hehuan Cave are tied to the same story. An older name for the place is Diaoyutai. A map of 1944 labels it SANSENDAI. The land ends at the headland, with the island just across the water. Before there was a bridge, visitors could only wade over at low tide.

In 1987 the footbridge across the water was completed. Eight linked arches, nearly 400 m in all, join the headland to the island. The deck rises and falls like a row of waves. The scenic area's own image for it is a great dragon lying on the sea. It was built so local Amis people could reach the island to gather seafood, and for visitors to cross. The island holds an older piece of building. In 1915, under Japanese rule, a white light pole was set up here. The pole is 7 m tall and stands on the second peak, Lü Dongbin Rock. Its light is 61.5 m above high water, the earliest light station on the east coast.

In 1999 it was converted to solar-charged batteries; today it is unattended. Chenggong town, to the southwest, was once called Malaolou. The Japanese renamed it Xingang in 1921; it became Chenggong after the war. The harbour is still known as Xingang Fishing Port. Looking northeast from the harbour, Sanxiantai sits at the edge of the sea. Back at the bridge: its steel structure is worn year after year by salt. In 2021 a seismic retrofit of the bridge was completed. After the earthquake of 18 September 2022, inspection found displacement and damaged piers. That December the administration closed the bridge.

It reopened in February 2024, with conditions. It shuts in advance when winds reach force 10, and holds at most 800 people at a time. Full repairs began in May 2026: anti-corrosion coating, paving, railings and pier strengthening. The bridge is closed during the work, which is due to finish at the end of 2027. The visitor centre on the mainland side was rebuilt and opened in October 2016. The whole site is run by the East Coast National Scenic Area Administration. Legend, light and footbridge: three eras layered on one small island. As the sky darkens, the island's light comes on as it always has.

人文 Culture

比西里岸：羊、海與曙光

Pisirian: Goats, Sea and First Light

阿美族的地名、採集的礁岸與一座漁港
Amis place names, a gathering shore and a fishing harbour

旁白全文 Full narration

天剛亮，光從太平洋那一側照過來。住在這片海岸的人，怎麼稱呼這座島、怎麼使用它？岬角西北邊的濱海部落叫比西里岸。阿美語 siri 是山羊，Pisirian 意思是養羊的地方。過去族人利用潮水起落，在三仙台島上放養羊群。東管處也記載，三仙台原名就是 PiSiLiAng。另有記載的阿美語名 nuwalian，意為最東之地。漢人說三位仙人來過；阿美族說的是另一個故事。傳說島下海底洞穴住著海龍「及發烏安」，守護此地。

島邊的珊瑚礁岩區，是海岸阿美族採集的地方。海藻、海菜、螺類，還有魚。2022年封橋時，管理處請白守蓮部落居民暫時不要過橋採集。居民說，會改到鄰近的地方去。部落裡有十一處與幾米品牌合作的彩繪，還有漂流木做的羊。Pawpaw 在阿美語裡指海上的浮球。藝術家用廢棄浮球做成鼓，部落孩子組成寶抱鼓樂團。成功鎮的聚落由北而南排在海岸平原上，以阿美族為主。每年七月到八月初，各部落陸續舉行豐年祭。

新港漁港是東海岸最大的漁港。鰹魚、旗魚、鬼頭刀、鮪魚，是主要的漁獲。這片海造就了成功獨特的鏢旗魚技藝。下午魚市開拍，喊價從小魚一路賣到大魚。港邊萬善廟有一尊木製旗魚神，長3.6公尺。元宵遶境時祂是主角；每年十月前後還有旗魚季。成功海濱公園的觀海平台，可以眺望三仙台。草地音樂會和迎曙光活動，也在這裡舉行。每年元旦，三仙台有迎曙光活動。曙光音樂會、淨灘，還有太陽寶寶郵筒。

攝影的人來得更早，等星軌、銀河和晨曦。礫石區的小水池，映出日出的光影。2026年橋梁整修動工前，管理處先蒐集了在地部落的意見。養羊的地方、海龍守護的島、迎接元旦曙光的舞台。同一片海岸，在不同的語言裡有不同的名字。

Day is breaking, and the light comes in from the Pacific side. What do the people of this coast call the island, and how have they used it? The seaside village northwest of the headland is Pisirian. Siri is Amis for goat: Pisirian means the place where goats are kept. Villagers once used the rise and fall of the tide to graze goats on the island. The scenic area records that Sanxiantai itself was first named PiSiLiAng. Another recorded Amis name, nuwalian, is glossed as the easternmost land. Han settlers told of three immortals; the Amis tell a different story. In their legend a sea dragon lives in an undersea cave below the island and guards this place.

The coral-rock flats by the island are where the coastal Amis gather food. Seaweed, sea vegetables, sea snails, and fish. When the bridge closed in 2022, Baishoulian villagers were asked not to cross to gather for a while. They said they would gather in nearby areas instead. The village has eleven murals made with the Jimmy Liao brand, and goats built from driftwood. Pawpaw is the Amis word for a float out on the sea. An artist made drums from discarded floats, and village children formed the PawPaw drum group. Chenggong's settlements run north to south along the coastal plain; the Amis are the main community. From July into early August, the villages hold their harvest festivals one after another.

Xingang, the harbour of Chenggong, is the largest fishing port on the east coast. Bonito, marlin, mahi-mahi and tuna make up most of the catch. These waters shaped Chenggong's distinctive craft of harpooning marlin. In the afternoon the fish auction begins, working up from small fish to the largest. The harbourside Wanshan Temple keeps a wooden marlin deity 3.6 m long. It stars in the Lantern Festival procession, and a marlin festival is held around October. From the viewing deck of Chenggong's seaside park, Sanxiantai is in sight. Lawn concerts and sunrise gatherings are held here as well. Every New Year's Day, Sanxiantai holds a first-sunrise event. There is a dawn concert, a beach clean-up, and a 'Sun Baby' postbox.

Photographers come earlier still, for star trails, the Milky Way and the dawn. Small pools among the pebbles mirror the light of sunrise. Before the bridge repairs began in 2026, the administration first gathered the views of local villages. A place for goats, an island with a dragon guardian, a stage for the New Year's dawn. One stretch of coast carries different names in different languages.

自然 Nature

礫灘、礁岩與濱海植物

Pebbles, Reef and Shore Plants

一座小島為什麼被列為保護區
Why a small island is listed for protection

旁白全文 Full narration

一道岬角、一座小島，四周是太平洋。這麼小的地方，為什麼被列為自然保護區？岬角北側是一片礫石海灘。浪來回沖刷石頭，整片海灘轟轟作響。礫岩之間有岩鷺、東方環頸鴴和黃足鷸出沒。夏天，還常見燕鷗類在這一帶飛舞。通往島上的橋做成一段段階梯，是為了避免生態被破壞。上島只能步行，來回約需兩小時。三仙台島區被列為自然保護區。人為干擾少，這裡成為研究海岸植物生態的重要據點。

從拱橋往島內的步道兩旁，幾乎全是林投。白水木和臺灣海棗，也長在這座島上。官方列出的稀有濱海植物，還有水芫花、毛苦參等。所以島上有一條規定：不採集任何動植物。島上的環島步道全長約1.57公里。人走在固定的路線上，兩旁留給植物和岩石。島邊的淺海裡是珊瑚礁。從空中看，水色由淺到深，一層一層往外變化。島的南端到基翬一帶海域，有珊瑚礁群和熱帶魚群。這片海域同樣已列為自然保護區。

再往外，是黑潮經過的海。這道暖流從北赤道帶著能量一路北上。附近海域魚類豐富，是有名的漁場與磯釣場。回到陸側的礫灘。元旦迎曙光活動裡，有一項叫「給寄居蟹一個家」。2026年橋梁整修前，管理處先完成了生態檢核。封橋期間，礫石海岸仍可觀浪、做生態觀察。西南方三公里多的成功海濱公園，面對同一片海。公園提醒遊客：不撿拾海邊的石頭和貝殼。

回頭看三仙台。被列入保護的不只是島，還有島旁的那片海。礫灘、珊瑚礁、濱海植物和黑潮，在這裡緊緊相鄰。來的人留下的痕跡越少，它們越能保持原樣。

A headland, a small island, and the Pacific on every side. Why is so small a place listed as a nature protection area? North of the headland lies a beach of pebbles. As surf drags the stones back and forth, the whole beach rumbles. Pacific reef herons, Kentish plovers and grey-tailed tattlers frequent the rocks here. In summer, terns are often seen flying over this stretch. The bridge to the island was built as flights of steps, to keep the ecology from being damaged. The island is reached only on foot, about two hours there and back. The island is listed as a nature protection area. With little human disturbance, it is an important site for studying coastal plant ecology.

Along the trail leading in from the bridge, screw pine grows almost without a gap. Tree heliotrope and the Formosan date palm grow on the island too. The scenic area's list of rare shore plants also names Pemphis acidula and Sophora tomentosa. Hence one rule on the island: collect no plant and no animal. The loop trail around the island is about 1.57 km long. People keep to one fixed line; the ground on either side is left to plants and rock. In the shallows beside the island lies coral reef. From the air the water shades from pale to deep, band after band, toward open sea. From the island's south end to Jihui, the sea holds coral reefs and schools of tropical fish. These waters, too, are listed as a nature protection area.

Farther out is the sea where the Kuroshio passes. The warm current arrives from the north equatorial region, carrying its energy north. The surrounding sea is rich in fish, a well-known fishing ground and rock-fishing spot. Back on the pebble beach of the mainland side. One activity at the New Year sunrise event is called 'A Home for the Hermit Crab'. Before the bridge repairs of 2026, the administration first completed an ecological check. While the bridge is closed, the pebble shore stays open for watching waves and wildlife. Chenggong's seaside park, a little over 3 km to the southwest, faces the same sea. It asks visitors not to pick up stones or shells from the shore.

Looking back at Sanxiantai. What is listed for protection is not only the island but the sea beside it. Pebble beach, coral reef, shore plants and the Kuroshio lie side by side here. The lighter the trace each visitor leaves, the longer they stay as they are.

地理 Geography

岬角如何變成島

How a Headland Became an Island

火山集塊岩、海蝕地形與隆起的珊瑚礁
Volcanic agglomerate, wave-cut landforms and raised reef

在 YouTube 觀看 Watch on YouTube在模擬器飛這段 Fly this tour

旁白全文 Full narration

從太平洋這一側看，小島、步橋、岬角排成一線。這座島原本和陸地相連，它是怎麼分開的？三仙台島由都巒山層的火山集塊岩構成。也就是說，它的身世要從火山說起。這裡原是一處伸入海中的岬角。海水侵蝕，岬角的頸部逐漸斷開，前端成了離岸島。斷開的缺口，如今由步橋從上方跨過。全島面積約22公頃，最高點海拔約77公尺。島上三個大型海蝕柱，就是遠看的三座岩峰。

島上散布著海蝕溝、海蝕柱和海蝕凹壁。這些形狀，都是海浪長期侵蝕留下來的。岩面上還有壺穴。最大的一個，直徑達5公尺、深4公尺。島的東北岸有一座隧道型的海蝕門。長58公尺、寬約3公尺、高3到4公尺。島的周圍分布著隆起的珊瑚礁。珊瑚在海裡生長，如今礁體卻高出海面。整個三仙台，由離岸小島、珊瑚礁海岸和礫石海岸組成。

從三仙台向西望，陸地是多層的海階。海階是被抬高的舊海岸平台，一階一階往山邊升高。台11線從岬角西側經過，三仙台就在約110.5公里處。北邊約六公里的石雨傘，是另一道伸入海中的岬角。長約一公里，屬石灰岩隆起的海蝕地形。上層是較硬的珊瑚礁岩，下層是較易侵蝕的礫岩。下層先被侵蝕，留下上大下小、像傘的平衡岩。這裡的濱台上也有壺穴，直徑約3.5公尺、深約1.5公尺。

兩處岬角岩石不同，雕刻它們的卻是同一片海。回到三仙台。岬角變成島，並不是故事的結尾。海浪仍在侵蝕這些岩石，地形還在改變。火山岩、海蝕、珊瑚礁和海階，四條線索寫在同一段海岸。依序讀下來，就是三仙台的來歷。

Seen from the Pacific side, island, footbridge and headland fall into one line. The island was once joined to the land. How did it come apart? The island is built of volcanic agglomerate of the Duluanshan Formation. Its story, in other words, begins with volcanoes. This was once a headland reaching out into the sea. Wave erosion slowly cut through its neck, and the tip became an offshore island. The gap where the neck gave way is now spanned by the footbridge. The island covers about 22 hectares and rises to roughly 77 m above the sea. Its three large sea stacks are the three rock peaks seen from a distance.

Sea-cut gullies, stacks and notches are scattered across the island. All of these shapes were left by long wave erosion. The rock surface also carries potholes. The largest is 5 m across and 4 m deep. On the island's northeast shore is a tunnel-shaped sea arch. It is 58 m long, about 3 m wide and 3 to 4 m high. Raised coral reef is found around the island. Coral grows under water, yet here the reef now stands above the sea. Sanxiantai as a whole is an offshore island, a coral-reef shore and a pebble shore.

Looking west from Sanxiantai, the land rises in several marine terraces. Each terrace is an old shore platform since lifted, stepping up toward the hills. Provincial Highway 11 passes west of the headland; Sanxiantai is near its 110.5 km mark. About 6 km to the north, Shiyusan is another finger of rock reaching into the sea. Some 1 km long, it is raised limestone shaped by wave erosion. Harder coral-reef rock lies on top of conglomerate that erodes more easily. The lower layer wears away first, leaving a top-heavy balanced rock like an umbrella. The shore platform here has potholes too, about 3.5 m across and 1.5 m deep.

The two headlands are made of different rock, yet the same sea has carved them both. Back at Sanxiantai: becoming an island was not the end of the story. Waves are still wearing at this rock, and the landform is still changing. Volcanic rock, wave erosion, coral reef and terraces: four clues written on one stretch of coast. Read in order, they are the making of Sanxiantai.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

東部海岸國家風景區管理處曾於2018年預告：三仙台遊憩區周邊除公務、緊急搜救外，未經該處核准不得從事遙控無人機飛航。實際飛行前請用民航局「Drone Map」App 查詢並依規定申請。

In 2018 the East Coast National Scenic Area Administration gave notice of a rule barring drone flights around the Sanxiantai recreation area without its approval, except for official duties and emergency rescue. Before any real flight, check the CAA "Drone Map" app and apply as required.
