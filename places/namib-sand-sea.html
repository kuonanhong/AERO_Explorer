歷史 History

世界上最古老的沙漠

The World's Oldest Desert

六千萬年前出現的沙漠、一千五百萬年前的砂岩、九百年的枯樹，到2013年的世界遺產
A desert begun 60 million years ago, ancient sandstone, 900-year-old trees and World Heritage in 2013

納米布沙海，在非洲西南部的大西洋岸，。位於納米比亞的納米布-諾克盧福國家公園。大約六千萬年前，這裡就出現了最早的沙漠跡象；。一千六百萬到兩千萬年前，沙漠達到高峰。塞斯瑞姆一帶的宗達布砂岩，。是大約一千五百萬年前的沙海，固結成了岩石。岩層裡保存著化石，。主要是植物根部的痕跡和白蟻的巢穴。塞斯瑞姆峽谷，大約在五百萬年前的上新世形成，。長約一公里，最深約三十公尺。

早期的阿非利卡移民，。把六條劍羚皮帶接在一起，才能從峽谷底打到水。這些沙子的旅程，從奧蘭治河上游的侵蝕開始，。從內陸被搬運了數千公里。河流把沙帶到海岸，再由南風與沿岸流往北推送。這套搬運沙子的系統，已經運作了五百多萬年。托普納爾人，是納米比亞最古老的居民之一，。幾百年來，一直使用這片土地和它的資源。他們採集野生的納拉瓜果實，。這是沙漠裡重要的食物。

索蘇斯維旁的死亡谷，枯樹大約有九百年的歷史，。大約在六、七百年前死去。沙丘擋住了查烏查布河，水再也流不到那片窪地，。樹木因此缺水而死。2013年6月21日，納米布沙海列入世界遺產，。同時符合全部四項自然遺產標準。依國際自然保護聯盟的說法，。這是十年來第一次有遺產地同時符合四項標準。在這裡，探勘鑽石的許可已被取消，。採礦在遺產範圍內是被禁止的。

從六千萬年前開始的沙漠，。到今天，這片沙海還在慢慢地移動。

The Namib Sand Sea lies on the Atlantic coast of south-western Africa, in Namibia's Namib-Naukluft National Park. The first signs of a desert here appeared about 60 million years ago; it reached its peak 16 to 20 million years ago. The Tsondab Sandstone around Sesriem is the sand sea of about 15 million years ago, turned to stone. It holds fossils, mostly traces of plant roots and termite structures. Sesriem Canyon formed about 5 million years ago, in the Pliocene; it is about a kilometre long and up to 30 metres deep.

Early Afrikaans settlers joined six oryx-hide belts to draw water up from the canyon floor. The journey of this sand begins with erosion at the headwaters of the Orange River, carried thousands of kilometres from the interior. Rivers brought it to the coast, and southerly winds and longshore drift pushed it north. This sand-moving system has been at work for more than 5 million years. The Topnaar are among the oldest inhabitants of Namibia, and have used this land and its resources for centuries. They harvest the fruit of the wild !nara melon, an important food in the desert.

Near Sossusvlei, the dead trees of Deadvlei are about 900 years old; they died some 600 to 700 years ago. Dunes cut the pan off from the Tsauchab River, and the trees died for lack of water. On 21 June 2013 the Namib Sand Sea was inscribed as a World Heritage Site, meeting all four natural criteria. According to IUCN it was the first such inscription in ten years. Diamond prospecting licences here are being cancelled, and mining is prohibited in the property.

A desert that began 60 million years ago is still, today, a sea of sand slowly on the move.

A Nama "no return", a dune at kilometre 45 and one of the most photographed places in Africa

「索蘇斯維」大約是「死路沼澤」的意思。「維」是阿非利卡語的沼澤，「索蘇斯」是納馬語的「不歸」。「死亡谷」的原名 Deadvlei，意思其實是「死去的沼澤」；。翻成「死亡山谷」，是誤譯。塞斯瑞姆大門，。是遊客進入國家公園前往索蘇斯維的主要入口。清晨，熱氣球從大門出發；。公園四分之三的遊客，都集中在塞斯瑞姆這一帶。從大門到索蘇斯維約六十六公里：。前六十公里是柏油路，最後六公里是沙地，要四輪傳動車。

45號沙丘的名字，。來自它就在這條路的第四十五公里。它高超過一百七十公尺，。是遊客很喜歡停下來的地方。2023年，索蘇斯維有十九萬八千多名遊客。它是撒哈拉以南非洲最常被拍攝的地方之一。電影《The Cell》和《The Fall》，。都有部分在死亡谷拍攝。離停車場約四公里的隱藏谷，。是這一帶最少人去的窪地。

觀光也帶來壓力：。違法又有害的越野駕駛、衛生與污染問題。依納米比亞環境部2025年的說明，。沒有官方許可，國家公園內不准使用無人機。帶著無人機的遊客，。也必須在公園入口申報並登記。托普納爾人在這片土地上生活了好幾百年；。今天，它也是全世界旅人的目的地。名字、道路與鏡頭，。把這片沙海帶到了世界各地。

Sossusvlei means roughly "dead-end marsh": "vlei" is Afrikaans for marsh, and "sossus" comes from Nama, meaning "no return". The name Deadvlei means "dead marsh"; "dead valley" is a mistranslation. The Sesriem gate is the main entrance to the park for visitors heading to Sossusvlei. Hot-air balloons set off from the gate early in the morning; three-quarters of the park's visitors are concentrated around Sesriem. From the gate to Sossusvlei is about 66 km: about 60 km of tarmac, then 6 km of sand that needs a four-wheel drive.

Dune 45 takes its name from its place at kilometre 45 of this road. It rises more than 170 metres, and is a popular stop for visitors. In 2023 Sossusvlei had more than 198,000 visitors. It is among the most photographed places in sub-Saharan Africa. The films The Cell and The Fall were partly shot in Deadvlei. Hiddenvlei, about 4 km from the parking area, is the least visited of the pans here.

Tourism brings pressures too: illegal and harmful off-road driving, sanitation and pollution. According to Namibia's environment ministry in 2025, drones may not be used in national parks without an official permit. Visitors carrying drones must also declare and register them at the park gates. The Topnaar have lived on this land for centuries; today it is also a destination for travellers from around the world. Names, roads and cameras have carried this sea of sand around the world.

自然 Nature

靠霧活下來的生命

Life That Lives on Fog

Fog as the main water source, endemic arachnids, a beetle that drinks fog, and oryx, springbok and ostriches

冷的本格拉洋流，讓這裡一年的雨量不到五十公釐，。也在海岸形成寬達五十公里的霧帶。在這片沙海裡，霧是最主要的水源；。動植物都演化出了收集霧水的方法。納米布沙漠甲蟲，。靠背上的小突起，在清晨收集霧裡的水。只在沙海裡才有的物種，包括八種植物，。占沙海植物的百分之五十三。蛛形類有三十七種是特有種，占了百分之八十四；。昆蟲有一百零八種，占百分之五十二。

爬蟲類有八種特有種，占百分之四十四；。還有一種鳥和兩種哺乳類。較大的動物有劍羚、跳羚和鴕鳥，。也有胡狼、囓齒類、爬蟲類和許多小節肢動物。有些無脊椎動物，。甚至能在沙裡「游泳」和「潛水」。查烏查布河是一條季節河，。只有在諾克盧福山區難得下大雨時才有水。河道兩旁，長著一條綠色的植物帶，。季節性的河水，也會把外來植物的種子帶進來。

外來的阿根廷蟻和菜粉蝶，。已經侵擾了百歲蘭和納拉瓜的群落。北方海岸的三明治港，是候鳥重要的中繼站；。2005年，最多記錄到四十萬隻水鳥。氣候變遷與上游的用水，。是這裡潛在的重大威脅。在幾乎不下雨的沙漠裡，。這麼多生命，靠的就是海上飄來的霧。霧、沙與偶爾流過的河水，。撐起了這片看似空曠的沙海。

The cold Benguela Current keeps rainfall here under 50 mm a year and creates a coastal fog belt up to 50 km wide. In the sand sea fog is the main source of water; plants and animals have adapted to harvest it. The Namib Desert beetle collects water from the morning fog with the bumps on its back. Species found only in the sand sea include eight plants, 53% of its plant species. Thirty-seven arachnids are endemic, 84% of those here; so are 108 insects, 52%.

Eight reptiles are endemic, 44% of the total, along with one bird and two mammals. Larger animals include oryx, springbok and ostriches, alongside jackals, rodents, reptiles and many small arthropods. Some invertebrates even "swim" and "dive" through the sand. The Tsauchab is a seasonal river, flowing only after rare heavy rain in the Naukluft Mountains. A green band of plants grows along its bed, and its seasonal floods also carry in the seeds of invasive alien plants.

Introduced Argentine ants and cabbage butterflies have infested colonies of welwitschia and !nara. Sandwich Harbour on the northern coast is a key stopover for migrating birds; up to 400,000 waterbirds were counted there in 2005. Climate change and water use upstream are rated high potential threats here. In a desert where it hardly rains, so much life depends on fog drifting in from the sea. Fog, sand and the occasional river hold up this sea of sand that looks so empty.

地理 Geography

沙丘的海

A Sea of Dunes

三百多萬公頃的沙海、十六種沙丘、越老越紅的沙，與三百多公尺高的「大老爹」
Over three million hectares of sand, sixteen dune types, sand that reddens with age and the 325 m Big Daddy

世界遺產的範圍超過三百萬公頃，。另有約九十萬公頃的緩衝區。沙丘地占了百分之八十四，。大約是納米布-諾克盧福國家公園的六成。這裡辨認出十六種不同的沙丘：。海岸是橫向沙丘，中間是線形沙丘，東邊是星形沙丘。納米布沙漠，沿著納米比亞海岸，。形成一條寬八十到一百二十公里的帶。45號沙丘的沙，大約有五百萬年；。是奧蘭治河從喀拉哈里帶出來，再被風吹到這裡的。

沙丘的橘紅色，來自沙粒上的鐵慢慢氧化；。沙丘越老，顏色就越紅。往西，查烏查布河朝沙海深處流去，。全長約一百五十公里。它在索蘇斯維結束，。這裡是一個沒有出口的內流盆地。附近最高的沙丘「大老爹」，。高約三百二十五公尺。死亡谷四周的沙丘，。被認為是世界上最高的沙丘之一。

從太空看，查烏查布河留下的鹽沉積，。在衛星影像裡是一片亮白。東邊的塞斯瑞姆峽谷，有些地方只有兩公尺寬；。峽谷裡有一段終年有水，動物會來這裡喝水。一千萬到五百萬年前，曾有一段較濕的時期，。在大斷崖東邊留下了河流沉積。北邊的奎塞布河，。是沙海的北界。風把沙子堆成一座座山，。這片沙的海，還在慢慢改變形狀。

The World Heritage Site covers over three million hectares, with a buffer zone of about 900,000 hectares. Dune fields make up 84% of it, and it covers about 60% of the Namib-Naukluft National Park. Sixteen dune types are recognised here: transverse dunes on the coast, linear dunes in the centre and star dunes in the east. The Namib runs along the coast of Namibia as a belt 80 to 120 km wide. The sand of Dune 45 is about 5 million years old, carried from the Kalahari by the Orange River and then blown here by the wind.

The orange of the dunes comes from iron slowly oxidising; the older the dune, the redder it gets. To the west the Tsauchab River runs into the heart of the sand sea, about 150 km long in all. It ends at Sossusvlei, a basin with no outflow. The tallest dune nearby, Big Daddy, is about 325 metres high. The dunes around Deadvlei are said to be among the highest in the world.

Seen from space, the salt deposits of the Tsauchab show up bright white in satellite images. Sesriem Canyon to the east is only two metres wide in places; one stretch holds water all year, and animals come to drink. Between 10 and 5 million years ago a wetter spell left river deposits east of the Great Escarpment. The Kuiseb River forms the northern edge of the sand sea. The wind piles the sand into mountains, and this sea of sand keeps slowly changing shape.
