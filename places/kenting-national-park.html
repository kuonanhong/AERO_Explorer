歷史 History

燈塔、墾荒者與第一座國家公園

A Lighthouse, Settlers and the First National Park

從十九世紀的船難到1984年
From the shipwrecks of the nineteenth century to 1984

鵝鑾鼻，是臺灣本島的最南端。岬角外有七星岩暗礁與強勁的海流，。十九世紀常有船隻在這裡失事。船難引發1867年的羅發號事件與1874年日本出兵，。美、日兩國要求清廷在此建燈塔。1875年買下土地，1881年動工，。1883年4月1日點燈。工程由海關聘請的英國工程師負責，。清廷派了五百名士兵保護工地。

燈塔四周有壕溝與槍眼，。是島上唯一的武裝燈塔。它在甲午戰爭與二次大戰中兩度受損，。1898年修復、1947年重建。1877年，清廷從廣東潮州一帶招募壯丁來此墾荒，。「墾丁」因此得名。1906年，日本人在這片高地。設立恆春熱帶植物園。1984年1月1日，墾丁國家公園成立，。是臺灣第一座國家公園。

公園涵蓋恆春、車城與滿州三個鄉鎮。1982年12月25日，。鵝鑾鼻公園對外開放。現在的燈塔高21.4公尺，。燈光可以照到27.2浬外。一座為了船難而蓋的燈塔，。後來成了國家公園的地標。

Cape Eluanbi is the southernmost point of Taiwan's main island. The Qixingyan reefs and strong currents lie off the cape; in the nineteenth century ships were often wrecked here. Wrecks led to the Rover incident of 1867 and a Japanese expedition in 1874; the United States and Japan pressed the Qing to build a lighthouse. The land was bought in 1875, work began in 1881, and the light first shone on 1 April 1883. A British engineer of the Maritime Customs ran the work, guarded by 500 Qing soldiers.

Ringed by a ditch and pierced with gunports, it was the only armed lighthouse on the island. It was badly damaged in the Sino-Japanese War and again in the Second World War, then repaired in 1898 and rebuilt in 1947. In 1877 the Qing recruited men from Chaozhou in Guangdong to clear this land; that is how Kenting got its name. In 1906 the Japanese founded the Hengchun Tropical Botanical Garden on this high ground. On 1 January 1984 Kenting National Park was established, Taiwan's first national park.

The park takes in Hengchun, Checheng and Manzhou townships. Eluanbi Park opened to the public on 25 December 1982. The present tower is 21.4 metres tall, and its light reaches 27.2 nautical miles. A lighthouse built because of shipwrecks became the landmark of a national park.

人文 Culture

東亞之光下的半島

A Peninsula under the Light of East Asia

墾丁在屏東縣恆春半島的南端，三面環海。「墾丁」這個地名的意思，。是來開墾的壯丁。往北是恆春，。半島就以它為名。恆春古城與瓊麻工業歷史展示區，。是公園介紹的人文景點。龍鑾潭西岸有一座自然中心，。展示臺灣的鳥類生態。

再往東南，回到半島尖端的鵝鑾鼻。鵝鑾鼻燈塔的光力是全臺最強，。被稱為「東亞之光」。第一任看守人泰勒在這裡待到1889年，。還學會了排灣語。船帆石、青蛙石與大尖山，。公園都規定不可以攀爬。船帆石立在海岸邊，。名字來自它像船帆的形狀。

每年三月的「春天吶喊」音樂祭，。從1995年辦到現在。電影《海角七號》與《少年Pi的奇幻漂流》，。都有部分在這裡拍攝。森林遊樂區旁的社頂社區，。二十年來是公園社區生態旅遊的先驅。2016年，有584萬人次來到這座國家公園。

Kenting lies at the southern end of the Hengchun Peninsula in Pingtung, with the sea on three sides. The name Kenting means the men who came to clear the land. To the north is Hengchun, the town that gives the peninsula its name. The old walled town of Hengchun and the Sisal Industry Historical Exhibition are among the park's cultural sites. A nature centre on the western shore of Longluan Lake presents Taiwan's bird life.

South-east again, to Eluanbi at the tip of the peninsula. Eluanbi's light is the most intense in Taiwan; it is known as the Light of East Asia. Its first keeper, George Taylor, served here until 1889 and learned the Paiwan language. Sail Rock, Frog Rock and Mount Dajian are all closed to climbing under park rules. Sail Rock stands at the edge of the shore, named for its sail-like shape.

The Spring Scream rock festival has been held here every March since 1995. The films Cape No. 7 and Life of Pi were both partly shot here. The Sheding community beside the forest recreation area has pioneered the park's community ecotourism for two decades. In 2016 the national park received 5.84 million visits.

自然 Nature

陸蟹、過境鷹與礁上的森林

Land Crabs, Passing Hawks and a Forest on the Reef

臺灣本島唯一的熱帶區域
The only tropical corner of Taiwan's main island

墾丁，是臺灣本島唯一的熱帶區域。公園總面積三萬三千多公頃，。陸地與海域大約各占一半。墾丁森林遊樂區是一片「高位珊瑚礁森林」，。樹木直接長在隆起的礁岩上。茄苳緊抓著礁岩，銀葉樹長出板根，。白榕垂下無數氣根。這裡常見臺灣獼猴與赤腹松鼠；。每年十月，灰面鵟鷹過境。

公園記錄到310種鳥。與216種蝴蝶。龍鑾潭面積175公頃、平均水深3.5公尺，。冬天是候鳥的棲地。潭在低窪處，雨季容易淹水，。東、北兩側因此築了堤。公園裡有26種陸蟹，。被稱為全世界單一地區陸蟹種類最多的地方。周圍的海裡，。有玳瑁、鬼蝠魟與鯨鯊。

梅花鹿1969年在野外絕跡，。1994年重新野放到這裡。公園的北部是山區，。南仁湖就在那裡。龍磐的草原、風吹砂的沙丘植物、。鵝鑾鼻的海濱植物，各有不同的植群。在這個熱帶的尖端，。海、礁、森林與候鳥擠在同一塊土地上。

Kenting is the only tropical region on Taiwan's main island. The park covers more than 33,000 hectares, roughly half land and half sea. The Kenting forest recreation area is an "uplifted coral reef forest": trees grow straight out of raised reef rock. Bishopwood trees grip the reef, looking-glass trees spread buttress roots, and white banyans drop countless aerial roots. Taiwan macaques and red-bellied tree squirrels are common here; grey-faced buzzards pass through each October.

The park has recorded 310 bird species and 216 kinds of butterfly. Longluan Lake covers 175 hectares and averages 3.5 metres deep; in winter it shelters migratory birds. The lake lies low and floods in the rainy season, so barriers were built on its east and north sides. The park has 26 species of land crab, said to be the highest diversity in any single area in the world. The surrounding sea is home to hawksbill turtles, manta rays and whale sharks.

The Formosan sika deer died out in the wild in 1969 and was reintroduced here in 1994. The north of the park is hill country, where Nanren Lake lies. The grassland of Longpan, the dune plants of Fengchuisha and the shore plants of Eluanbi each form a different community. On this tropical tip, sea, reef, forest and migrants share one piece of land.

地理 Geography

抬出海面的珊瑚礁

Coral Reef Lifted out of the Sea

兩個岬角、一片縱谷與五十萬年的隆起
Two capes, a valley plain and half a million years of uplift

恆春半島三面臨海：。東邊太平洋、西邊臺灣海峽、南邊巴士海峽。貓鼻頭是南灣的西界，。隔著海灣與東南方的鵝鑾鼻相對。兩個岬角之間的這片海，就是南灣。鵝鑾鼻燈塔的燈，。高出海面56.4公尺。往北是龍磐公園，。一片朝東面向太平洋的開闊草原。

草原上經常颳著強風；。西邊是臺地與珊瑚礁崖。大約五十萬年前，。板塊運動把海裡的珊瑚礁抬出水面。礁岩裡有鐘乳石與石筍，。例如銀龍洞與石筍寶穴。從上面看，森林蓋住了整塊隆起的礁岩。西海岸是珊瑚礁海崖與裙礁，。北部是山地，南部是珊瑚礁臺地。

中間是恆春縱谷平原，。龍鑾潭就在這片低地上。半島大部分地勢低於八百公尺，。海風很容易吹過整片土地。這裡是熱帶季風氣候；。冬天的落山風甚至會影響恆春機場的航班。珊瑚在海裡長成的石頭，。如今是臺灣最南端的陸地。

The Hengchun Peninsula meets the sea on three sides: the Pacific to the east, the Taiwan Strait to the west, the Bashi Channel to the south. Cape Maobitou forms the western edge of South Bay, facing Cape Eluanbi to the south-east. The water between the two capes is South Bay. The light of Eluanbi Lighthouse stands 56.4 metres above the sea. To the north is Longpan Park, an open grassland facing east over the Pacific.

Strong winds often sweep the grassland; to the west are terraces and coral reef cliffs. About 500,000 years ago, plate movement lifted the coral reef out of the sea. Inside the reef rock are stalactites and stalagmites, in caves such as Silver Dragon Cave and Stalagmite Cave. From above, forest covers the whole block of raised reef. The west coast has coral cliffs and fringing reefs; mountains lie to the north and coral tablelands to the south.

Between them runs the Hengchun Longitudinal Valley Plain, with Longluan Lake on its low ground. Most of the peninsula lies below 800 metres, so sea winds cross it easily. The climate is tropical monsoon; in winter, downslope winds even disrupt flights at Hengchun Airport. Rock that corals built under the sea is now the southernmost land of Taiwan's main island.
