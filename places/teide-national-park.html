歷史 History

大西洋上的火山燈塔

A Volcanic Beacon in the Atlantic

關契人的聖山、哥倫布看見的火、1706年被熔岩改變的港口，到2007年的世界遺產
The Guanches' sacred peak, the fire Columbus saw, a port changed by lava in 1706, World Heritage in 2007

泰德峰在大西洋上的特內里費島，。海拔三千七百一十五公尺，是西班牙的最高峰。島上原本的居民是關契人，。他們把泰德峰看作神聖的山。他們相信，惡靈瓜約塔住在山裡；。火山噴發時，他們點起篝火，想把他嚇走。關契人主要以放牧為生，。夏天會把羊群帶到高處的卡尼亞達斯。國家公園裡發現了許多關契人的考古遺跡，。證明這片高地曾經是他們的生活場所。

1492年，哥倫布航向美洲途中，。記下他看見「奧羅塔瓦山谷裡的大火」。後來的定年顯示，那時奧羅塔瓦並沒有噴發，。噴發的是另一處火山口。北岸的加拉奇科，在1496年西班牙征服之後建立，。曾是出口馬姆齊葡萄酒的重要港口。1706年5月5日，西北方的火山裂谷噴發，。熔岩流進舊港灣，斷了小鎮的生計。第一次有紀錄的登頂，是在1582年。1798年，老峰的西側噴發了三個月。

1909年11月18日，西北方的奇涅羅火山口噴發，。是特內里費島到今天為止的最後一次噴發。1954年1月22日，泰德國家公園成立，。面積後來擴大到約一百九十平方公里。1964年，泰德天文台在海拔兩千三百九十公尺啟用，。由加那利天文物理研究所管理。1989年，公園獲得歐洲理事會的歐洲文憑最高等級；。2007年6月28日，列入世界遺產。2016年，公園有四百零七萬多人次造訪，。是西班牙遊客最多的國家公園。

從關契人的聖山、航海者的地標，。到研究星空的高地，泰德峰一直站在大西洋上。

Mount Teide stands on Tenerife in the Atlantic, 3,715 metres high, the highest peak in Spain. The original people of the island were the Guanches, who held Teide to be sacred. They believed the evil being Guayota lived inside the mountain; during eruptions they lit bonfires to frighten him away. The Guanches were mainly herders who spent their summers up in Las Cañadas. Many Guanche archaeological remains have been found in the park, showing these uplands were part of their life.

In 1492, on his way to the Americas, Columbus recorded seeing "a great fire in the Orotava Valley". Later dating suggests there was no eruption in the Orotava Valley then; another vent was erupting. Garachico, on the north coast, was founded in 1496 after the conquest and became an important port exporting Malmsey wine. On 5 May 1706 the north-west rift zone erupted, and lava ran into the old bay, ending the town's livelihood. The first recorded ascent of Teide was in 1582. In 1798 the western flank of Pico Viejo erupted for three months.

On 18 November 1909 the Chinyero vent to the north-west erupted, the last eruption on Tenerife to date. Teide National Park was created on 22 January 1954 and later enlarged to about 190 square kilometres. In 1964 the Teide Observatory opened at 2,390 metres, run by the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias. In 1989 the park received the Council of Europe's European Diploma in its highest category; on 28 June 2007 it became a World Heritage Site. In 2016 the park had more than 4.07 million visits, the most of any national park in Spain.

From the Guanches' sacred peak and the sailors' landmark to a high place for studying the sky, Teide has always stood over the Atlantic.

在關契人的信仰裡，泰德峰是神聖的山，。惡靈瓜約塔被認為就住在火山裡。火山噴發時，人們點燃篝火，。希望把瓜約塔嚇回山裡。加西亞岩群裡的辛查多岩，高約二十七公尺，。也被叫做「石樹」或「上帝的手指」。它曾經印在西班牙一千元比塞塔紙鈔的背面，。背景就是泰德峰。從觀景台出發的環狀步道約三點五公里，。大約兩小時，遊客必須走在圍起來的步道上。

纜車從海拔兩千三百五十六公尺，。大約八分鐘就升到三千五百五十五公尺。最後兩百公尺到山頂，需要免費的許可證，。通常每天限兩百人。這片像外星球的地景，常被電影拿來當外景，。包括1966年與2010年的幾部電影。加拉奇科是由熱那亞的銀行家德蓬特建立的。2025年，小鎮約有四千九百四十位居民。常聽說1706年的熔岩毀了整座城鎮，。但實際上，小鎮只是部分被毀。

天文台這裡的大氣視寧度很好，。吸引了許多國家來這裡設置望遠鏡。德國的格雷戈爾太陽望遠鏡，口徑一點五公尺，。2012年5月開始運作。法國與義大利的忒彌斯太陽望遠鏡，在1996年建成；。德國的真空塔望遠鏡，在1989年裝設。依規定，在泰德國家公園飛無人機，必須有明確的許可；。休閒用途在國家公園裡是不允許的。傳說、紙鈔、電影與天文台，。都把這座火山，寫進了人們的生活裡。

In Guanche belief Teide was a sacred mountain, and the evil being Guayota was thought to live inside it. When the volcano erupted, people lit bonfires hoping to scare Guayota away. Roque Cinchado, in the Roques de García, is about 27 metres tall, and is also called the Stone Tree or the Finger of God. It once appeared on the back of Spain's 1,000-peseta banknote, with Teide behind it. A circular trail of about 3.5 km from the viewpoint takes about two hours, and visitors must keep to the fenced path.

The cable car climbs from 2,356 metres to 3,555 metres in about eight minutes. The last 200 metres to the summit need a free permit, normally limited to 200 people a day. This otherworldly landscape is a favourite film location, including One Million Years B.C. (1966) and Clash of the Titans (2010). Garachico was founded by the Genoese banker Cristóbal de Ponte. In 2025 it had about 4,940 residents. It is often said that the lava of 1706 destroyed the whole town, but in fact it was only partly destroyed.

The seeing here is very good, and it has drawn telescopes from many countries. The German GREGOR solar telescope, 1.5 metres across, has operated since May 2012. The French–Italian THEMIS solar telescope was built in 1996; the German Vacuum Tower Telescope was installed in 1989. By the rules, flying a drone in Teide National Park needs express permission; recreational use is not allowed in national parks. Legends, a banknote, films and an observatory have all written this volcano into people's lives.

公園裡有兩百多種維管束植物：。三十一種是加那利群島特有，三十三種只在特內里費島。其中有六種，。全世界只長在這座國家公園裡。泰德藍薊，可以長到三公尺高，。春天開出數百朵紅色的小花。泰德堇菜長在海拔兩千五百公尺以上的火山渣上，。一直分布到山頂附近。最常見的灌木，是泰德白金雀花。2002到2017年，它在公園裡的覆蓋率少了百分之十七點五。

在海拔約一千到兩千一百公尺的山腰，。是加那利松的森林。無脊椎動物估計約有一千四百種，。大約一半是群島特有。岩石間常見的加那利蜥蜴，。是公園三種特有的爬蟲類之一。特有的藍燕雀在松林裡築巢，。也常常飛到公園裡來。公園裡還有五種蝙蝠。綿羊和山羊，在原住民時代就被帶到島上。

兔子和老鼠，在十五世紀末來到島上；。為了打獵引進的兔子，至今仍需控制。外來的歐洲盤羊，也在二十世紀後期被引進；。2021年春天的普查，只數到十二隻。2021年的一場火災，。燒到了稀有的瓜哈拉堇菜族群附近。每年約四百三十萬名遊客中，。大約七成開車、兩成八搭遊覽車進來。在看似荒涼的火山地上，。開著世界上其他地方沒有的花。

The park has over 200 vascular plant species: 31 are endemic to the Canaries and 33 to Tenerife. Six of them grow nowhere in the world but this national park. Teide bugloss can grow 3 metres tall, covered in spring with hundreds of small red flowers. The Teide violet grows on scoria and pumice above 2,500 metres, right up towards the summit. The dominant shrub is the Teide white broom. From 2002 to 2017 it lost 17.5% of its cover in the park.

On the middle slopes, from about 1,000 to 2,100 metres, grow forests of Canary pine. There are an estimated 1,400 invertebrate species, about half of them endemic to the islands. The Canary lizard, often seen among the rocks, is one of the park's three endemic reptiles. The blue chaffinch nests in the pine belt and regularly visits the park. The park also has five species of bat. Sheep and goats arrived in aboriginal times.

Rabbits and rats arrived in the late 15th century; the rabbits, brought in for hunting, still have to be controlled. The Corsican mouflon was introduced in the late 20th century; the spring 2021 census counted only 12. A fire in 2021 came close to a population of the rare Guajara violet. Of some 4.3 million visitors a year, about 70% arrive by car and 28% by tour bus. On ground that looks barren, flowers bloom that grow nowhere else on Earth.

地理 Geography

從海底算起七千五百公尺

7,500 Metres from the Sea Floor

Teide grown after a collapse, the 17 km Las Cañadas caldera, the crater of Pico Viejo and the last eruptions

從海底算起，泰德峰高約七千五百公尺，。是地球上第三高的火山結構。它大約在十七萬年前，。一場災難性的山崩之後開始成形。山頂高約三千七百一十八公尺，。頂上的火山口直徑大約八十公尺。山下是卡尼亞達斯火山口，寬約十七公里，。四周的岩壁最高約七百公尺。加西亞岩群，是島上舊山頂殘留下來的岩石，。被侵蝕分割成一座座岩塔。

辛查多岩的上部，有兩道熔岩岩床侵入，。讓它比下面更耐侵蝕。岩群旁的烏坎卡平原，。是國家公園裡最大的谷地。往西北，是老峰，海拔約三千一百三十公尺，。是加那利群島第二高的火山。老峰的火山口直徑大約七百二十公尺。1798年西側噴發了三個月，流出約一千兩百萬立方公尺熔岩。那次噴發留下的黑色熔岩，。被叫做「泰德的鼻孔」。

1706年，西北裂谷上的火山噴發，。熔岩流進了加拉奇科的港灣。1909年，西北裂谷上的奇涅羅噴發，。是特內里費島最近一次的噴發。東北方的多爾薩爾山脊，是一條火山裂谷；。1704到1705年，這條裂谷上曾接連噴發。不過，這個「第三高」的排名，。是把夏威夷的火山當成一個整體來算的。從海底升起的這座火山，。還在慢慢書寫特內里費島的地形。

Measured from the ocean floor, Teide rises about 7,500 metres, the third-tallest volcanic structure on Earth. It began to form about 170,000 years ago, after a catastrophic landslide. The summit is about 3,718 metres high, with a crater about 80 metres across. Below lies the Las Cañadas caldera, about 17 km across, its walls up to 700 metres high. The Roques de García are remnants of the island's former summit, split by erosion into towers of rock.

Two lava sills intruded into the upper part of Roque Cinchado, making it more resistant to erosion than the rock below. The Llano de Ucanca beside the rocks is the largest valley in the national park. To the north-west rises Pico Viejo, about 3,130 metres, the second-highest volcano in the Canary Islands. The crater of Pico Viejo is about 720 metres across. In 1798 its western flank erupted for three months, pouring out some 12 million m³ of lava. The black lava of that eruption is known as the Narices del Teide, "the nostrils of Teide".

In 1706 a vent on the north-west rift erupted, and lava poured into Garachico's harbour. In 1909 El Chinyero erupted on the north-west rift, the most recent eruption on Tenerife. The Cordillera Dorsal ridge to the north-east is a volcanic rift; it erupted several times in 1704 and 1705. That "third-tallest" ranking, though, counts the volcanoes of Hawaii as a single structure. This volcano, risen from the sea floor, is still slowly shaping the land of Tenerife.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

依當地規定，在泰德國家公園飛無人機，只有在取得明確許可時才允許，許可主要只核發給有正當理由的專業拍攝；國家公園、野生動物保護區與生物圈保護區內，不允許休閒用途。2016年曾有人因未經許可以無人機拍攝泰德峰，被罰款600歐元。實際飛行前請先確認最新規定。

Under local rules, drones may only be flown in Teide National Park with express permission, granted mainly for justified professional filming; recreational use is not allowed in national parks, wildlife conservation zones or biosphere reserves. In 2016 a man was fined €600 for filming Teide with a drone without permission. Check the current rules before any real flight.
