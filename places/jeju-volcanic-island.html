歷史 History

從耽羅到世界遺產

From Tamna to World Heritage

三姓穴的傳說、1105年併入高麗、1653年的哈梅爾，到2007年的世界遺產
The Samseonghyeol legend, annexation in 1105, Hamel in 1653, and World Heritage in 2007

濟州島位在朝鮮半島南方約一百三十公里的海上。這座島，從前是一個叫「耽羅」的王國。傳說耽羅的三位始祖高、梁、夫，從地上的三個洞穴走出來。這三個洞穴叫「三姓穴」，至今保存在濟州市中心。根據《三國史記》，耽羅在476年成為百濟的藩屬。百濟衰落後，它轉向新羅稱臣。938年，高麗征服了耽羅，1105年正式併入。島上的自治一直保留到1404年的朝鮮時代。1274年，三別抄的抗爭在濟州島結束。隨後的1275到1301年，這裡設為元朝的耽羅總管府。

城邑村在1423年、世宗在位時成為縣的官署所在地，。一直到1914年，都是行政中心。這座有城牆的村子，已經有人住了大約六百年。城牆保存完好，村裡還留著約一百一十棟建築。1653年8月17日，荷蘭商船「雀鷹號」在島的西南外海觸礁。包括哈梅爾在內，三十六人生還。他們在朝鮮生活了十三年，1666年才逃到日本出島。哈梅爾的遊記1668年出版，是西方人對朝鮮的第一份親身記錄。1966年，漢拏山海拔八百公尺以上劃為自然保護區，。列為天然紀念物第182號。

1970年3月24日，漢拏山國家公園成立，。是韓國第九座國家公園，面積153平方公里。拒文岳在2005年指定為天然紀念物第444號。2007年，它和漢拏山、城山日出峰一起列入世界遺產。2002年起，濟州是聯合國教科文組織的生物圈保護區，。2010年又成為世界地質公園。2020年起，登漢拏山要事先預約。城板岳與觀音寺兩條步道，每天各限1,500人。從耽羅的傳說、高麗與朝鮮，到國家公園與世界遺產，。漢拏山一直站在這段歷史的中央。

Jeju lies some 130 km off the southern coast of the Korean Peninsula. The island was once a kingdom called Tamna. Legend says Tamna's three founders, Go, Yang and Bu, emerged from three holes in the ground. Those holes, the Samseonghyeol, are still kept in the centre of Jeju City. According to the Samguk Sagi, Tamna became a tributary of Baekje in 476. As Baekje declined, it turned to Silla instead. Goryeo subjugated Tamna in 938 and formally annexed it in 1105. The island kept local autonomy until 1404, under Joseon. In 1274 the Sambyeolcho rebellion came to its end on Jeju. From 1275 to 1301 the island was administered by the Yuan as Tamna Prefecture.

Seongeup became a county seat in 1423, under King Sejong, and remained an administrative centre until 1914. People have lived in this walled village for about 600 years. Its walls are well preserved, and about 110 structures remain. On 17 August 1653 the Dutch ship De Sperwer was wrecked off the island's south-west. Hendrick Hamel was among the 36 survivors. They spent thirteen years in Korea before escaping to Dejima in Japan in 1666. Hamel's journal, published in 1668, was the first Western first-hand account of Korea. In 1966 the land above 800 metres on Hallasan became a nature reserve, listed as Natural Monument No. 182.

Hallasan National Park was founded on 24 March 1970, Korea's ninth national park, covering 153 square kilometres. Geomunoreum became Natural Monument No. 444 in 2005. In 2007 it joined Hallasan and Seongsan Ilchulbong on the World Heritage List. Since 2002 Jeju has been a UNESCO biosphere reserve, and in 2010 it became a UNESCO Global Geopark. Since 2020 climbers on Hallasan have needed to book in advance. The Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa trails are each limited to 1,500 people a day. From the legend of Tamna through Goryeo and Joseon to a national park and World Heritage, Hallasan has stood at the centre of this history.

人文 Culture

海女、石爺爺與黑石牆

Haenyeo, Stone Grandfathers and Black Walls

不帶氧氣筒下海的女性、守護城門的石像，以及兩萬多公里的田間石牆
Women who dive without air tanks, stone guardians at the gates, and twenty thousand km of field walls

濟州的海邊，有一群叫「海女」的女性潛水員。她們不帶氧氣設備下海，有些人已經八十多歲。她們潛到約十公尺深，每次憋氣大約一分鐘。一年約下海九十天，一天最長工作七小時。她們採的主要是鮑魚、海膽等貝類。海女依經驗分成下軍、中軍、上軍，由上軍帶領。下水前，她們會祈求平安與豐收。2016年，濟州海女文化列入人類非物質文化遺產。島上常見的石像，濟州話叫「石爺爺」。1971年，這個稱呼成為官方名稱。

石爺爺用火山岩雕成，從前成對立在城門前，。標示聚落的邊界，也守護城門。現存的古老石爺爺約有四十七、八尊。濟州市有二十一尊，城邑村有十二尊。田地四周的黑色石牆叫「田墻」，。用島上的玄武岩乾砌而成，不用任何灰漿。石牆故意留下縫隙，讓颱風的強風穿過去，。牆不會倒，還能當防風牆。有人估計，全島的田墻加起來約有兩萬兩千公里。蜿蜒的模樣，被稱為「萬里黑龍」。

每年元旦前後，城山日出峰舉行日出節。太陽升起時，漁民會點亮漁船、鳴笛迎接。往漢拏山的城板岳步道長9.6公里，。單程約四個半小時，是最長的一條。濟州市是濟州道的首府，人口將近五十萬。濟州國際機場，是島上唯一的機場。海女的呼吸聲、石爺爺的守望、黑石牆的線條，。是濟州人和火山島相處的方式。

Along the Jeju coast work women divers known as haenyeo. They dive without breathing equipment, and some are in their eighties. They dive to about 10 metres, holding their breath for around a minute each time. They work about 90 days a year, up to seven hours a day. They gather mainly shellfish such as abalone and sea urchins. Divers are ranked hagun, junggun and sanggun; the most experienced, the sanggun, lead. Before diving they pray for safety and a good catch. In 2016 the culture of Jeju haenyeo joined the Intangible Cultural Heritage list. The stone statues seen all over the island are called dol hareubang, "stone grandfather". In 1971 this became their official name.

Carved from volcanic stone, they once stood in pairs before fortress gates, marking the edge of a settlement and guarding its entrance. Some 47 or 48 old statues survive. Jeju City has 21 of them and Seongeup 12. The black walls around the fields are called batdam, stacked from the island's basalt without any mortar. Gaps are left in the walls on purpose so typhoon winds can pass through; the walls stay up and act as windbreaks. One estimate puts the field walls of the island at about 22,100 km. Their winding lines have earned the name "ten-thousand-li black dragon".

Around New Year's Day, Seongsan Ilchulbong holds its Sunrise Festival. As the sun comes up, fishermen light their boats and sound their whistles. The Seongpanak trail up Hallasan is 9.6 km long, about four and a half hours one way, the longest of them. Jeju City is the provincial capital, home to nearly half a million people. Jeju International Airport is the only airport on the island. The breath of the divers, the watch of the stone grandfathers, the lines of black walls: these are how the people of Jeju live with a volcanic island.

Three Climates on One Mountain

Over 1,500 plant species, Korean fir forest, life in the lava tubes, and dolphins at sea

濟州島記錄到1,565種維管束植物，。其中三十三種只生長在這座島上。從海邊到山頂，植被分成三個垂直帶：。亞熱帶、溫帶與寒帶。漢拏山上有一千八百多種植物，。大約是韓國全部植物種類的一半。靠近山頂的坡地，長著韓國冷杉林，。還有北極與高山型的植物。山頂的火山口裡，有一座叫白鹿潭的火口湖。湖的四周就是寒帶的植物。

拒文岳長滿濃密的松樹與杉樹林，。名字可能和林下陰暗的樣子有關。熔岩洞的頂部與地面，覆蓋著多彩的碳酸鹽沉積，。像鐘乳石與石筍。洞穴裡還住著稀有的洞穴蜘蛛。龍泉洞的地下湖裡，有看不見的盲魚。島上記錄到198種鳥類。狍子和豹貓，是這裡受到保護的物種。外海住著印太瓶鼻海豚，。還有江豚的穩定族群。

遊客越來越多，造成踐踏與土壤侵蝕。登山預約與人數上限，就是為了減輕這些壓力。濟州是唯一同時擁有世界遺產、生物圈保護區、。世界地質公園與拉姆薩濕地四種國際身分的地方。從海邊的亞熱帶到山頂的寒帶，。一座火山島，裝著好幾種氣候的生命。

1,565 species of vascular plants have been recorded on Jeju, 33 of them found only on this island. From shore to summit the vegetation falls into three vertical belts: subtropical, temperate and frigid. Hallasan has more than 1,800 plant species, about half of all Korea's species. Near the summit grows a forest of Korean fir, along with arctic-alpine plants. In the summit crater lies a crater lake called Baengnokdam. Around it grow the plants of the frigid belt.

Geomunoreum is covered in dense pine and cedar forest; its name may come from the darkness under the trees. The roofs and floors of the lava tubes carry multicoloured carbonate deposits, stalactites and stalagmites among them. Rare cave spiders live in these caves. The underground lake of Yongcheon Cave holds blind fish. 198 bird species have been recorded on the island. Siberian roe deer and leopard cats are among its endangered species. Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins live off the coast, along with resident finless porpoises.

Rising visitor numbers bring trampling and erosion. Trail bookings and daily caps are meant to ease that pressure. Jeju is the only place holding four international designations at once: World Heritage, biosphere reserve, Global Geopark and Ramsar site. From subtropical shores to a frigid summit, one volcanic island holds the life of several climates.

地理 Geography

火山造的島

An Island Built by Volcanoes

盾狀火山漢拏山、三百六十座寄生火山、熔岩洞與海中的凝灰岩丘
The shield volcano Hallasan, 360 satellite cones, lava tubes and a tuff cone from the sea

濟州島面積1,846平方公里，是韓國最大的島。整座島，都是火山噴發造出來的。島中央的漢拏山，海拔1,947公尺，是南韓最高峰。它是一座盾狀火山，坡度平緩。盾狀火山由裂隙噴發的熔岩，一層層堆疊而成。山頂的火山口裡，積成了白鹿潭。山坡上散布著約三百六十座寄生火山，。濟州話叫「岳」，也就是小火山丘。大約一萬年前，海平面還很低，。濟州島最後一次和朝鮮半島相連。

拒文岳海拔456.6公尺，約在二、三十萬年前形成。它的火口呈馬蹄形，向東北打開。火口裡的溝寬八十到一百五十公尺，。是地下熔岩管塌陷後留下的。熔岩從這裡往北北東流了約十三公里，一直流到海邊，。沿途留下一連串的熔岩洞。萬丈窟長約8.9公里，。洞裡7.6公尺高的熔岩柱，是已知最大的一根。東端的城山日出峰，高182公尺，是一座凝灰岩丘。它是岩漿在淺海碰到海水，猛烈噴發而成。

它的岩壁幾乎垂直，從海裡直直升起，。是研究這類「蘇特西式」噴發的絕佳露頭。它原本是一座離島，。後來泥沙堆積，才和濟州本島連在一起。盾狀火山、寄生火山、熔岩洞與海中的凝灰岩丘：。濟州島，是一座把火山的各種樣子都攤開來的島。

Jeju covers 1,846 square kilometres, the largest island in South Korea. The whole island was built by volcanic eruptions. Hallasan at its centre, 1,947 metres high, is the highest peak in South Korea. It is a shield volcano with gentle slopes. A shield volcano grows from lava poured out of fissures, layer upon layer. Its summit crater holds the lake of Baengnokdam. Some 360 satellite volcanoes are scattered over its slopes, called oreum in the Jeju language. About 10,000 years ago, when the sea stood lower, Jeju was last joined to the Korean Peninsula.

Geomunoreum, 456.6 metres high, formed some 200,000 to 300,000 years ago. Its crater is horseshoe-shaped, open to the north-east. The trench in the crater, 80 to 150 metres wide, formed where an underground lava tube collapsed. Lava ran from here about 13 km north-north-east to the sea, leaving a chain of lava tubes along the way. Manjanggul is about 8.9 km long; its 7.6-metre lava column is the largest known. Seongsan Ilchulbong at the eastern tip, 182 metres high, is a tuff cone. It formed when magma met seawater in a violent shallow-sea eruption.

Its walls rise almost vertically from the sea, an outstanding exposure for studying Surtseyan eruptions. It was once a separate island; later, sediment piled up and joined it to Jeju. Shield volcano, satellite cones, lava tubes and a tuff cone from the sea: Jeju lays out the many forms a volcano can take.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

依韓國的規定，飛行高度150公尺以上須事先申請飛行核准，夜間禁止飛行，並應避免在人群上空飛行；申請透過「드론원스톱」民願服務（drone.onestop.go.kr）辦理。濟州島有機場，部分區域另有限制。實際飛行前請先確認最新規定。

Under Korean rules, flights at 150 m or higher need prior approval, night flights are prohibited and flying over crowds should be avoided; approvals are applied for through the Drone One-Stop service (drone.onestop.go.kr). Jeju has an airport, and some areas carry further restrictions. Check the current rules before any real flight.
