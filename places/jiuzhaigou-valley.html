歷史 History

九個村寨的山谷

The Valley of Nine Villages

藏族與羌族的家園、1979年禁伐、1992年世界遺產，到2017年的地震與重生
Home of Tibetan and Qiang peoples, a logging ban in 1979, World Heritage in 1992, and the 2017 earthquake

九寨溝位在四川北部、岷山山脈的南端，。在成都北方約三百三十公里。「九寨溝」的意思，是「九個村寨的山溝」。它的藏語名字叫 Dzitsa Degu。好幾百年來，藏族與羌族的人們住在這片偏遠的山區。這裡有人居住的痕跡，可以追溯到大約三千年前。九寨溝屬於阿壩藏族羌族自治州，。在行政上屬於九寨溝縣。溝裡有九個藏族村寨，荷葉、樹正、則查窪都是其中之一。現在，有兩個寨子已經沒有人住。

直到1970年代，外界對這片山谷所知甚少。山谷裡的伐木，一直持續到1979年才被禁止。1982年，九寨溝成為國家公園；。1984年設立管理局，開放觀光。1984年，來訪的遊客大約只有五千人。1992年，九寨溝列入世界遺產。1997年，它又加入聯合國的人與生物圈保護區網。1999年起，私人車輛禁止入園，改由低污染的巴士接駁。2003年，九寨黃龍機場啟用，。機場蓋在海拔三千四百四十八公尺的山坡上。

遊客從1991年的約十七萬人，。增加到2015年的大約五百萬人。2001年起，每天的遊客上限是一萬兩千人；。2013年起，提高到四萬一千人。2017年8月8日，規模七點零的地震襲擊這一帶，。震中在九寨溝縣的漳扎鎮。諾日朗瀑布嚴重受損，景區關閉了兩年。地震之後，約一萬八千名居民失去了工作。2018年3月起，景區分階段重新開放；。2021年9月，九寨溝全面重新開放。

從九個村寨、禁伐與世界遺產，到地震後的重生，。這座山谷的故事還在繼續。

Jiuzhaigou lies at the southern end of the Minshan range in northern Sichuan, about 330 km north of Chengdu. The name Jiuzhaigou means "Nine Village Valley". Its Tibetan name is Dzitsa Degu. For centuries Tibetan and Qiang peoples lived in this remote region. Traces of people living here go back about 3,000 years. Jiuzhaigou belongs to the Aba Tibetan and Qiang Autonomous Prefecture, within Jiuzhaigou County. Its nine Tibetan villages include Heye, Shuzheng and Zechawa. Two of them are no longer inhabited.

Until the 1970s the valley was little known to the outside world. Logging went on here until it was banned in 1979. In 1982 Jiuzhaigou became a national park; an administration bureau was set up and it opened to tourism in 1984. In 1984 only about 5,000 visitors came. In 1992 Jiuzhaigou was inscribed as a World Heritage Site. In 1997 it also joined UNESCO's Man and the Biosphere network. In 1999 private vehicles were banned and replaced by low-polluting buses. In 2003 flights began to Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport, built on a mountainside 3,448 metres up.

Visitor numbers grew from about 170,000 in 1991 to around five million in 2015. From 2001 visitors were capped at 12,000 a day; from 2013 the cap was raised to 41,000. On 8 August 2017 a magnitude 7.0 earthquake struck the area, with its epicentre at Zhangzha town in Jiuzhaigou County. Nuorilang Waterfall was badly damaged and the park closed for two years. After the earthquake some 18,000 residents were left without work. The park began reopening in stages from March 2018, and reopened in full in September 2021.

From nine villages and a logging ban to World Heritage and recovery after the earthquake, the valley's story goes on.

人文 Culture

經幡與村寨

Prayer Flags and Villages

藏族村寨的生活、五色經幡的意義、苯教的傳統，與保護山谷的規定
Tibetan village life, five-coloured prayer flags, the Bon tradition and rules that protect the valley

九寨溝裡住著一千多位居民，。分屬一百一十多戶人家。溝裡的村寨都是藏族村寨，。許多傳統的習俗與生活方式，至今仍保留著。荷葉、樹正、則查窪三個寨子，。是遊客最容易走到的村寨。村寨與河邊，常可以看到一串串的經幡。藏語叫「隆達」，是印著經文的布片或紙片。紙做的隆達，會被拋向空中；。布做的，就在風裡飄動，或掛在河邊。

另一種長長的宗教旗幡叫「果達」，。從幾公尺到幾十公尺長都有。人們相信，風與水會把旗上的祈禱，。帶到更遠的地方。經幡有藍、白、紅、綠、黃五種顏色，。代表天空、白雲、生命、草木與土地。這一帶最早的宗教，是佛教傳入以前的苯教，。公元前二世紀就傳到了阿壩一帶。苯教在六世紀成為這裡的主要信仰；。七世紀，藏傳佛教也傳了進來。

傳統的食物和飲料，常用青稞做成，。也就是高原上的大麥。樹正一帶的河上，還有架在水上的藏式磨坊，。木板步道沿著河流與石灰華穿過樹林。今天的樹正寨，有餐廳、咖啡館和小店，。是遊客在溝裡休息的地方。為了保護生態，風景區禁止任何外來動植物進入，。連寵物也不能帶進來。五色經幡與九個村寨，。讓九寨溝不只有湖泊與瀑布，也是人們的家。

Just over 1,000 people live inside Jiuzhaigou, in more than 110 households. The villages in the valley are Tibetan, and many traditional customs and ways of life are still kept. Heye, Shuzheng and Zechawa are the villages that visitors can reach most easily. Strings of prayer flags hang in the villages and by the rivers. Called longda, they are pieces of cloth or paper printed with scriptures. Paper longda are thrown into the air; cloth ones flutter in the wind or hang beside the rivers.

Another kind, the guoda, are long religious banners, from a few metres to dozens of metres long. People believe the wind and the water carry the prayers on them out into the world. The flags come in five colours, blue, white, red, green and yellow, standing for sky, clouds, life, plants and soil. The earliest religion here is Bon, which is older than Buddhism. It reached the Aba region in the 2nd century BC. Bon became the dominant faith here in the 6th century, and Tibetan Buddhism arrived in the 7th.

Traditional food and drink are often made from barley, the highland barley of the plateau. Along the river near Shuzheng stand Tibetan millhouses built over the water, and boardwalks follow the stream past travertine through the woods. Today Shuzheng village has restaurants, cafes and shops, and is a resting place for visitors in the valley. To protect its ecology, no outside plants or animals may enter the scenic area, and that includes pets. The five-coloured flags and the nine villages make Jiuzhaigou not only lakes and waterfalls, but also a home.

自然 Nature

大熊貓與五彩的水

Pandas and Coloured Water

約兩千五百種植物、金絲猴與扭角羚、湖底的倒木，與不結冰的湖
About 2,500 plant species, golden monkeys and takin, fallen trees on lake beds, and lakes that never freeze

九寨溝有大約兩千五百種植物，。其中兩百多種是水生植物。森林覆蓋了景區一半以上的面積。這裡住著大熊貓、川金絲猴和四川扭角羚。這三種，都是瀕危的動物。不過景區裡的大熊貓，已經不到二十隻。箭竹海旁邊的熊貓海，相傳熊貓會來喝水，。但已經很多年沒有人見過了。熊貓海的水流下去，成了熊貓瀑布，。分三段落下，總共七十八公尺。

這裡受到保護的，有三百一十三種脊椎動物，。和七十八種哺乳類。小熊貓、林麝、岩羊、斑羚與金錢豹，。也都在這裡的保護動物名單上。天鵝海是一片半沼澤的湖，。長約兩千兩百五十公尺，寬約一百二十五公尺。五花海海拔兩千四百七十二公尺，水深只有約五公尺。湖底躺著許多古老的倒木。湖水溶了大量的石灰質，。所以呈現深淺不同的藍綠色。

石灰質沉澱在倒木與水草之間，。留下白色的沉積。這裡年均溫約七點八度；。一月約零下三點七度，七月約十六點八度。一年約有七百六十一公釐的雨，。八成落在五月到十月。寒冬時，這裡大部分的湖不會結凍，。只有長海會結上幾十公分厚的冰。到了秋天，蘆葦海的蘆葦轉成金黃，。一條碧綠的溪流從中間穿過，叫做「玉帶」。

從大熊貓到湖底的倒木，。九寨溝的生命，都和這些水連在一起。

Jiuzhaigou has about 2,500 plant species, more than 200 of them aquatic. Forest covers more than half of the scenic area. Giant pandas, Sichuan golden monkeys and Sichuan takin live here. All three are endangered. But fewer than 20 giant pandas remain in the park. At Panda Lake, near Arrow Bamboo Lake, pandas were said to come to drink, but none has been seen there for many years. Panda Lake spills over the Panda Waterfalls, which drop 78 metres in three steps.

Protected here are 313 vertebrate species and 78 mammals. Red pandas, forest musk deer, blue sheep, gorals and leopards are also on the list of protected animals here. Swan Lake is a semi-marsh lake, about 2,250 metres long and 125 metres wide. Five Flower Lake lies at 2,472 metres and is only about 5 metres deep. Ancient fallen tree trunks lie on its bed. The water carries a great deal of dissolved limestone, which gives it its many shades of turquoise.

Limestone settles between the trunks and the water plants, leaving white deposits. The mean annual temperature is about 7.8 °C: about −3.7 °C in January and 16.8 °C in July. About 761 mm of rain falls each year, 80% of it between May and October. In deep winter most of the lakes here do not freeze, but Long Lake ices over, tens of centimetres thick. In autumn the reeds of Reed Lake turn golden, with a turquoise brook, the "Jade Ribbon", running through them.

From giant pandas to the trunks on the lake beds, life in Jiuzhaigou is bound up with its water.

地理 Geography

Y字形的三條山谷

Three Valleys in a Y

樹正、日則、則查窪三條溝，約一百個湖、石灰華的堤壩，與板塊之間的斷層
The Shuzheng, Rize and Zechawa valleys, about a hundred lakes, travertine dams and faults between plates

九寨溝的三條山谷，排成一個「Y」字。諾日朗，就在三條溝交會的地方。從北邊的溝口往上到諾日朗，是樹正溝，。長約十四點五公里。樹正溝有十九個湖，諾日朗一帶又有十八個，。一個接著一個，順著山谷排列。臥龍海深約二十公尺，。湖底有一道石灰質的堤，看起來像一條臥龍。諾日朗瀑布海拔兩千三百六十五公尺，。高二十四點五公尺，寬三百二十公尺。

它是中國最寬的瀑布；。名字的意思是「雄偉壯觀」。往西南是日則溝，長約十八公里，。景點最多，一路通到原始森林。珍珠灘是一片平緩的斜坡，薄薄的水流過，。水裡的石灰華正在慢慢沉積。珍珠灘瀑布寬三百一十公尺，。海拔兩千四百三十三公尺。日則溝的盡頭是原始森林，。林邊有一面高約五百公尺的岩壁，叫做劍岩。

往東南的則查窪溝，也長約十八公里，。一路爬升到海拔三千一百多公尺的長海。長海長約七點五公里，最深約一百零三公尺。它沒有出水口，水來自融雪與雨水。湖水從地下流走，。補給下游的五彩池等湖泊。這裡位在青藏板塊與揚子板塊之間的大斷層上，。地震也參與塑造了這片地形。湖泊源自冰河作用，再被崩落的岩石堵住；。碳酸鈣沉積下來，把這些天然堤壩固定住。

核心區約七百二十平方公里，。海拔從約兩千公尺，一直到四千七百六十四公尺。

Jiuzhaigou's three valleys form a Y. Nuorilang sits where they meet. From the entrance in the north up to Nuorilang runs the Shuzheng Valley, about 14.5 km long. The Shuzheng Valley holds 19 lakes, and the Nuorilang group 18 more, strung one after another down the valley. Sleeping Dragon Lake is about 20 metres deep; a calcareous dyke on its bed looks like a dragon lying down. Nuorilang Waterfall is at 2,365 metres, 24.5 metres high and 320 metres wide.

It is the widest waterfall in China; its name means "majestic and sublime". To the south-west runs the Rize Valley, about 18 km long, with the most sights, all the way up to the Primeval Forest. Pearl Shoal is a gentle slope under a thin sheet of water, where tufa is still being laid down. Pearl Shoal Waterfall is 310 metres wide, at an elevation of 2,433 metres. The Rize Valley ends in the Primeval Forest, beside which rises Sword Rock, a cliff about 500 metres high.

The Zechawa Valley to the south-east, also about 18 km long, climbs to Long Lake at over 3,100 metres. Long Lake is about 7.5 km long and up to 103 metres deep. It has no outlet; its water comes from snowmelt and rain. Its water drains away underground, feeding lakes downstream such as the Five-Colour Pond. The park sits on major faults between the Qinghai-Tibet and Yangtze plates, and earthquakes have helped shape the land. The lakes began with glaciers; rockfalls then dammed them, and carbonate deposits later cemented the dams in place.

The core area covers about 720 square kilometres, rising from about 2,000 metres to 4,764 metres.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

依中國《無人駕駛航空器飛行管理暫行條例》（2024年1月1日施行），真高120公尺以上的空域屬於管制空域，未經空中交通管理機構批准不得飛行，一般須在飛行前一日12時前提出申請；民用無人機須實名登記。鄰近的黃龍景區已公告全域禁止擅自飛行無人機。實際飛行前，請先向九寨溝景區管理單位確認，並依規定申請。

Under China's Interim Regulations on the Flight Management of Unmanned Aircraft (in force since 1 January 2024), airspace more than 120 m above ground is controlled: flying there needs approval from air traffic management, normally applied for by 12:00 the day before, and civil drones must be registered under the owner's real name. The nearby Huanglong scenic area bans any unauthorised drone flight. Before any real flight, check with the Jiuzhaigou scenic area administration and apply as required.
