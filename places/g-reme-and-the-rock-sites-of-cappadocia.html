歷史 History

岩石裡的修道院

Monasteries in the Rock

Fourth-century hermits, cities hidden underground, 11th-century rock churches and World Heritage in 1985

這裡是土耳其中部的卡帕多奇亞，。格雷梅一帶的山谷，藏著上百座鑿在岩石裡的教堂。四世紀起，隱修者在這裡鑿洞而居；。他們遵循凱撒利亞主教、大巴西略的教導。大巴西略生於329年；。凱撒利亞，就是今天的開塞利。七到十三世紀，。格雷梅是重要的基督教中心。蘋果教堂大約建於1050年，。黑暗教堂建於十一世紀末到十二世紀初。

黑暗教堂一直到1950年代還被當成鴿舍，。後來花了十四年才修復完成。往南約三十公里，是代林庫尤地下城，。它最早可能由弗里吉亞人在西元前八到七世紀開鑿。拜占庭時代，為了躲避阿拉伯人的侵襲，。地下城被擴建完成，最深約八十五公尺。它最多可容納兩萬人和牲畜，。每一層，都能用滾動的大石門從裡面封住。1923年，希臘與土耳其交換人口，。卡帕多奇亞的希臘人離開，地下城也就荒廢了。

1963年，一位居民整修房子時，。在牆後發現了一個密室，地下城才重見天日。1969年，代林庫尤開放參觀；。它和凱馬克勒地下城之間，有八到九公里的地道。烏奇沙的岩石城堡，。最早出現在十四世紀的一部編年史裡。1985年，「格雷梅國家公園與卡帕多奇亞岩石遺址」同時以文化與自然價值，列入世界遺產。從隱修者的洞穴到地下的城市，。這片軟岩，收藏了將近兩千年的人類歷史。

This is Cappadocia in central Türkiye; the valleys around Göreme hide many churches cut into the rock. From the 4th century hermits carved homes here, following the teachings of Basil the Great, Bishop of Caesarea. Basil the Great was born in 329; Caesarea is the modern city of Kayseri. Between the 7th and 13th centuries Göreme was an important Christian centre. The Apple Church was built around 1050, the Dark Church in the late 11th and early 12th centuries.

The Dark Church was used as a dovecote until the 1950s, and its restoration took fourteen years. Some 30 km south lies the underground city of Derinkuyu, perhaps first dug by the Phrygians in the 8th–7th centuries BC. In Byzantine times, as shelter from Arab raids, it was completed, reaching about 85 metres deep. It could hold up to 20,000 people with their animals, and each floor could be sealed from inside with rolling stone doors. In 1923, with the population exchange between Greece and Türkiye, Cappadocia's Greeks left and the tunnels were abandoned.

In 1963 a resident renovating his house found a hidden room behind a wall, and the city came to light again. Derinkuyu opened to visitors in 1969; some 8 to 9 km of tunnels linked it to Kaymaklı. The castle rock of Uçhisar is first mentioned in a 14th-century chronicle. In 1985 Göreme National Park and the Rock Sites of Cappadocia became World Heritage, for both cultural and natural values. From hermits' caves to cities underground, this soft rock holds almost two thousand years of human history.

人文 Culture

住在岩石裡的人

Living in the Rock

鴿舍與鴿糞肥料、紅河邊的陶藝、清晨的熱氣球，與每年數百萬的遊客
Dovecotes and pigeon manure, pottery by the Red River, dawn balloons and millions of visitors

烏奇沙城堡是一座高約六十公尺的岩石，。過去可能住過大約一千人。小鎮海拔一千兩百七十公尺，。2022年約有三千五百人。山谷兩側，鑿出了許多鴿舍；。鴿糞一直到1970年代，都被拿來當肥料。從烏奇沙走鴿子谷到格雷梅，。大約要兩個小時。格雷梅露天博物館，大約有十幾座教堂，。是這一帶參觀人數最多的地方。

每年來卡帕多奇亞的遊客，估計有兩百萬到四百萬人；。研究認為，博物館在多數月份都超過了承載量。北邊的阿瓦諾斯，在土耳其最長的河流克孜勒河畔，。「克孜勒」的意思，是「紅色」。阿瓦諾斯最重要的產業是陶藝，。用的是河裡紅色淤泥的黏土，可能從西臺時代就開始了。古代它叫做維內薩，。2022年，小鎮約有一萬五千人。卡帕多奇亞也是熱氣球旅遊的重鎮；。2024年，土耳其的熱氣球乘客達九十三萬多人次。

那一年全國飛了四萬三千多趟，。由七十一家業者、四百四十一顆熱氣球載客。卡帕多奇亞是熱氣球旅遊成長的主力，。也帶動了土耳其自己製造、出口熱氣球。越野四輪車的導覽，也帶來壓力，。估計每天約有四百輛，在這一帶來回。2019年，這裡和兩座地下城一起，。劃入約八萬六千公頃的「卡帕多奇亞區」管理。鴿舍、陶窯與清晨的熱氣球，。人們一直在這片軟岩上找生活的方法。

Uçhisar castle is a rock about 60 metres high; perhaps a thousand people once lived inside it. The town stands at 1,270 metres and had about 3,500 people in 2022. Many dovecotes were carved into the valley sides; pigeon droppings were used as fertiliser into the 1970s. The walk through Pigeon Valley from Uçhisar to Göreme takes about two hours. The Göreme Open Air Museum holds about a dozen churches and is the most visited place in the area.

Some 2 to 4 million people visit Cappadocia each year; a study found the museum over its carrying capacity in most months. To the north, Avanos lies on the Kızılırmak, the longest river in Türkiye; its name means "Red River". Pottery is Avanos's main trade, using clay from the river's red silt; it probably goes back to Hittite times. In antiquity it was called Venessa; in 2022 the town had about 15,000 people. Cappadocia is also a centre of balloon tourism; in 2024 Türkiye counted over 933,000 balloon passengers.

That year saw more than 43,000 flights, by 71 companies with 441 balloons. Cappadocia has led the growth of balloon tourism, and Türkiye now builds balloons and exports them. ATV tours add pressure too: an estimated 400 vehicles a day run through the area. In 2019 the area and two underground cities became part of the Cappadocia Area, about 86,000 hectares. Dovecotes, potters' kilns and balloons at dawn: people have always found ways to live on this soft rock.

卡帕多奇亞的高原很乾，。每年雨量只有大約三百八十公釐。除了河谷沿岸，。這裡的植被都很稀疏。格雷梅丘陵一帶，記錄到約六百五十種植物，。其中一百一十八種，是土耳其特有種。這裡也有二十三種全國罕見的植物，。新的特有種，還不斷被發現。山谷兩側的鴿舍，。收集鴿糞，給田地當肥料。

克孜勒河是土耳其最長的河，。河邊的綠色河谷，在乾燥的高原上特別顯眼。河水帶來的紅色淤泥，。給了阿瓦諾斯的陶匠黏土。越來越多的遊客、車輛與建築，。是這片土地最大的壓力。大約八百棟違建已經拆除，。還有將近一千件案子等待處理。灌溉用的水壩，讓空氣變得更潮濕，。也加快了軟岩的風化。

精靈煙囪目前只有大約三到三成五，。完成了調查與編目。2025年，世界自然保護聯盟的評估，。把這裡的保護前景列為「令人高度關切」。不過，同一份評估也指出，。這裡其他的生物多樣性價值「良好」，而且正在「改善」。往南的代林庫尤，地下有一條深五十五公尺的通風井，。它也兼作取水的水井。乾燥的高原、稀有的植物與脆弱的岩石，。需要更小心的照顧。

The Cappadocian plateau is dry, with only about 380 mm of rain a year. Apart from the river corridors there is little vegetation here. About 650 plant taxa are recorded in the Göreme hills, 118 of them found only in Türkiye. There are 23 plants rare in the whole country, and new endemic species are still being found. The dovecotes on the valley sides collected pigeon droppings to fertilise the fields.

The Kızılırmak, the longest river in Türkiye, runs through a green corridor that stands out on the dry plateau. The red silt carried by the river gives the potters of Avanos their clay. More and more visitors, vehicles and buildings are the greatest pressure on this land. About 800 illegal buildings have been removed, with nearly a thousand more cases pending. Irrigation dams have made the air more humid, speeding the weathering of the soft rock.

Only about 30 to 35 per cent of the fairy chimneys have been surveyed and catalogued so far. In 2025 IUCN's assessment rated the outlook for the site as "significant concern". Yet the same assessment rates its other biodiversity values as "good" and "improving". To the south, Derinkuyu has a ventilation shaft 55 metres deep that also served as a well. A dry plateau, rare plants and fragile rock all need more careful looking after.

地理 Geography

火山灰雕成的大地

A Land Carved from Volcanic Ash

千萬年前的火山灰、九層熔結凝灰岩、風與水雕出的精靈煙囪
Volcanic ash ten million years old, nine ignimbrite layers, and fairy chimneys carved by wind and water

卡帕多奇亞是一片海拔約一千公尺的高原，。由凝灰岩構成，上面覆蓋著熔岩。凝灰岩是火山灰凝固而成的細粒岩石，。和埃爾吉耶斯山與哈桑山的火山活動有關。一千萬到五百萬年前的中新世，。大型火山口噴出的火山碎屑流，一層層堆積。地質學家辨識出九層熔結凝灰岩，。總厚度超過四百三十公尺。這些火山口如今已經被埋在地下，。整個火山區，長約三百公里，寬二十到五十公里。

地殼抬升之後，水與風開始侵蝕軟岩，。刻出懸崖、岩塔、帳篷岩與精靈煙囪。有些精靈煙囪頂著一塊熔岩，。堅硬的岩帽，保護了底下較軟的凝灰岩。精靈煙囪能不能形成，。要看岩層的厚度、熔結的程度與坡度。烏奇沙海拔一千兩百七十公尺，。從城堡岩上，可以俯瞰周圍的山谷。軟岩容易開鑿，。小鎮中心有一條在凝灰岩裡挖出的地道，長約一百公尺。

克孜勒河古代叫做哈利斯河，。它切過高原，從阿瓦諾斯旁流過。阿瓦諾斯位在海拔九百二十公尺，。比烏奇沙低了三百多公尺。代林庫尤的地下城，。在凝灰岩裡往下挖了大約八十五公尺。地震、地層下陷與乾旱，。也是這片軟岩地景面對的風險。火山灰堆成的高原，被水與風慢慢雕刻，。直到今天，雕刻還沒有停止。

Cappadocia is a plateau about 1,000 metres high, made of tuff and covered with lava. Tuff is a fine-grained rock of consolidated volcanic ash, linked to the volcanoes of Mount Erciyes and Mount Hasan. Between 10 and 5 million years ago, in the Miocene, flows of volcanic debris from great calderas piled up layer on layer. Geologists have identified nine ignimbrites, together more than 430 metres thick. Those calderas are now buried; the whole volcanic province is about 300 km long and 20 to 50 km wide.

After uplift, water and wind began to wear the soft rock, carving cliffs, rock towers, tent rocks and fairy chimneys. Some fairy chimneys wear a cap of lava, a hard top that shields the softer tuff below. Whether fairy chimneys form depends on the thickness of the layers, how welded they are, and the slope. Uçhisar stands at 1,270 metres; from its castle rock you look out over the surrounding valleys. The soft rock is easy to dig; a tunnel about 100 metres long runs through the tuff under the town centre.

The Kızılırmak, the ancient Halys, cuts across the plateau past Avanos. Avanos lies at 920 metres, more than 300 metres lower than Uçhisar. The underground city of Derinkuyu was dug about 85 metres down into the tuff. Earthquakes, sinkholes and drought are further risks for this landscape of soft rock. A plateau of volcanic ash, slowly carved by water and wind; the carving has not stopped yet.
