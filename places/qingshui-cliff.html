歷史 History

崖上的路：從北路到蘇花改

The Road on the Cliff: From the Northern Road to the Suhua Improvement

一百五十年來，人怎麼通過這面山壁
A century and a half of getting past one wall of rock

旁白全文 Full narration

從崇德往北看，山壁一路擋到海邊。人是怎麼在這面崖上開出路來的？1874年牡丹社事件後，沈葆楨下令開路。羅大春率隊開闢蘇澳到花蓮的「北路」。北路約兩年完成，但路基狹窄，。到清末大多已經荒廢。1916年，臺灣總督府開始修築東海徒步道，。七年後完成，但車輛仍無法通行。拓寬之後，1932年5月以「臨海道路」通車。全線約120公里，九座大橋、十四座隧道。

戰後改稱蘇花公路。最窄處僅3.5公尺，長期採單向管制通行。1980年代開始拓寬，。1990年10月25日改為雙向通車。公路改走隧道。匯德隧道長1500公尺，。是蘇花公路上最長的一座。南端的崇德隧道長325公尺；。舊的清水隧道則已經封閉。隧道通車後，一段舊路改成匯德步道。石欄杆用的是開鑿隧道挖出的石材。

北迴鐵路在1973年動工。和仁站在1979年2月8日啟用。和仁與崇德之間，火車走清水隧道。第一代隧道長2106公尺。北迴線在1980年2月1日全線通車，。全長79.2公里，共有91座隧道。鐵路通車以後，東部與北部之間的往來，。不再只依賴這條公路。新清水隧道長2210公尺，約2001年通車。和仁到崇德的雙軌在2002年完成。

2020年1月6日，蘇花改和中到大清水段通車，。這一段的車流改走新隧道。1937年，日本時代曾設立次高太魯閣國立公園。1986年，太魯閣國家公園成立。步道、車道、鐵道與新隧道，。都是不同年代面對同一面山壁的答案。

Seen from Chongde, the mountain wall runs right down to the sea. How did people open a road across this cliff? In 1874, after the Mudan Incident, Shen Baozhen ordered a road built. Luo Dachun led the work on the Northern Road from Su'ao to Hualien. The Northern Road took about two years, but its bed was narrow, and by the late Qing most of it lay abandoned. In 1916 the Government-General began building the Donghai Footpath; it was finished seven years later, still impassable to vehicles. After widening, it opened to vehicles in May 1932 as the Coastal Road: about 120 km, with nine large bridges and fourteen tunnels.

After the war it was renamed the Suhua Highway. Only 3.5 m wide at its narrowest, it long ran as controlled one-way traffic. Widening began in the 1980s, and two-way traffic started on 25 October 1990. The road moved into tunnels. Huide Tunnel, 1,500 m long, is the longest on the Suhua Highway. Chongde Tunnel, at the southern end, is 325 m long; the old Qingshui road tunnel has been closed. Once the tunnel opened, a stretch of old road became the Huide Trail. Its stone railings were built from rock excavated for the tunnel.

Work on the North-Link railway began in 1973. Heren Station opened on 8 February 1979. Between Heren and Chongde the trains run through the Qingshui Tunnel. The first-generation bore is 2,106 m long. The whole North-Link line opened on 1 February 1980: 79.2 km of track, with 91 tunnels. With the railway open, travel between the east and the north no longer depended on this highway alone. A new 2,210 m Qingshui Tunnel opened around 2001, and the double track from Heren to Chongde was completed in 2002.

On 6 January 2020 the Hezhong–Daqingshui section of the Suhua Improvement opened, moving through traffic here into new tunnels. In 1937, under Japanese rule, the Tsugitaka-Taroko National Park was created. The present Taroko National Park was established in 1986. Footpath, motor road, railway and new tunnels: each era's answer to the same wall of rock.

人文 Culture

崖腳下的聚落：崇德與和仁

Settlements Below the Cliff: Chongde and Heren

沖積扇、礫灘與兩座小站上的生活
Life on an alluvial fan, a gravel shore and two small stations

斷崖南端的崇德，是一片臨海的平地。誰住在這面山壁的腳下？這裡屬於花蓮縣秀林鄉，。面積約1642平方公里，是全臺最大的鄉。全鄉約一萬七千人，。多數居民是太魯閣族。太魯閣族的祖先原居濁水溪上游，。越過奇萊山，東遷到立霧溪流域。2004年，太魯閣族獲政府正式認定，。成為臺灣第十二個原住民族。

立霧溪口北岸最高的階地上，有崇德遺址。年代約在1500到900年前。2016到2019年間，考古學者多次在此發掘，。出土墓葬與陶器，屬於十三行文化。聚落外就是礫石海灘。崇德外海設有定置漁網。定置網是日治時期引進的漁法：。用一道垣網，把洄游的魚群引進網內。花蓮港離這裡較遠，。漁獲常用膠筏直接運上岸。

崇德車站在1979年2月8日啟用，。是只有區間列車停靠的三等站。2023年，平均每天約49人進出。往北進入斷崖。南段舊稱得其黎斷崖，。中段的清水斷崖，舊稱石硿仔斷崖。國家公園在這段海岸設有三處遊憩區：。和仁、匯德與崇德。崇德步道長約320公尺，。走十分鐘左右就能下到礫灘。

北段舊稱卡那崗斷崖，。盡頭就是和仁。和仁車站位在和平村，同樣在1979年啟用，。2023年每天約85人進出。再往北是和平，設有水泥專用港。斷崖讓聚落只能落腳在南北兩端，。公路、鐵路與海，把它們連在一起。

Chongde, at the southern end of the cliff, is a flat shelf beside the sea. Who lives at the foot of this wall? This is Xiulin Township, Hualien County: about 1,642 square kilometres, the largest township in Taiwan. The township has about 17,000 people, and most residents are Truku. Truku ancestors lived on the upper Zhuoshui River, then crossed Mount Qilai and moved east into the Liwu valley. In 2004 the Truku were formally recognised as Taiwan's twelfth Indigenous people.

On the highest terrace north of the Liwu River mouth lies the Chongde Site, dated to roughly 1,500 to 900 years ago. Archaeologists dug here several times between 2016 and 2019, finding burials and pottery of the Shisanhang culture. Beyond the village lies a gravel beach. Set nets are anchored in the sea off Chongde. The set net is a fishing method introduced in the Japanese era: a long leader net guides migrating fish into the trap. Hualien Port is some distance away, so the catch is often brought straight ashore by raft.

Chongde Station opened on 8 February 1979, a third-class stop served only by local trains. In 2023 it handled about 49 passengers a day. Northward the cliff begins. The southern part was once called Deqili Cliff; the middle part, Qingshui Cliff, was known as Shikongzi Cliff. The national park keeps three recreation areas on this coast: Heren, Huide and Chongde. The Chongde Trail is about 320 m long; roughly ten minutes on foot leads down to the gravel beach.

The northern part was once called Kanagang Cliff; at its far end lies Heren. Heren Station, in Heping Village, also opened in 1979; in 2023 it saw about 85 passengers a day. Farther north is Heping, with its dedicated cement port. The cliff leaves room for settlement only at its two ends; road, railway and sea tie them together.

自然 Nature

從海面到稜線：斷崖的生態界線

From Sea to Ridge: The Cliff as an Ecological Boundary

陡坡、山壁與溪流如何分隔生物
How steep slopes, a rock wall and a river sort living things

旁白全文 Full narration

秋天早晨的清水斷崖。灰白的岩壁之間，是成片的深綠。從海面到2408公尺的清水山頂，。這麼陡的山，生物怎麼分布？太魯閣國家公園的植被隨海拔改變：。闊葉林、針闊葉混合林，再到針葉林。園區最高處，則是高山草原與寒原。靠山的一側是茂密的闊葉林，。臨海的崖壁上長著山芙蓉、山鹽青等灌木。

山芙蓉在秋天開花，。為崖邊的灌叢添上花色。斷崖被劃為特別景觀區，。後方的清水山則屬於生態保護區。進入生態保護區必須事先申請許可。清水山路線約50公里，建議行程四天。對一些動物來說，斷崖是擴散的屏障。褐樹蛙的東、西兩個支系就以這裡為界。再往南，立霧溪也是一條分界。兩種近緣的草蜥分居溪的兩側。

牠們是翠斑草蜥與鹿野草蜥，。研究指出兩者以這條溪為界。溪水在這裡流進太平洋，。淡水與海水在河口交會。崇德的礫灘上，常見白色的大理岩卵石，。和山壁上的大理岩是同一種岩石。外海是洄游魚類經過的水域。花蓮定置網的漁獲以鰹魚類最多。夏季還常見鬼頭刀與曼波魚。

2026年3月，崇德定置網內發現鬚鯨遺體，。體長15.4公尺，初判為長須鯨或大村鯨。國家公園內禁止採集標本與設置陷阱，。也不能擅自操作遙控機具。陡坡分出植被，山壁與溪流隔開族群。這面斷崖，同時也是一條生態的界線。

Qingshui Cliff on an autumn morning. Between pale rock faces lie broad patches of deep green. From the sea to the 2,408 m summit of Qingshui Mountain: on a slope this steep, how does life arrange itself? Vegetation in Taroko National Park changes with elevation: broadleaf forest, then mixed forest, then conifers. At the park's highest levels come alpine grassland and tundra. The landward side carries dense broadleaf forest; on the seaward cliffs grow shrubs such as Taiwan cotton rose and sumac.

The cotton rose flowers in autumn, adding colour to the scrub along the cliff edge. The cliff is zoned as a Special Landscape Area; Qingshui Mountain behind it lies in an Ecological Protection Area. Entering the protection area requires a permit in advance. The Qingshui Mountain route is about 50 km, a suggested four days. For some animals the cliff is a barrier to dispersal. The eastern and western clades of the brown tree frog divide here. Farther south, the Liwu River is another dividing line: two sibling species of grass lizard live on either side.

They are Takydromus viridipunctatus and T. luyeanus; research places the boundary between them at this river. Here the river runs into the Pacific, and fresh water meets salt water at the mouth. White marble pebbles are common on the gravel beach at Chongde, the same rock as the marble in the cliffs. Offshore is a passage for migratory fish. Bonitos make up most of the catch in Hualien's set nets. In summer, mahi-mahi and ocean sunfish are also common.

In March 2026 a dead baleen whale, 15.4 m long, was found in the Chongde set net, first assessed as a fin whale or an Omura's whale. Inside the national park, collecting specimens and trapping are prohibited, as is operating remote-controlled craft without permission. Steep slopes sort the vegetation; rock wall and river divide populations. The cliff is at once a landmark and an ecological boundary.

地理 Geography

山直入海：清水斷崖的地形與岩石

Mountain into Sea: Landform and Rock of Qingshui Cliff

二十公里的海崖是怎麼站起來的
How twenty kilometres of sea cliff came to stand

花蓮縣秀林鄉，蘇花公路經過的海岸。山壁從太平洋邊直接拔起。這一段海崖，就是清水斷崖。它為什麼這麼高、這麼陡？依觀光署的說明，崇德到和平之間。約20公里的路段，都屬於清水斷崖。崖壁高達千餘公尺，。公路只是山腰上的一條細線。崖頂後方的清水山，海拔2408公尺，。是這片海崖的最高點。

稜線向南延伸到千里眼山與立霧山，。海拔分別是1624與1274公尺。岩壁主要由大理岩與片麻岩構成，。北段還有綠色片岩。大理岩原本是石灰岩，經變質作用而成。這套岩層的厚度估計有1500到2000公尺。這條大理岩帶從和平沿著海岸，。經清水山一路延伸進太魯閣峽谷。這裡位在歐亞板塊的邊緣，。受菲律賓海板塊碰撞，每年仍抬升約0.5公分。

從正上方看，海水常分成兩種顏色：。外海深藍，近岸粉白。兩種顏色之間，有一條清楚的界線。往南，山壁在崇德退開，。眼前出現一片平坦的沖積扇。崇德的房舍與鐵路都在這片扇面上，。背後是斷崖的南端。立霧溪發源於合歡山與奇萊北峰一帶，。蜿蜒約58公里後在這裡入海。

上游就是太魯閣峽谷，。同一套大理岩在那裡被溪水切開。太魯閣國家公園從這片海岸起算，。最高到南湖大山的3742公尺。大理岩與片麻岩構成的山體仍在抬升，。山與海之間，幾乎沒有平地。

Xiulin Township, Hualien: the coast followed by the Suhua Highway. The mountain wall rises straight from the edge of the Pacific. This stretch of sea cliff is Qingshui Cliff. Why is it so high, and so steep? By the Tourism Administration's account, about 20 km of road between Chongde and Heping counts as Qingshui Cliff. The walls stand more than a thousand metres high; the highway is a thin line cut into the slope. Qingshui Mountain, behind the cliff top, reaches 2,408 m, the highest point of this sea cliff.

The ridge runs south to Qianliyan and Liwu mountains, at 1,624 m and 1,274 m. The wall is built mainly of marble and gneiss, with green schist in the northern part. The marble began as limestone and was later metamorphosed. The formation is estimated to be 1,500 to 2,000 m thick. The marble belt runs from Heping along this coast, through Qingshui Mountain and on into Taroko Gorge. This is the edge of the Eurasian Plate, in collision with the Philippine Sea Plate; the land is still rising about 0.5 cm a year.

From directly above, the sea often shows two colours: deep blue offshore, milky pale near the shore. Between the two colours runs a sharp boundary line. To the south the wall steps back at Chongde, and a flat alluvial fan opens ahead. Chongde's houses and railway sit on this fan, with the southern end of the cliff behind them. The Liwu River rises near Hehuanshan and Qilai North Peak, and winds about 58 km before reaching the sea here.

Upstream lies Taroko Gorge, where the river has cut down through the same marble. Taroko National Park begins at this shoreline and climbs to 3,742 m on Nanhu Mountain. A mountain of marble and gneiss, still being lifted, leaves almost no flat ground between ridge and sea.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

清水斷崖位於太魯閣國家公園內。依〈太魯閣國家公園區域內禁止事項〉第6點，未經許可不得在指定地區以外操作遙控機具（包含無人機），違者罰鍰新臺幣1,500元、再犯3,000元；實際飛行須先取得太魯閣國家公園管理處同意，並遵守民航局規定（日間、400呎以下，飛行前查詢空域圖資）。

Qingshui Cliff lies inside Taroko National Park. Item 6 of the park's Prohibited Acts bans operating remote-controlled craft (drones included) outside designated areas without authorisation, with fines of NT$1,500, or NT$3,000 for repeat offences; get the park headquarters' consent first and follow CAA rules (daytime, below 400 ft, check the airspace map before flying).
