歷史 History

魔鬼的花園

The Devil's Garden

A name first written in 1777, Croatia's first national park, Easter 1991 and a cap on visitors

十六座湖泊，一層層嵌在克羅埃西亞的山林裡，。這裡是普利特維采湖群國家公園。六世紀時阿瓦爾人來到這一帶，。之後克羅埃西亞人在這裡定居下來。後來這裡成了軍事邊區，由邊防軍人守衛。這片地區，一度被叫做「魔鬼的花園」。1777年，奧托察茨的神父武卡索維奇，。第一次在文件裡寫下「普利特維采」這個名字。這個名字，來自那些積了水的淺淺盆地。1861年，大波利亞納蓋了第一處給旅人的住所。

1888年，為了迎接史蒂芬妮王儲妃來訪，。湖區第一次為觀光整理了步道。1893年，雅內切克成立了保護湖區的協會；。1898年，一座瀑布以捐出演唱會收入的歌手特爾尼娜命名。1949年4月8日，這裡成為克羅埃西亞第一座國家公園，。最初面積約一萬九千四百七十四公頃。1962到1968年，許多部卡爾·邁小說改編的電影在這裡拍攝，。包括《銀湖寶藏》。1979年10月26日，。普利特維采湖群列入世界遺產。

1991年3月31日復活節，這裡爆發了武裝衝突，。是克羅埃西亞獨立戰爭中第一次有人死亡的戰鬥。警察約西普·約維奇在衝突中殉職，。在克羅埃西亞，他被視為這場戰爭的第一位犧牲者。戰爭期間，旅館被當成軍營；。1997年公園移出瀕危名單，面積也擴大到約兩萬九千六百公頃。遊客從1998年的約三十五萬人，。增加到2017年的約一百七十萬人。2018年，聯合國教科文組織因污染問題提出警告；。2019年起，夏季每天最多一萬兩千人入園。

從魔鬼的花園、戰場，。到全世界遊客想看的湖泊，這裡一直在改變。

Sixteen lakes are set in tiers among the forested hills of Croatia: this is Plitvice Lakes National Park. The Avars settled the region in the 6th century, followed by the Croats, who stayed for good. Later it became part of the Military Frontier, guarded by frontiersmen. The region was once called "the garden of the devil". In 1777 Dominik Vukasović, the priest of Otočac, first wrote the name "Plitvice" in a document. The name comes from the shallow basins that filled with water. In 1861 the first lodging for travellers was built at Velika Poljana.

In 1888 the lakes were first arranged for tourism, for a visit by Crown Princess Stéphanie. In 1893 Gustav Janeček founded a society to preserve the lakes; in 1898 a waterfall was named after the singer Milka Trnina, who donated concert proceeds. On 8 April 1949 this became Croatia's first national park, covering 19,474 hectares at first. From 1962 to 1968 many films of Karl May's novels were shot here, including Treasure of the Silver Lake. On 26 October 1979 the Plitvice Lakes were inscribed on the World Heritage List.

On Easter Sunday, 31 March 1991, an armed clash broke out here, the first fatal fight of the Croatian War of Independence. The police officer Josip Jović was killed in the clash; in Croatia he is seen as the first casualty of the war. During the war the hotels were used as barracks. In 1997 the park was removed from the List in Danger and enlarged to 29,630 hectares. Visitor numbers rose from about 350,000 in 1998 to a record of about 1.7 million in 2017. In 2018 UNESCO warned the park over pollution; since 2019 summer entry has been capped at 12,000 a day.

From the devil's garden and a battlefield to lakes the whole world wants to see, this place has kept changing.

公園大約九成在利卡-塞尼縣，。另外一成在卡爾洛瓦茨縣。湖區附近，有一座修道院建在古代亞波德人與羅馬人聚落的遺址上，。今天只剩下幾段牆。中世紀時，蒙古人的侵襲威脅著居民；。後來茲林斯基與弗蘭科潘家族帶動了地方的復興。軍事邊區時代，逃難來的塞爾維亞東正教徒，。以服兵役換取這裡的土地。1888年，史蒂芬妮王儲妃來訪；。有兩條步道，以皇帝的女兒命名。

1898年，歌劇歌手特爾尼娜把演唱會收入捐給湖區，。一座瀑布因此以她的名字命名。1926年，植物學家佩瓦萊克，。最早主張要保護普利特維采。1943年6月14日，克羅埃西亞的反法西斯委員會，。在普利特維采召開了成立會議。1960年代，德國作家卡爾·邁的西部小說，。在這些湖泊和瀑布前被搬上銀幕。1980年代，南斯拉夫的觀光蓬勃發展，。普利特維采是熱門的目的地。

今天公園全年開放，。有七條遊覽路線和四條登山步道。2006年起，湖裡禁止游泳與戲水，。以保護湖水與石灰華。夏天的尖峰日，每個入口每小時最多進六百五十人，。整天最多一萬兩千人。公園的拍攝規定明文禁止。用遙控飛行器進行空中拍攝。王儲妃、歌手、電影與戰爭，。都在這些湖邊留下了足跡。

About 90% of the park lies in Lika-Senj County, the rest in Karlovac County. A monastery was built near here on the remains of a Japod and Roman settlement; only fragments of its walls survive. In the Middle Ages Mongol raids threatened the people here; later the Zrinski and Frankopan families led a recovery. In Military Frontier times Serb Orthodox refugees were given land here in return for military service. Crown Princess Stéphanie visited in 1888; two paths are named after the Emperor's daughters.

In 1898 the opera singer Milka Trnina gave her concert proceeds to the lakes, and a waterfall was named after her. In 1926 the botanist Ivo Pevalek was among the first to argue for protecting Plitvice. On 14 June 1943 ZAVNOH, the anti-fascist council of Croatia, held its founding session at Plitvice. In the 1960s the western novels of the German writer Karl May were filmed in front of these lakes and waterfalls. Tourism boomed in Yugoslavia in the 1980s, and Plitvice was a favourite destination.

Today the park is open all year, with seven tour routes and four hiking trails. Since 2006 bathing and swimming in the lakes have been banned to protect the water and the travertine. On busy summer days each entrance admits at most 650 people an hour, and no more than 12,000 a day. The park's filming rules expressly ban remote-controlled aircraft for aerial filming. A princess, a singer, films and a war have all left their footprints by these lakes.

森林覆蓋了公園大約百分之八十一的面積，。其中將近九成五是山毛櫸。公園西北部的喬爾科瓦灣，。有一片八十四公頃的老齡林。那裡有一棵超過五百歲的歐洲銀冷杉，。高五十八公尺，直徑一百五十公分。公園記錄到約一千四百種植物與亞種，。大約是克羅埃西亞全國的三成。這裡有大約六十種蘭花，。和兩百零七種苔蘚。

Forest covers about 81% of the park, and beech makes up nearly 95% of it. In the north-west of the park, Čorkova uvala holds 84 hectares of old-growth forest. There stands a European silver fir over 500 years old, 58 metres tall and 150 cm across. About 1,400 plant species and subspecies have been recorded, some 30% of Croatia's total. There are about 60 orchid species and 207 species of moss.

Mosses and algae in the water help calcium carbonate settle, building travertine barriers. These barriers can grow up to 13 mm higher each year; barrier by barrier, they hold the water back into lakes. On the bed of Lake Kozjak about 0.8 mm of sediment builds up each year. Brown bears, wolves and Eurasian lynx live in the forests, all three large carnivores still at home here. 168 bird species and 22 bat species have been recorded.

There are nine species of fish in the lakes, and the park has 14 amphibian and 13 reptile species. The main pressures on the park are excessive building for tourism, wastewater treatment and water drawn from Lake Kozjak. IUCN's 2025 assessment rated the outlook here "good with some concerns". More than 80% of the park is a zone of strict conservation. Forest, moss and running water have slowly built these lakes together.

十六座湖的階梯

A Staircase of Sixteen Lakes

Twelve upper lakes on dolomite, four lower lakes in a limestone canyon, a 133 m drop and a 78 m waterfall

十六座有名字的湖，從南向北一路往下排列。第一座到最後一座湖，高度相差一百三十三公尺。最上面的普羅錢斯科湖，海拔約六百三十九公尺，。最深約三十七公尺。十二座上湖，位在白雲岩上，。一座接著一座，往科濟亞克湖流去。加洛瓦茨湖海拔約六百公尺，。湖水從石灰華堤壩上，一層層溢流下去。再往下，就是最大的科濟亞克湖，。面積八十二公頃，最深四十七公尺。

湖泊的面積，。加起來還不到公園的百分之一。四座下湖，在一道石灰岩的峽谷裡，。米拉諾瓦茨、加瓦諾瓦茨、卡盧傑羅瓦茨與諾瓦科維查布羅德。峽谷旁，普利特維察溪從崖頂落下，。形成高七十八公尺的大瀑布。下湖在薩斯塔夫奇瀑布結束，。科拉納河就從瀑布底下流出。公園最低點在科拉納河橋，海拔三百六十七公尺；。最高點是塞利什基峰，一千兩百七十九公尺。

公園的面積約兩百九十七平方公里，。2000年，世界遺產範圍又擴大了一萬多公頃。石灰華的形成，。正是這裡被列為世界遺產的「傑出普世價值」。整個公園從最低點到最高點，。高低差達到九百一十二公尺。除了十六座有名字的湖，。還有好幾座沒有名字的小湖。十六座湖，像一道長長的階梯，。由水和石灰華一階一階地蓋起來。

Sixteen named lakes step down from south to north; from the first to the last the drop is 133 metres. The topmost, Lake Prošćansko, lies at about 639 metres and is up to 37 metres deep. The twelve Upper Lakes lie on dolomite, each flowing on into the next towards Lake Kozjak. Lake Galovac lies at about 600 metres, its water spilling tier by tier over travertine barriers. Further down lies the largest, Lake Kozjak, 82 hectares in area and 47 metres deep.

All the lakes together cover less than 1% of the park. The four Lower Lakes lie in a limestone canyon: Milanovac, Gavanovac, Kaluđerovac and Novakovića Brod. Beside the canyon the Plitvica stream drops off the cliff as the Great Waterfall, 78 metres high. The Lower Lakes end at the Sastavci waterfalls, where the Korana River springs from beneath them. The lowest point of the park is at the Korana bridge, 367 metres; the highest is Seliški vrh, 1,279 metres.

The park covers about 297 square kilometres; in 2000 the World Heritage Site was enlarged by over 10,000 hectares. The forming of travertine is the "outstanding universal value" for which the park is listed. From its lowest to its highest point the park spans a difference in height of 912 metres. Besides the sixteen named lakes there are several smaller, unnamed ones. Sixteen lakes form a long staircase, built step by step by water and travertine.
