歷史 History

傳教士、鐵橋與兩國的瀑布

A Missionary, a Bridge and a Shared Waterfall

Livingstone's 1855 sighting, the 1905 bridge and a colonial capital, to World Heritage in 1989

維多利亞瀑布位在尚比亞與辛巴威的邊界上，。尚比西河在這裡，一口氣落進深谷。1855年11月16日，蘇格蘭傳教士李文斯頓，。從河中的一座小島，第一次看見這座瀑布。他以維多利亞女王的名字，為瀑布命名；。他站過的小島，後來就叫做李文斯頓島。1860年，他和植物學家約翰・柯克一起回來，。仔細地研究這座瀑布。北岸的利文斯頓，在1905年建城，。1911到1935年，是北羅德西亞的首府。

1935年，首府遷到路沙卡；。2022年，利文斯頓約有十七萬七千人。1904年，鐵路通到瀑布南岸，。橫跨峽谷的大橋，也在這一年動工。鋼材在英國達靈頓預製，運到莫三比克的貝拉，。再用火車載到瀑布。塞西爾・羅德斯要火車經過時能沾到瀑布的水霧，。但他自己從沒來過，也沒看到大橋動工。1905年9月12日，大橋由喬治・達爾文爵士啟用；。它長一百九十八公尺，高出低水位的河面一百二十八公尺。

協助計算應力的工程師拉爾夫・弗里曼，。後來成為雪梨港灣大橋的主要設計者。1975年，各方在停在橋上的火車車廂裡和談，。談了九個半小時，沒有結果。1989年，這裡以「莫西奧圖尼亞」與「維多利亞瀑布」兩個名字，一起列入世界遺產。辛巴威這一側的維多利亞瀑布鎮，1901年開始有人定居，。2020年12月9日升格為市。從傳教士的紀錄、跨過峽谷的鐵橋，。到兩國共有的世界遺產，這裡一直是交會的地方。

Victoria Falls lie on the border of Zambia and Zimbabwe, where the Zambezi plunges in one drop into a gorge. On 16 November 1855 the Scottish missionary David Livingstone first saw the falls from an island in the river. He named the falls after Queen Victoria; the island he stood on became Livingstone Island. In 1860 he came back with the naturalist John Kirk to study the falls in detail. Livingstone, on the north bank, was founded in 1905, and from 1911 to 1935 it was the capital of Northern Rhodesia.

In 1935 the capital moved to Lusaka; in 2022 Livingstone had about 177,000 people. In 1904 the railway reached the southern side of the falls, and work began on a bridge across the gorge. Its steel was prefabricated in Darlington, England, shipped to Beira in Mozambique and brought to the falls by train. Cecil Rhodes wanted passing trains to catch the spray of the falls, but he never visited them and died before work began. On 12 September 1905 Sir George Darwin opened the bridge; it is 198 m long and 128 m above the low-water level of the river.

Ralph Freeman, who helped with its stress calculations, later became principal designer of the Sydney Harbour Bridge. In 1975 peace talks were held in a train carriage parked on the bridge; they lasted nine and a half hours and failed. In 1989 the falls became World Heritage under both names, Mosi-oa-Tunya and Victoria Falls. On the Zimbabwean side, the town of Victoria Falls was settled from 1901 and became a city on 9 December 2020. From a missionary's record and a bridge over the gorge to World Heritage shared by two countries, this has always been a meeting place.

人文 Culture

Lozi and Tonga names, the Devil's Pool, bungee from the bridge, and twin towns on two banks

洛齊語叫它「莫西奧圖尼亞」，。意思是「會打雷的煙」。東加人叫它「順古那穆提提瑪」，。意思是「沸騰的水」。李文斯頓也記下了更早的名字「塞翁戈」與「瓊威」，。意思是「彩虹的所在」。李文斯頓島西端的「魔鬼泳池」，是天然的無邊際水池，。通常在九月到十二月，才能下水。1990年代末，每年約有四十萬人來看瀑布；。辛巴威和尚比亞本國的遊客，比外國遊客更多。

大橋上的高空彈跳，落差一百一十一公尺；。它也是兩國之間唯一的鐵路連結。大橋原本叫做「大尚比西橋」，。過橋費在1906年就取消了。一列一百多年的燃煤蒸汽火車，。載著用餐的旅客，開到橋上和尚比亞的邊界。辛巴威的維多利亞瀑布市，2022年約三萬五千人；。1990年，大象步行街在這裡開幕。2019年，這裡提供兩百多種冒險活動，。泛舟、獨木舟、騎馬與觀光飛行，都在其中。

尚比亞這一側的利文斯頓，。和辛巴威的維多利亞瀑布市，結為姊妹市。利文斯頓博物館收藏考古、民族與歷史文物，。還有李文斯頓留下的紀念品。1941年，利文斯頓接納了一百七十名波蘭難民，。也設立了波蘭的領事據點。會打雷的煙、彩虹的所在，。不同的名字，說的是同一道瀑布。

In Lozi it is Mosi-oa-Tunya, "the smoke that thunders". The Tonga call it Shungu Namutitima, "boiling water". Livingstone also recorded the older names Seongo and Chongwe, meaning "the place of the rainbow". The Devil's Pool at the western tip of Livingstone Island is a natural infinity pool, usually open for a swim from September to December. By the late 1990s almost 400,000 people came every year; Zimbabwean and Zambian visitors outnumber those from abroad.

The bungee jump from the bridge is 111 metres; the bridge is also the only rail link between the two countries. It was first called the Great Zambezi Bridge, and its toll was abolished in 1906. A coal-fired steam train over a hundred years old carries diners out to the bridge and the Zambian border. Victoria Falls in Zimbabwe had about 35,000 people in 2022; its Elephant Walk pedestrian street opened in 1990. In 2019 it offered more than 200 adventure activities, from rafting, kayaking and riding to sightseeing flights.

Livingstone on the Zambian side is twinned with Victoria Falls in Zimbabwe. The Livingstone Museum holds archaeology, ethnography and history, along with memorabilia of David Livingstone. In 1941 Livingstone took in 170 Polish refugees, and a Polish consular post was set up. The smoke that thunders, the place of the rainbow: different names for the same falls.

自然 Nature

Spray-fed rainforest, hippos and elephants, raptors in the gorges, and a river that rises and falls

瀑布濺起的水霧，。在瀑布周圍養出了一片雨林。林子裡有莢果桃花心木、烏木，。還有象牙椰子與野海棗。瀑布上游的河裡，住著河馬與尼羅鱷；。旱季時，象群會從幾個地方渡河。瀑布上方的河面上，。常見鷺科鳥類、非洲魚鷹與各種水鳥。這一帶記錄到三十五種猛禽；。塔伊塔隼、黑鵰、遊隼與非洲鵟，都在峽谷裡繁殖。

瀑布下方有三十九種魚，上方有八十四種，。可見瀑布擋住了魚類的去路。尚比亞的莫西奧圖尼亞國家公園約六十六平方公里，。辛巴威的維多利亞瀑布國家公園約二十三平方公里。公園裡有非洲象、非洲水牛、長頸鹿，。格蘭特斑馬與多種羚羊。雨季從十一月底到四月初，。洪水季在二到五月，四月水量最大。平均流量是每秒一千零八十八立方公尺，。水量大的月份，平均可達每秒三千立方公尺。

十一月時，月平均只剩每秒約三百立方公尺，。有時一半的岩壁都會乾掉。2019年雨量特別少，。瀑布的流量降到一百年來最低。水霧常常升到四百公尺以上，有時再高一倍，。五十公里外都看得見。滿月的夜晚，。水霧裡有時看得見月虹。水霧、雨林與大河，。讓瀑布兩岸，成了許多生命的家。

The spray of the falls feeds a rainforest around them. It holds pod mahogany and ebony, ivory palms and wild date palms. Above the falls the river holds hippos and Nile crocodiles; in the dry season elephant herds cross it at certain points. On the river above the falls herons, African fish eagles and waterfowl are common. Thirty-five species of raptor are recorded here; Taita falcons, Verreaux's eagles, peregrines and augur buzzards breed in the gorges.

There are 39 fish species below the falls and 84 above, which shows the falls are a barrier. Zambia's Mosi-oa-Tunya National Park covers about 66 km², Zimbabwe's Victoria Falls National Park about 23. The parks hold African elephants, Cape buffalo, giraffes, Grant's zebras and many kinds of antelope. The rains last from late November to early April; the flood season runs from February to May, peaking in April. The mean flow is 1,088 cubic metres a second, and in the highest months it averages 3,000.

By November the monthly mean falls to about 300, and up to half the rock face can run dry. In 2019 unusually little rain fell, and the falls dropped to their lowest flow in a century. The spray usually rises over 400 metres, sometimes twice that, and can be seen up to 50 km away. On a night of full moon a lunar rainbow can sometimes be seen in the spray. Spray, rainforest and the great river make both banks of the falls a home for life.

地理 Geography

之字形的峽谷

Zigzag Gorges

世界最大的一片落水、深一百零八公尺的第一峽谷、兩億年前的玄武岩，與後退的瀑布
The world's largest sheet of falling water, a 108 m gorge, 200-million-year-old basalt, a retreating fall

瀑布寬一千七百零八公尺，高一百零八公尺，。形成世界上最大的一片落水。從西到東，依序是魔鬼瀑布、主瀑布，。最高的彩虹瀑布，與東瀑布。瀑布頂端的河中有兩塊陸地：。靠西岸的瀑布島，與靠中間的李文斯頓島。整條河一次垂直落入第一峽谷；。峽谷西端深八十公尺，中段深一百零八公尺。所有的河水，。只從一個一百一十公尺寬的缺口流出峽谷。

之後河道曲折，穿過一道又一道峽谷；。第二峽谷在瀑布南方兩百五十公尺，長約兩公里。第二峽谷旁的「沸騰鍋」，。是一個寬約一百五十公尺的深潭。瀑布南方五公里多的松圭峽谷，。深一百四十公尺，是最深的一段。這片玄武岩高原形成於侏羅紀，。大約在兩億年前。東西向的峽谷，沿著岩層的裂隙發育；。河水沿著弱帶侵蝕，瀑布就一段段往上游後退。

約兩百萬年前，地殼抬升，擋住了上尚比西河往南的出路，。形成馬卡迪卡迪古湖。約兩萬年前，古湖向東溢出，。切出了巴托卡峽谷。巴托卡峽谷裡看得到六道峽谷，。也留下了瀑布過去八個位置的痕跡。峽谷裡的水位，。雨季和旱季可以相差二十公尺。一層層玄武岩與一道道裂隙，。記錄了瀑布不斷後退的旅程。

The falls are 1,708 metres wide and 108 metres high, making the world's largest sheet of falling water. From west to east come the Devil's Cataract, the Main Falls, the Rainbow Falls, the highest, and the Eastern Cataract. Two land masses stand on the lip of the falls: Cataract Island near the western bank and Livingstone Island near the middle. The whole river drops in a single plunge into the First Gorge, 80 m deep at its western end and 108 m in the centre. All of that water leaves the First Gorge through a gap only 110 metres wide.

Then the river zigzags through gorge after gorge; the Second Gorge, 250 m south of the falls, is about 2 km long. Beside the Second Gorge lies the Boiling Pot, a pool about 150 metres across. The Songwe Gorge, more than 5 km south of the falls, is the deepest, at 140 metres. This basalt plateau formed in the Jurassic, about 200 million years ago. The east–west gorges follow fracture zones in the rock; eroding along these weak lines, the falls move upstream step by step.

About 2 million years ago uplift blocked the upper Zambezi from draining south, forming Lake Makgadikgadi. About 20,000 years ago the lake overflowed to the east and cut the Batoka Gorge. The Batoka Gorge shows six separate gorges and eight earlier positions of the falls. The water in the gorges can rise and fall by 20 metres between wet and dry seasons. Layer upon layer of basalt and line upon line of fractures record the long retreat of the falls.
