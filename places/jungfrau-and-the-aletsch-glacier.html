歷史 History

通往歐洲之巔

The Way to the Top of Europe

1811年首登少女峰、1912年鐵路通車、2001年世界遺產
First ascent in 1811, a railway in 1912, World Heritage in 2001

1811年，少女峰第一次被人登頂。隔年，芬斯特拉峰也被登頂。艾格峰高3,967公尺，北壁是阿爾卑斯最難攀登的岩壁之一。直到1938年，北壁才第一次被成功攀登。1894年，實業家阿道夫·古耶－策勒取得鐵路特許。他要把鐵路一路修到少女峰底下的鞍部。1896年開工，鐵路大部分在隧道裡，穿過艾格峰和僧侶峰。隧道能避開風雪與嚴寒。1903年，艾格北壁車站啟用；1905年，冰海車站啟用。乘客可以從岩壁上的窗口，看到外面的冰河。

古耶－策勒在1899年去世，沒有看到鐵路完工。工程又持續了十多年。1912年，鐵路通到少女峰車站，前後花了十六年。車站海拔3,454公尺，是歐洲最高的火車站。1937年，鞍部上方的斯芬克斯觀測站啟用。科學家在這裡研究高空的大氣與天氣。1997年，年遊客首次超過五十萬人次。2000年6月1日，單日紀錄達到8,148人。1960年，伯恩州開始保護勞特布魯嫩谷的上游。保護山區，比世界遺產的登錄更早開始。

2001年，少女峰－阿萊奇列入世界遺產。它是阿爾卑斯山的第一處世界自然遺產。2007年，範圍擴大到約824平方公里。其中56%在瓦萊州，44%在伯恩州。同一段時間，阿萊奇冰河持續後退。1860年時，冰河比現在長三公里，冰面高出兩百公尺。從第一次登頂、穿山的鐵路，到世界遺產，。人們用兩百年，走近了這片冰的國度。

In 1811 the Jungfrau was climbed for the first time. The Finsteraarhorn followed a year later. The Eiger, 3,967 metres, has one of the hardest walls in the Alps on its north side. The north face was not climbed until 1938. In 1894 the industrialist Adolf Guyer-Zeller won a concession for a railway to climb all the way to the saddle beneath the Jungfrau. Work began in 1896. Most of the line runs in a tunnel through the Eiger and the Mönch, sheltered from snow and cold. Eigerwand station opened in 1903, Eismeer station in 1905. Through windows in the rock, passengers looked out on the ice.

Guyer-Zeller died in 1899 and never saw it finished. The work went on for more than a decade. In 1912, sixteen years after work began, the line reached the Jungfraujoch. At 3,454 metres it is the highest railway station in Europe. In 1937 the Sphinx Observatory opened above the saddle, where scientists study the high atmosphere and weather. In 1997 annual visitors passed half a million for the first time. On 1 June 2000 a record 8,148 people came in a single day. In 1960 the canton of Bern took the upper Lauterbrunnen valley under protection, long before the World Heritage listing.

In 2001 Jungfrau-Aletsch joined the World Heritage List, the first natural World Heritage site in the Alps. In 2007 it was enlarged to about 824 square kilometres, 56 per cent in Valais and 44 in Bern. Through all those years the Aletsch kept retreating. In 1860 it was 3 km longer, its surface 200 metres higher. From the first ascents and a railway through the mountain to World Heritage, people have spent two centuries coming closer to this kingdom of ice.

人文 Culture

山谷與山峰之間

Between the Valleys and the Peaks

歌德筆下的瀑布、山中的鐵路，與冰河旁的村莊
Goethe's waterfall, a railway in the mountain and villages beside the ice

勞特布魯嫩谷的施陶巴赫瀑布，從將近三百公尺高的崖頂落下。水在半空中散成水霧。1779年，詩人歌德來到這裡。這道瀑布啟發他寫下〈水上精靈之歌〉。少女峰、僧侶峰和艾格峰，是瑞士最熟悉的山景之一。三座山的名字，常被人一起提起。艾格北壁的攀登史，寫成了許多書和電影。山下的村莊，用望遠鏡關注著每一次挑戰。少女峰鐵路被宣傳為「歐洲之巔」。火車在山體裡爬升，乘客從窗口看到冰河。

冰海車站海拔3,158公尺。鐵路採用一公尺軌距，靠齒軌爬上陡坡。格林德瓦是登山與旅遊的老鎮。從十九世紀起，就有旅人來看冰河。當年的冰河伸到村莊附近。今天，要走得更遠，才看得到冰。阿萊奇冰河東側的森林，長在古老的冰磧上。從樹林間，可以看到冰河留下的痕跡。馬薩峽谷的河水被用來發電。冰河融水，也是山谷居民的資源。

梅耶倫湖靠近貝特默阿爾卑村，是健行路線上的景點。步道沿著冰河邊緣延伸。遺產區橫跨瓦萊州和伯恩州。一邊說法語和德語的瓦萊，一邊是說德語的伯恩。從歌德的詩、穿山的火車，到冰河邊的步道，。人們一直在這些山谷裡，找到與山相處的方式。

Lauterbrunnen's Staubbach Falls drops from a cliff nearly 300 metres high, breaking into spray in mid-air. In 1779 the poet Goethe came here, and the falls inspired his "Song of the Spirits over the Waters". Jungfrau, Mönch and Eiger make one of the most familiar views in Switzerland. Their three names are often spoken together. The story of the Eiger's north face has filled many books and films. Villages below have followed each attempt through telescopes. The Jungfrau Railway is billed as the way to the Top of Europe. The train climbs inside the mountain; passengers glimpse glaciers through windows.

Eismeer station lies at 3,158 metres. The metre-gauge line climbs its steep grades on a rack. Grindelwald is an old town of climbers and travellers. Visitors have come to see its glaciers since the 19th century. Back then the glaciers reached close to the village. Today you must go much further to see the ice. East of the glacier, the Aletsch Forest grows on old moraines, and the traces left by the ice can be seen between the trees. The water of the Massa gorge is used to make electricity. Glacier melt is a resource for the valleys too.

Märjelensee, near Bettmeralp, is a stop on the hiking trails that run along the edge of the ice. The site spans the cantons of Valais and Bern: bilingual Valais on one side, German-speaking Bern on the other. From Goethe's poem to a train through the mountain and trails beside the ice, people have always found ways to live alongside these mountains.

自然 Nature

冰河退去之後

After the Ice Retreats

阿萊奇森林、冰河湖、懸谷瀑布，與正在消失的冰
The Aletsch Forest, glacier lakes, hanging falls and vanishing ice

冰河後退，露出大片裸露的岩石與碎石。植物從冰河邊緣開始，一步步長回來。冰河東側的阿萊奇森林，長在冰磧上。冰河在不同年代留下的堤岸，現在都覆蓋著樹。愈靠近冰河，樹愈年輕。從樹齡，就能讀出冰河後退的速度。冰面上的碎石帶，是兩條冰流交會留下的中磧。它們像高速公路的分隔線，一路延伸到下游。從正上方看，中磧把冰河分成幾條平行的帶子。每一條帶子，來自上游不同的冰原。

梅耶倫湖海拔2,301公尺，緊鄰冰河。冰河和湖，是相互影響的鄰居。冰河融水，在山裡匯成急流。特呂默爾巴赫瀑布，就是冰河融水在岩石裡切出的水道。勞特布魯嫩谷的瀑布，從冰河留下的懸谷落下。支流的谷底比主谷高，水只能直落而下。海拔四千公尺的山頂，終年積雪。雪壓成冰，供給下方的冰河。斯芬克斯觀測站監測高山的空氣。冰河的變化，也是氣候變化的紀錄。

格林德瓦的冰河，也退向山谷上方。冰退去的地方，留下新的岩壁和碎石坡。即使在後退，阿萊奇冰河在協和廣場仍有九百多公尺厚。這裡的冰，要很多年才會流到冰河末端。冰河退去，森林跟著長上來；。這片山，正在我們眼前改變模樣。

As the glacier retreats it lays bare wide stretches of rock and rubble. Plants return step by step from the edge of the ice. The Aletsch Forest on the east side grows on moraines, banks the ice left at different times, now covered in trees. The closer to the ice, the younger the trees. Their ages record how fast the glacier has withdrawn. The rubble bands on the ice are medial moraines, left where ice streams merged. Like lane markings, they run all the way downstream. From above, the medial moraines divide the glacier into parallel bands, each coming from a different snowfield upstream.

Märjelensee, at 2,301 metres, lies right beside the ice. Glacier and lake are neighbours that shape each other. Glacier melt gathers into torrents inside the mountain. Trümmelbach Falls is a channel the meltwater has cut through the rock. Lauterbrunnen's waterfalls drop from hanging valleys the glaciers left. The side valleys sit higher than the main one, so the water simply falls. Summits at 4,000 metres hold snow all year. Pressed into ice, it feeds the glaciers below. The Sphinx Observatory monitors the mountain air. The changing glaciers are a record of a changing climate.

Grindelwald's glaciers are drawing back up their valleys, leaving new rock walls and slopes of scree. Even in retreat, the Aletsch is still over 900 metres thick at Konkordiaplatz. Ice here takes many years to travel down to the tip. The ice withdraws and the forest follows it up the valley; these mountains are changing shape before our eyes.

地理 Geography

阿爾卑斯最大的冰

The Largest Ice in the Alps

三萬五千公頃的冰河、四條冰流交會的協和廣場，與阿萊奇冰河
35,000 ha of glaciers, the meeting of four ice streams, and the Aletsch

瑞士阿爾卑斯的少女峰－阿萊奇地區，有大片相連的冰河。冰河面積約三萬五千公頃，是阿爾卑斯山最大的連續冰區。協和廣場是四條冰流交會的地方。冰在這裡的厚度，量到過905公尺。從西北方來的是少女峰冰原，從北方來的是永雪原。兩側的冰原，也一起匯入。匯合後的冰流，就是阿萊奇冰河。它長約二十三公里，是阿爾卑斯山最長的冰河。冰河面積約82平方公里，冰量約15立方公里。冰面上的深色條紋，是兩側岩壁落下的碎石。

冰河的末端，融水匯成馬薩河。河水穿過馬薩峽谷，最後在布里格附近流進隆河。1870年以來，冰河已經後退了3.2公里。1980年以後，就退了1.3公里。到2016年，冰層變薄了三百多公尺。2006年一年，冰河末端就退了114.6公尺。冰河東側的梅耶倫湖，位在海拔2,301公尺。它是一座依偎在冰河邊的湖。芬斯特拉峰海拔4,270公尺，是這片遺產區的最高峰。區內還有八座山峰超過四千公尺。

少女峰海拔4,158公尺。它和僧侶峰、艾格峰，組成伯恩高地的天際線。北邊的勞特布魯嫩谷，是冰河切出的U形谷。陡峭的谷壁上，掛著一道道瀑布。特呂默爾巴赫瀑布藏在山體裡面。它收集了艾格、僧侶與少女峰的融水。東北邊的格林德瓦，也有冰河伸向山谷。這些冰河同樣在後退。四條冰流、二十三公里的冰河、一道道懸谷瀑布，。阿爾卑斯的地形，是冰河在數十萬年間雕出來的。

The Jungfrau-Aletsch region of the Swiss Alps is a sheet of connected glaciers. About 35,000 hectares of ice make it the largest continuous ice area in the Alps. Konkordiaplatz is where four streams of ice meet. The ice here has been measured at 905 metres thick. The Jungfraufirn comes in from the north-west, the Ewigschneefäld from the north, and more firn joins from either side. Together they become the Aletsch Glacier, about 23 kilometres long, the longest in the Alps. It covers about 82 square kilometres and holds some 15 cubic kilometres of ice. The dark stripes on its surface are rock fallen from the valley walls.

At the glacier's tip, meltwater gathers into the Massa. It runs through the Massa gorge and joins the Rhône near Brig. Since 1870 the glacier has retreated 3.2 kilometres, 1.3 kilometres of it since 1980. By 2016 the ice had thinned by more than 300 metres. In 2006 alone the tip drew back 114.6 metres. On the east side lies Märjelensee, at 2,301 metres, a lake cradled at the edge of the ice. The Finsteraarhorn, 4,270 metres, is the highest summit of the site. Eight more peaks here rise above 4,000 metres.

The Jungfrau stands 4,158 metres high. With the Mönch and the Eiger it forms the skyline of the Bernese Oberland. To the north, the Lauterbrunnen valley is a U-shaped trough cut by glaciers. Waterfalls hang from its sheer walls. Trümmelbach Falls is hidden inside the mountain, carrying meltwater from the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau. To the north-east, glaciers reach down towards Grindelwald. They too are retreating. Four streams of ice, a 23-kilometre glacier, waterfalls in hanging valleys: the Alps were carved by ice over hundreds of thousands of years.
