歷史 History

一百年的湖：從水力工事到國家風景區

A Century on the Lake: From Hydro Project to National Scenic Area

發電、遷廟、建塔與震後重建
Power works, relocated temples, a pagoda and rebuilding after the quake

在 YouTube 觀看 Watch on YouTube在模擬器飛這段 Fly this tour

旁白全文 Full narration

從水社碼頭望出去，湖面看似亙古不變。其實過去一百年，這面湖被改造了好幾次。清代文獻把湖中的小島記作珠嶼、珠山。道光以前，邵族聚落分布在水社、石印、頭社等地。1916年，涵碧半島的湖岸建起涵碧樓。同一時期輕便車道通行，日月潭成為知名的遊覽地。1919年，臺灣電力株式會社成立，水力發電工程動工。計畫引濁水溪的水入湖，再利用高度落差發電。工程一度因經費不足停擺，1931年發行外債後復工。1934年竣工，武界的取水壩也在這一年完成。

山下水里溪谷的第一發電所，發電量十萬瓩。這項工程當時號稱東洋第一，電廠在1946年改名大觀。為工程運輸而修築的鐵路，就是今天的集集線。1937年，下游的第二發電所完工，發電量43500瓩。湖水上升，湖畔的龍鳳宮與益化堂必須遷走。兩廟合資遷建，1938年在松柏崙落成，就是文武廟。現在的廟宇是1969年重建的北朝式建築。正殿高21公尺，三進殿宇沿山坡層層升高。工程竣工的1934年，邵族人遷到今天的伊達邵。這個聚落舊稱德化社。

日治時期稱為玉島的小島，1946年改名光華島。戰後，涵碧樓成為接待各國賓客的場所。1955年，玄奘大師頂骨舍利迎來臺灣，其後安奉在玄光寺。1965年11月玄奘寺落成，舍利移奉山上。慈恩塔1969年動工、1971年完工，高46公尺。建材先用船運過湖面，再以流籠送上山頂。塔由蔣中正為紀念母親而建，塔頂恰在海拔1000公尺。1985年8月，臺灣第一座抽蓄電廠明湖電廠完工。其後的明潭抽蓄電廠，總裝置容量約160萬瓩。

1999年9月21日的大地震重創日月潭周邊。島上的亭子震毀，邵族住屋約八成受損或倒塌。2000年1月24日，日月潭國家風景區管理處成立。同年，湖心小島恢復邵族的名字拉魯。2009年12月28日，通往九族文化村的纜車開始營運。2011年2月，向山遊客中心啟用。發電、遷村、建寺、地震與重建，都留在這道湖岸上。今天看到的日月潭，是一百年歷史疊出來的樣子。

From Shuishe Pier the lake looks as if it has always been this way. In fact, over the past century it has been reshaped several times. Qing-era records call the island in the lake Zhuyu or Zhushan, the pearl isle. Before the Daoguang era, Thao villages stood at Shuishe, Shiyin, Toushe and other sites. In 1916 the Hanbilou lodge was built on the shore of the Hanbi peninsula. Push-car tracks opened around the same time, and the lake became a known excursion. In 1919 the Taiwan Electric Power Company was founded and the hydro project began. The plan: lead Zhuoshui River water into the lake, then use the drop to generate power. The work stalled for lack of funds, and resumed in 1931 after foreign bonds were issued. It was completed in 1934, the same year the intake dam at Wujie was finished.

Down in the Shuili valley, the No. 1 power station generated 100,000 kW. The project was billed as the greatest in the Orient; the plant was renamed Daguan in 1946. The railway built to serve the construction is today's Jiji Line. In 1937 a second station downstream was completed, rated at 43,500 kW. As the lake rose, the lakeside Longfeng Temple and Yihua Hall had to move. They pooled their compensation and rebuilt together here by 1938, as Wenwu Temple. The present temple was rebuilt from 1969 in a northern-dynasties palace style. Its main hall is 21 m tall, and three halls step up the hillside. In 1934, the year the works were finished, the Thao moved to today's Ita Thao. The settlement was formerly known as Dehua Village.

The islet, called Jade Island under Japanese rule, was renamed Guanghua Island in 1946. After the war, Hanbilou became a guesthouse for visiting dignitaries. In 1955 a skull relic of the monk Xuanzang came to Taiwan, and was kept at Xuanguang Temple. When Xuanzang Temple was completed in November 1965, the relic moved uphill. Ci'en Pagoda was begun in 1969 and finished in 1971; it is 46 m tall. Materials crossed the lake by boat and were hauled to the summit by cableway. Chiang Kai-shek built it in memory of his mother; its tip stands exactly 1,000 m above sea level. In August 1985 Minghu, Taiwan's first pumped-storage plant, was completed. The later Mingtan pumped-storage plant has about 1,600 MW installed.

The earthquake of 21 September 1999 struck the lake area hard. The pavilion on the island was destroyed, and about 80% of Thao houses were damaged or lost. On 24 January 2000 the Sun Moon Lake National Scenic Area Administration was founded. That same year the island regained its Thao name, Lalu. On 28 December 2009 the ropeway to the Formosan Aboriginal Culture Village began service. In February 2011 the Xiangshan Visitor Center opened. Power works, relocation, temples, an earthquake and rebuilding have all left marks on this shore. The lake seen today is the layered result of a hundred years.

人文 Culture

湖邊的人：邵族、信仰與環湖日常

People of the Lake: The Thao, Faith and Life on the Shore

伊達邵的祭儀、湖上的泳渡與環潭單車
Ita Thao's rites, the mass swim and the lakeside cycle route

日月潭東南岸的伊達邵，是邵族文化的核心地帶。住在湖邊的人，怎麼看待這面湖。邵族是日月潭最早的居民，族名來自邵語的「人」。2001年8月15日，正式認定為臺灣原住民族第十族。從上方看，商圈的主要街道呈三豎一橫。邵語稱這個聚落為Barawbaw。相傳祖先追逐白鹿來到土亭仔，白鹿躍入湖中。族人因此發現這面湖，舉族遷來定居。這裡是石印社舊址，入口的貓頭鷹石雕象徵守護山林。

拉魯島是邵族最高祖靈的居所，一般人不能登島。島上種植的茄苳樹，象徵族群文化代代延續。農曆三月有播種祭，七月有狩獵祭與白鰻祭。八月初一的祖靈祭Lus'an，是邵族的新年。狩獵祭由女祭司主持，向族人的祖靈籃獻祭。碼頭廣場定時有杵音表演，原是新年祭儀的一部分。北岸的文武廟，前殿供奉文昌帝君。中殿祀關聖帝君與岳武穆王，後殿祀孔子。玄奘寺是仿唐式建築，紀念唐代高僧玄奘。他赴印度取經十七年，帶回佛經657部。

每年中秋前後的萬人泳渡，從朝霧碼頭下水。全程約3.3公里，終點是伊達邵碼頭；活動始於1983年。平日，遊艇往返水社、玄光寺與伊達邵三座碼頭。遇到颱風或濃霧，碼頭會暫時關閉。環潭自行車道一圈約30公里。水社到向山這段長約3.4公里，其中約400公尺架在水面上。永結、同心兩座橋專供自行車與行人通行，白色橋墩配木棧橋面。向山遊客中心由日本建築師團紀彥設計，採清水混凝土。兩片屋頂各長34公尺、高8公尺。

纜車全長約1.877公里，越過卜吉山到九族文化村。86個車廂分為紅、黃、藍三色，代表日、月、潭。貓囒山的茶園裡，留有日治時期的製茶工廠。步道口立著被稱為臺灣紅茶之父的新井耕吉郎像。祖靈的島、遷建的廟、泳渡與單車的路線，都繞著同一面湖。對湖邊的人來說，日月潭是家，也是信仰的所在。

Ita Thao, on the south-east shore, is the heart of Thao culture. How do the people who live beside this lake regard it? The Thao are the lake's earliest residents; their name comes from their word for 'person'. On 15 August 2001 they were officially recognised as Taiwan's tenth indigenous people. From above, the main streets form three uprights and one crossbar. In the Thao language the settlement is called Barawbaw. Tradition says the ancestors chased a white deer to Puzi, where it leapt into the lake. So the people found the lake, and moved here to settle. This is the old Shiyin village site; an owl carving at the entrance stands for guarding the forest.

Lalu Island is home to the Thao's highest ancestral spirits; visitors may not land. The bishop wood trees planted there stand for the culture continuing through generations. The sowing rite falls in the third lunar month; hunting and white-eel rites in the seventh. Lus'an, the ancestral-spirit festival on the first day of the eighth month, is the Thao new year. Women priests lead the hunting rite, with offerings to the ancestral-spirit baskets. Pestle music, once part of the new-year rites, is performed on the pier plaza. At Wenwu Temple on the north shore, the front hall enshrines Wenchang, god of letters. The middle hall honours Guan Yu and Yue Fei; the rear hall, Confucius. Xuanzang Temple, in Tang style, honours the Tang-dynasty monk Xuanzang. He spent seventeen years on his journey to India and brought back 657 scriptures.

Each year around Mid-Autumn, the mass swim sets off from Chaowu Pier. The course is about 3.3 km, ending at Ita Thao Pier; the event began in 1983. On ordinary days boats link the Shuishe, Xuanguang and Ita Thao piers. In typhoons or dense fog the piers close for a time. The cycle route around the lake is about 30 km. The Shuishe-Xiangshan section is about 3.4 km, some 400 m of it built over the water. The Yongjie and Tongxin bridges, white piers with timber decks, carry only cyclists and walkers. Xiangshan Visitor Center, by Japanese architect Norihiko Dan, is built in exposed concrete. Its two roof spans are each 34 m long and 8 m high.

The ropeway runs about 1.877 km over Mount Buji to the Formosan Aboriginal Culture Village. Its 86 cabins are red, yellow and blue, standing for sun, moon and lake. Among the tea gardens of Maolan Mountain stands a Japanese-era tea factory. At the trailhead is a statue of Arai Kokichiro, called the father of Taiwanese black tea. The spirits' island, the temples, the swim course and the cycle route all circle one lake. For those who live here, Sun Moon Lake is both home and a place of belief.

自然 Nature

霧、森林與湖水：日月潭的生態

Mist, Forest and Water: The Living Lake

從晨霧到螢火，一座山間湖泊的生物相
From morning mist to fireflies: life around a mountain lake

旁白全文 Full narration

清晨的水社，湖面常籠罩一層薄霧。氣候、地形與水文共同造成的朝霧，是日月潭八景之一。日月潭位在中低海拔，雨量豐沛、濕度高。溫和潮濕的環境，孕育出豐富的生態。環湖的山坡因為人為開墾，多半是人造林或次生林。只有山頭與溪谷還留著少量原始植生。林相包括天然闊葉林、混合林、竹林和柳杉造林地。土亭仔步道的樹蔭裡，有五色鳥、樹鵲與繡眼畫眉。終年潮濕多雨，蕨類的種類與數量都很多。水蛙頭步道穿過孟宗竹林，木荷在夏天開出白花。

林下還有蜥蜴與蛇，蜥蜴吃小昆蟲，蛇捕食蛙類和鼠類。湖裡調查到約33種魚類。最為人熟悉的，是俗稱總統魚的翹嘴鮊，以及奇力魚。草魚、白鰱、鯽魚屬於湖泊型，粗首鱲等則屬溪流型。違規放生帶來外來魚種，衝擊原生魚類。環湖聚落的污水送進日月、水社兩座處理廠。處理達標後才排回水庫。大竹湖因淤積長出大片蘆葦，成為魚蝦與水鳥的棲地。這裡有鷺鷥、夜鷺和翠鳥，候鳥季還有成群的雁鴨。

月潭這一側的自行車道旁，常見大冠鷲在空中盤旋。調查記錄的鳥類約有12科37種。每年5月到9月是賞蝶的季節，區內有一百多種蝴蝶。青龍山步道沿途種著含笑、夜合花與七里香。山稜另一側的九族文化村，有新八景之一的櫻花湖。櫻花季大約從1月中旬到3月上旬。貓囒山頂設有中央氣象署的日月潭氣象站。步道穿過茶園與山林，沿途也是看日出與雲霧的地點。每年4月到5月是螢火蟲的季節。水社、頭社、大林等地可以看到黑翅螢等螢火蟲發光。

青蛙與蟾蜍多在夜間活動。入夜後，環潭的水邊、溝渠與竹林都聽得到蛙鳴。夜間，環潭公路的路燈下常聚集獨角仙、鍬形蟲等甲蟲。晨霧、次生林、蘆葦濕地與受管理的湖水，構成今天的生態。它不是原始的自然，卻仍是許多生物的棲地。

At dawn a thin mist often lies over the water at Shuishe. Shaped by climate, terrain and hydrology, the morning mist is one of the lake's eight views. The lake lies at low-to-middle elevation, with ample rain and high humidity. This mild, damp setting supports a rich ecology. Because of past clearing, the slopes around the lake are mostly plantation or secondary forest. Only hilltops and stream valleys keep a little of the original vegetation. The cover includes natural broadleaf forest, mixed forest, bamboo and Japanese cedar plantations. In the shade of the Puzi trail live Taiwan barbets, grey treepies and Morrison's fulvettas. With damp, rainy weather all year, ferns are many in kind and number. The Shuiwatou trail passes moso bamboo; the Schima trees along it flower white in summer.

The undergrowth also shelters lizards that eat small insects, and snakes that hunt frogs and rodents. Surveys have recorded about 33 fish species in the lake. Best known are the topmouth culter, nicknamed 'president fish', and the sharpbelly. Grass carp, silver carp and crucian carp are lake types; others are stream types. Illegally released alien fish put pressure on the native species. Wastewater from the lakeside settlements goes to the Riyue and Shuishe treatment plants. Only after meeting standards is it returned to the reservoir. Silt at Dazhuhu has grown into reed beds, a habitat for fish, shrimp and waterbirds. Egrets, night herons and kingfishers live here; wild ducks gather in the migration season.

Along the cycle path on the Moon side, crested serpent eagles often circle overhead. Surveys list about 37 bird species in 12 families. May to September is butterfly season; over a hundred species occur here. Along the Qinglong trail grow banana shrub, coconut magnolia and orange jasmine. Beyond the ridge, the Formosan Aboriginal Culture Village holds a cherry-blossom lake. The blossom season runs from about mid-January to early March. On the summit of Maolan Mountain stands the Sun Moon Lake weather station. The trail there crosses tea gardens and woods, a place to watch sunrise and cloud. April and May are firefly season. At Shuishe, Toushe and Dalin, the black-winged firefly and other species light up the dusk.

Frogs and toads are mostly active at night. After dark their calls rise from the shore, ditches and bamboo around the lake. At night, rhinoceros beetles and stag beetles gather under the street lamps of the lake road. Morning mist, secondary forest, reed wetland and managed water make up today's ecology. It is not untouched nature, yet it remains home to many living things.

地理 Geography

山裡的水盆：日月潭如何成為大湖

A Basin in the Hills: How Sun Moon Lake Became a Large Lake

陷落盆地、引水隧道與兩道土壩
A subsidence basin, a diversion tunnel and two earth dams

旁白全文 Full narration

南投縣魚池鄉的山間，一面湖水停在海拔約748公尺。這麼高的地方，水從哪裡來，又為什麼留得住。這裡屬於埔里盆地群，地殼褶曲、陷落後形成山間盆地。盆地蓄了水，就成為湖沼。鄰近的頭社、魚池、埔里，原本也是積水的盆地。後來因淤填或決堤而乾涸，成了肥沃的盆地平原。從正上方看，拉魯島像湖心的一枚界標。一側湖面圓如日輪，另一側彎如月鉤，湖名由此而來。玄光寺所在的半島伸向湖心，位在日潭與月潭的交界。寺前離水面只有十餘公尺。

發電工程以前，湖面海拔726.8公尺，面積4.55平方公里。最深處只有6公尺；拉魯島是後來被湖水圍住的山丘。濁水溪並不直接流進日月潭。工程在東北方的武界築壩取水，引水路全長15.12公里。八座隧道合計約13公里，穿過軟弱的板岩山體。出口在大竹湖，湧出的水被稱為日月湧泉。要把水留住，得堵住湖水原本溢流的缺口。水社壩高28.9公尺、長372公尺，擋在通往水尾溪的出口。月潭南岸的頭社壩高18.8公尺、長166公尺。兩座都是土壩，中央有鋼筋混凝土心牆防滲。

兩壩完成後，水位上升18.18公尺。湖面擴大為原來的約1.7倍，蓄水量約為6.72倍。今天的滿水位是海拔748.48公尺，最深約27公尺。有效蓄水量約1.24億立方公尺。湖的西側山後，地勢陡降到水里溪谷。湖水經壓力鋼管落下約320公尺，推動水輪機發電。水里溪上的水庫是抽蓄發電的下池，日月潭是上池。深夜用離峰電力把水抽回湖裡，尖峰時再放水發電。水蛙頭岸邊的九隻疊蛙，標出745.90到748.48公尺的水位。抽蓄發電讓水位一天內可升降近2公尺。

環湖的山稜比湖面高出兩三百公尺。北側的貓囒山約1020公尺，南側的沙巴蘭山954公尺。天然的陷落盆地，加上引水隧道與兩道土壩。日月潭是地形與工程共同造就的湖。

In the hills of Yuchi, Nantou, a lake rests about 748 m above sea level. At this height, where does the water come from, and what keeps it here? This is part of the Puli basin group, hollows formed where the crust folded and subsided. Where a basin held water, it became a lake. Nearby Toushe, Yuchi and Puli were once water-filled basins too. They later silted up or breached and dried out, becoming fertile basin plains. From directly above, Lalu Island works like a boundary marker in mid-lake. One side is round like the sun, the other curved like a crescent moon: hence the name. The peninsula of Xuanguang Temple reaches toward mid-lake, where the Sun and Moon sides meet. The temple stands only a dozen or so metres from the water.

Before the hydro works the surface lay at 726.8 m and covered 4.55 square kilometres. It was at most 6 m deep; Lalu was a hill that the rising water later surrounded. The Zhuoshui River does not flow into the lake directly. A dam at Wujie to the north-east diverts it along a 15.12 km waterway. Eight tunnels, about 13 km in all, pass through weak slate mountains. The outlet is at Dazhuhu, where the upwelling is called the Riyue Spring. To hold the water, the gaps where the lake once overflowed had to be closed. Shuishe Dam, 28.9 m high and 372 m long, blocks the old overflow to Shuiwei Creek. On the south shore of the Moon side, Toushe Dam is 18.8 m high and 166 m long. Both are earth dams with a reinforced-concrete core wall to stop seepage.

With both dams finished, the water level rose 18.18 m. The surface grew to about 1.7 times its former size, and storage to about 6.72 times. Today the full water level is 748.48 m, with a maximum depth of about 27 m. Effective storage is about 124 million cubic metres. Behind the western ridge the land drops steeply to the Shuili valley. Lake water falls about 320 m through penstocks to drive the turbines. Reservoirs on the Shuili River are the lower pools of pumped storage; the lake is the upper pool. Off-peak power pumps water back up at night; at peak hours it runs down again. Nine stacked frog statues at Shuiwatou mark water levels from 745.90 to 748.48 m. Pumped storage can move the lake level by nearly 2 m within a day.

The enclosing ridges stand two to three hundred metres above the water. Maolan Mountain to the north is about 1,020 m; Shabalan Mountain to the south, 954 m. A natural subsidence basin, plus a diversion tunnel and two earth dams. Sun Moon Lake is a lake made jointly by landform and engineering.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

日月潭國家風景區管理處公告朝霧、水社、伊達邵、玄光四座碼頭與水社壩、向山遊客中心、婚紗廣場等7處禁止擅自操作遙控無人機，纜車站、九族文化村及電廠進出水口等另由其他單位列為禁飛區。其餘開放的綠區依民用航空法規飛行，起飛前請用民航局「Drone Map」App或遙控無人機管理資訊系統查詢。

The Sun Moon Lake National Scenic Area Administration prohibits unauthorised drone flights at seven sites: the Chaowu, Shuishe, Ita Thao and Xuanguang piers, Shuishe Dam, Xiangshan Visitor Center and the Wedding Plaza; the ropeway station, the Formosan Aboriginal Culture Village and power-plant intakes and outlets are no-fly zones set by other bodies. Elsewhere, open green zones may be flown under civil aviation rules; check the CAA Drone Map app or drone information system before take-off.
