歷史 History

探險、國界與國家公園

Explorers, a Border and a National Park

從莫雷諾的探險、1902年的國界仲裁，到1937年的國家公園
From Moreno's travels and the 1902 border award to the park of 1937

巴塔哥尼亞南部的草原，原本是特維爾切人的家園。他們在草原上追獵原駝與美洲鴕。十九世紀，探險家開始深入這片土地。法蘭西斯科·莫雷諾，是其中最重要的一位。莫雷諾在十九世紀研究這個地區。他後來協助阿根廷，在1902年的國界仲裁中爭取主張。阿根廷與智利的國界，沿著安地斯山脈劃分。冰原地帶的邊界，一直是兩國談判的難題。1899年，伊格萊西亞斯中尉把這條冰河命名為「佩里托·莫雷諾」。「佩里托」是專家的意思，用來致敬莫雷諾。

在這之前，它曾被叫作法蘭西斯科·戈爾馬斯冰河，。也曾被叫作俾斯麥冰河。1917年，冰河第一次被記錄到堵住里科灣。隨後，也記錄到第一次冰壩崩塌。1937年，這裡成立了冰川國家公園。公園面積約7,270平方公里。烏普薩拉冰河的名字，來自瑞典的烏普薩拉大學。早年研究這裡的考察，得到了這所大學的支持。麥哲倫半島上建起了觀景步道。遊客可以從對岸，近距離看冰壁崩落。

1981年，冰川國家公園列入世界遺產。登錄的理由，是壯麗的景觀與持續進行的冰河作用。冰壩崩塌的時間並不規律。2013年甚至發生了兩次，之後在2016、2018和2019年也都有。2020年起，冰河北側的前緣開始後退。科學家認為，可能和氣候變遷有關。從原住民的草原、探險家的路線，到國界與國家公園，。這片冰的國度，也寫滿了人的歷史。

The steppe of southern Patagonia was the homeland of the Tehuelche. They hunted guanaco and rhea across the plains. In the 19th century explorers began to push into this land. Francisco Moreno was among the most important. Moreno studied the region in the 19th century, and later helped argue Argentina's case in the arbitral award of 1902. The border between Argentina and Chile follows the Andes. Across the ice field it long tested negotiators on both sides. In 1899 Lieutenant Iglesias named the glacier Perito Moreno, "perito" meaning expert, in tribute to Moreno.

Before that it had been called the Francisco Gormaz Glacier, and also the Bismarck Glacier. In 1917 the glacier was first recorded closing off Brazo Rico, followed by the first recorded break of the ice dam. In 1937 Los Glaciares National Park was created. It covers about 7,270 square kilometres. The Upsala Glacier is named after Uppsala University in Sweden, which supported early research here. Walkways were built on the Magallanes Peninsula, letting visitors watch the wall calve from across the water.

In 1981 Los Glaciares joined the World Heritage List, for its scenery and its ongoing glacial processes. The ice-dam ruptures keep no regular schedule. 2013 saw two; others followed in 2016, 2018 and 2019. Since 2020 the glacier's northern front has been retreating, possibly because of climate change. From the Tehuelche steppe and explorers' routes to borders and a national park, this kingdom of ice is full of human history too.

人文 Culture

看冰的人

People Who Come to Watch the Ice

步道、遊船、冰上健行，與整個國家關注的冰橋崩塌
Walkways, boats, ice trekking, and ruptures a nation stops to watch

麥哲倫半島的步道，正對著莫雷諾冰川。冰壁和觀景台之間，隔著一段湖面。遊客在步道上等待冰塊崩落。先是一聲巨響，接著冰塊落水，濺起大浪。遊船沿著冰河北側航行，從水面仰望冰壁。冰壁高出水面七十多公尺，比二十層樓還高。也可以穿上冰爪，走上冰河。短程健行約一個半小時，長程的「大冰」行程約五小時。冰面上有裂隙和融水溝。嚮導會帶著隊伍，避開危險的地方。

冰壩崩塌的時候，許多人會趕來觀看。冰橋何時倒下，沒有人能準確預測。從前估計每三、四年發生一次，實際上間隔差異很大。等待，也成了看冰的一部分。北邊的烏普薩拉冰河與斯佩加齊尼冰河，要搭船才能抵達。湖面上漂著藍色的浮冰。浮冰的藍色，來自緊密的冰會吸收紅光。冰愈緻密，看起來就愈藍。阿根廷湖東岸的小鎮卡拉法特，是旅人的出發點。小鎮的名字，來自當地一種叫卡拉法特的漿果灌木。

當地有一句老話：吃過卡拉法特果的人，總會再回到巴塔哥尼亞。這條冰河的名字，紀念探險家莫雷諾。1903年，他捐出土地，成為阿根廷國家公園制度的起點。旅遊指南形容莫雷諾冰川「天生就是觀光景點」。冰壁就在眼前，又從不重複同樣的樣子。國家公園禁止遊客操作無人機。要看冰河，就在步道上、船上，或走上冰面。從步道、遊船到冰上健行，。人們用不同的方式，靠近這面活著的冰牆。

The walkways on the Magallanes Peninsula face the Perito Moreno Glacier, with a stretch of lake between ice and viewers. Visitors wait on the walkways for the ice to fall. First comes a crack like thunder, then the block hits the water in a burst of spray. Boats run along the glacier's northern flank, looking up at the ice. The wall rises more than 70 metres, taller than a 20-storey building. Or you can strap on crampons and walk on the glacier: a mini-trek of about 90 minutes, or the "Big Ice" route of about five hours. The surface is cut by crevasses and meltwater channels. Guides lead groups around the dangerous places.

When the ice dam is about to break, crowds gather to watch. No one can say exactly when the bridge will fall. It was once reckoned to happen every three or four years; in fact the gaps vary widely. Waiting is part of watching the ice. The Upsala and Spegazzini glaciers to the north can be reached only by boat. Blue icebergs drift on the lake. The blue of the icebergs comes from dense ice absorbing red light. The denser the ice, the bluer it looks. The town of El Calafate, east of the lake, is where travellers set out from. It is named after the calafate, a local berry shrub.

A local saying goes that whoever eats the calafate berry will return to Patagonia. The glacier honours the explorer Francisco Moreno. In 1903 he donated land that became the seed of Argentina's national parks. A guidebook called the Perito Moreno "born to be a tourist attraction". The wall is right in front of you, and never looks the same twice. Visitors may not fly drones in the national park. To see the glacier, use the walkways, a boat or your own feet on the ice. From walkways and boats to treks on the ice, people find their own ways to come close to this living wall.

自然 Nature

冰河邊的生命

Life Beside the Ice

倫加山毛櫸林、草原、安地斯神鷲與瀕危的南安地斯鹿
Lenga forest, steppe, Andean condors and the endangered huemul

莫雷諾冰川長期處於平衡狀態。它增加的冰，和融化崩落的冰差不多。冰河每年6月到12月前進，。12月到隔年4月後退。冰河邊的山坡上，長著倫加山毛櫸森林。這是公園裡最主要的樹種。林中還有矮生的尼雷山毛櫸，以及常綠的金多樹。秋天，倫加的葉子轉成紅色和金色。往東走，森林很快就變成乾燥的草原。耐旱的灌木與科伊隆草，覆蓋著巴塔哥尼亞高原。

公園保護著南安地斯鹿，牠是瀕危的原生鹿種。這裡是阿根廷最南端的南安地斯鹿族群。山谷上空，盤旋著安地斯神鷲。牠展開的翅膀，寬約三公尺。美洲獅是巴塔哥尼亞的頂級掠食者。草原上的原駝，是牠的主要獵物之一。和莫雷諾冰川不同，烏普薩拉冰河一直在後退。後退的冰河，留下大片新的湖面。冰河退去之後，裸露的岩石要很久才會長出植物。生命，跟在冰的後面慢慢前進。

別德馬冰河流進別德馬湖。冰河、湖泊與草原，在這裡緊緊相鄰。崩落的冰塊漂在水道裡，慢慢融化。浮冰讓周圍的湖水保持冰冷。阿根廷湖已有一萬五千多年的歷史。它是冰河時期的冰河，挖出來的湖。高山地帶的年平均氣溫只有7.5度。強勁的西風，把太平洋的水氣帶到冰原上。冰河旁的森林、草原上的原駝、天上的神鷲，。在冰與風之間，生命找到了自己的位置。

For decades the Perito Moreno Glacier stayed in balance, gaining about as much ice as it lost. The glacier advances from June to December and pulls back from December to April. On the slopes beside the glacier grows a forest of lenga beech, the most widespread tree in the park. There is also ñire, a smaller beech, and the evergreen guindo. In autumn the lenga leaves turn red and gold. Go east and the forest soon gives way to dry steppe, where hardy shrubs and coirón grass cover the Patagonian plateau.

The park protects the huemul, an endangered native deer, and holds the southernmost population in Argentina. Andean condors circle above the valleys. Their wings span about three metres. The puma is the top predator of Patagonia, and the guanaco of the steppe one of its main prey. Unlike Perito Moreno, the Upsala Glacier has kept retreating, leaving wide stretches of new open water. Rock laid bare by the ice takes a long time to grow plants. Life follows the retreating ice, slowly.

The Viedma Glacier flows into Lake Viedma. Glacier, lake and steppe lie side by side here. Fallen ice drifts down the channel and slowly melts, keeping the water around it cold. Lago Argentino is more than 15,000 years old, a basin dug by the glaciers of the Ice Age. The high mountain zone averages just 7.5 degrees Celsius a year. Strong westerly winds carry Pacific moisture onto the ice field. Forest beside the glacier, guanacos on the steppe, condors overhead: between ice and wind, life has found its place.

地理 Geography

冰原流下的河

Rivers of Ice from the Ice Field

一萬兩千五百平方公里的冰原、48條大冰河，與阿根廷最大的湖
12,500 km² of ice field, 48 major glaciers and Argentina's largest lake

安地斯山脈南段，覆蓋著南巴塔哥尼亞冰原。冰原面積約一萬兩千五百平方公里，由阿根廷與智利共有。冰原上的雪年年堆積，壓成冰。冰向四周的山谷流出，形成一條條冰河。冰川國家公園裡，有48條主要冰河。另外還有一百多條較小的冰河。最有名的是莫雷諾冰川。它長30公里，面積約250平方公里。冰河的前端寬約五公里，直接伸進湖裡。冰壁平均高出水面74公尺。

水面下還有更深的部分，冰的總厚度約170公尺。冰塊從冰壁崩落，落進湖裡成為浮冰。冰河的南邊是里科灣，北邊是浮冰水道。冰河的前端，把阿根廷湖的這一段隔成兩半。冰河前進時，會頂住對岸，把里科灣堵起來。堵住的水位最多可以比主湖高出三十公尺。最後水壓沖破冰壩，形成冰橋崩塌。突然釋放時，流量可達每秒八千立方公尺。北邊的烏普薩拉冰河，近幾十年明顯後退。冰河後退留下的地方，變成了湖面。

斯佩加齊尼冰河，從陡峭的山谷流下。它也是遊船行程常到的冰河。公園北部的別德馬冰河，面積975平方公里。它是阿根廷最大的冰河。冰河融水，最後流進阿根廷湖。阿根廷湖是阿根廷最大的湖，湖面海拔177公尺。湖水帶著冰河磨出的細粉，呈現乳白的藍綠色。湖的西半部伸進山區，東半部面向乾燥的草原。冰原產生冰河，冰河切出山谷、堵住湖泊，。冰川國家公園，是一座仍在運作的冰的工廠。

The southern Andes carry the Southern Patagonian Ice Field. It covers about 12,500 square kilometres, shared by Argentina and Chile. Snow piles up on it year after year and is pressed into ice. The ice flows out into the valleys as glaciers. Los Glaciares National Park holds 48 major glaciers, and more than a hundred smaller ones. The most famous is the Perito Moreno Glacier. It is 30 kilometres long and covers about 250 square kilometres. Its front is about 5 kilometres wide and runs straight into the lake. The ice wall stands on average 74 metres above the water.

More lies below the surface: the ice is about 170 metres thick in all. Blocks break from the wall and fall into the lake as icebergs. South of the front lies Brazo Rico; north of it, the Canal de los Témpanos. The glacier tip splits this part of Lago Argentino in two. When the glacier advances it presses against the far shore and dams Brazo Rico. The trapped water can rise as much as 30 metres above the main lake. In the end the water breaks through the ice dam, and the ice bridge collapses. At its peak the release can reach 8,000 cubic metres a second. To the north, the Upsala Glacier has retreated markedly in recent decades. Where the ice has pulled back, the lake has taken its place.

The Spegazzini Glacier pours down a steep valley. It too is a regular stop for the boats. In the north of the park, the Viedma Glacier covers 975 square kilometres. It is the largest glacier in Argentina. Meltwater finally drains into Lago Argentino, Argentina's largest lake, 177 metres above sea level. Fine powder ground by the glaciers turns the water a milky blue-green. Its western arms reach into the mountains; its east opens onto dry steppe. The ice field feeds the glaciers; the glaciers carve valleys and dam lakes. Los Glaciares is a factory of ice that is still at work.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

冰川國家公園的官方資訊明載：園內禁止使用無人機。阿根廷的民航規定另由國家民航管理局（ANAC）訂定。實際飛行前請先確認國家公園與阿根廷民航的最新規定。

The official information of Los Glaciares National Park states that drones are forbidden in the park. Civil aviation rules in Argentina are set by ANAC. Check the current park and Argentine aviation rules before any real flight.
