歷史 History

西班牙人沒有找到的城

The City the Spanish Never Found

約1450年的印加莊園、八十年後被遺棄、1911年的賓漢姆，到世界新七大奇蹟
An Inca estate of about 1450, abandoned eighty years on, Bingham in 1911 and a New Wonder of the World

馬丘比丘建在海拔兩千四百三十公尺的山脊上，。在庫斯科西北方約八十公里。它大約建於1450年，。一般認為是印加皇帝帕查庫特克的莊園。帕查庫特克在位於1438到1471年。碳定年顯示，這裡約在1420到1530年間有人居住。住在這裡的大約七百五十人，。多半是服務皇室的僕役。這座莊園只使用了大約八十年，。大約在西班牙征服的時期就被遺棄了。

許多居民，可能在西班牙人到來前就死於天花。西班牙人始終沒有找到這裡，所以它沒有被洗劫。1902年，秘魯探險家利薩拉加，。在三窗之室的牆上，用木炭留下了字跡。1911年，當地的農民兼旅店主人阿爾特亞加說，。他知道華納比丘上有遺跡。7月24日，他帶著美國人賓漢姆登上馬丘比丘。山上有一對克丘亞夫婦，正在古老的梯田上耕種。1912年，耶魯大學與國家地理學會資助了發掘，。1914與1915年又再發掘。

1931年鐵路通車以後，。山下的鐵路工人營地，發展成今天的熱水鎮。1981年，秘魯把這一帶劃為歷史保護區；。1983年列入世界遺產，是文化與自然的雙重遺產。發掘出的文物，很多被運到耶魯大學。2010年雙方達成協議，最後一批在2012年送回秘魯。2007年，馬丘比丘入選世界新七大奇蹟。2024年，遊客超過一百五十萬人。2011年起，遺址每天限兩千五百人；。2026年旺季每天最多五千六百人，其他時間四千五百人。

西班牙人沒有找到的這座莊園，。五百多年後，成了全世界都想來看的地方。

Machu Picchu stands on a mountain ridge 2,430 metres up, about 80 km north-west of Cusco. It was built around 1450 and is thought to have been an estate of the Inca emperor Pachacuti. Pachacuti reigned from 1438 to 1471. Radiocarbon dating shows the site was lived in from about 1420 to 1530. About 750 people lived here, most of them servants of the royal household. The estate was used for only about 80 years, and was abandoned around the time of the Spanish conquest.

Many of its people may have died of smallpox before the conquistadors arrived. The Spanish never found it, so it was never plundered. In 1902 the Peruvian explorer Agustín Lizárraga left a charcoal inscription on the wall of the Room of the Three Windows. In 1911 Melchor Arteaga, a local farmer and innkeeper, said he knew of ruins on Huayna Picchu. On 24 July he led the American Hiram Bingham up to Machu Picchu. At the top a Quechua couple were farming some of the old terraces. In 1912 Yale University and the National Geographic Society sponsored excavations, with more in 1914 and 1915.

After the railway opened in 1931, a railway workers' camp below grew into today's Aguas Calientes. In 1981 Peru declared the area a historic sanctuary; in 1983 it became a World Heritage Site, both cultural and natural. Many excavated artefacts were taken to Yale. An agreement came in 2010, and the last of them returned to Peru in 2012. In 2007 Machu Picchu was named one of the New Seven Wonders of the World. In 2024 it received more than 1.5 million visitors. From 2011 the citadel was limited to 2,500 visitors a day; in 2026 the limit is 5,600 a day in high season and 4,500 at other times.

The estate the Spanish never found has, five centuries on, become a place the whole world wants to see.

人文 Culture

太陽、聖山與石頭

The Sun, Holy Mountains and Stone

拴住太陽的石柱、對準昴宿星團的窗、聖山「阿普」，與不用灰漿的石牆
A stone that ties up the sun, a window on the Pleiades, holy mountains and walls without mortar

在印加人的信仰裡，太陽神叫做「印蒂」。馬丘比丘有不少建築，和太陽與星空有關。遺址分成都市區與農業區，也分成上城與下城；。神廟在上城，倉庫在下城。因蒂瓦塔納石柱，在神聖建築群的最高處。名字的意思，大約是「拴住太陽的地方」。每年11月11日與1月30日的正午，。太陽幾乎就在石柱正上方，照不出影子。半圓形的太陽神殿，建在一塊巨岩上；。岩石下方的石縫，被闢成祭祀印蒂的空間。

神殿這座塔的一扇窗，。對準十五世紀時從這裡看見的昴宿星團。三窗之室的石工非常精細，。這裡也是一處觀察天象的地方。遺址裡還有一塊雕成神鷹形狀的石頭，。可能是用來獻祭的石台。太陽門建在東南方的山脊上，。每年夏至，初升的太陽會從門中穿過。它也是一座要塞，。守著從首都庫斯科過來的主要入口。

當地的克丘亞人，把馬丘比丘、華納比丘和普圖庫西山，。都看作「阿普」，也就是聖山。印加人把石頭看成有生命的東西。蓋房子用的花崗岩，都在當地開採。石塊不用灰漿，靠精準的切割緊緊相扣；。不規則的接縫，反而讓牆更能抵抗地震。遺址有十四處天然泉水，。水由十六道石砌的水道引進城裡。華納比丘的意思是「年輕的山」，。印加人在山頂也蓋了神廟與梯田。

太陽、聖山與石頭，。在這道山脊上被細心地安排在一起。

In Inca belief the sun god was Inti. Several buildings at Machu Picchu are linked to the sun and the stars. The site is divided into urban and farming sectors, and an upper and lower town; the temples are in the upper town, the storehouses in the lower. The Intihuatana stone stands at the highest point of the sacred complex. Its Quechua name means roughly a place to "tie up the sun". At midday on 11 November and 30 January the sun stands almost straight above the pillar, and it casts no shadow. The semicircular Temple of the Sun is built on a great rock; a cleft beneath it was made into a shrine to Inti.

A window in this tower lines up with the Pleiades as they appeared from here in the 15th century. The Room of the Three Windows has very fine stonework, and was also a place for watching the sky. There is also a block of stone carved into the shape of a condor, probably a stone for sacrifices. The Sun Gate stands on the ridge to the south-east; each year at the summer solstice the rising sun shone through it. It was also a fortress, guarding the main approach from the capital, Cusco.

Local Quechua people regard Machu Picchu, Huayna Picchu and Putucusi as apus, holy mountains. The Incas regarded stone as a living thing. The granite for the buildings was quarried on the spot. The blocks were fitted without mortar, cut so precisely that they lock together; their irregular joints make the walls highly resistant to earthquakes. The site had 14 natural springs, their water led through 16 cut-stone channels. Huayna Picchu means "young peak"; the Incas built temples and terraces on its top.

The sun, the holy mountains and the stone were carefully brought together on this ridge.

自然 Nature

雲霧林裡的熊與蘭花

Bears and Orchids in the Cloud Forest

四百二十三種蘭花、四百四十四種鳥、安地斯熊，與二十四種生態系
423 orchid species, 444 bird species, Andean bears and 24 ecosystems

馬丘比丘歷史保護區，在1981年1月8日設立，。它同時保護遺址，和四周的山林。這裡位在安地斯山脈的東坡，。是一片崎嶇的雲霧林。保護區裡一共有二十四種生態系，。從熱帶雨林一直到高山的冰雪。這裡記錄到四百二十三種蘭花，。和三百三十二種樹木。鳥類有四百四十四種，。包括色彩鮮豔的安地斯動冠傘鳥。

哺乳類有七十五種，兩棲類十四種，。爬蟲類二十四種，蝴蝶多達三百七十七種。安地斯熊，克丘亞語叫「烏庫馬里」，。是保護區裡瀕危的物種之一。2014到2015年，研究人員調查了三百六十八平方公里，。在超過九成五的範圍裡，都找到熊的蹤跡。他們找的是糞便、腳印，。還有熊啃食地上鳳梨科植物的痕跡。這裡的熊，透過海拔約三千四百公尺的高山草原，。和更大的熊族群連在一起。

研究也發現，放牧的牛。可能對熊造成威脅。這裡夏天潮濕、冬天乾冷，。大部分的雨落在十月到三月。一年的雨量超過一千八百公釐。遺址的農業梯田，約有四點九公頃。烏魯班巴河往西北，。從保護區的中央流過。遺址四周的雲霧林，。是熊、鳥與蘭花共同的家。

The Historic Sanctuary of Machu Picchu was created on 8 January 1981 to protect both the ruins and the forested mountains around them. It lies on the eastern slope of the Andes, in rugged cloud forest. The sanctuary holds 24 ecosystems, from tropical rainforest up to permanent snow. 423 orchid species have been recorded here, along with 332 kinds of tree. 444 bird species live here, among them the brilliantly coloured Andean cock-of-the-rock.

There are 75 mammal species, 14 amphibians, 24 reptiles and as many as 377 butterflies. The Andean bear, ukumari in Quechua, is one of the endangered species of the sanctuary. From 2014 to 2015 researchers surveyed 368 square kilometres and found signs of bears in more than 95% of it. They looked for scat and footprints, and for signs of bears feeding on ground bromeliads. The bears here are linked to a larger population through montane grasslands at about 3,400 metres.

The study also found that grazing cattle may pose a risk to the bears. Summers here are wet and humid, winters dry and frosty; most rain falls between October and March. Annual rainfall here is over 1,800 mm. The farming terraces of the site cover about 4.9 hectares. The Urubamba River flows north-west through the middle of the sanctuary. The cloud forest around the ruins is a shared home for bears, birds and orchids.

地理 Geography

山脊上的城

A City on the Ridge

兩座山之間的山脊、三面環繞的烏魯班巴河、兩條斷層，與印加古道
A ridge between two peaks, the Urubamba on three sides, two fault lines and the Inca Trail

馬丘比丘的意思是「古老的山」。它坐落在兩座山之間的山脊上。南邊的馬丘比丘山，海拔三千零五十一公尺；。北邊的華納比丘，海拔兩千六百九十三公尺。華納比丘比遺址高出大約兩百六十公尺，。山腰較低處，有月亮神殿與大洞穴。烏魯班巴河從三面繞過這座山，。懸崖直落大約四百五十公尺到河邊。河的東北岸，是圓圓的普圖庫西山，。在華納比丘東方約一點二公里。

山下的熱水鎮海拔兩千零四十公尺，。名字來自鎮上的天然溫泉。這個小鎮沒有公路對外連接，。只能坐火車或走路抵達。從鎮上到遺址大約六公里，。走路大約要一個半小時。遺址蓋在兩條斷層上，灰漿幾乎派不上用場，。所以石塊必須精準地相扣。牆的每一層彼此錯開，。門和窗都做成梯形。

遺址大約有兩百棟建築，。排在梯田上，圍繞著中央的廣場。太陽門海拔約兩千七百四十五公尺，。比遺址高出大約兩百九十公尺。印加古道最長約四十二點五公里，。最高點在海拔四千兩百公尺的沃米瓦努斯卡隘口。古道的終點就是太陽門；。從這裡，第一次能看見整座遺址。遺址西邊的懸崖上有一座印加橋，。建在崖壁上一段六公尺寬的缺口。

保護區的面積約三百二十六平方公里，。遠方的山脈，有超過五千七百公尺的高峰。

Machu Picchu means "old hill". It sits on a ridge between two mountains. To the south Machu Picchu Mountain rises to 3,051 metres; to the north Huayna Picchu reaches 2,693 metres. Huayna Picchu stands about 260 metres above the ruins; lower on its flank are the Temple of the Moon and the Great Cavern. The Urubamba River wraps around the site on three sides, with cliffs dropping about 450 metres to the water. On the north-east bank stands the rounded Putucusi, about 1.2 km east of Huayna Picchu.

Aguas Calientes, below, lies at 2,040 metres; its name, "hot waters", comes from its natural hot springs. The town has no road link to the outside; it is reached by train or on foot. The ruins are about 6 km from the town, roughly a 90-minute walk. The site was built over two fault lines, which made mortar almost useless, so the stones had to fit exactly. The walls are offset row by row, and the doors and windows are trapezoidal.

Some 200 buildings stand on terraces around a central square. The Sun Gate is about 2,745 metres up, some 290 metres above the ruins. The Inca Trail runs for up to about 42.5 km, reaching 4,200 metres at the pass of Warmi Wañusqa. The trail ends at the Sun Gate, the first place from which the whole site comes into view. On the cliff west of the site is the Inca Bridge, built where a 6-metre gap breaks the cliff.

The sanctuary covers about 326 square kilometres; the ranges beyond hold peaks of more than 5,700 metres.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

依馬丘比丘管理單位的第002-99號決議，禁止無人機飛越這處世界遺產；遊客須知也禁止未經官方事先授權攜帶或使用無人機與類似裝置。2019年曾有遊客因在遺址上空飛無人機而被追究。實際飛行前請先確認最新規定。

Resolution 002-99 of the Machu Picchu management unit prohibits drone flights over this World Heritage Site, and the visitor rules ban drones and similar devices without prior official authorisation. In 2019 a visitor faced proceedings for flying a drone over the ruins. Check the current rules before any real flight.
