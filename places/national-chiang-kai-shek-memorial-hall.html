歷史 History

從營區到廣場

From Barracks to Square

一塊市中心土地，百餘年的用途轉換
A century of changing uses on one block of central Taipei

旁白全文 Full narration

臺北市中正區，藍瓦白牆的中正紀念堂。這塊市中心的土地，用途換過好幾次。清代，這裡是臺北城東門外的營地。管理處的沿革記載，定海正營曾駐紮於此。日治時期此地屬旭町，是步兵與山砲部隊的營區。戰後，陸軍總部等機關設在這裡。它一度是臺北市區內最大的營區。1975年4月蔣中正逝世，行政院核定成立籌建小組。同年7月，小組選定這片「營邊段」土地。

公開徵求設計，共收到43件建議書。1976年7月，錄取建築師楊卓成的方案。1976年10月31日破土，舊房舍分區拆除。1977年12月，結構工程正式動工。1979年，牌樓、迴廊與角亭接著開工。1980年3月31日，建堂工程竣工。1980年4月4日舉行落成典禮。隔天4月5日，紀念堂正式開放參觀。1987年，廣場兩側的國家戲劇院與音樂廳落成。紀念園區從此多了表演藝術的功能。

1990年3月16日至22日，近六千名大學生在廣場靜坐。訴求包括解散國民大會、廢除臨時條款。結束前，學生立起自製的「民主野百合」。這場靜坐後來被稱為野百合學運。2007年12月，牌樓題字由「大中至正」改為「自由廣場」。同年11月，園區被指定為國定古蹟。2007年紀念堂一度更名為臺灣民主紀念館。2008年恢復原名，2009年7月匾額重新掛回。中軸上的瞻仰大道，2007年改稱民主大道。2024年7月起，儀隊移出銅像大廳，改在大道上操演。

2017年起，文化部推動紀念堂轉型的社會討論。2022年的公共徵件，共有142組提案參加。2025年，行政院指示朝民主教育園區轉型。空間計畫與社會溝通，目前仍在進行。營區、紀念堂、廣場，同一塊地留下不同年代的痕跡。它的下一個樣貌，仍在公共討論之中。

Zhongzheng District, Taipei: the blue-roofed, white-walled Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall. This block of central Taipei has been put to several different uses. In the Qing era this was camp ground outside the East Gate of walled Taipei. The hall's own history records a garrison, the Dinghai battalion, stationed here. Under Japanese rule the area, then called Asahi-cho, held infantry and mountain-artillery barracks. After 1949 the Army headquarters and related offices occupied the site. For a time it was the largest military compound inside the city. After Chiang Kai-shek died in April 1975, the Executive Yuan approved a planning group. That July the group chose this parcel, known as the Yingbian section, as the site.

An open call for designs brought in 43 proposals. In July 1976 the scheme by the architect Yang Cho-cheng was selected. Ground was broken on 31 October 1976 while the old buildings were cleared in stages. Structural work formally began in December 1977. In 1979 work started on the gates, corridors and corner pavilions. Construction of the hall was completed on 31 March 1980. The inauguration ceremony was held on 4 April 1980. The next day, 5 April, the hall opened to visitors. In 1987 the National Theater and Concert Hall were completed beside the square. The memorial park gained a second role as a home for the performing arts.

From 16 to 22 March 1990 nearly six thousand university students sat in on this square. Their demands included dissolving the National Assembly and ending the Temporary Provisions. Before leaving, the students raised a hand-made "Wild Lily of Democracy". The sit-in became known as the Wild Lily student movement. In December 2007 the inscription on the main gate was changed to "Liberty Square". A month earlier the site had been designated a national monument. In 2007 the hall was renamed National Taiwan Democracy Memorial Hall. The earlier name was restored in 2008 and its plaque re-hung in July 2009. The central avenue, once the Boulevard of Homage, became Democracy Boulevard in 2007. Since July 2024 the honour guard has drilled here instead of inside the statue hall.

Since 2017 the Ministry of Culture has led public discussion on transforming the hall. An open call for ideas in 2022 drew 142 entries. In 2025 the Executive Yuan directed that the site become a park for democracy education. The spatial plan and public consultation are still under way. Barracks, memorial, public square: one block carries the marks of several eras. What it becomes next is still a matter of public discussion.

人文 Culture

廣場上的舞臺

A Stage on the Square

兩廳院、公共廣場與公園裡的日常
Two national halls, a public square and everyday life in the park

旁白全文 Full narration

自由廣場兩側，是兩座宮殿式屋頂的表演廳。這趟飛行看的是廣場上的藝文與日常。南側是國家戲劇院，北側是國家音樂廳。兩廳院在1975年核定興建，1987年完工啟用。設計者是楊卓成與和睦建築師事務所。現代劇場藏在傳統宮殿的外觀裡。戲劇院上演戲劇、舞蹈、京劇與兒童節目。大廳約有一千五百個座位。音樂廳以室內樂與交響樂演出為主，約兩千席。1987年安裝的管風琴，當時是亞洲最大的一座。

兩廳之間的廣場，本身也是一座露天舞臺。演出可以在室內與戶外同時進行。1992年6月，雲門舞集在廣場演出《薪傳》。管理處的紀錄寫著，現場有近六萬名觀眾。2005年，柏林愛樂在音樂廳演出。數千人聚在廣場上，觀看戶外轉播。1990年2月，觀光局首次在這裡舉辦大型燈會。到2000年為止，臺灣燈會都以這裡為會場。主堂體的白牆，也曾被當成巨大的投影幕。1997年，電影《一○一真狗》把它投成黑白斑點。

堂體下層有六間展廳與一間演藝廳。2009年曾在這裡舉辦安迪．沃荷展。廣場也是公眾集會的場所。2008年的野草莓運動等公民行動，都曾在此聚集。沿人行道而建的迴廊，每隔4.5公尺有一扇花窗。其中燈籠窗共有26種造型。清晨與傍晚，附近居民到園區散步運動。園內人行步道總長約五公里。園內有忠勇、親民、自強三處健身場。其中一處就在堂前右側的大榕樹下。

堂內還有一間圖書室，1993年7月開放。紀念建築裡，陸續加入了閱讀與展覽的功能。兩廳院現在隸屬於國家表演藝術中心。國家交響樂團是音樂廳的常客。同一片廣場，容納演出、集會、節慶與每天的運動。它是臺北市民共用的一塊開放空間。

Two performance halls with palace-style roofs stand on either side of Liberty Square. This flight looks at the arts and the everyday life that fill the square. The National Theater is on the south side, the National Concert Hall on the north. Construction was authorised in 1975 and the two halls opened in 1987. They were designed by Yang Cho-cheng with his firm, Architects and Engineers Associates. Modern stages are housed inside forms borrowed from traditional palaces. The Theater stages drama, dance, Peking opera and children's programmes. Its main auditorium seats about fifteen hundred. The Concert Hall, with about two thousand seats, hosts chamber and symphonic music. Its pipe organ, installed in 1987, was then the largest in Asia.

The square between the two halls is itself an open-air stage. Performances can run indoors and outdoors at the same time. In June 1992 Cloud Gate Dance Theatre performed "Legacy" on the square. The hall's records put the audience at nearly sixty thousand. In 2005 the Berliner Philharmoniker under Simon Rattle played in the Concert Hall. Thousands gathered on the square to watch the outdoor broadcast. In February 1990 the Tourism Bureau held its first large lantern festival here. The Taiwan Lantern Festival stayed on this site until 2000. The white walls of the main hall have also served as a giant projection screen. In 1997 a light show for the film "101 Dalmatians" covered them in black spots.

The lower floors of the hall hold six exhibition galleries and a performance hall. An Andy Warhol exhibition was held here in 2009. The square is also a place of public assembly. Civic actions such as the 2008 Wild Strawberry movement have gathered here. The corridor built along the pavements has a window every 4.5 metres. Its lantern-shaped windows come in 26 different designs. Early in the morning and towards evening, nearby residents come to walk and exercise. The park's footpaths add up to about five kilometres. Three exercise grounds, Zhongyong, Qinmin and Ziqiang, serve martial arts, dance and morning drills. One of them lies under a large banyan to the right front of the hall.

Inside there is also a library, opened in July 1993. Reading and exhibitions were gradually added to a building made for commemoration. The two halls now belong to the National Performing Arts Center. The National Symphony Orchestra is among the ensembles that play here regularly. One square holds performances, assemblies, festivals and daily exercise. It is an open space shared by the people of Taipei.

自然 Nature

市中心的水池與樹

Ponds and Trees Downtown

兩座水池、成排的南洋杉，和住在市區的鳥
Two ponds, rows of araucarias and the birds that live in the city

臺北市中心的樓房之間，這座園區是一大片樹冠。除了建築，基地其餘部分都是庭園。東邊一公里半外，是26公頃的大安森林公園。西邊則有收集兩千多種植物的臺北植物園。園區前段，左右各有一座水池。雲漢池在戲劇院旁，光華池在音樂廳旁。兩池都是不規則的人工池，各約三千平方公尺。池裡有假山、瀑布和半圓形的拱橋。雲漢池被拱橋分成上、下兩池。池邊種著緋寒櫻。

池中的錦鯉，最早是1980年開放之初各界捐贈的。1981年又有110尾錦鯉送到這裡。光華池裡有一座長滿水生植物的浮島。2016到2019年，兩池整修了池體與過濾系統。夜鷺和紅冠水雞終年住在池邊。偶爾也能見到白腹秧雞與小白鷺。園內還記錄到黑冠麻鷺、綠繡眼和赤腹松鼠。蝴蝶與蜻蜓也在花木間活動。信義路側的大忠門內，種了八十多株宮粉梅。粉紅色的重瓣花在二到三月開放。

通往東北角門的步道旁，有三十多株山櫻花。1982年，畫家張大千曾贈送12株梅樹。堂體後方，有一條與杭州南路平行的南洋杉步道。樹種以肯氏南洋杉為主，少數是小葉南洋杉。1986年3月，各縣市首長在園內栽下蘇鐵與肯氏南洋杉。步道的泥土被踩得太硬，後來改鋪碎石並換土。步道穿過的虎背生態園區，種的是臺灣低海拔原生植物。植株依北、中、南的自然分布排列。園內的熱帶雨林區，以茂密的榕樹為主體。區內的生態池用來調節水量。

常綠樹之外，還有杜鵑與開黃花的臺灣欒樹。不同季節，樹冠的顏色跟著改變。中軸大道兩側，是如意圖案的草花花壇。依管理處資料，園內大小花木超過五十萬株。水池、林蔭與花季，讓這裡不只是一座紀念建築。它也是市中心的鳥類和居民共用的一片綠地。

Among the buildings of central Taipei, this park shows up as one broad canopy. Apart from its buildings, the whole site is laid out as gardens. A kilometre and a half to the east is the 26-hectare Daan Forest Park. To the west, the Taipei Botanical Garden keeps more than two thousand plant species. Towards the front of the park, a pond lies on each side. Yunhan Pond is beside the Theater, Guanghua Pond beside the Concert Hall. Both are irregular artificial ponds of about three thousand square metres each. Each has rockwork, waterfalls and a half-round arched bridge. An arched bridge divides Yunhan Pond into an upper and a lower pool. Taiwan cherry trees are planted along its banks.

The koi go back to donations made when the park opened in 1980. Another 110 koi arrived as a gift in 1981. Guanghua Pond holds a floating island thick with aquatic plants. Between 2016 and 2019 both ponds were rebuilt and their filtration improved. Black-crowned night herons and common moorhens live by the ponds all year. White-breasted waterhens and little egrets are seen from time to time. Malayan night herons, white-eyes and red-bellied squirrels are also recorded in the park. Butterflies and dragonflies move among the trees and flower beds. Just inside Dazhong Gate on the Xinyi Road side grow more than eighty pink-flowered mei trees. Their double blossoms open in February and March.

Along the path to the northeast corner gate stand some thirty Taiwan cherry trees. In 1982 the painter Chang Dai-chien gave the park twelve mei trees. Behind the hall, an araucaria walk runs parallel to Hangzhou South Road. Most of the trees are hoop pines, with a few Norfolk Island pines. In March 1986 mayors and county chiefs planted cycads and hoop pines in the park. The walk's trampled soil was later loosened with gravel paving and fresh earth. The walk passes an ecology garden planted with native lowland species of Taiwan. They are arranged by their natural ranges in the north, centre and south of the island. A corner called the rainforest area is built around densely growing banyans. A small ecological pond there helps regulate water.

Besides evergreens there are azaleas and yellow-flowered Taiwan golden-rain trees. The colour of the canopy shifts with the seasons. Flower beds in an auspicious ruyi pattern line both sides of the central boulevard. By the management's count the park holds more than half a million trees and flowering plants. Ponds, shade and flowering seasons make this more than a monument. It is also a green block shared by the city's birds and its residents.

地理 Geography

軸線與對稱

Axis and Symmetry

用尺寸讀一座對稱園區的配置
Reading the layout of a symmetric park through its measurements

旁白全文 Full narration

臺北盆地平坦的市區裡，有一塊方正的綠地。中正紀念堂園區約二十五公頃，沿一條中軸對稱展開。往東約四公里，是高508公尺的臺北101。盆地周圍有山，北邊是陽明山，東南是雪山山脈。園區四邊各臨一條道路。西為中山南路，北為信義路，東為杭州南路，南為愛國東路。正面牌樓面向中山南路，高30公尺、寬80公尺。形式是五間六柱十一樓。牌樓距主堂體中心線470公尺。其間的民主大道長380公尺、寬40公尺。

大道兩旁各有一條寬9公尺的林蔭道。路面的花崗石由金門軍民捐獻。中軸上的民主廣場，長寬各120公尺。地下是可停六百多輛車的停車場。兩座表演廳隔著廣場相對，位置互為鏡像。水池、側門與角亭，也都成對配置。主堂體位於基地東側，面向西方。從地面到寶頂，總高約70公尺。三層臺基高14.5公尺，牆身高24公尺。斗拱到寶頂，再加上31.5公尺。

從正上方看，藍色琉璃瓦的屋頂呈八角形。下方的大廳則是40公尺見方。正面石階加上大廳內的階梯，共89階。這個數字對應蔣中正享壽的歲數。白色牆面、藍色屋瓦，花壇種的是紅花。三種顏色呼應國旗的配色。兩座側門分別開在信義路與愛國東路。大忠門在北，大孝門在南，各寬19.7公尺。園區外緣的迴廊高5.5公尺。轉角處的角亭高11.1公尺。

羅斯福路地下的中正紀念堂站，是兩條捷運線的轉乘站。主堂體朝西，視線越過牌樓。一公里多之外，是總統府所在的街區。東北方約五公里是松山機場，跑道長2605公尺。機場四周依法劃有禁止無人機活動的範圍。一條中軸，兩側對稱，四面臨路。從空中看，園區的配置就像一張清楚的平面圖。

On the flat city floor of the Taipei Basin lies a neat rectangle of green. The memorial park covers about 25 hectares, laid out symmetrically along one axis. About four kilometres east rises Taipei 101, 508 metres tall. Mountains ring the basin: Yangmingshan to the north, the Xueshan Range to the southeast. Each of the four sides meets a road. Zhongshan South Road on the west, Xinyi on the north, Hangzhou South on the east, Aiguo East on the south. The main gate faces Zhongshan South Road; it is 30 metres high and 80 metres wide. Its form has five bays, six pillars and eleven roofs. The gate stands 470 metres from the centre line of the main hall. Between them, Democracy Boulevard is 380 metres long and 40 metres wide.

A tree-lined avenue nine metres wide runs along each side of the boulevard. Their granite paving was donated by the soldiers and residents of Kinmen. Democracy Square, on the axis, measures 120 metres on each side. Beneath it is a car park for more than six hundred vehicles. The two performance halls face each other across the square in mirrored positions. Ponds, side gates and corner pavilions also come in matching pairs. The main hall sits at the east end of the site and faces west. From the ground to the tip of its finial it stands about 70 metres high. The three-tiered base is 14.5 metres high and the walls rise another 24. Brackets, roof and finial add the last 31.5 metres.

Seen from directly above, the blue glazed-tile roof is an octagon. The hall beneath it is a square 40 metres on each side. The front stairs, together with the steps inside the hall, total 89. The number matches Chiang Kai-shek's age at his death. White walls, blue roof tiles, and red flowers in the beds around them. The three colours echo those of the national flag. Two side gates open onto Xinyi Road and Aiguo East Road. Dazhong Gate on the north and Daxiao Gate on the south are each 19.7 metres wide. The corridor around the edge of the park is 5.5 metres high. The pavilions at its corners stand 11.1 metres high.

Under Roosevelt Road, the metro station named after the hall is an interchange of two lines. The hall looks west, out over its own gate. A little over a kilometre away lies the district around the Presidential Office. Songshan Airport lies about five kilometres to the northeast, its runway 2,605 metres long. By law, a zone around the airport is closed to drone activity. One axis, two mirrored sides, four bounding roads. From the air the park reads like a clearly drawn ground plan.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

依民用航空法第99條之13，禁航區、限航區與機場四周一定範圍禁止遙控無人機活動；其餘400呎以下空域依臺北市政府公告的禁止、限制區域管理（現行公告自2024年6月26日生效），且不得飛越人群聚集或集會上空。本園區在市中心，距總統府約1公里、松山機場約5公里，實際飛行前必須先用民航局「Drone Map」App查明是否屬禁限區；禁限區內僅政府機關（構）、學校或法人可於活動15日前申請，未經許可不得飛行。

Under Article 99-13 of the Civil Aviation Act, drones are banned in prohibited and restricted airspace and within set distances of airports; elsewhere below 400 ft, Taipei City's announced no-fly and restricted zones apply (current notice in force since 26 June 2024), and flying over crowds or assemblies is not allowed. The park is in the city centre, about 1 km from the Presidential Office and 5 km from Songshan Airport: check the CAA "Drone Map" app before any real flight; inside prohibited or restricted zones only government bodies, schools or legal persons may apply (normally 15 days ahead), and no flight is allowed without that permission.
