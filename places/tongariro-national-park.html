歷史 History

送給國家的聖山

Sacred Peaks Given to the Nation

1887年酋長的託付、紐西蘭第一座國家公園、1953年的災難與雙重遺產
A Māori chief's gift in 1887, New Zealand's first national park, Tangiwai in 1953 and dual World Heritage

東加里羅國家公園在紐西蘭北島的中央，。三座活火山，站在一片高原上。毛利部族圖霍瑞托亞的祖先納托羅伊朗吉，。三十多個世代以前登上這些山，為它們命名。1887年9月23日，酋長霍羅努庫·特修修·圖基諾四世，。把三座山的山頂託付給國家。這份契約保證山頂永遠不會被賣掉；。部族原本的意思，是和政府一起守護聖山。最初的土地約兩千六百三十八公頃，。成了紐西蘭第一座、世界第四座國家公園的核心。

1894年10月，東加里羅國家公園法通過；。1922年，公園擴大到約五百八十七平方公里。1913年，米德與德雷克帶來北島第一副滑雪板，。在魯阿佩胡山上試著滑雪。1925年，通往法卡帕帕的路修好，第一輛汽車開了進來；。1929年，城堡飯店蓋好，有九十五間客房。1953年12月24日晚上10點21分，。火山泥流沖斷了坦吉瓦伊的鐵路橋墩。從威靈頓開往奧克蘭的快車隨即墜河，。一百五十一人罹難，是紐西蘭最嚴重的鐵路事故。

泥流來自魯阿佩胡的火口湖，。擋住湖水的火山灰堤壩突然崩潰。1990年，公園以自然價值列入世界遺產；。1993年，它成為第一處以新標準列入的「文化景觀」。1995年9月，魯阿佩胡噴發，滑雪場的人緊急撤離；。2007年，火口湖的堤壩崩潰，又引發了泥流。2012年8月6日與11月21日，。東加里羅山的特瑪里火山口兩度噴發。城堡飯店在2023年停業。遊客中心裡，有一座特修修·圖基諾四世的胸像。

一位酋長的託付，。讓這三座聖山，成了所有人共同守護的地方。

Tongariro National Park lies in the centre of New Zealand's North Island, three active volcanoes standing on a high plateau. Ngātoroirangi, ancestor of the Ngāti Tūwharetoa people, climbed these mountains over 30 generations ago and named them. On 23 September 1887 the chief Horonuku Te Heuheu Tūkino IV entrusted the summits of the three mountains to the nation. The deed ensured the summits could never be sold; the iwi intended to share the duty of protecting them with the government. The original 2,638 hectares became the core of New Zealand's first national park, the fourth in the world.

The Tongariro National Park Act was passed in October 1894; in 1922 the park grew to about 587 square kilometres. In 1913 Bill Mead and Bernard Drake brought the first skis seen in the North Island and tried skiing on Ruapehu. In 1925 the road to Whakapapa was finished and the first car drove in; in 1929 the Chateau Tongariro opened, with 95 bedrooms. At 10:21 p.m. on 24 December 1953 a lahar swept away a pier of the railway bridge at Tangiwai. The Wellington–Auckland express plunged into the river moments later; 151 people died in New Zealand's worst rail accident.

The lahar came from the crater lake on Ruapehu, when a dam of volcanic ash holding back the lake gave way. In 1990 the park was inscribed for its natural values; in 1993 it became the first site listed under the new cultural landscape criteria. In September 1995 Ruapehu erupted and skiers were evacuated; in 2007 the crater lake's dam collapsed, sending down another lahar. On 6 August and 21 November 2012 the Te Māri craters on Mount Tongariro erupted. The Chateau Tongariro closed in 2023. The visitor centre holds a bust of Te Heuheu Tūkino IV.

A chief's gift made these three sacred mountains a place everyone shares in protecting.

人文 Culture

有生命的山

Mountains That Are Alive

毛利部族眼中的聖山、火山名字的由來，與第一處「文化景觀」世界遺產
Sacred mountains of the iwi, the stories behind their names, and the first cultural landscape

對圖霍瑞托亞與朗吉兩個部族來說，。東加里羅的群山，是有靈性與文化生命的存在。在毛利語裡，這種山叫做「maunga」；。希凱羅部族，是這些聖山的守護者。瑙魯赫伊這個名字，由祖先納托羅伊朗吉所取，。傳說紀念他在火焰到來前凍死的僕人。另一種說法，指的是他召喚火焰時，。把手杖插進地裡的動作。「魯阿佩胡」的意思，。是「發出聲響的坑」或「爆炸的坑」。

山南方的坦吉瓦伊，。名字的意思是「哭泣的水」。1953年災難發生時，英國女王伊莉莎白二世正在紐西蘭訪問；。她在奧克蘭的聖誕廣播，以向紐西蘭人致哀作結。過去，世界遺產的文化標準，需要寺廟或聚落這類有形的證據。紐西蘭主張，無形的文化連結也應該被承認。1993年，東加里羅成為第一處。以修訂後的「文化景觀」標準列入的世界遺產。它是全世界少數同時具有自然與文化價值的雙重遺產，。也是紐西蘭三處世界遺產之一。

2006年，圖霍瑞托亞的最高酋長圖穆·特修修，。當選聯合國世界遺產委員會主席。2010年5月，韓國濟州道。和東加里羅國家公園結為姊妹公園。瑙魯赫伊的山形，。也在電影《魔戒》裡化身為「末日火山」。山區有部分曾經失火的土地，。目前實施為期十年的「拉胡伊」，也就是毛利人的禁入令。山不只是風景，。也是祖先、故事，與部族的身分。

To Ngāti Tūwharetoa and Ngāti Rangi the Tongariro mountains are spiritual and cultural beings. In Māori such a mountain is a maunga; Ngāti Hikairo ki Tongariro are the guardians of the sacred peaks. The name Ngauruhoe was given by the ancestor Ngātoroirangi; one story says it honours his slave, who died of cold before the volcanic fire came. Another says it refers to the thrusts of his staff into the ground as he called up the fire. The name Ruapehu means "pit of noise" or "exploding pit".

Tangiwai, south of the mountain, means "weeping water". Queen Elizabeth II was on her first tour of New Zealand at the time; her Christmas broadcast from Auckland ended with sympathy for New Zealanders. World Heritage cultural criteria once required tangible evidence such as a temple or settlement. New Zealand argued that intangible cultural links should count too. In 1993 Tongariro became the first site inscribed under the revised criteria for cultural landscapes. It is one of only a few dozen sites in the world with dual natural and cultural status, and one of New Zealand's three World Heritage Sites.

In 2006 Tumu te Heuheu, paramount chief of Ngāti Tūwharetoa, was elected chair of the World Heritage Committee. In May 2010 the Jeju provincial office in Korea formed a sister relationship with Tongariro National Park. Ngauruhoe's cone stood in for Mount Doom in the Lord of the Rings films. Part of the area was burned by fire, and a ten-year rāhui, a Māori restriction, now covers it. The mountains are not only scenery; they are ancestors, stories and the identity of the iwi.

公園的植物，隨著海拔一層層改變：。低處是羅漢松類與闊葉樹的雨林。往上，大約八百到一千五百公尺，是山毛櫸森林；。再往上，是灌叢、草叢與高山環境。魯阿佩胡山的山坡上，。有北島僅有的冰河，一共十八條。山頂的火口湖叫「特瓦伊阿莫埃」，。湖水溫暖，而且是酸性的。公園記錄到五十六種以上的鳥類，。還有兩種原生的蝙蝠。

藍鴨與奇異鳥，受到外來掠食者的威脅；。這裡也有一群受監測的褐色奇異鳥。這裡也住著受威脅的庫佩石龍子。公園記錄到的脊椎動物超過五十六種。二十世紀初，為了獵松雞引進了石楠。2024年的研究發現，它已覆蓋公園三分之一以上。1996年引進了石楠甲蟲做生物防治，。但要完全清除，已經不可能。外來的扭葉松也廣泛分布，。工作人員持續清除，不讓它長進高山與草叢地帶。

滑雪場大約占魯阿佩胡山的百分之十，。是整個世界遺產範圍的百分之三。2024年5月的熱影像空中調查，沒有發現山羊；。2025年3月的調查，則發現了幾頭鹿。國際自然保護聯盟2025年的評估，。把這裡的保育前景評為「良好」。東加里羅高山步道每年有十三萬多人走過，。保護與觀光，在這裡需要細心平衡。從山腳的雨林到山頂的冰河，。短短幾公里內，就跨過了好幾個世界。

The plants of the park change with altitude: lowland rainforest of podocarps and broadleaf trees at the bottom. Higher, at about 800 to 1,500 metres, comes montane beech forest; above it lie shrubland, tussock and alpine ground. On Ruapehu's slopes are the North Island's only glaciers, eighteen in all. The summit crater lake, Te Wai ā-moe, holds warm, acidic water. More than 56 bird species have been recorded in the park, along with two native bats.

Whio, the blue duck, and kiwi are threatened by introduced predators; a brown kiwi population here is monitored. The threatened Kupe skink also lives here. Over 56 vertebrate species have been recorded. Heather was brought in early in the 20th century for grouse hunting. A 2024 study found it covers over a third of the park. A heather beetle was released in 1996 as biological control, but wiping heather out is no longer possible. Introduced lodgepole pine is widespread too, and is kept out of the alpine and tussock zones by ongoing control.

Ski areas cover about 10% of Ruapehu and 3% of the World Heritage Site. A thermal aerial survey in May 2024 found no goats; one in March 2025 detected several deer. IUCN's 2025 assessment rated the conservation outlook here as "good". More than 130,000 walkers cross the Tongariro Alpine Crossing each year, so protection and tourism need careful balance here. From rainforest at the foot to glaciers at the top, a few kilometres cross several worlds.

地理 Geography

三座活火山

Three Active Volcanoes

北島最高的魯阿佩胡、最活躍的瑙魯赫伊、十二座火山錐的東加里羅
Ruapehu, the North Island's highest, Ngauruhoe, most active of the 20th century, and Tongariro's many cones

魯阿佩胡山的最高點塔胡朗吉，海拔兩千七百九十七公尺，。是北島的最高點。它最古老的岩石，大約有二十五萬年。過去一百五十年，至少有四十次蒸汽與火山灰噴發和泥流。火口湖在兩次大噴發之間會慢慢蓄滿，。1995到1996年的大噴發，曾把湖水全部噴空。往北，是錐形的瑙魯赫伊山，。海拔兩千兩百九十一公尺。它大約在兩千五百年前第一次噴發，。是東加里羅火山群裡最年輕的火山口。

二十世紀它噴發了四十五次，。是紐西蘭那個世紀最活躍的火山；最後一次在1977年。東加里羅山最高一千九百七十八公尺，。至少由十二座火山錐組成，瑙魯赫伊也是其中之一。現在的層狀火山，從大約二十七萬五千年前開始堆積，。由安山岩的熔岩與火山碎屑層層疊成。紅色火山口是高山步道的最高點，海拔一千八百八十六公尺，。1926年最後一次噴出火山灰，至今還在冒著噴氣。翡翠湖與藍湖，。是爆炸火山口積水形成的湖泊。

高山步道從海拔一千一百二十公尺的曼加特波波山谷出發，。在海拔七百六十公尺的凱特塔希路結束。走這條步道的人，。從1990年的一萬人，增加到2015年的十二萬五千人。法卡帕帕村海拔將近一千兩百公尺，。南邊就是魯阿佩胡山的滑雪場。公園的面積約七百九十六平方公里。附近的皮漢加山，最後一次噴發已在兩萬多年前。三座還在活動的火山，。至今仍在改變北島中央的這片高原。

Tahurangi, the top of Mount Ruapehu at 2,797 metres, is the highest point of the North Island. Its oldest rocks are about 250,000 years old. In the last 150 years it has had at least 40 steam and ash eruptions and lahars. The crater lake fills up between major eruptions; the big eruptions of 1995–96 emptied it completely. To the north rises the cone of Ngauruhoe, 2,291 metres high. It first erupted about 2,500 years ago, the youngest vent of the Tongariro volcanic complex.

It erupted 45 times in the 20th century, New Zealand's most active volcano of that era; the last time was in 1977. Mount Tongariro reaches 1,978 metres and is made of at least 12 cones, Ngauruhoe among them. The present stratovolcano began building about 275,000 years ago, from layer on layer of andesite lava and tephra. Red Crater, the high point of the crossing at 1,886 metres, last erupted ash in 1926 and still has active fumaroles. The Emerald Lakes and Blue Lake formed where water filled explosion craters.

The Alpine Crossing starts in the Mangatepōpō Valley at 1,120 metres and ends at Ketetahi Road at 760 metres. Walkers on the crossing grew from 10,000 in 1990 to 125,000 in 2015. Whakapapa Village sits just under 1,200 metres up, with Ruapehu's ski field to the south. The park covers about 796 square kilometres. Nearby Pihanga last erupted more than 20,000 years ago. Three volcanoes still at work go on reshaping this plateau at the heart of the North Island.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

依紐西蘭保育部的規定，在任何國家公園飛無人機，不論大小或用途，都須取得保育部許可；東加里羅國家公園屬於「原則上不允許、不鼓勵申請」的公園。此外，依民航規定，飛行高度不得超過地面120公尺，機場附近4公里內須事先核准。實際飛行前請先確認最新規定。

In New Zealand a Department of Conservation permit is needed to fly a drone in any national park, whatever its size or purpose, and Tongariro is one of the parks where drone use is not generally allowed and applications are discouraged. Civil aviation rules also cap flights at 120 m above the ground and require approval within 4 km of an aerodrome. Check the current rules before any real flight.
