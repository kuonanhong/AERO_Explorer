歷史 History

海峽中間的要塞

A Stronghold in the Middle of the Strait

元朝的巡檢司、荷蘭人的城堡與清法戰爭的砲臺
A Yuan inspection office, a Dutch fort and the batteries of the Sino-French War

澎湖群島在臺灣海峽中，。距離臺灣本島約五十公里。群島總面積141平方公里，。是臺灣面積最小的縣。1281年，元朝在澎湖設立巡檢司，。比臺灣納入版圖早了403年。澎湖天后宮是全臺灣最早建立的媽祖廟，。現在是國定古蹟。1622年，荷蘭人占領澎湖，。在蛇頭山上蓋了全臺第一座西式城堡。

1624年，明朝派出四五十艘戰船與五千名士兵，。把荷蘭人逐出澎湖。1683年7月12日，清軍在澎湖海戰初期。先拿下了桶盤嶼。這一戰擊敗了鄭氏的軍隊，。澎湖成為臺灣府轄下的一個廳。1885年3月，法軍在清法戰爭中攻下澎湖；。西嶼東臺當時還沒裝上新式大砲。戰後，劉銘傳在西嶼的內垵與外垵之間。修築了西臺。

同樣在戰後興建的媽宮城，。是臺灣最晚由官方興建的城池。漁翁島燈塔，。在西嶼西南端的高地上。1970年，跨海大橋通車，。把白沙與西嶼連了起來。元、明、荷蘭、清與法國的軍隊，。都在這片群島留下了據點。

The Penghu Islands lie in the Taiwan Strait, about 50 kilometres from Taiwan's main island. The archipelago covers 141 square kilometres, making it Taiwan's smallest county. In 1281 the Yuan dynasty set up an inspection office in Penghu, 403 years before Taiwan itself was incorporated. Penghu Tianhou Temple is the earliest Mazu temple in Taiwan and is now a national monument. In 1622 the Dutch occupied Penghu and built Taiwan's first Western-style fort on Shetou Hill.

In 1624 a Ming fleet of 40 to 50 warships and 5,000 troops drove the Dutch out of Penghu. On 12 July 1683, early in the Battle of Penghu, Qing forces took Tongpan Island. The battle defeated the Zheng forces, and Penghu became a sub-prefecture of Taiwan Prefecture. In March 1885 the French took Penghu in the Sino-French War; the East Fort on Xiyu had not yet received its modern guns. After the war, Liu Mingchuan built the West Fort on the high ground between Nei'an and Wai'an on Xiyu.

The walled town of Magong, also built after the war, was the last official city wall raised in Taiwan. The Yuwengdao Lighthouse stands on the heights at the south-west end of Xiyu. In 1970 the Great Bridge opened, joining Baisha and Xiyu. Yuan, Ming, Dutch, Qing and French forces all left strongholds on these islands.

人文 Culture

用石頭過日子

Living with Stone

菜宅、古榕、灰窯與一座橋
Walled gardens, an old banyan, a lime kiln and a bridge

小門嶼在西嶼北端，。面積只有0.5平方公里。島上的「菜宅」用石牆擋風，。保護牆內的作物。步道邊還看得到石敢當，。當地人用它來鎮煞。往東是跨海大橋，。全長2,494公尺。大橋橫跨吼門水道，。完工當時是遠東最長的跨海大橋。

最初的橋寬只有5.1公尺，。重建後在1996年以雙線通車。橋頭通梁村的古榕超過三百年，。有九十五根氣根。澎湖七成以上的居民，。祖先來自福建同安。漁業一直是這裡最主要的產業。東邊的菓葉海岸，。有一座像城堡的灰白色建築。

菓葉灰窯是1960年民眾自己蓋的石灰窯，。牆用玄武岩加灰漿砌成。桶盤嶼2018年有378位居民，。每天有兩班交通船往返本島。西嶼西南端的漁翁島燈塔，。屬於外垵村。2018年，有一百八十萬名遊客來到澎湖。

Xiaomen Islet lies at the north end of Xiyu and covers only half a square kilometre. Its "vegetable houses" use stone walls to break the wind and shelter the crops inside. Beside the path stand shigandang stones, set up by villagers to ward off harm. To the east is the Great Bridge, 2,494 metres long. It crosses the Houmen Channel; when finished it was the longest sea bridge in the Far East.

The first bridge was only 5.1 metres wide; rebuilt, it reopened with two lanes in 1996. The banyan of Tongliang village by the bridge is more than 300 years old and has 95 aerial roots. More than 72 per cent of Penghu's people descend from settlers from Tong'an in Fujian. Fishing has always been the main industry here. On the Guoye shore to the east stands a grey-white building like a small castle.

The Guoye Lime Kiln was built by local people in 1960, its walls of basalt laid in mortar. Tongpan Island had 378 residents in 2018, with a ferry to the main island twice a day. The Yuwengdao Lighthouse at the south-west end of Xiyu belongs to Wai'an village. In 2018 1.8 million tourists visited Penghu.

自然 Nature

燕鷗、沙洲與潮間帶

Terns, Sandbars and the Shore between Tides

三座無人島的保留區，與風、雨都少的群島
A reserve of three uninhabited islets in an archipelago short of rain

澎湖本島東北方海上，。有三座受保護的小島。小白沙嶼、雞善嶼與錠鉤嶼，。在1992年3月12日公告為自然保留區。保留區滿潮時19.13公頃，。低潮時30.87公頃。在這之前，軍方曾經用這些島做砲擊演習。夏天，蒼燕鷗、白眉燕鷗與岩鷺來到島上。

牠們在玄武岩的崖壁上築巢，。通常八、九月離開。過去，漁民與遊客。曾經上島撿拾鳥蛋。澎湖年均溫約22.3度，。年雨量只有一千公釐左右。北寮奎壁山外，退潮時會露出。一條長約三百公尺的S形礫石步道。青螺濕地有岩礁、礫石、沙灘、泥灘、。紅樹林與潮池，是澎湖濕地的縮影。

澎澎灘原本是海流堆出的沙洲，。1986年韋恩颱風之後，滿潮也不再被淹沒。它的沙來自吉貝、鳥嶼與員貝嶼。桶盤嶼附近有珊瑚礁，。浮潛就看得到。沒有人住的三座小島，。現在留給海鳥。

Out at sea north-east of the main island are three protected islets. Xiaobaisha, Jishan and Dinggou were proclaimed a nature reserve on 12 March 1992. The reserve measures 19.13 hectares at high tide and 30.87 at low tide. Before that, the armed forces used these islets for artillery practice. In summer, black-naped terns, bridled terns and reef egrets come to the islets.

They nest on the basalt cliffs and usually leave by August or September. In the past, fishermen and visitors collected birds' eggs here. Penghu averages about 22.3°C over the year and receives only about 1,000 millimetres of rain. Off Kuibishan at Beiliao, the ebb tide uncovers an S-shaped gravel path about 300 metres long. Qingluo Wetland has reef, gravel, sand, mud, mangrove and tide pools: Penghu's wetlands in miniature.

Pengpeng Beach began as a sandbar piled up by currents; after Typhoon Wayne in 1986 it stayed above even the high tide. Its sand came from Jibei, Niaoyu and Yuanbei. Coral reefs lie close to Tongpan Island, near enough to see by snorkelling. Three islets where no one lives are now left to the seabirds.

地理 Geography

玄武岩的柱子與平頂的島

Columns of Basalt and Flat-Topped Islands

熔岩怎麼變成一根根石柱
How lava became row upon row of stone columns

旁白全文 Full narration

桶盤嶼是一座玄武岩方山，。整圈海岸都是柱狀玄武岩的海崖。這座島面積約0.34平方公里，。2008年1月成立地質公園。西南岸有階梯狀凹地「蓮花臺」，。還有蜂巢狀的「貓公石」。澎湖是中新世盾狀火山的殘餘，。有二到四層玄武岩，夾著砂岩與泥岩。熔岩冷卻收縮，。就裂成六角形或多角形的直立石柱。

西嶼的大菓葉，是日治時期為了交通開挖，。才露出來的一片柱狀玄武岩。西嶼面積約17.8平方公里，。海拔只有52公尺左右。小門嶼的鯨魚洞，。是海浪侵蝕出來的海蝕拱門。往東飛到鳥嶼：。玄武岩柱從東岸一直延伸到北岸。崖下的海蝕平臺看得到放射狀的節理，。風化後像一層層洋蔥。

小白沙嶼的石柱是彎的；。北邊的海蝕平臺被切成豆腐狀。雞善嶼分成大小兩島，退潮時相連；。六角石柱直接從海床立起。錠鉤嶼的石柱朝著不同方向；。「錠鉤」就是船錨。一千多萬年的日曬、雨淋與風，。把熔岩修成今天平頂的群島。

Tongpan Island is a basalt mesa, its whole shoreline a cliff of basalt columns. The island covers about 0.34 square kilometres; a geology park was established here in January 2008. On the south-west shore is the stepped basin called Lotus Terrace, and the honeycombed Cat Rock. Penghu is the remnant of a Miocene shield volcano: two to four layers of basalt with sandstone and mudstone between. As lava cooled and shrank, it split into upright columns with six or more sides.

At Daguoye on Xiyu, a wall of columnar basalt was exposed by excavation for transport in the Japanese era. Xiyu covers about 17.8 square kilometres and rises only some 52 metres above the sea. Whale Cave on Xiaomen Islet is a sea arch cut by the waves. East to Niaoyu, where basalt columns run from the east shore round to the north. On the wave-cut platform below, the joints fan out like rays, weathering into onion-like shells.

On Xiaobaisha the columns are curved, and the wave-cut platform to the north is diced like bean curd. Jishan is two islets joined at low tide; its six-sided columns rise straight from the sea bed. On Dinggou the columns point in many directions; the name means a ship's anchor. More than ten million years of sun, rain and wind have shaped the lava into the flat-topped islands of today.
