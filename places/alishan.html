歷史 History

為檜木而建的鐵路

A Railway Built for Cypress

From the 1900 survey to the full reopening of 2024: a century and more on Alishan

這裡是嘉義縣阿里山鄉的阿里山，。海拔兩千多公尺的森林遊樂區。這片山區原本是鄒族的土地。日本統治臺灣之後，。探勘隊在這裡發現大片檜木林。1900年開始測量，1906年藤田組動工，。1908年因經費與地形而放棄。1910年政府接手，1912年通車；。奮起湖站就在這一年啟用。

1914年，沼平車站啟用，。這裡成了日治時期最大的集材中心。1906年，日本人在這裡發現一棵紅檜巨木，。後來被稱為阿里山神木。這棵樹樹齡超過三千年、高五十二公尺，。1998年因雷擊與豪雨枯萎而放倒。2006年，民眾票選出第二代神木：。四十五公尺高的香林神木。伐木持續到1970年代，。森林資源耗盡之後，觀光取代了林業。

1982年阿里山公路完工，。1986年，看日出的祝山線通車。祝山線是第一段由臺灣自己興建的登山鐵路。2009年莫拉克颱風重創鐵路，。直到2024年7月才全線恢復通車。這條鐵路，被文化部列為臺灣的世界遺產潛力點。

This is Alishan in Chiayi County, a forest recreation area more than 2,000 metres up. These mountains were first settled by the Tsou people. After Japan took Taiwan, expeditions found large stands of cypress here. Surveying began in 1900; the Fujita company started building in 1906 and gave up in 1908 over money and terrain. The government resumed work in 1910 and the line opened in 1912; Fenqihu station opened that year.

Zhaoping station opened in 1914 and became the largest log collection centre of the Japanese era. In 1906 the Japanese found a giant red cypress here, later known as the Alishan Sacred Tree. More than 3,000 years old and 52 metres tall, it withered after lightning and heavy rain and was felled in 1998. In 2006 a public vote chose its successor: the 45-metre Xianglin Sacred Tree. Logging went on into the 1970s; once the forest was exhausted, tourism took its place.

The Alishan Highway was completed in 1982, and in 1986 the Zhushan line opened for sunrise trains. The Zhushan branch was the first stretch of mountain railway built by Taiwan itself. Typhoon Morakot badly damaged the line in 2009; it fully reopened only in July 2024. Taiwan's Ministry of Culture lists the railway as a potential World Heritage site.

特富野古道原是鄒族的獵徑，。也是通婚、探親走的路。日治時期，古道後段改建成。運送木材的水山線鐵路。回到遊樂區裡，。最大的廟是受鎮宮。受鎮宮在1969年改建，供奉玄天上帝，。是當地人的信仰中心。姊妹潭是一大一小兩座相鄰的高山湖泊，。傳說有一對姊妹在此殉情。

Zhushan station, at 2,451 metres, is the highest railway station now operating in Taiwan. Early trains bring people up to watch the sun rise over Jade Mountain. The forest railway, giant trees, sea of clouds, sunrise and sunset glow are known as Alishan's five wonders. Before Han settlers and the Japanese, these mountains were home to the Tsou. Dabang is one of the Tsou villages in these mountains today.

The Tefuye trail began as a Tsou hunting path, also walked for marriages and family visits. In the Japanese era its upper section became the Shuishan logging railway. Back inside the recreation area, the largest temple is Shouzhen Temple. Rebuilt in 1969 and dedicated to Xuantian Shangdi, it is the centre of local worship. The Sister Ponds are two neighbouring mountain pools, one large, one small; legend says two sisters died here for love.

In spring the Plum Garden area fills with cherry blossom, mostly Yoshino cherry. In 2014 the Alishan National Scenic Area received 2.76 million visits. The area is also known for high-mountain tea and wasabi. The Tsou's mountain, the loggers' mountain and the sunrise mountain are one and the same Alishan.

自然 Nature

阿里山國家森林遊樂區，。面積約一千四百公頃。這裡年雨量將近四千公釐，。一年有一百六十多天下雨。年平均相對濕度百分之八十六，。雲霧是森林日常的一部分。森林鐵路從海拔30公尺爬到2,216公尺，。沿途經過熱帶、暖帶、溫帶三種林相。紅檜與臺灣扁柏，。是這裡最主要的兩種檜木。

香林神木是紅檜，高四十五公尺、。樹圍12.3公尺，樹齡約兩千三百年。伐木之後，大約還有四十棵紅檜巨木留下來，。就在巨木群棧道旁。林下長著臺灣一葉蘭等稀有植物。這裡有臺灣獼猴、帝雉、黃胸藪眉、。栗背林鴝與阿里山山椒魚。北邊的大塔山海拔2,663公尺，。是阿里山山脈的最高點。

阿里山榆，是只長在阿里山地區的一種榆樹。舊的阿里山工作站，。現在改成了生態教育館。春天，櫻花與杜鵑在林間開放。被砍伐過的山，。如今重新長成一片森林。

The Alishan National Forest Recreation Area covers about 1,400 hectares. Nearly 4,000 millimetres of rain fall here each year, on more than 160 days. Relative humidity averages 86 per cent; cloud and mist are part of the forest's daily life. The forest railway climbs from 30 to 2,216 metres through tropical, warm-temperate and temperate forest. Taiwan red cypress and Taiwan yellow cypress are the two main cypresses here.

The Xianglin Sacred Tree is a red cypress 45 metres tall and 12.3 metres around, about 2,300 years old. About 40 giant red cypresses survived the logging; they stand beside the giant-tree boardwalks. Rare plants such as the Taiwan pleione grow beneath the trees. Formosan macaque, Mikado pheasant, Steere's liocichla, collared bush robin and the Alishan salamander live here. Datashan to the north, 2,663 metres, is the highest point of the Alishan Range.

Ulmus uyematsui, an elm, is found only in the Alishan region. The old Alishan forestry station is now an eco-education centre. In spring, cherry blossom and rhododendron flower among the trees. A mountain once logged has grown back into forest.

地理 Geography

從平原爬上兩千公尺

Up Two Thousand Metres from the Plain

阿里山山脈的高度、氣候，與一條爬山的窄軌鐵路
The height and climate of the Alishan Range, and a narrow-gauge line that climbs it

這裡是阿里山山脈，。平均高度約兩千五百公尺。山脈裡有二十五座山峰超過兩千公尺。遊樂區的入口在臺18線88.2公里處，。海拔2,216公尺。要把木材運下山，。鐵路得從海拔30公尺的嘉義一路爬上來。鐵路採用762公釐的窄軌。

全線有五十多座隧道、。七十七座木橋。途中在獨立山繞了一段螺旋，再用之字形折返爬升，。最大坡度百分之6.26。主線之外還有支線：。沼平線長1.3公里。眠月線在1999年921地震後停駛，。現在成了登山步道。從祝山往東看，。大約十四公里外就是玉山。

玉山主峰海拔3,952公尺，。比阿里山高出一千多公尺。阿里山年均溫約十度，。高海拔地區冬天偶爾下雪。這裡的最低溫紀錄，。是零下11.5度。從平原到雲海之上，。一條窄軌鐵路連起兩千多公尺的落差。

This is the Alishan Range, about 2,500 metres high on average. Twenty-five of its peaks rise above 2,000 metres. The entrance to the recreation area, at the 88.2 km mark of Highway 18, is 2,216 metres above sea level. To bring the timber down, the railway had to climb all the way from Chiayi at 30 metres. The line uses a narrow gauge of 762 millimetres.

It has more than 50 tunnels and 77 wooden bridges. On the way it loops in a spiral at Dulishan, then zigzags up through switchbacks; the steepest gradient is 6.26 per cent. Beyond the main line there are branches: the Zhaoping line is 1.3 kilometres long. The Mianyue line closed after the 1999 earthquake and is now a mountain trail. Looking east from Zhushan, Jade Mountain stands about 14 kilometres away.

The main peak of Yushan, 3,952 metres, stands more than 1,000 metres higher than Alishan. The yearly mean temperature on Alishan is about 10°C; snow sometimes falls high up in winter. The lowest temperature recorded here is minus 11.5°C. From the plain to above the clouds, one narrow-gauge line joins more than 2,000 metres of height.
