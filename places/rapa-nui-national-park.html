Polynesian settlers on a remote Pacific island, and what they left behind

這裡是太平洋上的復活節島，。島上的人稱它拉帕努伊。它在智利本土以西三千七百公里，。是智利的特別領地。拉帕努伊人在公元三〇〇到一二〇〇年之間。來到這座島定居。他們在沒有外來影響下，。發展出獨特的巨石雕像與建築傳統。島上現存八百八十七尊摩艾石像。

約百分之九十五的摩艾，。石材來自這座拉諾拉拉庫火山口。這座採石場使用了大約五百年，。直到十八世紀初。山坡上還留著許多沒有完成的摩艾。後來，鳥人信仰取代了摩艾崇拜，。中心在西南端的奧龍戈。奧龍戈是一座石屋村落與祭典中心。

1995年，拉帕努伊國家公園。列入世界遺產。2017年12月1日，國家公園的祖傳土地。交還給原住民社群「馬烏亨努亞」管理。2022年10月的一場火災，。對遺址裡的濕地與摩艾造成無法挽回的損害。一座孤島，一千多年的歷史，。寫在石頭上。

This is Easter Island in the Pacific; its people call it Rapa Nui. It lies 3,700 kilometres west of mainland Chile and is a special territory of Chile. The Rapa Nui people settled here between AD 300 and 1200. Without outside influence they developed a unique tradition of monumental sculpture and architecture. 887 moai survive on the island.

About 95 per cent of the moai were carved from the stone of this crater, Rano Raraku. The quarry was worked for about 500 years, until the early eighteenth century. Many unfinished moai remain on its slopes. Later the Birdman cult replaced moai worship, centred on Orongo in the south-west. Orongo is a stone village and ceremonial centre.

In 1995 Rapa Nui National Park was inscribed as World Heritage. On 1 December 2017 the park's ancestral lands were returned to indigenous control under the Ma'u Henua community. A fire in October 2022 did irreparable damage to wetlands and moai within the site. An isolated island, with more than a thousand years of history written in stone.

Platforms, Stone Houses and the Birdman

拉帕努伊人的祭典地景，與今天的共同管理
The ceremonial landscape of the Rapa Nui, and how it is cared for today

西岸的塔海，。是一處考古建築群。拉帕努伊人把摩艾立在石砌平台上。國家公園保護的，。是整個島上的這些遺跡。公園面積六千八百公頃，。涵蓋島上大部分土地。往西南，是奧龍戈。

鳥人信仰的祭典，。就在這裡的馬塔恩加勞舉行。祭典地點在海拔約二百五十公尺、。一道狹窄的山脊上。鳥人信仰取代摩艾崇拜之後，。這裡成了祭典的中心。北岸的阿納克納，。是海灘，也是考古遺址。海灘與古代遺跡，。就在同一段海岸上。

2017年起，國家公園由原住民社群。「馬烏亨努亞」管理。2012年，國家公園有五萬兩千兩百零二名遊客。採石場的山坡上，。還留著一尊尊未完成的摩艾。島的名字、土地與石像，。如今回到它的人民手中照顧。

Tahai, on the west coast, is an archaeological complex. The Rapa Nui set their moai on stone platforms. The national park protects these remains across the island. The park covers 6,800 hectares, much of the island. To the south-west is Orongo.

The Birdman cult was centred here, at the ceremonial site of Mata Ngarau. The site sits about 250 metres up, on a narrow ridge. Once the Birdman cult had replaced moai worship, this became its ceremonial centre. Anakena, on the north coast, is a beach and an archaeological site. The beach and the ancient remains share the same stretch of coast.

Since 2017 the park has been run by the indigenous Ma'u Henua community. In 2012 the park recorded 52,202 visitors. On the quarry slopes unfinished moai still remain. Its name, its land and its statues are now in the care of its own people.

From forest to grassland: the plants, wetlands and fires of Easter Island

今天從空中看復活節島，。幾乎整座島都覆蓋著草。但在玻里尼西亞人到來之前，。島上曾有大片森林。原生的托羅米羅樹，。在島上已經絕跡。島上記錄到的一百五十種植物裡，。有四十五種是特有種。草原、火山坡與零星的樹林，。構成了今天的地景。

往東，是拉諾拉拉庫火山口。2022年10月的森林火災，。燒毀了遺址內的濕地。火也傷及摩艾，。損害被形容為「無法挽回」。島的東端是波伊克火山。它是一座已經熄滅的火山，坡上同樣是一片草原。

沿著北岸往西，。是阿納克納海灘。阿納克納是北岸的一處海灘。皮特肯島在西邊約兩千兩百公里外，。四周只有大海。這麼孤立的島，。失去的東西很難再回來。

Seen from the air today, Easter Island is almost entirely grass-covered. Before Polynesian settlement, the island had extensive forest cover. The native toromiro tree is extinct on the island. Of 150 plant species recorded here, 45 are found nowhere else. Grassland, volcanic slopes and scattered trees make up today's landscape.

To the east lies the crater of Rano Raraku. The forest fire of October 2022 burned wetlands within the site. It also damaged moai; the harm was described as "irreparable". At the eastern end of the island is the volcano Poike. It is extinct, its slopes now grassland too.

West along the north coast is Anakena beach. Anakena is a beach on the north coast. Pitcairn Island lies about 2,200 km to the west; all around is open ocean. On an island this isolated, what is lost is hard to bring back.

地理 Geography

三座火山的三角形

A Triangle of Three Volcanoes

特雷瓦卡、波伊克、拉諾考與凝灰岩採石場
Terevaka, Poike, Rano Kau and a quarry of volcanic tuff

旁白全文 Full narration

復活節島是一座火山島。特雷瓦卡、波伊克與拉諾考三座火山，。撐起了這座島。特雷瓦卡山在島的北半部。從山上往四周看，每一個方向都是海。拉諾考火山口最後一次噴發，。大約在十五萬到二十一萬年前。

島的西南角，海崖陡直落入海中。往東北，經過塔海，。飛向拉諾拉拉庫。拉諾拉拉庫是一座火山口，。由凝固的火山灰，也就是凝灰岩構成。這裡黃褐色的凝灰岩，。成了摩艾的主要石材。從上面看，火山口與採石的山坡一目了然。

東邊的波伊克，。是島的東角。從空中看，島的輪廓大致是個三角形。國家公園六千八百公頃，。把大部分火山地景都劃了進去。在三千七百公里的海洋中間，。三座火山撐起一座小島。

Easter Island is a volcanic island. Three volcanoes, Terevaka, Poike and Rano Kau, make up the island. Terevaka rises in the northern half of the island. From its top, the sea lies in every direction. Rano Kau last erupted some 150,000 to 210,000 years ago.

At the south-west corner, cliffs drop straight into the sea. North-east past Tahai, toward Rano Raraku. Rano Raraku is a volcanic crater of consolidated volcanic ash, called tuff. Its yellow-brown tuff became the main stone of the moai. From above, the crater and the quarried slopes are plain to see.

Poike to the east forms the eastern corner. From the air, the outline of the island is roughly a triangle. The 6,800-hectare national park takes in most of this volcanic landscape. In the middle of 3,700 kilometres of ocean, three volcanoes hold up a small island.
