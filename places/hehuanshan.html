歷史 History

越嶺之路：從山徑到公路

The Road over the Ridge

一條翻越中央山脈的路，怎麼一段一段走到今天
How a route across the Central Mountain Range took shape, stage by stage

旁白全文 Full narration

公路在兩座山峰之間的鞍部，翻過中央山脈。這條路的前身，要從山兩側的族人說起。太魯閣族的祖先，原本住在濁水溪上游。十八世紀，他們向東越過奇萊山（Klbiyun），遷入立霧溪流域。1912年9月下旬，臺灣總督佐久間左馬太登上合歡主峰。為了進入東邊的山區，日方前後做了五次地形探查。1913年3月，一支測量隊在主峰一帶遇上暴風雪。八十九人罹難，是臺灣山難史上傷亡最重的一次。1914年夏天，日本當局出兵攻打太魯閣族，族人起而抵抗。由霧社東進的隊伍越過這處鞍部，此後稱為「佐久間峠」。

戰事結束後，道路延伸到太魯閣峽口，成為管理山區部落的警備道。幾經改線，合歡越嶺道在1935年完工。古道西起霧社，東到太魯閣，全長約105公里。標準路寬1.8公尺，沿途設有警察駐在所。中部橫貫公路1956年動工，1960年通車。大禹嶺原名合歡埡口，1958年改為今名。從大禹嶺向南分出的支線，經武嶺通往霧社、埔里。部分路段，就開在合歡越嶺道的舊線上。公路修築之後，鞍部改名為「武嶺」。古道很快荒廢，如今只剩零星的路跡。

1963年，林務局在合歡山設立臺灣第一座森林遊樂區。山上開始提供住宿、餐飲與解說服務。東峰山腳的松雪樓，1966年落成。建築一度關閉，重建後在2009年重新開放。1960到1980年代，東峰山坡上有滑雪活動和吊椅纜車。步道旁還留著當年纜車站的遺跡。1986年，太魯閣國家公園成立。小風口設有國家公園的合歡山管理站。森林遊樂區北起小風口、南到昆陽，全區都在國家公園裡。林業機關與國家公園，共同照管這片高山。

族人的山徑、軍用道路、警備道，再到省道。同一道稜線上，疊著不同時代的路。今天開車翻過武嶺，只需要片刻。腳下這條路，卻花了好幾代人的時間才成形。

Here a highway crosses the Central Mountain Range through a saddle between two peaks. The story of this route begins with the peoples living on either side of it. Ancestors of the Truku people once lived in the upper Zhuoshui River region. In the 18th century they moved east past Mount Qilai, Klbiyun in Truku, into the Liwu valley. In late September 1912 Governor-General Sakuma Samata climbed the Main Peak with an expedition. The Japanese made five terrain surveys in all before entering the mountains to the east. In March 1913 a survey party was caught by a blizzard near the Main Peak. Eighty-nine people died, the deadliest mountaineering disaster in Taiwan's history. In summer 1914 the Japanese authorities sent forces against the Truku, who fought back. Columns from Wushe crossed this saddle heading east; it was then named Sakuma Pass.

After the fighting the road was extended to Taroko Gorge and used to police mountain villages. Rerouted more than once, the Hehuan Traversing Trail was completed in 1935. The trail ran about 105 km from Wushe in the west to Taroko in the east. Its standard width was 1.8 metres, with police posts set along the way. Work on the Central Cross-Island Highway began in 1956 and it opened in 1960. Dayuling was known as Hehuan Pass until it was renamed in 1958. From Dayuling a branch road runs south over Wuling to Wushe and Puli. Parts of it were cut along the line of the old Hehuan trail. After the highway was built the saddle was renamed Wuling. The old trail soon fell out of use; only scattered traces remain.

In 1963 the Forestry Bureau set up Taiwan's first forest recreation area on Hehuanshan. Lodging, meals and interpretation became available on the mountain. Songsyue Lodge, at the foot of the East Peak, was completed in 1966. It later closed, was rebuilt, and reopened in 2009. From the 1960s to the 1980s there was skiing, with a chairlift, on the East Peak's slopes. Remains of the lift stations still stand beside the trail. Taroko National Park was established in 1986. Its Hehuanshan station stands here at Xiaofengkou. The forest recreation area runs from Xiaofengkou south to Kunyang, all inside the national park. The forestry agency and the national park look after the same high ground together.

A mountain path, a military road, a police trail, then a provincial highway. Routes from different eras lie layered along the same ridge. Today a car crosses Wuling in moments. The road beneath it took generations to become what it is.

人文 Culture

山上的日常：住宿、單車與星空

Life at 3,000 Metres: Lodges, Bicycles and Dark Skies

人們怎麼使用這座高山，又怎麼約束自己
How people use this high mountain, and the limits they set themselves

旁白全文 Full narration

海拔三千公尺以上，公路、旅館和遊客中心都在這裡。因為公路直達，這裡被視為臺灣最容易親近的高山。石門山頂落在南投縣仁愛鄉與花蓮縣秀林鄉的交界上。稜線以西的仁愛鄉，住著賽德克、泰雅、布農等族。稜線以東的秀林鄉，是太魯閣族主要居住的鄉鎮之一。「太魯閣」這個地名，就來自族名 Truku。松雪樓海拔約3,150公尺，是臺灣海拔最高的旅館。一旁的合歡山莊，現在作為遊客中心。合歡尖山的步道只有400公尺，就在山莊後方。合歡群峰中，有五座名列「臺灣百岳」。

石門山步道長784公尺，被稱為最平易近人的百岳。官方介紹也推薦在這裡看日出。2012年起舉辦的 KOM 自行車賽，從花蓮七星潭海邊出發。車手從海平面一路爬升，終點就在武嶺。騎單車上武嶺，有東、西、北三條路線。其中由東邊上來的一條距離最長。2026年杜鵑花季的假日清晨，小型車須乘載三人以上才能上山。入冬後，管理站會提醒駕駛先備妥雪鏈。天色暗下來，這裡還有另一種資源：黑夜。遠離城市燈光，海拔又高，全年都適合觀星。

2019年，合歡山通過國際暗空協會的認證。這是臺灣第一座國際暗空公園。暗空公園沿台14甲線兩側劃設，面積13.45平方公里。範圍從鳶峰一路延伸到小風口。2018年提出的申請，由民間團體與南投縣政府發起。太魯閣國家公園和林務單位也一同具名。沿公路往下的清境，旅宿業者簽了星空友善公約。鳶峰的觀星平台，也拆掉二十多盞過亮的燈。遊樂區的守則寫著：車輛停妥後請熄燈。全區禁菸，也禁止露營和野炊。

這裡是國家公園，未經許可不得操作遙控機具。想帶無人機上山，要先向管理處申請。住宿、健行、騎車、觀星，都集中在這一小段稜線上。能留下多少黑夜與安靜，取決於每一位上山的人。

Above 3,000 metres, a highway, a hotel and a visitor centre share this stretch of ridge. Because the road reaches it, this is regarded as Taiwan's most accessible high mountain. The summit of Mount Shimen sits on the line between Ren'ai, Nantou and Xiulin, Hualien. West of the ridge, Ren'ai Township is home to Seediq, Atayal and Bunun communities. East of the ridge, Xiulin is one of the main townships of the Truku people. The name Taroko itself comes from Truku, the people's own name. Songsyue Lodge, at about 3,150 metres, is the highest hotel in Taiwan. The former Hehuan Lodge beside it now serves as the visitor centre. The path up Hehuan Jianshan is only 400 metres long, right behind the lodge. Five of the Hehuan peaks are on Taiwan's list of 100 Peaks.

The Mount Shimen trail is 784 metres long; it is called the most approachable of the 100 Peaks. The official guide also recommends it for watching the sunrise. The Taiwan KOM Challenge, held since 2012, starts at Qixingtan Beach on the Hualien coast. Riders climb from sea level and finish here at Wuling. Cyclists reach Wuling by three routes, from the east, the west and the north. The eastern approach is the longest of the three. On holiday mornings in the 2026 azalea season, cars needed at least three people aboard to drive up. In winter the park station reminds drivers to carry snow chains. As the light fades, another resource appears here: darkness. Far from city lights and high in altitude, the area suits stargazing all year.

In 2019 Hehuanshan was certified by the International Dark-Sky Association. It became Taiwan's first International Dark Sky Park. The park follows both sides of Highway 14A and covers 13.45 square kilometres. It extends from Yuanfeng all the way to Xiaofengkou. The application filed in 2018 was initiated by civic groups and the Nantou County Government. Taroko National Park and the forestry agency signed on as co-applicants. Down the road in Cingjing, lodging operators signed a star-friendly pledge to dim their lights. At the Yuanfeng viewing platform, more than twenty lamps judged too bright were removed. The recreation area's code asks drivers to switch off their lights once parked. Smoking is banned throughout, as are camping and open-air cooking.

This is national park land, where remote-controlled devices may not be operated without permission. Anyone wishing to fly a drone here must apply to the park headquarters first. Lodging, hiking, cycling and stargazing all share this one short stretch of ridge. How much darkness and quiet remain depends on everyone who comes up.

自然 Nature

箭竹草原與冷杉林

Bamboo Grassland and Fir Forest

森林界線上下，植物與動物怎麼過日子
How plants and animals live at and above the treeline

旁白全文 Full narration

山坡上有兩種綠：深色的冷杉林，淺色的箭竹草原。兩者之間的界線，是這趟飛行要看的主角。臺灣冷杉分布在海拔2,800到3,500公尺的冷溫帶。在這裡，它們多半長在凹谷和背風的山坡。冷杉林以上，是成片的玉山箭竹。土壤淺薄貧瘠，低溫又多風，箭竹長得低矮。玉山箭竹被稱為「林火適存植物」。地上的部分燒掉了，地下莖仍能很快恢復生長。越過冷杉林的上緣，就不再有高大的樹木。從空中看，草原像一層貼著稜線的絨毯。

五、六月，玉山杜鵑在東峰的箭竹叢間盛開。粉紅到紫紅的花，成片鋪在山坡上。春天開花的還有紅毛杜鵑。官方資料把這兩種杜鵑並列為合歡山的春季景觀。夏天接著開的，有臺灣百合、虎杖、玉山薄雪草和高山沙參。秋天則輪到臺灣烏頭與玉山石竹。高山植物多半貼地生長，把根扎進岩縫。葉子細小或呈針狀，表面常有毛或鱗片。冷杉與箭竹之間，是高山鳥類活動的地方。岩鷚、酒紅朱雀、火冠戴菊鳥、金翼白眉、煤山雀都有紀錄。

這裡記錄到的哺乳動物，絕大部分是臺灣特有種。官方介紹以臺灣高山小黃鼠狼作為代表。山椒魚是冰河時期留下來的有尾兩棲類。臺灣是牠們在全球分布的最南界。雪山草蜥是臺灣特有種。牠們就生活在這樣的箭竹大草原上。北峰的山坡，是整片開闊的箭竹草原。氣候寒冷，蝴蝶只在夏季花開時偶爾出現。同一片山坡，一年換四種樣子。春天開花，夏天翠綠，秋天蕭瑟，冬天覆雪。

遊樂區的守則很簡單：不離開步道，不攀折花木。也不餵食動物，不放生。冷杉、箭竹、杜鵑，還有住在其間的鳥獸。公路把人直接送到森林界線，責任也跟著上山。

Two greens cover these slopes: dark fir forest and pale bamboo grassland. The boundary between them is what this flight sets out to follow. Taiwan fir grows in the cool-temperate belt between 2,800 and 3,500 metres. Here the trees keep mostly to hollows and leeward slopes. Above the fir forest spreads Yushan cane, a dwarf bamboo. Thin, poor soil, cold and constant wind keep it low to the ground. Yushan cane is described as a plant adapted to survive fire. When the growth above ground burns, its rhizomes live on and quickly resprout. Beyond the upper edge of the fir forest there are no more tall trees. From the air the grassland looks like felt laid over the ridges.

In May and June the Yushan rhododendron blooms among the bamboo on the East Peak. Pink to fuchsia flowers spread across the slope in sheets. The red-hair rhododendron (hongmao dujuan) flowers in the same spring season. Official guides name the two species together as the sight of spring on Hehuanshan. Summer brings Formosa lily, knotweed, Yushan edelweiss and alpine ladybell. In autumn it is the turn of Taiwan monkshood and Yushan pink. Alpine plants here mostly hug the ground and root into rock crevices. Their leaves are small or needle-like, often covered in hairs or scales. The edge between fir and bamboo is where the high-mountain birds are active. Alpine accentor, vinaceous rosefinch, Taiwan firecrest, white-whiskered laughingthrush and coal tit.

Most of the mammals recorded here are found only in Taiwan. The official guide picks a small high-mountain weasel as their representative. The salamanders here are tailed amphibians left behind from the ice age. Taiwan marks the southern limit of their range worldwide. The Xueshan grass lizard is found only in Taiwan. It lives in open bamboo grassland like this. The slopes of the North Peak are one wide expanse of bamboo grassland. In this cold climate butterflies appear only now and then, when summer flowers open. The same slope changes its look four times a year. Flowers in spring, green in summer, bare in autumn, snow in winter.

The recreation area's code is simple: stay on the trails and pick nothing. Do not feed the animals, and do not release any. Fir, bamboo and rhododendron, and the birds and animals living among them. The road delivers people straight to the treeline, and responsibility comes up with them.

地理 Geography

中央山脈上的鞍部與群峰

Saddles and Summits on the Central Mountain Range

七座山峰、兩處埡口，和三條溪的分水嶺
Seven peaks, two passes and the divide of three rivers

在 YouTube 觀看 Watch on YouTube在模擬器飛這段 Fly this tour

旁白全文 Full narration

這裡是中央山脈的稜線，南投與花蓮的交界。一條公路為什麼能爬到這麼高，答案在地形裡。武嶺海拔3,275公尺，是臺灣公路的最高點。它是主峰與東峰之間的一處鞍部。主峰海拔3,417公尺。它是立霧溪與濁水溪兩個流域的分水嶺。東峰海拔3,421公尺，比主峰略高。叫做「主峰」，並不表示它是群峰裡最高的。合歡群峰共有七座，包括主峰、東峰、北峰和西峰。另外三座是石門山、合歡尖山與石門北峰。

合歡尖山海拔3,217公尺，山形尖聳。官方解說稱它是早期冰河侵蝕而成的冰斗峰。往北是石門山，步道大致與公路平行。稜線上望得見奇萊連峰、南湖大山，遠到玉山。東南方的奇萊北峰，海拔3,607公尺。合歡山正是合歡群峰與奇萊山之間的中樞。北峰海拔3,422公尺，是合歡群峰的最高點。稜線上的反射板，是途中最明顯的地標。合歡山是大甲溪、濁水溪與立霧溪的分水嶺。落在不同山坡的雨和雪，分別流向臺灣的東西兩岸。

大禹嶺海拔2,565公尺，是中橫公路的最高點。這處埡口位在合歡山與畢祿山之間。這一帶的岩層以板岩、千枚岩和石英岩為主。岩石葉理發達，當年開鑿隧道時容易崩落。台14甲線從大禹嶺向南爬升，約十公里後到武嶺。兩地的海拔相差約七百公尺。山上的氣溫，比平地低約十八度。一、二月間，鞍部和旅館一帶常見積雪。雪在臺灣其他地方很少見。在合歡山的冬天，降雪卻相對常見。

森林遊樂區面積457.61公頃，海拔2,900到3,421公尺。它是全臺海拔最高的國家森林遊樂區。鞍部讓公路得以翻越，群峰把水分向三條溪。讀懂這段稜線，就讀懂了中央山脈的一小段。

This is the crest of the Central Mountain Range, on the Nantou-Hualien border. Why a road can climb this high is a question the landform answers. Wuling, at 3,275 metres, is the highest point on Taiwan's highways. It is a saddle between the Main Peak and the East Peak. The Main Peak stands at 3,417 metres. It divides the Liwu River basin from that of the Zhuoshui. The East Peak reaches 3,421 metres, slightly higher than the Main Peak. Being called the Main Peak does not make it the highest of the group. The Hehuan group has seven peaks, among them the Main, East, North and West Peaks. The other three are Mount Shimen, Hehuan Jianshan and Shimen North Peak.

Hehuan Jianshan, 3,217 metres, rises to a sharp point. Official interpretation describes it as a cirque peak carved by early glaciers. To the north is Mount Shimen, where the trail runs roughly parallel to the road. From the ridge the view takes in the Qilai peaks, Nanhu Mountain and, far off, Yushan. To the south-east, Qilai North Peak rises to 3,607 metres. Hehuanshan forms the hub linking the Hehuan peaks with the Qilai range. The North Peak, 3,422 metres, is the highest of the Hehuan group. A reflector panel on its ridge is the clearest landmark on the way up. Hehuanshan is the divide between the Dajia, Zhuoshui and Liwu rivers. Rain and snow falling on different slopes drain to opposite coasts of Taiwan.

Dayuling, at 2,565 metres, is the highest point of the Central Cross-Island Highway. The pass lies between Hehuanshan and Mount Bilu. The rock around the pass is mainly slate, phyllite and quartzite. Its strong foliation made tunnelling here difficult and prone to collapse. From Dayuling, Highway 14A climbs south for about ten kilometres to Wuling. The two points differ in elevation by roughly 700 metres. Temperatures up here run about 18 degrees Celsius lower than on the plains. In January and February snow is common around the pass and the lodge. Snow is rare almost everywhere else in Taiwan. On Hehuanshan in winter it is comparatively common.

The forest recreation area covers 457.61 hectares between 2,900 and 3,421 metres. It is the highest national forest recreation area in Taiwan. The saddles let a road cross; the peaks send water to three rivers. To read this ridge is to read one short stretch of the Central Mountain Range.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

合歡山國家森林遊樂區全區位在太魯閣國家公園內；園區公告的禁止事項第六點規定，未經許可不得在指定地區以外操作遙控機具，無人機須先取得太魯閣國家公園管理處同意。實際飛行前另請用民航局「Drone Map」App 查詢空域並依規定申請。

The whole Hehuanshan forest recreation area lies inside Taroko National Park, whose posted prohibitions (item 6) ban operating remote-controlled devices outside designated areas without permission, so a drone needs prior approval from the park headquarters. Also check the CAA "Drone Map" app and apply as required before any real flight.
