歷史 History

鐵路與國家公園

A Railway and a National Park

1882年的湖、1883年的溫泉、1885年加拿大第一座國家公園
A lake in 1882, hot springs in 1883, Canada's first national park in 1885

斯通尼族稱這座湖為「小魚之湖」。早在歐洲人到來以前，原住民就熟悉這片山區。1882年，斯通尼族嚮導艾德溫·杭特帶著湯姆·威爾遜來到湖邊。威爾遜是第一位來到這裡的非原住民。威爾遜起初把它叫作「翡翠湖」。後來改以路易絲公主命名，她是維多利亞女王的女兒。當時，加拿大太平洋鐵路正要穿越洛磯山脈。路線選在踢馬隘口翻越大陸分水嶺。1858年，探險家詹姆斯·赫克特在這裡被馬踢傷。隘口因此得名「踢馬」。

隘口西側的「大坡」，是北美最陡的幹線鐵路之一。1909年，鐵路在山裡挖出兩座螺旋隧道，降低坡度。火車在山體裡繞一圈，從自己的上方或下方再出來。長列車有時能同時看到頭尾從兩個洞口出現。1883年，三名鐵路工人在東邊的硫磺山發現溫泉。這件事，促成了加拿大第一座國家公園。1885年，政府劃出26平方公里的溫泉保留區。1887年擴大為665平方公里的洛磯山公園，即今天的班夫。鐵路公司在湖的東岸蓋起旅館。二十世紀初改建成今天的露易絲湖城堡飯店。

1899年，湯姆·威爾遜開出通往夢蓮湖的第一條步道。1924年，車輛終於可以開上一條簡陋的道路。1969年，夢蓮湖的景色印上加拿大二十元紙鈔。這片山谷，成了全國熟悉的風景。1909年，古生物學家查爾斯·沃爾科特在幽鶴發現伯吉斯頁岩。它後來成為世界聞名的化石產地。1984年，加拿大洛磯山脈的國家公園群列入世界遺產。班夫、幽鶴、賈斯珀和庫特尼國家公園都在其中。一條鐵路帶來了旅館、遊客和國家公園。加拿大的國家公園體系，就從這片山區開始。

The Stoney Nakoda call it Ho-run-num-nay, the Lake of the Little Fishes. Indigenous peoples knew these mountains long before Europeans came. In 1882 the Stoney Nakoda guide Edwin Hunter led Tom Wilson to the shore. Wilson was the first non-Indigenous visitor. Wilson first called it Emerald Lake. It was later named for Princess Louise, Queen Victoria's daughter. At the time, the Canadian Pacific Railway was pushing through the Rockies. Its route crossed the Continental Divide at Kicking Horse Pass. In 1858 the explorer James Hector was kicked by his horse here, and the pass took its name from that.

West of the pass, the Big Hill was among the steepest main-line grades in North America. In 1909 two spiral tunnels were cut into the mountains to ease it. Trains loop inside the mountain and emerge above or below themselves. A long train can be seen leaving one portal while still entering another. In 1883 three railway workers claimed hot springs on Sulphur Mountain to the east. That claim led to Canada's first national park. In 1885 the government set aside a 26 square kilometre hot springs reserve. In 1887 it grew to 665 km² as Rocky Mountains Park, today Banff National Park. The railway company built a hotel on the eastern shore, rebuilt in the early 20th century as the Chateau Lake Louise.

In 1899 Tom Wilson cut the first trail to Moraine Lake. By 1924 a rough road let vehicles in. In 1969 Moraine Lake appeared on Canada's twenty-dollar note. The valley became a view the whole country knew. In 1909 the palaeontologist Charles Walcott found the Burgess Shale in Yoho. It became one of the most famous fossil sites in the world. In 1984 the Canadian Rocky Mountain Parks joined the World Heritage List, among them Banff, Yoho, Jasper and Kootenay national parks. A railway brought hotels, visitors and a national park. Canada's national park system began in these mountains.

山中的旅人

Travellers in the Mountains

原住民的山、鐵路旅館、山中茶屋與紙鈔上的湖
Indigenous mountains, railway hotels, a tea house and a lake on a banknote

班夫國家公園位在多個原住民族的傳統領域裡。包括庫特尼、斯通尼、布拉德、皮根、錫克西卡和楚提納族。露易絲湖的斯通尼名字，是「小魚之湖」。1882年，正是斯通尼嚮導帶外人來到這裡。湖東岸的城堡飯店，由加拿大太平洋鐵路公司興建。鐵路旅館，把歐洲式的山居度假帶進洛磯山。許多遊客清晨來到湖邊，等待湖面最平靜的時刻。山影倒映在湖上，是露易絲湖最常見的明信片。沿著步道往上爬，可以到達艾格尼絲湖。湖邊有一座山中茶屋，登山客在這裡歇腳。

茶屋只能靠步行抵達。補給有時要靠直升機送上山。夢蓮湖曾印在1969年的加拿大二十元紙鈔上。「十峰山谷」，因此成為全國熟悉的風景。1899年，威爾考克斯形容第一眼看到它的時刻，。是他一生中「最快樂的半小時」。湖邊的山莊由建築師亞瑟·埃里克森設計。它被要求與自然融為一體，每天接待約四千名遊客。九月下旬，落葉松谷的高山落葉松轉成金黃色。這是一年中最多人上山的時節之一。

冬天，露易絲湖結冰。湖面成為溜冰場，四周還有冰攀、雪鞋與狗拉雪橇。觀景台上，遊客等著看火車穿過螺旋隧道。鐵路本身，也成了一種觀光。塔卡考的名字，來自克里語，意思是「真壯觀」。一個詞，就說盡了眼前的瀑布。從原住民的山、鐵路帶來的旅人，。到今天每年湧入的遊客，這些湖一直被人們珍惜。

Banff National Park lies in the traditional territory of several First Nations: the Kootenay, Stoney, Blood, Peigan, Siksika and Tsuut'ina. The Stoney name for Lake Louise means Lake of the Little Fishes. In 1882 it was a Stoney guide who led an outsider here. The Chateau on the eastern shore was built by the Canadian Pacific Railway. Railway hotels brought European-style mountain holidays to the Rockies. Many visitors come at dawn for the stillest water, when the mountains mirrored in the lake make the classic postcard. A trail climbs from the shore to Lake Agnes, where a mountain tea house gives walkers a rest.

The tea house can be reached only on foot. Supplies sometimes come up by helicopter. Moraine Lake was pictured on Canada's twenty-dollar note of 1969. The Valley of the Ten Peaks became a view the whole nation knew. In 1899 Wilcox called his first sight of it the happiest half-hour of his life. Its lodge was designed by the architect Arthur Erickson to blend with nature, while serving about 4,000 visitors a day. In late September the alpine larches of Larch Valley turn gold, one of the busiest times on the trail.

In winter Lake Louise freezes. It becomes a skating rink, with ice climbing, snowshoeing and dog sledding around it. At the viewpoint, people wait to watch trains wind through the Spiral Tunnels. The railway itself became an attraction. Takakkaw comes from a Cree word meaning "it is magnificent". One word says all there is to say about the falls. From Indigenous mountains to railway travellers to the crowds of today, these lakes have always been treasured.

自然 Nature

灰熊、落葉松與野生動物天橋

Grizzlies, Larches and Wildlife Bridges

53種哺乳動物，與世界知名的公路生態廊道
53 mammal species, and a famous system of highway wildlife crossings

班夫國家公園面積6,641平方公里。園內記錄了53種哺乳動物和至少280種鳥類。灰熊、黑熊、美洲獅、麋鹿、駝鹿和大角羊都住在這裡。這是北美最完整的山地生態系之一。橫貫加拿大公路穿過公園。1981年起，公路從兩線道拓寬成四線道。為了讓動物安全通過，公路沿線蓋了44座動物通道。其中38座是地下涵洞，6座是天橋。1996年到2012年，十一種大型哺乳動物使用通道超過十五萬次。圍欄加上通道，讓動物與車輛的碰撞減少八成以上。

麋鹿和鹿的車禍，更減少了96%以上。這套設計，後來成為世界各地參考的案例。落葉松谷長著高山落葉松。它是少數會在秋天落葉的針葉樹。九月下旬，整片山谷轉成金黃。接著葉子落盡，迎接冬天。湖邊的森林，以雲杉和冷杉為主。再往上，樹木逐漸稀少，換成高山草甸與岩石。山谷裡的冰河，正在逐漸後退。融水的多寡，決定了下游湖泊的顏色與水量。

冰河是這些湖泊的源頭。冰河變小，湖的未來也會跟著改變。幽鶴的翡翠湖，周圍是茂密的森林。這裡也是駝鹿和熊出沒的地方。塔卡考瀑布的水，來自上方冰原的融水。夏天融冰最多時，水量最大。從灰熊、麋鹿到金黃的落葉松，。班夫證明了人和野生動物，可以共用同一片山谷。

Banff National Park covers 6,641 square kilometres. It records 53 species of mammals and at least 280 of birds. Grizzly and black bears, cougars, elk, moose and bighorn sheep live here. It is one of the most complete mountain ecosystems in North America. The Trans-Canada Highway runs through the park. From 1981 it was widened from two lanes to four. To let animals cross safely, 44 wildlife crossings were built along it: 38 underpasses and six overpasses. From 1996 to 2012, eleven large mammal species used them more than 150,000 times. Fences and crossings cut wildlife–vehicle collisions by over 80 per cent.

For elk and deer, collisions fell by more than 96 per cent. The design has since been studied around the world. Larch Valley is named for its alpine larches, one of the few conifers that drop their needles in autumn. In late September the whole valley turns gold, then the needles fall before winter. The lakeshore forest is mostly spruce and fir. Higher up, trees thin out into alpine meadow and rock. The glaciers in the valley are slowly retreating. How much they melt sets the colour and level of the lakes below.

The glaciers are where these lakes begin. As the ice shrinks, the lakes will change with it. Yoho's Emerald Lake is ringed by dense forest, country where moose and bears roam. Takakkaw Falls is fed by meltwater from the icefield above. It runs fullest in summer, when the most ice melts. From grizzlies and elk to golden larches, Banff shows that people and wildlife can share one valley.

地理 Geography

冰河留下的湖

Lakes Left by the Ice

岩粉染出的湖色、堰塞的湖，與分隔兩洋的隘口
Rock flour colours, a rockslide-dammed lake and a pass between two oceans

加拿大洛磯山脈裡的露易絲湖，湖面海拔1,750公尺。湖長約兩公里，寬約五百公尺，最深七十公尺。湖水的青綠色，來自冰河融水帶來的岩粉。極細的岩石粉末懸浮在水中，散射出這種顏色。湖的盡頭，是維多利亞山與它的冰河。冰河磨碎岩石，才有了岩粉。湖的主要水源，是上游的勒弗羅伊冰河。冰河後退，留下了一路下降的山谷。山坡上方的艾格尼絲湖，是一座冰斗湖。冰河在山壁上挖出的凹地，積水成湖。

南邊的夢蓮湖，位在十峰山谷。1899年，探險家華特·威爾考克斯為它命名。他以為湖是被冰磧堵住的，所以叫它「冰磧湖」。後來發現，堵住湖口的其實是山崩落下的岩堆。湖後並排著十座山峰。它們屬於分隔亞伯達與卑詩兩省的山脈。坦普爾山海拔3,544公尺，是這一帶最高的山。山上的岩層，是古老的海底沉積。踢馬隘口位在北美大陸分水嶺上。東側的水流向哈德遜灣，西側的水流向太平洋。

越過隘口，就從亞伯達省進入卑詩省。西側是幽鶴國家公園。幽鶴的塔卡考瀑布，從冰原融水直落山谷。它是加拿大最高的瀑布之一。山脊上的伯吉斯頁岩，保存了約五億年前的海洋生物。連柔軟的身體構造，都留在岩石裡。翡翠湖同樣由冰河融水供給。湖岸的森林，倒映在綠色的湖面上。從正上方看，冰河、河流和湖泊連成一串。水從冰河出發，一路流進湖裡。

冰河磨出的岩粉、山崩堵出的湖、分隔兩洋的隘口，。洛磯山脈的地景，是冰與岩石共同寫成的。

Lake Louise, in the Canadian Rockies, lies 1,750 metres above sea level. It is about 2 km long, 500 m wide and up to 70 m deep. Its turquoise comes from rock flour carried in by glacial meltwater. Fine particles of ground rock hang in the water and scatter this colour. At the head of the lake rise Mount Victoria and its glacier. Grinding the rock, the ice makes the flour. The lake is fed mainly by the Lefroy Glacier upstream. As the ice retreated it left this stepped valley. Lake Agnes, higher on the slope, is a cirque lake. Water filled a hollow the ice scooped from the mountainside.

To the south, Moraine Lake lies in the Valley of the Ten Peaks. The explorer Walter Wilcox named it in 1899. He thought a glacial moraine dammed it, hence the name. In fact the dam is a pile of rock from a landslide. Ten peaks stand in a row behind the lake. They belong to the range that divides Alberta from British Columbia. Mount Temple, at 3,544 metres, is the highest peak around Lake Louise. Its layers were laid down on ancient sea floors. Kicking Horse Pass lies on the Continental Divide. Water on the east drains to Hudson Bay; on the west, to the Pacific.

Cross the pass and Alberta becomes British Columbia. On the west side lies Yoho National Park. Yoho's Takakkaw Falls drops meltwater from an icefield into the valley. It is one of Canada's highest waterfalls. On the ridge, the Burgess Shale preserves sea life from about half a billion years ago, down to the soft parts of their bodies. Emerald Lake is also fed by glacial meltwater. Its forested shores reflect in the green water. From straight above, glacier, stream and lake form one chain. Water sets out from the ice and runs into the lake.

Rock flour ground by ice, a lake dammed by a landslide, a pass between two oceans: the Rockies are written by ice and stone together.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

加拿大國家公園管理局（Parks Canada）規定，所有國家公園都禁止休閒用途的無人機；未經許可在公園內操作無人機，最高可處25,000加元罰款。只有資源管理、公共安全、執法或公園管理等用途，才可能由園長核發許可。實際飛行前請先查詢加拿大運輸部與公園的規定。

Parks Canada prohibits recreational drone flying at all its places; operating a drone in a park without a permit can bring a fine of up to $25,000. The superintendent may permit drones only for resource management, public safety, law enforcement or park administration. Check Transport Canada and park rules before any real flight.
