歷史 History

登上非洲之巔

To the Top of Africa

1848年的雪山報告、1889年首登，到1973年的國家公園
A report of snow in 1848, the first ascent in 1889, a national park in 1973

查加人世代住在吉力馬札羅山的南坡與東坡。他們在火山土壤上耕作。1848年，傳教士約翰內斯·雷布曼報告看見這座山。他是第一個描述這座山的歐洲人。他說赤道附近的山頂上有雪，當時許多人不相信。南坡的一條冰河，後來以他命名。1889年10月6日，漢斯·梅耶與路德維希·普切勒登上頂峰。這是第一次有紀錄的登頂。當地人約哈尼·勞沃，被認為是梅耶團隊的成員。來自潘加尼的姆維尼·阿馬尼，負責供應高地營地。

1912年，山頂冰的面積約有11.4平方公里。到2011年，已經不到1平方公里。將近一百年，冰減少了將近85%。研究者認為，冰河可能在幾十年內消失。國家獨立後，最高點改名為「烏呼魯峰」。烏呼魯在斯瓦希里語裡，意思是「自由」。1973年，吉力馬札羅山被劃為國家公園。當時只保護了山上的部分森林。2005年，公園邊界重劃，納入更多山地森林。森林是山下居民的水源。

1987年，吉力馬札羅國家公園列入世界遺產。登錄的理由，是它壯麗的自然景觀。今天，每年約有五萬人來攀登。平均約七到八成的人能登頂。從雪山的傳聞、第一次登頂，到國家公園，。吉力馬札羅一直吸引著人們往上走。

The Chagga have long lived on the southern and eastern slopes, farming the rich volcanic soil. In 1848 the missionary Johannes Rebmann reported seeing the mountain, the first European to describe it. He reported snow on a summit near the Equator, and many did not believe him. A glacier on the southern side was later named after him. On 6 October 1889 Hans Meyer and Ludwig Purtscheller reached the top, the first recorded ascent. Yohani Kinyala Lauwo, a local man, is recognised as a member of Meyer's team, and Mwini Amani of Pangani kept the high camps supplied.

In 1912 the ice on the summit covered about 11.4 square kilometres. By 2011 it was under 1 square kilometre. In nearly a century, almost 85 per cent of the ice was lost. Researchers think the glaciers may vanish within decades. After independence the highest point was renamed Uhuru Peak. Uhuru means freedom in Swahili. In 1973 Kilimanjaro became a national park, though only small corridors of forest were protected at first. In 2005 the boundary was redrawn to take in more montane forest, the source of water for the people below.

In 1987 Kilimanjaro National Park joined the World Heritage List for its outstanding natural beauty. Today some 50,000 trekkers come each year, and on average 70 to 80 per cent reach the top. From rumours of snow to the first ascent and a national park, Kilimanjaro has always drawn people upward.

人文 Culture

山腳下的家園

Homeland at the Foot

查加人的家園菜園、登山口的小鎮，與帶人上山的嚮導和挑夫
Chagga home gardens, the towns at the gates, and the guides and porters

吉力馬札羅山的南坡，是查加人的家園。肥沃的火山土壤，讓這裡成為重要的農業區。人們在山坡上種玉米和豆子，經營家園菜園與咖啡園。山上的森林，把雨水留住，供應農田。查加人過去分成多個小王國。這些王國在1963年被廢除。這一帶出產的咖啡，常以「吉力馬札羅」為名銷往各地。山的名字，也跟著咖啡走進世界各地的咖啡館。山腳下的莫希，是登山客的出發地。嚮導、挑夫與裝備，大多在這裡集合。

東南邊的馬蘭古登山口，是最早開放的路線之一。其他路線分別從南面和西面上山。登山客穿過雨林，再走進荒原。嚮導和挑夫，背著帳篷、食物和水，一路同行。高山上的空氣稀薄，登山需要時間適應。慢慢走，是在這座山上最常聽到的建議。登頂通常在半夜出發，在日出前後到達。站在烏呼魯峰上，非洲在腳下展開。1936年，海明威發表短篇小說《吉力馬札羅的雪》。赤道上的雪山，從此也成了文學裡的意象。

山的名字來源，至今仍有爭論。有人說是「偉大之山」，有人說是「白色的山」。也有人認為，它來自查加語的「無法攀登」。如今，每年有約五萬人嘗試登上它。登頂者在山頂拍照留念，。而山下的家園，靠著這座山的水與土地生活。從山腳的農田、登山口的小鎮，到雲上的頂峰，。吉力馬札羅是一座有人住在它身邊的山。

The southern slopes of Kilimanjaro are the homeland of the Chagga. Rich volcanic soil makes this an important farming region. People grow maize and beans, and keep home gardens and coffee farms. The forest above holds the rain that waters them. The Chagga were once organised into several small states, abolished in 1963. Coffee from these slopes is often sold under the Kilimanjaro name, carrying the mountain's name into cafés around the world. Moshi, at the foot of the mountain, is where climbers set out. Guides, porters and gear mostly gather here.

Marangu Gate on the south-east is one of the oldest routes up, and other routes climb from the south and west. Climbers pass through rainforest, then out onto the moorland. Guides and porters walk with them, carrying tents, food and water. The air is thin up high, and the body needs time to adjust. "Slowly, slowly" is the advice most often heard on this mountain. Summit attempts usually start at midnight and arrive around sunrise. From Uhuru Peak, Africa spreads out below. In 1936 Ernest Hemingway published his story "The Snows of Kilimanjaro". The snowy summit on the Equator became an image in literature too.

The origin of the mountain's name is still debated: "mountain of greatness", some say; "white mountain", say others. Others trace it to a Chagga word for "unclimbable". Today some 50,000 people a year try to climb it. Those who reach the top take their photographs, while the homes below live on the mountain's water and soil. From the farms at its foot and the towns at its gates to the summit above the clouds, Kilimanjaro is a mountain that people live beside.

自然 Nature

從雨林到冰河

From Rainforest to Glacier

一座山上的五個世界：雨林、荒原、高山荒漠與赤道冰河
Five worlds on one mountain: rainforest, moorland, alpine desert and equatorial ice

海拔1,800到2,800公尺，是一圈茂密的山地雨林。從空中看，像一條深綠色的帶子圍著山。森林接住從印度洋吹來的水氣。山下的溪流與泉水，大多從這裡開始。林中有黑白疣猴等靈長類。研究人員在森林帶裡，區分出好幾種不同的森林類型。森林之上，是寒冷乾燥的荒原。這裡長著巨型千里光和巨型半邊蓮。這些植物長得又高又奇特。它們適應了白天日曬、夜晚結凍的環境。

四千公尺以上，植物愈來愈稀少。高山荒漠白天炎熱，夜晚寒冷，雨水很少。馬文濟的岩峰上，只剩地衣與苔蘚。生命在這裡，退到最低限度。山頂上的富特文格勒冰河，是殘存的赤道冰河之一。冰的邊緣，是垂直的冰崖。南坡的雷布曼冰河，也在逐漸縮小。從1912年到2011年，山上的冰減少了將近85%。山下的農田一直延伸到森林邊緣。1973年設立的公園，只保護了小段森林走廊。

2005年擴大範圍後，更多山地森林受到保護。保護森林，就是保護山下的水源。從正上方看，基博火山口的冰，只剩零星幾塊。赤道上的冰，正在一年一年地變少。從雨林、荒原、荒漠到冰河，。一座山就是一趟從赤道走到極地的旅程。

Between 1,800 and 2,800 metres a dense ring of montane rainforest circles the mountain, a dark green band seen from the air. The forest catches moisture blowing in from the Indian Ocean. Most streams and springs below begin here. Black-and-white colobus and other primates live in the forest, and researchers distinguish several forest types within the belt. Above the forest lies cold, dry moorland, home to giant groundsels and giant lobelias. These plants grow tall and strange, adapted to sun by day and frost by night.

Above 4,000 metres plants grow ever more scarce. The alpine desert is hot by day, freezing by night, and dry. On the crags of Mawenzi only lichens and mosses cling on. Life here is pared down to the minimum. The Furtwängler Glacier on the summit is one of the last equatorial glaciers. Its edges are vertical ice cliffs. The Rebmann Glacier on the southern side is shrinking too. Between 1912 and 2011 the mountain lost almost 85 per cent of its ice. Below, farmland reaches right up to the forest edge. The park of 1973 protected only small forest corridors.

Since the 2005 expansion more montane forest is protected, and protecting the forest protects the water below. From straight above, only scattered patches of ice remain on Kibo's crater. Year by year, the ice on the Equator grows smaller. From rainforest and moorland to desert and ice, one mountain is a journey from the Equator to the poles.

地理 Geography

非洲屋脊

The Roof of Africa

三座火山錐、從草原到冰河的五個氣候帶
Three volcanic cones, and five climate zones from savanna to ice

坦尚尼亞的吉力馬札羅山，是非洲最高峰。最高點烏呼魯峰，海拔5,895公尺。它是世界上最高的獨立山峰。山不屬於任何山脈，直接從草原上拔起。吉力馬札羅山由三座火山錐組成。中間最高的是基博，東邊是馬文濟，西邊是希拉。基博火山的火山口寬約2.5公里。烏呼魯峰，就在火山口的邊緣上。火山口內還有羅伊施火山口，以及深350公尺的灰坑。這裡至今仍有冒著氣體的噴氣孔。

基博最後一次火山活動，約在十五萬到二十萬年前。它是一座休眠中的火山。東邊的馬文濟峰，海拔5,149公尺。它被侵蝕得很厲害，山頂佈滿尖銳的岩峰。基博與馬文濟之間，是寬約五公里的鞍部高原。這裡是一片荒涼的高山荒漠。西邊的希拉，是三座火山中最古老的一座。它的海拔約4,005公尺，頂部是一片高原。從山腳到山頂，海拔上升超過五千公尺。一路經過五個氣候帶。

海拔800到1,800公尺是灌木帶，。1,800到2,800公尺是雨林帶。2,800到4,000公尺是石南與荒原帶。4,000到5,000公尺，是高山荒漠。五千公尺以上，是極地般的山頂帶。赤道附近的山頂，竟然有冰河。三座火山、五個氣候帶、赤道上的冰，。吉力馬札羅把一整個大陸的景色，疊在一座山上。

Tanzania's Mount Kilimanjaro is the highest mountain in Africa. Its summit, Uhuru Peak, stands 5,895 metres above sea level. It is the highest free-standing mountain in the world, part of no range, rising straight from the plains. Kilimanjaro is made of three volcanic cones: Kibo, the highest, in the middle; Mawenzi to the east; Shira to the west. Kibo's caldera is about 2.5 kilometres wide. Uhuru Peak sits on its rim. Inside lie the Reusch Crater and the 350-metre-deep Ash Pit, where fumaroles still give off gas.

Kibo's last volcanic activity was some 150,000 to 200,000 years ago. It is a dormant volcano. To the east, Mawenzi reaches 5,149 metres. Deeply eroded, it bristles with jagged pinnacles. Between Kibo and Mawenzi lies the Saddle, about 5 kilometres wide, a bleak alpine desert. To the west, Shira is the oldest of the three cones. About 4,005 metres high, it is topped by a plateau. From foot to summit the mountain climbs more than 5,000 metres, through five climate zones.

From 800 to 1,800 metres is bushland; from 1,800 to 2,800, rainforest. From 2,800 to 4,000 metres come heather and moorland, and from 4,000 to 5,000, alpine desert. Above 5,000 metres lies an arctic summit zone, with glaciers near the Equator. Three volcanoes, five climate zones, ice on the Equator: Kilimanjaro stacks a continent's worth of scenery on one mountain.
