歷史 History

懸在空中的修道院

Monasteries in the Air

這裡是希臘中部塞薩利的邁泰奧拉，。岩柱頂上，蓋著一座座修道院。「邁泰奧拉」的意思是「高懸的」，。和「流星」這個字，有同樣的字源。九世紀起，隱修者搬上了這些岩柱；。到十一世紀，岩洞裡已經住著修士。他們在杜皮亞尼岩腳下的小教堂聚會禮拜，。那座十三世紀初的教堂，到今天還在使用。1344年，來自聖山阿陀斯的阿塔納修斯，。帶著追隨者來到邁泰奧拉。

1356到1372年間，他在最寬的岩柱上，。建起了大邁泰奧拉修道院。資助他們的，是當地領主西美昂・烏羅什，。他在1356年自稱「塞爾維亞人與希臘人的皇帝」。到了十六世紀，這裡一共有二十四座修道院；。保存到今天的，只剩六座。過去上下岩柱，只能靠長梯，。或坐在大網裡，被繩索吊上去。相傳繩子只有在「上帝讓它斷掉」時才換；。直到1920年代，才在岩石上鑿出階梯。

山腳下的卡蘭巴卡，十世紀叫做斯塔戈伊，。是拜占庭的要塞，也是主教的駐地。鎮上的聖母安眠主教座堂，。建於十一世紀末到十二世紀初，是唯一留存的中世紀建築。鄂圖曼帝國統治這裡，一直到1881年；。那一年，塞薩利併入希臘王國。第二次世界大戰期間，這裡遭到轟炸；。1988年，邁泰奧拉列入世界遺產。從岩洞裡的隱修者，到岩柱頂上的修道院，。這裡的信仰，一直離天空很近。

This is Meteora in Thessaly, central Greece, where monasteries stand on top of rock pillars. "Meteora" means "lofty" or "elevated", from the same root as "meteor". From the 9th century hermits moved onto the pinnacles; by the 11th century monks were living in the caves. They met for worship at a chapel at the foot of the Doupiani rock; the early 13th-century chapel there is still in use. In 1344 Athanasios, a monk from Mount Athos, brought his followers to Meteora.

Between 1356 and 1372, on the broadest rock, he founded the Monastery of Great Meteoron. They were sponsored by the local lord Simeon Uroš, who proclaimed himself "Emperor of Serbs and Greeks" in 1356. At their peak in the 16th century there were twenty-four monasteries; six survive today. For centuries the only way up was by long ladders, or in large nets hauled up on ropes. The story goes that ropes were replaced only "when the Lord let them break"; steps were cut into the rock only in the 1920s.

Kalabaka below was called Stagoi in the 10th century, a Byzantine fortress and the seat of a bishop. Its Cathedral of the Dormition, from the late 11th or early 12th century, is its only surviving medieval building. Ottoman rule lasted until 1881, when Thessaly passed to the Kingdom of Greece. The site was bombed in the Second World War; in 1988 Meteora became World Heritage. From hermits in caves to monasteries on the pinnacles, faith here has always lived close to the sky.

Over 600 codices, a convent of nuns, Holy Trinity in a Bond film, and the town at the foot of the rocks

大邁泰奧拉是最大的修道院，。海拔六百一十三公尺，比河谷高出四百一十五公尺以上。六座修道院登錄的一千一百二十四部手抄本，。有六百一十部收藏在大邁泰奧拉。瓦爾拉姆修道院海拔五百九十五公尺，。腳下的懸崖，比河谷高出三百七十三公尺。聖斯德望修道院海拔五百七十五公尺，。1961年起，成為修女的修道院。旁邊海拔五百一十公尺的庫庫拉斯峰，。俯瞰著這座修道院。

聖三一修道院在四百多公尺高的岩壁頂上，。穿過隧道，再爬一百多級石階，才到得了門口。它的全盛時期約有五十位修士，。到二十世紀初，只剩下五位。1981年的龐德電影《最高機密》，。把聖三一修道院拍成了反派的藏身處。它也出現在1957年的《海豚男孩》，。和1961年的《丁丁與金羊毛》。從卡蘭巴卡有一條三公里的步道，。可以一路走到聖三一修道院。

山腳下的卡蘭巴卡，。2021年鎮上約有八千五百人。聖施洗約翰教堂牆上的希臘銘文，。證明這裡古代有一個叫做艾吉尼翁的聚落。2018年，卡蘭巴卡所在的市鎮，。改名為「邁泰奧拉」。夏天，定期的遊覽巴士不斷開上山，。交通的壓力，也隨著遊客增加。手抄本、修女的祈禱與電影的鏡頭，。都在這些岩柱上留下了痕跡。

Great Meteoron is the largest monastery, at 613 metres, over 415 metres above the floor of the Pineios valley. Of the 1,124 codices catalogued in the six monasteries, 610 are kept at Great Meteoron. Varlaam monastery stands at 595 metres, on a cliff 373 metres above the valley floor. The Monastery of St Stephen stands at 575 metres; since 1961 it has been a convent of nuns. The 510-metre peak of Kukulas looks down on the convent.

Holy Trinity sits on a precipice over 400 metres high; you reach its door through tunnels and up more than a hundred stone steps. At its height it had about fifty monks; by the early 20th century five remained. The 1981 James Bond film For Your Eyes Only made Holy Trinity the villain's hideout. It also appears in Boy on a Dolphin (1957) and Tintin and the Golden Fleece (1961). A 3 km footpath leads from Kalabaka up to Holy Trinity.

Kalabaka, at the foot of the rocks, had about 8,500 people in the town in 2021. A Greek inscription in the wall of the Church of St John the Baptist shows an ancient settlement here called Aiginion. In 2018 the municipality of Kalabaka was renamed Meteora. In summer, regular bus tours climb to the monasteries, and traffic pressure grows with the visitors. Manuscripts, the nuns' prayers and the film camera have all left their mark on these rocks.

邁泰奧拉的岩柱之間，。記錄到一百六十三種鳥，其中一百二十種在這裡築巢。猛禽有黑鳶、長腿鵟，。小烏鵰和地中海隼。白兀鷲曾在這些懸崖上繁殖，。但族群已經崩潰。2016年，保護區裡只剩一對；。2020年，整個區域只剩下一隻。這裡記錄到十八種蝙蝠，。還有水獺、棕熊、狼與野貓。

河谷裡，長著東方懸鈴木的河岸森林；。附近的山丘，有特有的矢車菊。其中一種，就以卡蘭巴卡命名，。叫做「卡蘭巴卡矢車菊」。2020年，塞薩利的保護區管理單位成立，。負責照顧這裡的自然環境。緩衝區A禁止興建建築，。但正式的管理計畫，到現在還沒有完成。世界遺產的核心區約兩百七十二公頃，。緩衝區約一千八百八十四公頃。

聖三一修道院的岩頂上，。有一座零點八公頃的花園。2025年，世界自然保護聯盟的評估，。把這裡的前景列為「良好，但有些問題要注意」。同一份評估認為，這裡受到的威脅「低」，。保護與管理則「有些令人關切」。猛禽、蝙蝠與稀有的花，。和修士們一起，住在這些岩柱之間。

Among the pillars of Meteora 163 bird species have been recorded, 120 of them nesting here. The raptors include black kites and long-legged buzzards, lesser spotted eagles and lanner falcons. Egyptian vultures once bred on these cliffs, but their population has collapsed. By 2016 only one pair remained in the site; by 2020 only one bird was left in the wider area. Eighteen species of bat have been recorded here, along with otters, brown bears, wolves and wildcats.

Riverine forests of oriental plane grow in the valley, and endemic knapweeds grow on the nearby hills. One of them is named after the town: Centaurea kalambakensis. In 2020 a management unit for Thessaly's protected areas was set up to look after the natural environment here. Building is forbidden in buffer zone A, but a management plan is still not available. The World Heritage core covers about 272 hectares, with a buffer zone of about 1,884.

On the summit rock of Holy Trinity there is a garden of 0.8 hectares. IUCN's 2025 assessment rated the outlook as "good, with some concerns". The same assessment rates the threats as low, and protection and management as "some concern". Raptors, bats and rare flowers share these pillars with the monks.

地理 Geography

三角洲變成的岩柱

Pillars from an Ancient Delta

湖邊三角洲的砂岩與礫岩、六千萬年前的抬升、沿著斷層雕出的岩柱
Sandstone and conglomerate from a lakeside delta, uplift 60 million years ago, pillars carved along faults

邁泰奧拉的岩柱，是砂岩與礫岩，。不是火山留下的岩頸。數百萬年間，溪流把石頭、砂與泥，。帶進湖邊的三角洲，慢慢堆成了礫岩。大約六千萬年前，一連串地殼運動，。把這片沉積抬升成高原，也產生了垂直的斷層。水、風和劇烈的溫差，。沿著斷層侵蝕岩石，留下一根根岩柱。岩柱高出地面四百多公尺，。從平地上看，就像一座座石塔。

過去的隱修小室，。最高在比平地高出五百五十公尺的地方。大邁泰奧拉海拔六百一十三公尺，。是修道院之中最高的一座。它的正南方是「魔鬼塔」，。旁邊的伊普西洛特拉岩上，還留著一座修道院的廢墟。魯薩努修道院海拔四百八十四公尺，。比大邁泰奧拉低了一百多公尺。岩柱群位在卡蘭巴卡與卡斯特拉基村之間，。靠近皮尼奧斯河與品都斯山脈。

卡蘭巴卡位在皮尼奧斯河的左岸，。岩柱一帶的水，都流進這條河谷。瓦爾拉姆修道院下方的南側，。有一個叫做「龍洞」的岩洞。化學分析推算，這些岩壁大約有六千萬年。而侵蝕，到今天都還在繼續。湖邊的三角洲、抬升的高原與一道道斷層，。一起雕出了這片懸在空中的岩柱。

The pillars of Meteora are sandstone and conglomerate, not volcanic plugs. Over millions of years streams carried stone, sand and mud into a delta at the edge of a lake, building up the conglomerate. About 60 million years ago a series of earth movements pushed these deposits up into a plateau, cracked by vertical faults. Water, wind and extremes of temperature wore the rock away along the faults, leaving pillar after pillar. The pillars rise more than 400 metres above the ground, like towers of stone from the plain below.

The hermits' cells reached as high as 550 metres above the plain. Great Meteoron, at 613 metres, is the highest of the monasteries. Directly south of it stands the Devil's Tower, and on the Ypsilotera rock beside it are the ruins of a former monastery. Rousanou monastery stands at 484 metres, more than a hundred metres below Great Meteoron. The pillars rise between Kalabaka and the village of Kastraki, near the Pineios river and the Pindus Mountains.

Kalabaka lies on the left bank of the Pineios, and the water of the rocks drains into its valley. Below Varlaam monastery, to the south, lies a cave called the Dragon Cave. Chemical analysis puts the cliffs at about 60 million years old, and the weathering goes on today. A lakeside delta, a raised plateau and line after line of faults together carved these pillars in the air.
