這裡是印尼的科莫多國家公園，。在松巴哇島與佛羅勒斯島之間。公園裡有科莫多、林卡、帕達爾三座大島，。和二十六座小島，全都是火山島。1910年，「陸地鱷魚」的傳聞，。傳到荷蘭軍官范斯泰恩・范亨斯布魯克的耳中。1912年，彼得・烏文斯發表論文，。科莫多龍從此進入科學的紀錄。當地人叫牠「歐拉」，。也叫牠「陸地鱷魚」。

1926年，美國人伯登的探險隊，帶回十二具標本和兩隻活龍；。這趟探險，啟發了1933年的電影《金剛》。1928年，科莫多村只住著三十個人；。1930年前後，林卡島上約有兩百五十人。1938年，帕達爾島和林卡島的一部分，。成為自然保護區。1965年，科莫多島也成為自然保護區；。1977年1月，這裡列入聯合國教科文組織的生物圈保護區。1980年，科莫多、帕達爾、林卡、吉利莫唐島和周圍海域，。一起成為國家公園。

1984年，公園範圍擴大到二十一萬九千多公頃；。1991年，列入世界遺產。原本的科莫多族人，在1980年代消失了；。今天的居民，是來自蘇拉威西的布吉斯人和比馬人。2017年，科莫多村有一千七百六十四人；。2007年，公園裡九成七的居民，只靠海維生。2005年，公園成為東協遺產公園；。2018年，拉布安巴焦被列為五大「超級優先」觀光地之一。從傳聞中的陸地鱷魚，到世界遺產，。這些島嶼的故事，都圍著同一種動物。

This is Komodo National Park in Indonesia, between the islands of Sumbawa and Flores. The park has three large islands, Komodo, Rinca and Padar, and 26 smaller ones, all volcanic. In 1910 rumours of a "land crocodile" reached the Dutch lieutenant van Steyn van Hensbroek. In 1912 Peter Ouwens published a paper on the animal, bringing the Komodo dragon into the scientific record. Local names include ora and buaya darat, "land crocodile".

W. Douglas Burden's 1926 expedition brought back 12 preserved specimens and two live dragons; it inspired the 1933 film King Kong. In 1928 thirty people lived in Komodo village; around 1930 about 250 lived on Rinca. In 1938 Padar and part of Rinca became nature reserves. Komodo Island became a nature reserve in 1965, and in January 1977 a UNESCO biosphere reserve. In 1980 Komodo, Padar, Rinca, Gili Motang and the surrounding waters were declared a national park.

In 1984 the park was extended to 219,322 hectares; in 1991 it became World Heritage. The original Komodo people had died out by the 1980s; today the villagers are Bugis from Sulawesi and Bima people. In 2017 Komodo village had 1,764 people; in 2007, 97% of park residents lived only from the sea. In 2005 the park became an ASEAN Heritage Park; in 2018 the gateway town of Labuan Bajo was named one of five super-priority tourism destinations. From a rumoured land crocodile to World Heritage, these islands' story has always circled one animal.

佛羅勒斯島西端的拉布安巴焦，。是前往科莫多國家公園的門戶。它是西曼加萊縣的首府，。科莫多機場，離鎮中心大約三公里。2009年，來訪的遊客有三萬六千人；。到2019年，增加到二十二萬一千七百零三人。不加限制的話，預估2030年會有二十八萬多人，。2045年會有將近四十八萬人。2019年，鎮上有十三家五星級旅館，。和六十八家沒有星級的旅館。

Labuan Bajo, on the western tip of Flores, is the gateway to Komodo National Park. It is the capital of West Manggarai Regency, and Komodo Airport is about 3 km from the town centre. In 2009 the park had 36,000 visitors; by 2019 there were 221,703. Without limits, visitors are projected to reach 283,686 by 2030 and 479,240 by 2045. In 2019 the town had 13 five-star hotels and 68 hotels without a star rating.

From 2017 to 2023 the built-up area grew by 246 hectares; the park and coast produce an average of 13 tonnes of rubbish a day. Day trips run by small boat from Labuan Bajo to Rinca. At Loh Buaya there is a jetty and a park office, and rangers guide visitors on short or medium walks. Reports describe a "Jurassic Park" tourism development on Rinca; the government says environmental impact assessments will now be required. From 1974 to 2012 the park recorded 24 dragon attacks on people, five of them fatal.

Park residents have on average about four years of schooling, and fewer than 10% of graduates go on to high school. The park has six well-known dive sites, among them Manta Alley and Batu Bolong. In June 2008 five divers drifted 32 km from their boat; two days later they were found on Rinca's south coast, living on shellfish. The park has 94 staff, who ran 79 land and marine patrols a year in 2022–2024. Tourism has brought hotels and jobs, and also rubbish and pressure.

In 2023 the park held 3,396 Komodo dragons, up from 2,897 in 2018. In 2021 the IUCN listed the species as Endangered. Males reach 3 metres and up to about 150 kg; females are about 2 to 2.5 metres long. They eat mostly deer, wild pigs and carrion, can eat 80% of their body mass at once and survive on 12 meals a year. It has about sixty serrated teeth, replaced every 40 days; it can sprint at 20 km/h and smell carrion 4 to 9.5 km away.

Females lay up to 20 eggs that incubate for 7 to 8 months; in one study 60% of nests were in mounds of the orange-footed scrubfowl. Komodo Island has about 1,700 dragons and Rinca about 1,300; Flores across the strait has about 2,000. The islands are also home to Timor deer, water buffalo, wild boar, crab-eating macaques and the endemic Rinca rat. Dragons were last seen on Padar in 1975; in 2004 a survey reported at least twelve, including a hatchling. Seventy-two bird species have been recorded here; the lesser sulphur-crested cockatoo is critically endangered.

The sea holds 385 species of hard coral and more than 1,000 reef fish species. Ten dolphin species, five sea turtles and at least fourteen cetaceans live here, along with dugongs and manta rays. Fringing and patch reefs are best developed on Komodo's northeast coast; these waters are part of the Coral Triangle. Illegal fishing was reported to have doubled between 2018 and 2019; boat anchoring and coral bleaching are rated high threats. Dragons on land and coral in the sea make these dry islands a treasure house of life.

The national park covers 1,733 square kilometres, of which just 603 are land; the rest is sea. It lies in a "shatter belt" where two continental plates meet, in the Wallacea region between Australian and Sunda ecosystems. The islands are volcanic in origin; the park's highest point is 735 metres. Annual rainfall is only 800 to 1,000 mm, among the driest in Indonesia. The dry season runs from May to October, with temperatures of around 40 °C.

The hillsides are dry savanna, with monsoon forest and patches of near cloud forest. Padar is the third-largest island in the park, with four deep bays between steep volcanic ridges. Most of its beaches are white sand, and some are grey or pink. Strong currents run between the islands, drawing sea turtles, whales, dolphins and dugongs. After the 1984 extension the sea made up 60% of the park's area.

Labuan Bajo has a tropical savanna climate: dry from April to November, with heavy rain from December to March. Mitochondrial DNA shows that the dragon's closest relative is the Australian lace monitor. Fossils from Queensland date back about 3.8 million years; the oldest records on Flores are about 1.4 million years old. With climate change its habitat is projected to shrink by between 8.4% and 71% by 2050. Volcanic islands between two continents have kept a giant lizard from the deep past.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

印尼規定：外國遊客無法登記無人機（只有印尼公民與公司能透過SIDOPI-GO登記）；未管制空域的高度上限120公尺，機場安全區或管制空域須事先許可（官方曾回覆至少離機場15公里），離禁航與限制區至少500公尺，不得飛越人群，原則上只能在日出到日落之間飛。科莫多國家公園內沒有有效的特別許可（SIMAKSI，透過SIORA申請，至少提前七天）就禁止使用無人機，還要繳無人機使用費。實際飛行前請先確認最新規定。

Indonesia: tourists cannot register drones (only Indonesian citizens and companies can, through SIDOPI-GO); the limit is 120 m in uncontrolled airspace, airport safety zones and controlled airspace need a permit (one official answer gave at least 15 km from airports), keep 500 m from no-fly and restricted areas, never fly over crowds, and fly only between sunrise and sunset. In Komodo National Park drone use is prohibited without a valid SIMAKSI special permit (apply through SIORA at least 7 days ahead), and a drone fee is charged. Check the current rules before any real flight.
