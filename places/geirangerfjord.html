歷史 History

崖上的農場

Farms on the Cliffs

峭壁上的農家、遊船時代，與2005年的世界遺產
Cliff-ledge farms, the age of cruise ships, and World Heritage in 2005

峽灣兩側的峭壁上，有幾處廢棄的山中農場。斯卡格夫洛、克尼夫斯夫洛和布倫貝格，是最多人造訪的幾處。農家在狹窄的岩棚上，開出小塊田地。他們靠峽灣的船，和外面的世界往來。斯卡格夫洛可以從蓋倫格村步行到達。其他農場，則要搭船才能抵達。克尼夫斯夫洛位在七姐妹瀑布附近。地方團體修復了一些老農舍，讓後人看見山中生活。這些農場後來陸續被放棄。岩棚上的田地，又慢慢還給了山。

蓋倫格村的教堂，是一座八角形的木造教堂。它立在村子上方，俯瞰著峽灣。十九世紀起，遊客開始搭船進入峽灣。之後，大型郵輪成了蓋倫格最常見的訪客。蓋倫格與海勒蘇爾特之間，有渡輪航線。渡輪沿途經過七姐妹瀑布。公路的開通，讓峽灣不再只靠船對外聯繫。老鷹之路的十一個髮夾彎，從北岸爬上山。1934年，鄰近的塔峽灣發生山崩海嘯。十七公尺高的海浪，奪走了41條人命。

這段記憶，讓人們認真看待奧克內斯的裂縫。2004年，斯特蘭達設立了監測中心。2005年，蓋倫格峽灣與納柔依峽灣一起列入世界遺產。合稱「挪威西峽灣」。2026年1月起，一萬總噸以下的客船，在遺產峽灣內必須零排放。較大的郵輪，則從2032年起適用。從崖上的農家、郵輪的時代，到零排放的峽灣，。人們和這片峽灣相處的方式，一直在改變。

On the cliffs above the fjord stand a few abandoned mountain farms. Skageflå, Knivsflå and Blomberg are the most visited. Families cleared small fields on narrow rock ledges, and reached the outside world by boat on the fjord. Skageflå can be reached on foot from Geiranger; the others need a boat. Knivsflå lies near the Seven Sisters. A local society has restored some of the old farm buildings. In time the farms were abandoned, and the ledge fields slowly went back to the mountain.

Geiranger Church is an octagonal timber church. It stands above the village, looking down the fjord. From the 19th century, visitors began to come into the fjord by boat. Later, big cruise ships became the commonest visitors to Geiranger. A ferry runs between Geiranger and Hellesylt, passing the Seven Sisters on the way. Roads ended the fjord's dependence on boats. The Eagle Road climbs the north side in eleven hairpins. In 1934 a rockslide tsunami struck the nearby Tafjord. A wave about 17 metres high killed 41 people.

That memory is why Åkernes is taken so seriously. In 2004 a monitoring centre was set up in Stranda. In 2005 the Geirangerfjord and the Nærøyfjord joined the World Heritage List together as the West Norwegian Fjords. From 1 January 2026, passenger ships under 10,000 gross tons must be zero-emission here. Larger cruise ships must follow from 2032. From cliff-ledge farms and the age of cruise ships to a zero-emission fjord, the way people live with this fjord keeps changing.

人文 Culture

瀑布的傳說

Legends of the Waterfalls

七姐妹與求婚者、山中農家，與從世界來的旅人
Seven sisters and a suitor, mountain farms, and travellers from everywhere

北岸的七姐妹瀑布，由七道並排的水流組成。當地傳說，七位姐妹在山上嬉戲。對岸的「求婚者」瀑布，正努力向姐妹們求婚。他隔著峽灣，始終沒有成功。附近的「新娘面紗」，讓故事更完整。三道瀑布，被編進同一個傳說。峭壁上的農家，曾在這些岩棚上耕作、放牧。要到峽灣邊，得沿著陡峭的小路下山。今天，健行客從蓋倫格村走上斯卡格夫洛。在岩棚上，可以同時看見瀑布和峽灣。

蓋倫格村只是一個小村，夏天卻擠滿了遊客。郵輪停在峽灣中，接駁船把乘客送上岸。八角形的木造教堂，是村子的地標。從教堂邊，就能俯瞰整個峽灣口。弗呂達爾峽谷的觀景點，是拍攝峽灣的經典角度。很多旅遊照片，都從這裡拍出。夏天，遊客開車上達爾斯尼巴。在約一千五百公尺高處，同時看到雪山與峽灣。老鷹之路的觀景彎，是另一個欣賞峽灣的地方。從這裡俯瞰，就像用老鷹的眼睛看峽灣。

峽灣另一端的海勒蘇爾特，村中有一道瀑布穿過。渡輪在這兩個村子之間來回。2026年起，遺產峽灣開始實施零排放規定。電動或零排放的船，讓峽灣更安靜、更乾淨。七姐妹與求婚者的故事，還在峽灣上流傳；。只是今天，聽故事的人，來自世界各地。

On the north shore, the Seven Sisters are seven streams side by side. Local legend says seven sisters dance on the mountainside. Across the fjord, the Suitor is forever trying to woo the sisters, and across the water he never succeeds. The nearby Bridal Veil completes the tale: three waterfalls woven into one legend. Farm families once tilled and grazed these cliff ledges. Reaching the fjord meant a steep path down. Today walkers climb from Geiranger to Skageflå, where the ledge shows waterfalls and fjord together.

Geiranger is a small village, yet crowded with visitors in summer. Cruise ships anchor in the fjord and tenders bring passengers ashore. The octagonal timber church is the village landmark. From beside it you look straight down the fjord. The Flydalsjuvet viewpoint is the classic angle on the fjord. Countless travel photographs were taken here. In summer, visitors drive up to Dalsnibba, to see snow peaks and fjord together from about 1,500 metres. The bend on the Eagle Road is another place to take in the fjord, seen from here as if through an eagle's eyes.

At the other end, Hellesylt has a waterfall running through the village. Ferries shuttle between the two villages. From 2026, zero-emission rules began to apply in the World Heritage fjords. Electric and zero-emission boats make the fjord quieter and cleaner. The tale of the Seven Sisters and the Suitor still drifts over the fjord; only now the listeners come from all over the world.

自然 Nature

峭壁、瀑布與海水

Cliffs, Falls and Sea Water

海水與淡水交會的峽灣，與峭壁上的生命
A fjord where salt meets fresh water, and life on the cliffs

峽灣裡的水，是從外海灌進來的海水。山上的融雪與瀑布，又不斷帶進淡水。春夏融雪時，瀑布的水量最大。七姐妹的七道水流，這時最清楚。陡峭的岩壁上，只有少數植物能紮根。岩縫與岩棚，是它們的立足點。岩棚上的老農場，曾是放牧牲畜的地方。放牧停止後，草地慢慢變回灌叢與樹林。峽灣兩側的山坡下段，長著森林。愈往上，樹愈少，最後只剩岩石與積雪。

達爾斯尼巴一帶，初夏仍有積雪。從海面到山頂，短短幾公里內，就跨越了好幾種氣候。山上的冰河地形，留下許多小湖。湖水，最後也流向峽灣。蓋倫格河從山上帶來淡水，在村子流進峽灣。河口一帶，淡水浮在較重的海水上面。峽灣上空，常有猛禽在山壁的上升氣流裡盤旋。牠們從高處，俯瞰整片水面。山也在變。奧克內斯的裂縫，是峽灣地形仍在活動的證據。

零排放的船，減少了峽灣裡的空氣與噪音汙染。這對峽灣的生物，也是好消息。從正上方看，河流、村莊和峽灣，連在一起。水從雪山流下，最後回到海裡。海水、瀑布、峭壁與森林，。蓋倫格峽灣把海與山，放進了同一個畫面。

The water in the fjord is sea water from the open ocean. Snowmelt and waterfalls keep pouring fresh water in. In spring and early summer, snowmelt makes the falls fullest, and the seven streams of the Sisters show most clearly. Few plants can root on such steep rock. Cracks and ledges are their foothold. The old ledge farms were once grazing land. Since grazing stopped, meadows have been turning back to scrub and woodland. Forest clothes the lower slopes on either side. Higher up the trees thin out, leaving rock and snow.

Around Dalsnibba, snow lingers into early summer. From sea to summit, a few kilometres span several climates. Glacial landforms up here hold many small lakes, whose water also ends up in the fjord. The Geirangelva brings fresh water down to the fjord at the village. At its mouth, fresh water floats on top of the heavier sea water. Birds of prey often circle in the updrafts along the walls, watching the water from high above. The mountains are changing too. The crack at Åkernes is evidence that the fjord landscape is still active.

Zero-emission boats cut air and noise pollution in the fjord, good news for the life it holds as well. From straight above, river, village and fjord join up. Water runs down from the snow and returns to the sea. Sea water, waterfalls, cliffs and forest: the Geirangerfjord fits sea and mountain into a single frame.

地理 Geography

冰河挖出的海灣

A Sea Inlet Dug by Ice

十五公里長的峽灣、懸谷瀑布，與一座正在裂開的山
A 15-km fjord, waterfalls from hanging valleys, and a mountain that is cracking

挪威西部的蓋倫格峽灣，長約十五公里。它從松呂爾文峽灣分出，再往外連到斯圖爾峽灣。峽灣兩側是近乎垂直的山壁。這是冰河時期的冰河，沿著河谷往下挖出的深谷。冰河融化後，海水灌進谷裡，形成峽灣。所以峽灣是海，而不是湖。北岸的七姐妹瀑布，從高處的懸谷落下。主谷被冰河挖得比支谷深，支流只能從崖上直落。對岸是「求婚者」瀑布。附近還有一道「新娘面紗」瀑布。

新娘面紗從一道岩緣落下。逆光時，水幕薄得像一層面紗。峽灣最內側，蓋倫格河流進海裡。蓋倫格村，就建在河口的小平地上。從村子往南，公路沿著山坡爬升。每升高一段，峽灣就看得更完整。達爾斯尼巴觀景台，海拔約一千五百公尺。從這裡往下看，峽灣像一條細長的藍色彩帶。北岸的老鷹之路，有十一個髮夾彎。公路在陡坡上，一彎一彎地爬上去。

西北方的奧克內斯山，山體上有一道深約七十公尺的裂縫。裂縫每年最多擴大約九公分。如果山體崩落入海，可能引發大海嘯。海浪大約十分鐘就會到達蓋倫格和海勒蘇爾特。研究人員用雷射與儀器，日夜監測裂縫。一旦偵測到加速，就會啟動疏散。從正上方看，峽灣窄而彎曲。兩岸山頂與海面的落差，超過一千公尺。冰河挖谷、海水灌入、瀑布從懸谷落下，。蓋倫格峽灣，是冰與海共同留下的地形。

Western Norway's Geirangerfjord is about 15 kilometres long. It branches off the Sunnylvsfjorden, itself a branch of the Storfjorden. Near-vertical mountain walls rise on both sides. Ice Age glaciers carved this trough by deepening an older river valley. When the ice melted, the sea flooded in and made a fjord. So a fjord is sea, not a lake. On the north shore, the Seven Sisters fall from a hanging valley. Ice deepened the main valley more than its side valleys, so the streams spill over the cliffs. Across the water is the Suitor, and nearby falls the Bridal Veil.

The Bridal Veil drops over a single rock edge. With the sun behind it, the water looks as thin as a veil. At the head of the fjord, the Geirangelva river runs into the sea. Geiranger village sits on the small flat at its mouth. South of the village the road climbs the slope, and with every bend the fjord opens further. The Dalsnibba viewpoint stands about 1,500 metres up. From here the fjord looks like a thin blue ribbon. On the north side, the Eagle Road climbs in eleven hairpin bends, zigzagging up the steep slope.

To the north-west, the mountain at Åkernes has a crack about 70 metres deep. It widens by as much as 9 centimetres a year. If the rock mass falls into the fjord, it could set off a huge tsunami, reaching Geiranger and Hellesylt in about ten minutes. Lasers and instruments watch the crack day and night. If it starts to accelerate, evacuation begins. From straight above, the fjord is narrow and winding. The drop from summit to sea exceeds a thousand metres. Ice that dug the valley, sea that flooded it, waterfalls from hanging valleys: the Geirangerfjord is a landscape left by ice and sea together.
