歷史 History

從信仰之山到世界遺產

From Sacred Peak to World Heritage

從713年的文獻、江戶的登山信仰，到1964年的山頂雷達
From a record of 713 and Edo pilgrims to a summit radar in 1964

旁白全文 Full narration

北麓的北口本宮富士淺間神社，是吉田口登山道的起點。數百年來，從江戶來的信徒大多從這裡出發。傳說日本武尊在西元110年，在附近遙拜富士山。神社在788年遷到現在的位置。「富士」之名，最早見於713年的《常陸國風土記》。十二世紀起，這座山成為修行的道場。江戶時代，富士講的信徒結伴登山。十八世紀起，登山道沿途陸續建起小屋與神社。十九世紀中葉起，信徒可在忍野八海一天完成淨身巡禮。每處湧泉旁，都立著一座龍王石碑。

當時女性被禁止登上山頂。1832年，高山辰女扮男裝登頂，是第一位登頂的女性。1867年，英國公使夫人范妮·帕克斯也登上山頂。1872年，明治政府正式解除女性登山的禁令。1707年，寶永大地震發生後49天，富士山噴發。火山灰一路落到江戶。火山灰覆蓋農作物，歉收引發了大範圍的饑荒。寶永山，就是那次噴發在山腰堆出的小火山。明治以後，登富士山不只是修行，也成了休閒活動。今天超過一半的登山者，從吉田口的五合目出發。

1936年，富士山一帶劃入富士箱根伊豆國立公園。公園範圍從富士山一路延伸到伊豆半島。1964年8月15日，山頂的富士山雷達完工。它能觀測八百公里以外的颱風。雷達在1999年停用。2001年，雷達罩被移到山麓富士吉田市的博物館。新倉山淺間公園的忠靈塔，建於1962年，紀念戰爭中的亡者。爬上398級石階，五重塔與富士山就在同一個畫面裡。南麓的富士山本宮淺間大社，也是世界遺產的構成資產。山頂八合目以上，大多屬於它的奧宮境內。

2013年6月22日，富士山列入世界遺產。共有25個構成資產，分布在山上與山麓。從遙拜、苦行到休閒登山，。人們登上這座山的理由，一直在改變。

Kitaguchi Hongū Fuji Sengen Shrine on the north foot is where the Yoshida Trail begins. For centuries most pilgrims from Edo set out from here. Legend says Prince Yamato Takeru paid homage to Fuji near here in the year 110. The shrine was set up on its present site in 788. The name first appears as Fuji no Yama in a gazetteer of 713. From the 12th century it became a centre of ascetic training. In the Edo period, Fuji-kō pilgrim societies climbed the mountain together. From the 18th century huts and shrines rose along the trails. From the mid-1800s, pilgrims could make their purification round here in one day. Each of the eight springs has a stone to one of the dragon kings.

Women were then barred from the summit. In 1832 Tatsu Takayama, dressed as a man, became the first woman to reach the top. In 1867 Fanny Parkes, wife of the British envoy, also reached the summit. In 1872 the Meiji government formally lifted the ban on women. In 1707, 49 days after the great Hōei earthquake, Fuji erupted. Its ash fell as far as Edo. The ash smothered crops, and failed harvests brought widespread hunger. Mount Hōei is the small cone that eruption piled up on the flank. After the Meiji Restoration, climbing Fuji became recreation as well as devotion. Today more than half of all climbers start on the Yoshida Trail.

In 1936 the Fuji area became part of Fuji-Hakone-Izu National Park. The park reaches from Mount Fuji to the Izu Peninsula. On 15 August 1964 the Mount Fuji radar on the summit was completed. It could watch typhoons more than 800 kilometres away. The radar was switched off in 1999. In 2001 its dome was moved to a museum in Fujiyoshida below. The Chureito Pagoda in Arakurayama Sengen Park was built in 1962 as a war memorial. Climb its 398 steps and pagoda and mountain share one view. Fujisan Hongū Sengen Taisha on the south foot is also part of the World Heritage site. Most of the mountain above the 8th Station belongs to its inner shrine.

On 22 June 2013 Fujisan joined the World Heritage List. Its 25 component sites lie on the mountain and around its foot. From distant worship and ascetic training to holiday climbing, the reasons people climb this mountain keep changing.

人文 Culture

信仰的對象與藝術的泉源

Sacred Place, Source of Art

淺間神社、富士講、浮世繪，與從海邊望見的富士
Sengen shrines, pilgrim societies, woodblock prints, and Fuji seen from the shore

富士山不只是日本最高峰，也是信仰的對象。古人相信，山頂火口裡住著淺間大神。南麓富士宮的富士山本宮淺間大社，是祭祀這座山的神社。八合目以上的山頂，大多屬於它的奧宮境內。白絲瀑布寬約兩百公尺，像無數白絲垂下。1936年起，它被列為日本的天然紀念物。駿河灣邊的三保松原，松林沿著約七公里的海岸。隔著海灣遠望富士，是許多畫家筆下的景色。傳說羽衣天女降落在這裡的「羽衣之松」。這棵老松樹據說已有約六百五十年。

三保松原距富士山四十多公里，也是世界遺產的構成資產。世界遺產保護的，不只是山，也包括看山的地方。葛飾北齋的《富嶽三十六景》，在1826到1833年間出版。這組版畫後來深深影響了西方藝術。河口湖是五湖中唯一有島的湖。湖岸的富士山景色，常出現在海報與廣告上。北口本宮富士淺間神社的大鳥居上，寫著「三國第一山」。鳥居是神社的入口，也被視為富士山的入口。神社建築大多建於富士講興盛的十七、十八世紀。參道兩旁，是高大的古松。

忍野八海的八處湧泉，由富士山的地下水湧出。它們是國家天然紀念物，也是世界遺產的一部分。新倉山淺間公園約有650棵櫻花樹。春天，櫻花、五重塔與富士山構成同一幅畫面。江戶的富士講信徒，把登頂當作修行。各地還堆起小型的「富士塚」，讓不能遠行的人也能參拜。每年夏天，成千上萬的人登上山頂參拜。許多人在山頂迎接日出，稱為「御來光」。UNESCO把富士山列為文化遺產，而不是自然遺產。理由有兩項：活著的山岳信仰，與它啟發的藝術。

Fuji is not only Japan's highest peak but an object of worship. People believed the deity Asama no Okami dwelt in the summit crater. Fujisan Hongū Sengen Taisha in Fujinomiya, on the south foot, is a shrine to the mountain. Most of the summit above the 8th Station is the ground of its inner shrine. Shiraito Falls, about 200 m wide, hangs like countless white threads. It has been protected as a Natural Monument since 1936. On Suruga Bay, the pines of Miho no Matsubara line about seven km of shore. Fuji seen across the bay became a favourite view of painters. Legend says a celestial maiden came down at the Hagoromo Pine here. The old pine is said to be about 650 years old.

Miho, more than 40 km from the summit, is part of the World Heritage site too. What is protected is not only the mountain but the places it is seen from. Hokusai's Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji appeared between 1826 and 1833. The prints went on to have a profound influence on Western art. Lake Kawaguchi is the only one of the five with an island. Its views of Fuji fill posters and advertisements. The shrine's great torii is signed 'The Greatest Mountain of the Three Countries'. It marks the entrance to the shrine, and to Mount Fuji itself. Most of its buildings date from the 17th and 18th centuries, when Fuji-kō flourished. Ancient pines line the approach.

The eight springs of Oshino Hakkai rise from Fuji's groundwater. They are a Natural Monument and part of the World Heritage site. Arakurayama Sengen Park has around 650 cherry trees. In spring, blossom, pagoda and Fuji fall into one frame. For the Fuji-kō societies of Edo, reaching the top was a spiritual exercise. Small mounds called Fujizuka let those who could not travel climb in miniature. Every summer thousands climb to the shrine on the summit. Many wait at the top to greet the sunrise, called goraikō. UNESCO listed Fuji as a cultural site, not a natural one. Two reasons: a living tradition of mountain worship, and the art it inspired.

From shrines and springs to the pines on the shore, Fuji's culture is spread across the mountain and all around it.

自然 Nature

從樹海到高山荒原

From Lava Forest to Alpine Desert

熔岩上的森林、湧泉與瀑布，以及森林之上的世界
Forest on lava, springs and falls, and the world above the trees

富士山西北麓，有一片長在熔岩上的森林。它叫青木原樹海，地面是西元864年噴發的熔岩。森林面積約三十五平方公里。從空中看，樹冠連成一片，像一片綠色的海。林中有扁柏、柳杉、赤松和鐵杉，。也有槭樹、蒙古櫟與樺木。多孔的熔岩會吸收聲音，林中顯得格外安靜。熔岩底下有鳴澤冰穴、富岳風穴等熔岩洞。鳴澤冰穴裡，一年四季都結著冰。熔岩表面先冷卻，內部流空，就留下這樣的洞穴。

樹海北邊是西湖，西岸緊鄰著森林。湖和森林，都是同一次噴發塑造的。山上的雨雪滲進熔岩，變成地下水。在忍野村，地下水湧出地面，形成八處泉池。西南麓的白絲瀑布，高約二十公尺，寬約兩百公尺。不遠處，是高約二十五公尺的音止瀑布。瀑布屬於富士箱根伊豆國立公園。1990年，它被選入日本瀑布百選。往山上走，森林的樹種隨海拔改變。海拔一千到一千八百公尺，以米栂等鐵杉為主。

一千八百到兩千兩百公尺，換成白檜曾冷杉。吉田口五合目一帶，已接近森林的上緣。寶永火口一帶，三百年前的火山砂礫仍大片裸露。植物要在鬆散的砂礫上紮根，相當困難。山頂一帶，幾乎只剩岩石與砂礫。強風與低溫，讓這裡的植物十分稀少。富士山孤立在平原之上，山頂常戴著一頂「笠雲」。雲的形狀，透露著高空的風。山腳的湖泊，海拔都在八百公尺以上。山中湖湖面海拔980公尺，是五湖中最高的一座。

熔岩上的森林、從山裡湧出的泉水、森林之上的荒原，。同一座火山，養出了好幾個不同的世界。

On Fuji's north-west foot, a forest grows on lava. This is Aokigahara; its floor is lava from the eruption of 864. The forest covers about 35 square kilometres. From the air its canopy merges into one green sea. Its trees include Japanese cypress, sugi cedar, red pine and hemlock, along with maples, Mongolian oak and birch. The porous lava absorbs sound, so the forest is unusually quiet. Beneath it lie lava caves such as the Narusawa Ice Cave and the Fugaku Wind Cave. Inside the Narusawa Ice Cave, ice stays frozen all year round. Such caves form when lava crusts over and its molten core drains away.

North of the forest lies Lake Sai, its western shore against the trees. Lake and forest were both shaped by the same eruption. Rain and snow on the mountain soak into the lava and become groundwater. In Oshino it surfaces again in eight spring-fed ponds. Shiraito Falls on the south-west foot is about 20 m high and 200 m wide. Nearby, Otodome Falls drops about 25 metres. The falls are part of Fuji-Hakone-Izu National Park. In 1990 they were named among Japan's Top 100 Waterfalls. Climb the mountain and the forest changes with height. From 1,000 to 1,800 metres, northern Japanese hemlock dominates.

From 1,800 to 2,200 metres, Veitch's fir takes over. Around the Yoshida Trail's 5th Station, the forest nears its upper edge. Around the Hōei craters, cinders from 300 years ago still lie bare. Plants struggle to take root in such loose ground. Near the summit there is little but rock and grit. Strong winds and cold keep plant life very sparse up here. Standing alone above the plains, Fuji often wears a cap cloud, kasagumo. Its shape reveals the winds high above. The lakes at the foot all lie more than 800 metres up. Lake Yamanaka, at 980 metres, is the highest of the five.

Forest on lava, water rising from the mountain, bare ground above the trees: one volcano supports several different worlds.

地理 Geography

火山上的火山

A Volcano Built on Volcanoes

從小御岳、古富士到新富士，以及1707年的寶永火口
From Komitake and Old Fuji to New Fuji, and the Hōei craters of 1707

旁白全文 Full narration

富士山高3,776公尺，是日本最高的山。它橫跨山梨與靜岡兩縣，距東京約一百公里。它是一座層狀火山，由一層層熔岩與火山灰堆成。山麓的直徑約有四十到五十公里。山頂火口直徑約五百公尺，深約兩百五十公尺。火口周圍環繞著八座山峰。今天看到的山體叫「新富士」，約一萬年前開始活動。它覆蓋在更古老的火山上面。最底下是小御岳火山，約七十萬年前就已存在。上面一層是約十萬年前開始成長的古富士。

山的東南側，有一道明顯的缺口。那是1707年寶永噴發留下的火口。噴發從1707年12月16日開始，在山腰打開三個火口。旁邊隆起的寶永山，海拔2,693公尺，是那次噴發的產物。這次噴發噴出的是火山灰，沒有熔岩流。火山灰飄到將近一百公里外的江戶，積了好幾公分。北麓排列著五座湖，合稱富士五湖。它們是熔岩流堵住河谷之後形成的。山中湖在最東邊，是五湖中面積最大的一座。湖面海拔980公尺。

西湖和精進湖，原本是一座叫「剗之海」的大湖。西元864年貞觀噴發的熔岩，填滿了大湖的大半。那片熔岩上，後來長成了青木原樹海。森林面積約三十五平方公里。本栖湖、西湖和精進湖，至今仍由地下水路相連。山上的雨雪滲進熔岩，再從山腳化成泉水湧出。西南麓的白絲瀑布，高約二十公尺。水流沿著約兩百公尺寬的岩壁落下。富士山大約每五百年會有一次較大的噴發。1707年以後它一直沉睡，但仍被列為活火山。

火山疊著火山，熔岩造出湖泊，泉水從山腳湧出。富士山的模樣，是數十萬年噴發累積的結果。

At 3,776 metres, Mount Fuji is the highest mountain in Japan. It straddles Yamanashi and Shizuoka prefectures, about 100 km from Tokyo. It is a stratovolcano, built from layer upon layer of lava and ash. Its base is roughly 40 to 50 kilometres across. The summit crater is about 500 metres across and some 250 metres deep. Eight peaks ring its rim. The cone we see, called New Fuji, became active about 10,000 years ago. It sits on top of older volcanoes. At the bottom lies Komitake, a volcano already active some 700,000 years ago. Above it is Old Fuji, which began growing about 100,000 years ago.

On the south-eastern flank there is an obvious gash. It is the crater left by the Hōei eruption of 1707. The eruption began on 16 December 1707 and opened three vents on the flank. Mount Hōei beside them, 2,693 m high, was built by that eruption. It threw out volcanic ash, with no lava flows. The ash reached Edo, almost 100 km away, where it lay several centimetres deep. Five lakes line the northern foot: the Fuji Five Lakes. They formed where lava flows dammed the valleys. Lake Yamanaka, the easternmost, is the largest of the five. Its surface lies 980 metres above sea level.

Lakes Sai and Shōji are the remains of one large lake, Senoumi. Lava from the Jōgan eruption of 864 filled most of it. On that lava the Aokigahara forest later grew. It covers about 35 square kilometres. Lakes Motosu, Sai and Shōji are still linked by underground waterways. Rain and snow soak into the lava and emerge again as springs at the foot. Shiraito Falls on the south-west foot is about 20 metres high. Its water spills along a rock face some 200 metres wide. Major eruptions have come roughly every 500 years. It has slept since 1707, yet geologists still class it as active.

Volcano on volcano, lakes dammed by lava, springs rising at the foot. Fuji's shape is the work of hundreds of thousands of years of eruptions.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

富士山官方登山網站說明：八合目以上大多屬富士山本宮淺間大社奧宮的境內，為維護山的神聖性，富士山一帶不允許使用無人機；其他地點也可能須依日本航空法向國土交通省申請，並取得土地管理者同意。實際飛行前請先查詢日本的規定。

The official Mt. Fuji climbing site states that drones may not be used around Mt. Fuji, most of the mountain above the 8th Station being the ground of the summit Okumiya shrine; elsewhere a flight may need permission from Japan's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism under the Civil Aeronautics Act, plus the landowner's consent. Check Japan's rules before any real flight.
