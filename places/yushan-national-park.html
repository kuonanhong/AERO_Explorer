歷史 History

新高山與八通關

Mount Niitaka and the Batongguan Trail

一座山的幾個名字，和橫越它的兩條古道
The several names of one mountain, and the two old roads that cross it

這是玉山，海拔3,952公尺，。臺灣的最高峰。布農族稱它「東谷沙飛」，。鄒族的稱呼則音譯為「八通關」。1875年，清朝開了一條橫越中央山脈的道路，。就以八通關為名。吳光亮率隊從南投竹山開工，。同年11月完成，全長265公里。路線經過東埔、越過大水窟，。終點是花蓮的玉里。

1891年，巡撫劉銘傳因維護費用太高，。放棄了東埔到玉里這一段。1857年，美國船長莫里森從海上望見這座山，。西方人因此叫它「摩里遜山」。日本人測出它比富士山還高176公尺，。把它命名為「新高山」。1900年，人類學者鳥居龍藏與森丑之助。登上了玉山。1919到1921年，日本人另外修築八通關越嶺道路，。沿途派駐警察。

1943年，北峰上的氣象站完工。排雲山莊同樣始建於1943年，。2013年重建完成。1985年玉山國家公園成立，。清代古道在1987年列為古蹟。東谷沙飛、摩里遜、新高山、玉山，。四個名字，一座山。

This is Yushan, 3,952 metres, the highest mountain in Taiwan. The Bunun call it Tongku Saveq; the Tsou name is transcribed as Batongguan. In 1875 the Qing built a road across the Central Mountain Range, and it took the name Batongguan. Wu Guangliang began it at Zhushan in Nantou; it was finished that November, 265 kilometres long. The route passed Dongpu and crossed Dashuiku to end at Yuli in Hualien.

In 1891 Governor Liu Mingchuan abandoned the section from Dongpu to Yuli because of the cost of upkeep. In 1857 the American captain W. Morrison sighted the peak from the sea, and Westerners came to call it Mount Morrison. The Japanese found it 176 metres higher than Mount Fuji and named it Niitakayama, the "new high mountain". In 1900 the anthropologists Torii Ryūzō and Mori Ushinosuke reached the summit. From 1919 to 1921 the Japanese built a new Batongguan trail and stationed police along it.

In 1943 the weather station on the North Peak was completed. Paiyun Lodge also dates from 1943; it was rebuilt in 2013. Yushan National Park was created in 1985, and the Qing trail became a protected monument in 1987. Tongku Saveq, Morrison, Niitaka, Yushan: four names for one mountain.

人文 Culture

登頂的路

The Way to the Summit

從塔塔加到排雲山莊，再到主峰
From Tataka to Paiyun Lodge, and on to the main peak

塔塔加遊客中心海拔約兩千六百公尺，。在南投信義鄉的臺21線旁。登玉山主峰的步道從這一帶出發，。登山口海拔2,610公尺。從登山口到排雲山莊，。要走八點五公里。抵達山莊前1.2公里，。步道經過「大峭壁」。排雲山莊海拔3,402公尺，。可以住大約116人。

床位用抽籤分配，。中籤率曾經低到百分之一。2016年起，山莊靠太陽能與鋰電池供電，。物資則由人力揹上山。從山莊到主峰，。還有二點四公里。1966年，山頂立了一尊于右任銅像，。1996年遭人鋸斷。2005年起，。玉山的圖像印在新臺幣一千元鈔票上。

布農族住在公園邊緣的東埔與梅山，。約三百年前從平原遷入山區。東埔一帶，出土過史前的石器與陶器。文化部把玉山國家公園。列為臺灣的世界遺產潛力點。2007年，鹿林天文臺發現的一顆小行星，。也以玉山命名。

The Tataka Visitor Center stands at about 2,600 metres, beside Highway 21 in Xinyi Township, Nantou. The trail to the main peak starts near here, from a trailhead at 2,610 metres. From the trailhead to Paiyun Lodge is a walk of 8.5 kilometres. About 1.2 kilometres before the lodge, the trail passes the Great Precipice. Paiyun Lodge, at 3,402 metres, sleeps about 116 people.

Beds are allocated by lottery; the chance of winning has been as low as one per cent. Since 2016 the lodge has run on solar power and lithium batteries, and supplies are carried up on foot. From the lodge to the main peak is another 2.4 kilometres. In 1966 a bronze statue of Yu Youren was set on the summit; it was cut down in 1996. Since 2005 Yushan has appeared on the NT$1,000 banknote.

The Bunun live at Dongpu and Meishan on the edge of the park; they moved into the mountains from the plains about 300 years ago. Prehistoric stone tools and pottery have been found around Dongpu. Taiwan's Ministry of Culture lists Yushan National Park as a potential World Heritage site. An asteroid discovered by Lulin Observatory in 2007 was named Yushan as well.

自然 Nature

從森林到箭竹草原

From Forest to Arrow-Bamboo Meadow

落差三千六百公尺的國家公園裡，住著什麼
What lives in a national park with 3,600 metres of relief

玉山國家公園，。是臺灣面積最大、海拔最高的國家公園。園區三分之二在海拔兩千公尺以上，。高低落差三千六百公尺。公園只占臺灣陸地約百分之三，。卻有一半的原生植物種類。已經記錄到的植物，。有2,522種。隨著海拔升高，。植被依序分成六個帶。

最高的地方長著箭竹，。大約只到人的腰部。主峰一帶每年平均下雪二十四天，。十月初雪，到五月才完全融化。這裡記錄到130種鳥、28種哺乳類。與186種蝴蝶。大型哺乳類有臺灣黑熊、水鹿、。臺灣獼猴與長鬃山羊。每年三到五月，。山谷裡有成群的蝴蝶通過。

這裡也是熊鷹在臺灣重要的繁殖地。春天，杜鵑在山坡上盛開。臺灣的特有種裡，。大約三分之一以玉山地區為棲地。一座山，從山腳到山頂裝下了好幾種氣候。

Yushan National Park is Taiwan's largest and highest national park. Two-thirds of it lies above 2,000 metres, and its relief spans 3,600 metres. The park covers about 3 per cent of Taiwan's land but holds half of its native plant species. The plants recorded so far number 2,522 species. As the land rises, the vegetation changes through six zones.

At the highest elevations grows arrow bamboo, reaching about waist height. Around the main peak it snows on about 24 days a year; the first snow comes in October and is gone only by May. Some 130 bird, 28 mammal and 186 butterfly species have been recorded here. The larger mammals include the black bear, sambar deer, Taiwan macaque and serow. From March to May, processions of butterflies pass through the valleys.

It is also an important nesting ground for the mountain hawk-eagle in Taiwan. In spring, azaleas bloom across the slopes. About one-third of Taiwan's endemic species find habitat in the Yushan area. One mountain holds several climates between its foot and its summit.

地理 Geography

北回歸線上的最高點

The Highest Point on the Tropic of Cancer

板塊、冰河與三條大河的源頭
Plates, ice and the sources of three great rivers

玉山主峰3,952公尺，。東、南、西、北各有一座山峰圍著它。東峰3,869公尺、北峰3,858公尺、。南峰3,844公尺、西峰3,467公尺。南峰，是一座黑色頁岩構成的尖峰。臺灣位在歐亞板塊與菲律賓海板塊交界，。玉山是板塊擠壓抬升出來的。大峭壁的岩層裡有古代海洋生物的化石：。約兩億五千萬年前，這裡是海底。

玉山，是北回歸線上的最高點。大約一萬七千年前，。冰帽曾經向下延伸到海拔兩千八百公尺。主峰與八通關之間，。荖濃溪谷有一道斷層崖。濁水溪、高屏溪與秀姑巒溪，。都從這座國家公園發源。陳有蘭溪切過斷層，。向源侵蝕出金門峒斷崖。

公園裡有三十多座山峰。超過三千公尺。玉山年雨量約三千公釐，。八月最多，十二月最少。東海岸外五十公里，。海水深度就超過四千公尺。從海底到近四千公尺的高空，。這段歷史都留在岩層裡。

The main peak of Yushan, 3,952 metres, is ringed by East, South, West and North peaks. East Peak 3,869 m, North Peak 3,858 m, South Peak 3,844 m, West Peak 3,467 m. The South Peak is a sharp pinnacle of black shale. Taiwan sits where the Eurasian and Philippine Sea plates meet; Yushan was lifted by their collision. The Great Precipice holds fossils of ancient sea life: about 250 million years ago this was sea floor.

Yushan is the highest point on the Tropic of Cancer. About 17,000 years ago, ice caps reached down to 2,800 metres. Between the main peak and Batongguan a fault scarp runs through the Laonong valley. The Zhuoshui, Gaoping and Xiuguluan rivers all rise in this national park. The Chenyoulan River cuts through a fault, eroding headward to form the Jinmentong Cliffs.

More than 30 peaks in the park rise above 3,000 metres. Yushan receives about 3,000 millimetres of rain a year, most in August and least in December. Fifty kilometres off the east coast, the sea is already more than 4,000 metres deep. From sea floor to almost 4,000 metres in the air: the rock keeps the whole story.
