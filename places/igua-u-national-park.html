歷史 History

邊界上的大水

Great Water on the Border

1541年的第一筆紀錄、航空先驅的呼籲、兩國的國家公園，與伊泰普水壩
A first record in 1541, an aviator's plea, two national parks and the Itaipu Dam

伊瓜蘇瀑布位在巴西與阿根廷的邊界上，。河的這一側，是巴西的伊瓜蘇國家公園。1541年，西班牙探險家卡貝薩・德・巴卡，。留下了歐洲人對這座瀑布最早的紀錄。1876年，工程師雷博薩斯在書中呼籲保護瀑布；。那時，世界第一座國家公園黃石，才成立四年。1916年4月，航空先驅桑托斯－杜蒙來到瀑布，。他說，這樣的奇景不能只屬於一個人。那時，這片土地還是私人所有；。不久之後，巴拉那州把它收歸公有。

對岸的阿根廷，在1934年設立伊瓜蘇國家公園；。巴西這一側，在1939年1月10日以聯邦命令設立。巴西的國家公園面積約十八萬五千公頃，。和阿根廷的公園相連，合起來約二十六萬公頃。1984年，阿根廷的公園列入世界遺產；。1986年，巴西的公園也列入名錄。1999到2001年，。巴西這一側曾被列入瀕危世界遺產名單。1973年4月26日，巴西與巴拉圭簽訂伊泰普條約，。要在巴拉那河上合建一座水壩。

1984年5月5日，第一部機組開始發電；。二十部機組，總容量一千四百萬瓩。1982年10月，水庫開始蓄水，。上游的瓜伊拉瀑布，從此沒入水中。伊瓜蘇河在這裡匯入巴拉那河，。巴西、阿根廷與巴拉圭，三國在此交會。三國各在自己的河岸立了一座方尖碑，。漆上國旗的顏色，從每一座都看得到另外兩國。從探險家的紀錄，到兩國的國家公園，。這道大水，一直由邊界兩邊共同守護。

Iguazu Falls lie on the border of Brazil and Argentina; this bank of the river is Brazil's Iguaçu National Park. In 1541 the Spanish explorer Álvar Núñez Cabeza de Vaca left the first European record of the falls. In 1876 the engineer André Rebouças called for the falls to be protected; Yellowstone, the first national park, was then only four years old. In April 1916 the aviation pioneer Alberto Santos-Dumont came to the falls; such wonders, he said, could not belong to one individual. The land was then privately owned; soon afterwards the state of Paraná took it into public hands.

Across the river Argentina set up its Iguazú National Park in 1934; Brazil created its park by federal decree on 10 January 1939. Brazil's park covers about 185,000 hectares; joined to Argentina's, the two protect some 260,000. In 1984 Argentina's park became World Heritage; Brazil's park followed in 1986. From 1999 to 2001 the Brazilian park was on the list of World Heritage in Danger. On 26 April 1973 Brazil and Paraguay signed the Itaipu Treaty to build a dam together on the Paraná.

On 5 May 1984 its first generating unit started; its 20 units give it 14 gigawatts. In October 1982 the reservoir began to fill, and the Guaíra Falls upstream disappeared beneath it. Here the Iguazu flows into the Paraná, where Brazil, Argentina and Paraguay meet. Each country has an obelisk on its own bank, painted in its national colours; from each one all three countries are in view. From an explorer's record to two national parks, this great water has been looked after from both sides of the border.

人文 Culture

大水的名字與傳說

The Name and Legend of the Great Water

A Guarani name, a lovers' legend, falls in films, and border towns at the Triple Frontier

「伊瓜蘇」的名字，來自瓜拉尼語或圖皮語：。「y」是水，「ûasú」是大，合起來就是「大水」。傳說，一位神祇看上了美麗的少女娜依琵，。她卻和心愛的塔羅巴，划著獨木舟逃走。神祇一怒之下，把河流劈開，。這對戀人，從此永遠墜落。桑托斯－杜蒙的紀念像，1979年立在瀑布旁，。紀念這位「航空之父」為設立公園奔走。據說，美國第一夫人愛蓮娜・羅斯福看到這裡，。脫口而出：「可憐的尼加拉！」

2014年，巴西這一側的國家公園，。接待了一百五十五萬多名遊客。在阿根廷那一側，雨林生態小火車，。把遊客載到一個叫做「咽喉」的車站。1986年的電影《教會》，就在這裡取景；。後來《007：太空城》與《印第安納瓊斯》也來過。2018年的電影《黑豹》，。也把這道瀑布放進了畫面。三國交界一帶住了大約九十五萬人，。是全世界人口最多的三國交界。

巴拉圭的東方市最大，2018年約二十九萬九千人；。巴西的伊瓜蘇市，約二十五萬八千人。阿根廷的伊瓜蘇港最小，約八萬兩千人；。坦克雷多・內維斯橋，把它和巴西連在一起。這裡也住著大約三萬名阿拉伯與其他亞洲移民，。讓三座邊城有了多元的面貌。「伊泰普」在瓜拉尼語裡，是「會發聲的石頭」，。名字來自工地附近原本的一座小島。瓜拉尼語的地名、戀人的傳說與三國的邊城，。都圍繞著這道大水而生。

"Iguazu" comes from Guarani or Tupi: y means water and ûasú means big — "big water". Legend says a god wanted the beautiful Naipí, but she fled in a canoe with her mortal lover, Tarobá. In his anger the god sliced the river apart, condemning the lovers to an eternal fall. A statue of Santos-Dumont was raised beside the falls in 1979, honouring the "Father of Aviation" who worked for the park. Eleanor Roosevelt, it is said, took one look and exclaimed, "Poor Niagara!"

In 2014 Brazil's park welcomed more than 1.55 million visitors. On the Argentine side the Rainforest Ecological Train carries visitors to a station named Garganta — "Throat". The 1986 film The Mission was shot here; Moonraker and Indiana Jones came too. In 2018 Black Panther put these falls on the screen as well. Some 950,000 people live around the Triple Frontier, the most populous tripoint in the world.

Paraguay's Ciudad del Este is the largest, with 299,255 people in 2018; Brazil's Foz do Iguaçu has about 258,000. Argentina's Puerto Iguazú is the smallest, at about 82,000; the Tancredo Neves Bridge links it to Brazil. About 30,000 Arab and other Asian immigrants live here too, giving the three border towns a varied face. Itaipu means "the sounding stone" in Guarani, after an island that once stood near the building site. Guarani names, a lovers' legend and three border towns have all grown up around this great water.

自然 Nature

水霧裡的雨林

A Rainforest in the Spray

大西洋森林的內陸殘存、美洲豹與巨獺、終年的水霧，與起伏的水量
A remnant of the interior Atlantic Forest, jaguars and giant otters, year-round spray and a changing flow

伊瓜蘇國家公園約九成是亞熱帶雨林，。屬於大西洋森林的內陸部分。這裡是巴拉那亞熱帶森林留存最大的保護區之一，。物種豐富，也有許多特有種。低處的林下，長著樹蕨、附生植物與藤本；。較高較濕的地方，有巴西松與阿薩伊棕櫚。美洲豹、美洲獅、虎貓、長尾虎貓與細腰貓，。都生活在這片森林裡。河邊有巨獺，林間有貘和大食蟻獸，。還有體型嬌小的侏儒短角鹿。

鳥類之中，有角鵰、黑額鳴冠雉，。還有橫斑虎鷺。河裡有伊瓜蘇特有的大型鯰魚，。也有巴西人叫做「皮拉坎茹巴」的魚。瀑布終年揚起水霧，。這片水雲，也塑造了四周的植物。魔鬼咽喉接住了河水的一半左右，。它的水霧與彩虹，七公里外都看得見。公園裡的佛羅里亞諾河流域，。是巴拉那州唯一完整保存的流域。

兩國的公園一起巡守、共同規劃，。也是三國生態廊道的一部分。這裡全年溫暖多雨，伊瓜蘇市年雨量約一千八百七十公釐，。水量通常在十二月到二月最大。2006年乾旱時，流量曾降到每秒三百立方公尺；。平均流量，大約是每秒一千七百五十立方公尺。2014年6月9日，流量創下每秒四萬五千七百立方公尺的紀錄，。是平均的二十多倍。上游的水壩會人為調節河水的高低，。公園也面對農地擴張與盜獵的壓力。

雨林、水霧與森林裡的動物，。都和這條大河一起活著。

About 90% of Iguaçu National Park is subtropical rainforest, part of the interior Atlantic Forest. It is one of the largest protected remnants of the Paraná subtropical forest, rich in species and in plants and animals found nowhere else. Lower down grow tree ferns, epiphytes and lianas; higher, wetter ground holds Brazilian pine and assaí palms. Jaguar, puma, ocelot, margay and jaguarundi all live in this forest. Giant otters live along the rivers, tapirs and giant anteaters in the woods, together with the small pygmy brocket deer.

Among the birds are the harpy eagle, the black-fronted piping guan and the fasciated tiger heron. The river holds a large catfish of the Iguaçu and the fish Brazilians call piracanjuba. The falls raise a cloud of spray all year round, and that cloud shapes the plants around them. The Devil's Throat takes about half the river, and its spray and rainbows can be seen more than 7 km away. The Floriano River basin inside the park is the only one in Paraná state preserved whole.

The two countries' parks are patrolled and planned together, and form part of a tri-national corridor. It is warm and wet all year — about 1,870 mm of rain a year at Foz do Iguaçu — and the water is usually highest from December to February. In the 2006 drought the flow fell to 300 cubic metres a second; on average it is about 1,750. On 9 June 2014 it reached a record 45,700 cubic metres a second, more than twenty times the average. Dams upstream regulate the river's level artificially, and the park faces farming at its edges and poaching.

Rainforest, spray and the animals of the forest all live together with this great river.

地理 Geography

熔岩上的瀑布

Falls on Ancient Lava

兩千七百公尺的瀑布邊緣、U形的魔鬼咽喉、一億多年前的玄武岩，與後退的岩壁

伊瓜蘇河發源於庫里奇巴附近，大部分流經巴西；。在這裡，它是阿根廷與巴西的界河。瀑布的邊緣長約兩千七百公尺，。依水量不同，分成一百五十到三百道瀑布。河水分兩級落下，各約三十五和四十公尺；。單一瀑布最高，約八十二公尺。魔鬼咽喉是一道U形的深谷，。長約七百公尺，寬約一百五十公尺。國界正好從魔鬼咽喉中間穿過，。一邊是巴西，一邊是阿根廷。

一億兩千五百萬到一億一千五百萬年前，。大量玄武岩熔岩，從地面的裂隙湧出。熔岩覆蓋約一百二十萬平方公里，最厚達一千五百公尺，。是陸地上記錄到最大的玄武岩熔岩流。這些岩層在南美洲叫做塞拉熱拉爾層，。和非洲西部的岩層相對應，見證了岡瓦納大陸的分裂。瀑布由三層玄武岩構成，。最上面八到十公尺，是非常堅硬的氣孔玄武岩。河水不斷侵蝕岩壁，。瀑布每年大約向上游後退一點四到二點一公分。

瀑布下方的峽谷又窄又深，。一路延伸到下游與巴拉那河相接的地方。在瀑布下游大約二十公里處，。伊瓜蘇河匯入巴拉那河。沿巴拉那河往上游約十五公里，。就是高一百九十六公尺的伊泰普水壩。它的水庫面積約一千三百五十平方公里，。2016年發電一千零三十一億度，創下紀錄。和尼加拉瀑布相比，伊瓜蘇的落差更大，。平均流量則比尼加拉小。

古老的熔岩、後退的岩壁與兩國的界河，。一起造出了這道大水。

The Iguazu rises near Curitiba and flows mostly through Brazil; here it forms the border of Argentina and Brazil. The edge of the falls is about 2,700 metres long, split into 150 to 300 separate falls depending on the water. The water drops in two steps, of about 35 and 40 metres; the tallest single fall is about 82 metres. The Devil's Throat is a U-shaped chasm about 700 metres long and 150 metres wide. The border runs right through the Devil's Throat, with Brazil on one side and Argentina on the other.

Between 125 and 115 million years ago vast basalt lavas poured from cracks in the ground. They covered about 1.2 million km², up to 1,500 metres thick, the largest basalt lava flow recorded on the continents. In South America these rocks are called the Serra Geral Formation; they match rocks in western Africa, witnesses of the break-up of Gondwana. The falls are made of three layers of basalt; the top 8 to 10 metres are a very hard vesicular basalt. The river keeps wearing at the rock, and the falls retreat upstream by about 1.4 to 2.1 cm a year.

Below the falls a narrow, deep canyon runs all the way down to the Paraná. About 20 km below the falls the Iguazu joins the Paraná. Some 15 km up the Paraná stands the 196-metre Itaipu Dam. Its reservoir covers about 1,350 square kilometres; in 2016 it generated a record 103.1 terawatt-hours. Compared with Niagara, Iguazu drops further, though its average flow is smaller.

Ancient lava, retreating rock and a border river together made this great water.
