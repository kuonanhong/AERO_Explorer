歷史 History

為她而建的二十二年

Twenty-Two Years for One Woman

沙賈汗、慕塔芝・瑪哈與莫臥兒王朝的陵墓工程
Shah Jahan, Mumtaz Mahal and a Mughal tomb that took two decades

這裡是印度北方邦的阿格拉，。亞穆納河右岸的白色陵墓，泰姬瑪哈陵。它是一座陵墓，。莫臥兒王朝第五位皇帝沙賈汗，為妻子而建。1631年6月17日，慕塔芝・瑪哈去世，。當時她正生下第十四個孩子。同一年，沙賈汗下令建造這座陵墓；。1632年動工。工程動員兩萬多名工匠與工人，。來自布哈拉、敘利亞、波斯與南印度。

還有俾路支的石匠與義大利工匠；。宮廷建築師烏斯塔德・艾哈邁德・拉合里主持。1643年2月6日，。在她逝世十二週年時舉行了第一次紀念儀式。陵墓主體在1648年完成，。整個園區據信到1653年才全部完工。沿著河往上游約兩公里，。是紅砂岩建成的阿格拉堡。後來兒子奧朗則布廢黜沙賈汗，。把他軟禁在阿格拉堡。

從堡裡，他看得見河彎另一頭的泰姬瑪哈陵。1666年沙賈汗去世，。葬在妻子身旁。1983年，它列入世界遺產，。被稱為「印度伊斯蘭藝術的瑰寶」。2007年又名列「世界新七大奇蹟」；。一座為一個人而建的建築，留下來給所有人看。

This is Agra, in the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh: the white tomb on the right bank of the Yamuna is the Taj Mahal. This building is a tomb, built by Shah Jahan, the fifth Mughal emperor, for his wife. Mumtaz Mahal died on 17 June 1631, giving birth to their fourteenth child. That same year Shah Jahan commissioned the tomb; work began in 1632. More than 20,000 workers and artisans took part, from Bukhara, Syria, Persia and southern India.

Stone cutters came from Baluchistan and artisans from Italy; the court architect was Ustad Ahmad Lahori. The first ceremony was held on 6 February 1643, on the twelfth anniversary of her death. The mausoleum was finished in 1648; the whole complex is believed to have been complete by 1653. About two kilometres upstream stands Agra Fort, built of red sandstone. Shah Jahan was later deposed by his son Aurangzeb and held under house arrest in Agra Fort.

From the fort he could see the Taj Mahal across the bend of the river. Shah Jahan died in 1666 and was buried beside his wife. In 1983 it was inscribed as World Heritage, described as "the jewel of Islamic art in India". In 2007 it was named one of the New 7 Wonders of the World: a building made for one person, left for everyone to see.

人文 Culture

對稱的天堂花園

A Symmetrical Garden of Paradise

大門、清真寺、「迴響」建築與一年五百萬名訪客
The gateway, the mosque, its mirror image and five million visitors a year

泰姬瑪哈陵是一整組建築，。不只是一座白色圓頂。從上面看，一切沿著南北中軸線對稱排列。南端是以大理石為主的正門，。樣式呼應陵墓本身。大門與陵墓之間是「四分花園」，。每一區又分成十六塊下沉花圃。花園正中央是抬高的大理石水池，。五座噴泉與一道倒影池在中軸線上。

陵墓兩側各有一棟紅砂岩建築，。其中一棟是清真寺。另一棟幾乎一模一樣，稱為「迴響」，。沒有壁龕，主要為了對稱。藝術史學者埃巴・科赫認為，。整個配置象徵塵世與來世。正門標誌著兩者之間的過渡。白色大理石會隨時間變色：。清晨帶點粉紅，月光下則泛著金色。

往西過了阿格拉堡，。是城裡的主麻清真寺。阿格拉至今仍是一座生活中的城市，。老城就在清真寺周圍。2022到2023年度，。約有五百萬人造訪泰姬瑪哈陵。他們沿著同一條中軸線走進來，。看見的，是三百多年前設計好的那一眼。

The Taj Mahal is a whole ensemble, not only a white dome. From above, everything is laid out symmetrically along a north–south axis. At the south end is the main gateway, built mostly of marble, echoing the tomb itself. Between gate and tomb lies a fourfold garden, each quarter divided into sixteen sunken beds. At the centre is a raised marble tank with five fountains, and a reflecting pool on the axis.

Two red sandstone buildings flank the tomb; one of them is a mosque. Its almost identical twin, the jawab or "answer", has no mihrab; it is there for symmetry. Art historian Ebba Koch reads the plan as a picture of earthly life and the afterlife. In that reading, the great gate marks the passage between the two. The white marble changes through the day, pinkish in the morning and golden in moonlight.

West, beyond Agra Fort, is the city's Jama Masjid. Agra is still a living city; the old town spreads around the mosque. In the year 2022–23 about five million people visited the Taj Mahal. They walk in along the same axis and see the view that was designed over three centuries ago.

自然 Nature

河、花園與變黃的大理石

A River, a Garden and Yellowing Marble

亞穆納河、月光花園與保護泰姬瑪哈陵的空氣管制
The Yamuna, Mehtab Bagh and the clean-air zone that protects the Taj

泰姬瑪哈陵蓋在亞穆納河彎的岸邊，。面河的一側沒有圍牆。其他三面圍著有雉堞的紅砂岩牆，。整個園區約十七公頃。對岸是月光花園，。和陵墓完全對齊。這座方形花園大約三百公尺見方，。面積約十公頃。雨季時，花園地面會局部淹水；。它由印度考古調查局管理。

考古調查局認為，。亞穆納河本身也被設計進天堂花園的意象。但河與空氣也帶來傷害：。污染讓大理石逐漸泛黃發褐。酸雨，以及包括馬圖拉煉油廠在內的污染源，。都被認為是原因之一。1997年依最高法院指示，。設立「泰姬梯形管制區」。管制區面積一萬零四百平方公里，。對排放有嚴格標準。

河谷地下水位下降，也引起結構上的擔憂；。2010年出現裂縫。2018年與2020年的暴風雨也造成損害。修復人員用泥漿敷面讓大理石恢復白色，。並更換破損的石材。保護一座建築，最後要照顧的，。是它周圍的河流與空氣。

The Taj Mahal stands on a bend of the Yamuna; the side facing the river has no wall. The other three sides are enclosed by crenellated red sandstone walls; the complex covers about 17 hectares. Across the river is Mehtab Bagh, the Moonlight Garden, perfectly aligned with the tomb. The square garden measures about 300 by 300 metres, some 10 hectares. In the rainy season the ground is partly flooded; the garden is run by the Archaeological Survey of India.

The Survey has suggested that the Yamuna itself was woven into the design as one of the rivers of Paradise. But river and air have also done harm: pollution has turned the marble yellow-brown. Acid rain and pollution, including from the Mathura refinery, are among the causes. In 1997, following Supreme Court directives, the Taj Trapezium Zone was set up. It covers 10,400 square kilometres and sets strict emission standards.

Falling groundwater in the Yamuna basin has raised structural concerns; cracks appeared in 2010. Storms in 2018 and 2020 caused damage as well. Restorers apply mud packs to bring back the white and replace broken marble. Protecting a building, in the end, means looking after the river and the air around it.

地理 Geography

大理石、砂岩與四十公尺的尖塔

Marble, Sandstone and Forty-Metre Minarets

從拉賈斯坦的採石場到河岸上的平台：泰姬瑪哈陵的尺寸與材料
From Rajasthan quarries to a riverside platform: the measurements and materials

這是一部用尺寸與石頭看泰姬瑪哈陵的影片。整個園區坐在河岸一座平台上，。長約三百公尺、高八點七公尺。陵墓本身立在高六公尺的台基上，。台基每邊九十五點五公尺。四角各有一座尖塔，。每座都超過四十公尺高。主圓頂高二十三公尺，。坐在十二公尺高的鼓座上。

頂端的尖頂還有九點六公尺；。整座建築高約七十三公尺。白色大理石來自拉賈斯坦邦的馬克拉納。周圍紅色的砂岩，。則來自北方邦的法特普希克里。大理石上鑲嵌了二十八種寶石與半寶石，。有些來自很遠的地方。玉與水晶來自中國，綠松石來自西藏，。青金石來自阿富汗。

藍寶石來自斯里蘭卡，。紅玉髓來自阿拉伯。為了運送材料，工人築了一條十五公里長的土坡道，。用牛和大象拖運。上游的阿格拉堡同樣是紅砂岩，。城牆長二點五公里。兩座建築隔著亞穆納河相望，。一紅一白，標出了這座城市。

This film looks at the Taj Mahal through its measurements and its stone. The complex sits on a riverside platform about 300 metres long and 8.7 metres high. The tomb stands on a plinth 6 metres high, 95.5 metres along each side. A minaret rises at each corner, each more than 40 metres tall. The main dome is 23 metres high, on a drum 12 metres tall.

The finial adds another 9.6 metres; the whole building is about 73 metres tall. The white marble came from Makrana in Rajasthan. The red sandstone around it came from Fatehpur Sikri, in Uttar Pradesh. Twenty-eight kinds of precious and semi-precious stone were inlaid in the marble, some from far away. Jade and crystal from China, turquoise from Tibet, lapis lazuli from Afghanistan.

Sapphire from Sri Lanka, carnelian from Arabia. To bring the materials in, a 15-kilometre earthen ramp was built, with oxen and elephants hauling the loads. Upstream, Agra Fort is also red sandstone, its walls 2.5 kilometres long. The two face each other along the Yamuna, one red, one white, marking out the city.
