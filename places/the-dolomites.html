歷史 History

山中的前線

A Front Line in the Mountains

以多洛米厄命名的岩石、一次大戰的山中戰線、兩屆冬奧與世界遺產
A rock named after Dolomieu in 1791, a First World War front, two Winter Olympics and World Heritage in 2009

多洛米蒂山脈在義大利東北部，。2009年6月26日列入世界遺產。這裡的岩層，記錄了大約兩億五千萬年的地球歷史，。細節豐富，而且連續不斷。1789到1790年，法國學者多洛米厄考察阿爾卑斯山，。1791年，他描述了一種新的礦物。這種礦物後來以他命名，叫做「白雲石」；。他最早以科學研究這片山區，山脈也因他得名。1864年，格羅曼首登馬爾莫拉達；。1869年8月21日，他又和兩位嚮導首登三峰最高的大峰。

1915到1917年，義大利與奧匈帝國的前線，。就穿過這些山峰。雙方都在拉加佐伊山裡挖掘坑道，。想從山的內部奪下對方的陣地。這些坑道今天開放參觀，。成了一座藏在山裡的戰爭博物館。在馬爾莫拉達，奧匈帝國的士兵，。就住在北面冰河裡挖出來的坑道中。三峰一直位在國界上，到1919年為止；。科蒂納在1920年正式割讓給義大利。

原定1944年在科蒂納舉行的冬季奧運，因二次大戰取消，。1956年才終於在這裡舉辦。那是第一屆現場直播的冬季奧運，。黑白畫面傳到了歐洲八個國家。2010年5月13日，多洛米蒂聯合國教科文基金會成立，。地址就在科蒂納丹佩佐。2022年7月3日，。馬爾莫拉達的冰塔崩塌，十一人罹難。2026年，科蒂納和米蘭共同主辦冬季奧運，。這是它第二次主辦奧運。

從學者的考察、戰爭的前線，。到全世界登山客的目的地，這些山記得一切。

The Dolomites rise in north-eastern Italy; they were inscribed as World Heritage on 26 June 2009. Their rocks record about 250 million years of Earth's history in rich and continuous detail. In 1789–90 the French geologist Déodat de Dolomieu studied the Alps, and in 1791 he described a new mineral. The mineral, dolomite, was named after him; he made the first scientific study of the region, which bears his name. Paul Grohmann made the first ascent of the Marmolada in 1864, and on 21 August 1869, with two guides, of Cima Grande, highest of the Tre Cime.

From 1915 to 1917 the front line between Italy and Austria-Hungary ran through these peaks. Both sides dug tunnels into Lagazuoi, trying to take the other's positions from inside the mountain. Those tunnels are open today, in effect a museum inside the mountain. On the Marmolada Austro-Hungarian soldiers lived in tunnels dug into the northern glacier. The Tre Cime stood on the border until 1919; Cortina d'Ampezzo was definitively ceded to Italy in 1920.

The Winter Olympics planned for Cortina in 1944 were cancelled by the Second World War; they were finally held here in 1956. They were the first Winter Games broadcast live, in black and white, to eight European countries. On 13 May 2010 the Dolomites UNESCO Foundation was established, with its address in Cortina d'Ampezzo. On 3 July 2022 a serac collapsed on the Marmolada, killing eleven people. In 2026 Cortina co-hosted the Winter Olympics with Milan, its second time hosting the Games.

From a scholar's survey and a wartime front to a destination for climbers from all over the world, these mountains remember it all.

人文 Culture

多洛米蒂有五個山谷，至今還說拉登語。它是從民間拉丁語流傳下來的羅曼語。它和羅曼什語、弗留利語是近親；。2011年，南提洛約有兩萬零五百人以它為母語。加爾代納谷約有八到九成居民說拉登語，。巴迪亞谷更高達九成五。馬爾莫拉達山下的法薩谷，。有百分之八十二點八的人以拉登語為母語。科蒂納的居民，多半說當地的安佩佐方言，。它和鄰近的卡多雷方言很接近。

1972年的自治法，讓拉登語在南提洛成為部分官方語言；。在貝盧諾，它從1999年起受到法律保護。科蒂納鎮中心海拔一千兩百二十四公尺，。平時約五千四百人，冬天會增加到大約四萬人。歐倫佐小屋海拔約兩千三百二十公尺，。有收費道路從米蘇里納通到這裡，是最容易抵達的據點。三峰東北方約一公里的洛卡特利小屋，。海拔約兩千四百三十八公尺。拉加佐伊纜車，從海拔兩千一百一十七公尺的法爾札雷戈隘口，。升到兩千七百五十二公尺的山屋。

義大利電視劇《Un passo dal cielo》，。從2010年起在布拉耶斯湖畔拍攝。2018年七到九月，湖邊來了超過一百二十萬人；。2020年8月的某一天，就有一萬七千八百七十四人。現在湖區實施了入園管理，。開車前往，必須事先預約停車位。2023年1月30日起，南提洛十八處保護區禁飛無人機，。包括三峰、法內斯-塞內斯-布拉耶斯與普埃茲-奧德勒自然公園。拉登語、山屋與纜車，。讓人們一代又一代地住在這些山裡。

Five valleys of the Dolomites still speak Ladin, a Romance language surviving from Vulgar Latin in the Alps. It is closely related to Romansh and Friulian; in 2011 about 20,500 people in South Tyrol gave it as their mother tongue. In Val Gardena 80 to 90% of the people speak Ladin; in Val Badia the figure is 95%. In the Fassa Valley below the Marmolada 82.8% are native Ladin speakers. Most people in Cortina speak Ampezzano, the local variety of Ladin, which is close to the neighbouring Cadore dialect.

The 1972 autonomy statute made Ladin partly official in South Tyrol; in Belluno it has been protected by law since 1999. The centre of Cortina lies at 1,224 metres; around 5,400 people live here, rising to some 40,000 in winter. Rifugio Auronzo, at about 2,320 metres, is the most accessible base, reached by a toll road from Misurina. Rifugio Locatelli, about a kilometre north-east of the peaks, stands at about 2,438 metres. The Lagazuoi cable car runs from the Falzarego Pass at 2,117 metres up to Rifugio Lagazuoi at 2,752 metres.

The Italian TV series Un passo dal cielo has been filmed on the shores of Lake Braies since 2010. From July to September 2018 over 1.2 million people came to the lake; on one day in August 2020 there were 17,874. An access system now regulates the flow of visitors to the lake, and drivers must book parking in advance. Since 30 January 2023 drones have been banned in 18 protected areas of South Tyrol, including the Tre Cime, Fanes-Sennes-Braies and Puez-Odle nature parks. Ladin, mountain huts and cable cars have let people live among these mountains, generation after generation.

多洛米蒂地區大約有兩千四百種植物；。光是貝盧諾多洛米蒂國家公園，就有一千三百五十種。那大約是義大利植物的四分之一，。公園裡還有五十五種森林類型。馬爾莫拉達冰河，是多洛米蒂最大的冰河，。位在山的北側，坡度比較平緩。2004到2014年，冰河失去了三成的體積，。預估到2050年就會消失。2022年的崩塌，讓超過二十萬立方公尺的冰與碎石滑下山坡，。另有八人受傷。

The Dolomites region has about 2,400 plant species; the Dolomiti Bellunesi National Park alone has 1,350. That is about a quarter of Italy's flora, and the park has 55 types of forest. The Marmolada Glacier is the largest in the Dolomites, lying on the gentler northern side of the mountain. Between 2004 and 2014 the glacier lost 30% of its volume, and it is expected to disappear by 2050. The 2022 collapse sent over 200,000 m³ of ice and debris down the slope, and eight more people were injured.

Storm Vaia in October 2018 tore down millions of trees across the region. After the storm a bark beetle infestation spread, turning large patches of spruce forest brown and dead. Local and regional authorities are monitoring and containing the outbreak; it is also seen as an opportunity for biodiversity. With so many different habitats, the fauna is very diverse. Large carnivores are slowly recolonising remote areas. Bears, partly reintroduced, and lynx have returned to these mountains.

An estimated 10 million people visit the Dolomites each year, with signs of more since the Covid-19 pandemic. The Tre Cime area is very crowded in summer, and a study has looked at how many visitors it can carry. In the South Tyrol municipalities of the site arrivals rose by 64.3% in fifteen years. In 2025 IUCN rated the outlook "good with some concerns", with the threats rated high. Retreating ice, fallen forests and returning bears all tell of mountains that are changing.

地理 Geography

蒼白的山

The Pale Mountains

九個山系、十八座三千公尺以上的山峰、古代熱帶環礁變成的山，與山崩造成的湖
Nine mountain systems, 18 peaks over 3,000 m, rock from tropical atolls and a lake made by a landslide

世界遺產由九個山系組成，。面積約十四萬一千九百零三公頃。範圍橫跨三個大區、五個省，。有十八座超過三千公尺的山峰。最高的是馬爾莫拉達的佩尼亞峰，海拔三千三百四十三公尺，。被稱為「多洛米蒂女王」。這些岩石裡，保存了乾燥的平原、熱帶的環礁、。火山與深海的化石紀錄。它們也記錄了地球史上最大的滅絕之後，。三疊紀海洋生物的復甦。

中生代的碳酸鹽台地，。今天變成了一座座陡直、顏色蒼白的山峰。拉加佐伊山高兩千八百三十五公尺，。山腳的法爾札雷戈隘口，海拔兩千一百一十七公尺。往東，是科蒂納丹佩佐所在的山谷；。鎮西的托法納迪梅佐峰，高三千兩百四十四公尺。往東北，就是塞斯托多洛米蒂的拉瓦雷多三峰，。位在南提洛與貝盧諾兩省的交界。三峰中，大峰高兩千九百九十九公尺，。西峰兩千九百七十三公尺，小峰兩千八百五十七公尺。

山上還留著第一次世界大戰的。工事、戰壕與坑道。北邊的布拉耶斯湖，是一座堰塞湖：。赫爾斯坦山的山崩擋住了布拉耶斯河，才形成了湖。湖面海拔一千四百九十六公尺，最深三十六公尺，。長約一點二公里，面積約三十一公頃。湖邊的塞科費爾山，高兩千八百一十公尺。山區常有山崩、洪水與雪崩，地形還在變。從古代的熱帶海洋，。到今天蒼白而陡峭的山，這是兩億五千萬年的故事。

The World Heritage Site is made up of nine mountain systems covering 141,903 hectares. It spans three regions and five provinces, with 18 peaks above 3,000 metres. The highest is Punta Penia on the Marmolada, 3,343 metres, known as the "Queen of the Dolomites". These rocks preserve fossil records of arid plains, tropical atolls, volcanoes and deep ocean floors. They also record the recovery of marine life in the Triassic after the greatest extinction in Earth's history.

Mesozoic carbonate platforms have become today's sheer, pale-coloured peaks. Lagazuoi rises to 2,835 metres; the Falzarego Pass at its foot is at 2,117 metres. To the east lies the valley of Cortina d'Ampezzo; west of the town, Tofana di Mezzo reaches 3,244 metres. To the north-east stand the Tre Cime di Lavaredo in the Sexten Dolomites, on the border of South Tyrol and Belluno. Of the three, Cima Grande is 2,999 metres, Cima Ovest 2,973 metres and Cima Piccola 2,857 metres.

The mountain still holds the fortifications, trenches and tunnels of the First World War. Lake Braies to the north is a barrage lake: a landslide from the Herrstein dammed the Prags river and created it. The lake lies at 1,496 metres and is up to 36 metres deep, about 1.2 km long and 31 hectares in area. Above the lake rises the Seekofel, 2,810 metres. Landslides, floods and avalanches are frequent here, and the land is still changing. From ancient tropical seas to the pale, sheer mountains of today: a story of 250 million years.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

南提洛自2023年1月30日起，在十八處保護區禁止飛行二十五公斤以下的無人機（不論是否裝有相機），包括三峰、法內斯-塞內斯-布拉耶斯與普埃茲-奧德勒自然公園；義大利民航局ENAC已把這項限制列為地理區ENV-002，標示在D-Flight地圖上。威尼托一側的安佩佐多洛米蒂自然公園，則規定未經公園書面許可不得飛越。此外，各類無人機飛行高度上限為120公尺，須在D-Flight登記並投保。實際飛行前請先確認最新規定。

Since 30 January 2023 South Tyrol has banned drones under 25 kg, camera or not, in 18 protected areas, including the Tre Cime, Fanes-Sennes-Braies and Puez-Odle nature parks; Italy's aviation authority ENAC lists this as geographic zone ENV-002 on the D-Flight map. On the Veneto side, the Ampezzo Dolomites park forbids overflights without its written permission. Every drone is also limited to 120 m and must be registered on D-Flight and insured. Check the current rules before any real flight.
