歷史 History

七千年的鹽

Seven Thousand Years of Salt

從石器時代的鹿角鋤到鐵器時代的「哈爾施塔特文化」
From a stag-horn hoe to the Iron Age culture that bears the town's name

這裡是奧地利上奧地利邦的哈爾施塔特，。夾在湖與達赫施泰因山之間。小鎮的財富來自一種東西：鹽。鎮上最古老的出土物，。是約公元前五千年的鹿角鋤與燧石斧。聯合國教科文組織寫道，。至少在公元前兩千年，人們就在這裡採鹽。鎮上的資料則寫，鹽礦從公元前一三〇〇年。開採至今，常被說成「七千年」。

鐵器時代早期，。約公元前八百到四五〇年的文化以這裡為名。那些出土文物，如今收藏在鎮上的世界遺產博物館。在史前礦道裡，還找到了約兩千八百年前的。木樓梯，是歐洲最古老的一座。1734年，礦工在鹽裡發現一具。保存完好的古代礦工遺體。大約一〇五〇年就有基督徒社群，。最晚在1181年已有一座羅馬式小教堂。

鹽讓這一帶繁榮，。一直到二十世紀中葉。1997年12月，這片鹽業與山湖交織的地景，。列入世界遺產。範圍包括哈爾施塔特、湖、達赫施泰因山塊。與對岸的上特勞恩。這座小鎮把名字留給了一個時代，。而鹽，至今仍在山裡開採。

This is Hallstatt in Upper Austria, squeezed between its lake and the Dachstein massif. The wealth of this small town came from one thing: salt. The oldest finds here are a stag-horn hoe and flint axes from around 5000 BC. UNESCO notes that salt was being mined here as early as 2000 BC. Local accounts date mining from 1300 BC to today, and the town speaks of "7,000 years of salt mining".

An Early Iron Age culture, about 800 to 450 BC, is named after this town: the Hallstatt culture. Its finds are kept in the town's World Heritage Museum. In the prehistoric tunnels, a wooden staircase about 2,800 years old was found, the oldest in Europe. In 1734 the body of an earlier miner was found, preserved in the salt. A Christian community is assumed from about 1050, and a small Romanesque church stood here by 1181 at the latest.

Salt kept the whole region prosperous until the middle of the 20th century. In December 1997 this landscape of salt, lake and mountain became World Heritage. It takes in Hallstatt, the lake, the Dachstein massif and Obertraun on the far shore. The small town gave its name to an era, and salt is still mined in the mountain today.

人文 Culture

湖邊的七百七十八人

Seven Hundred and Seventy-Eight People by a Lake

教堂、骨堂、木瓦，以及過度觀光的問題
Churches, a charnel house, larch shingles and the problem of too many visitors

哈爾施塔特很小，。2018年只有七百七十八位居民。房屋擠在湖的西南岸，。背後就是陡峭的山坡。小鎮海拔五百一十一公尺，。全鎮面積約六十平方公里。山坡上的天主教堂旁，。是著名的骨堂。鎮上也有一座新教的基督教堂。

山上的髑髏地教堂修復時，。用了大約兩萬四千片彎曲的落葉松木瓦。山上的魯道夫塔，過去是保護鹽場的防禦塔。鹽礦今天也是展場，。纜索鐵道爬升約三百六十公尺到高山谷。礦道裡大約攝氏八度，。導覽約九十分鐘，還能坐礦工的木滑梯。觀景台上有一段十二公尺長、。伸出懸崖的步道，俯瞰整個小鎮。

這樣的景色吸引大批遊客，。哈爾施塔特常被當作過度觀光的例子。往北大約十七公里，。是巴德伊施爾。城裡的皇帝別墅，。是薩爾茨卡默古特另一個知名去處。山與湖之間只有一條窄窄的岸，。小鎮就在這條岸上住了幾千年。

Hallstatt is small: in 2018 it had 778 residents. Its houses crowd the southwestern shore of the lake with steep slopes right behind them. The town lies at 511 metres and its municipality covers about 60 square kilometres. Beside the Catholic parish church on the slope stands the famous Beinhaus, the charnel house. The town also has a Protestant church, the Christuskirche.

When the Calvary church was restored, about 24,000 curved larch shingles were used. Up the hill, the Rudolfsturm once guarded the salt works. The salt mine is now also a visitor attraction; a funicular climbs about 360 metres to the high valley. Inside it is about 8 °C; the tour lasts about 90 minutes and includes riding the miners' wooden slides. A twelve-metre walkway juts out over the drop with the whole town below.

Views like this bring crowds; Hallstatt is often cited as a case of overtourism. About 17 kilometres north lies Bad Ischl. Its Kaiservilla is another well-known stop in the Salzkammergut. Between mountain and lake there is only a narrow shore, and the town has lived on it for thousands of years.

自然 Nature

冰河、冰洞與山湖

Glaciers, Ice Caves and a Mountain Lake

The Dachstein massif, the Gosaukamm and the lakes of the Salzkammergut

薩爾茨卡默古特是奧地利的湖區，。哈爾施塔特湖是其中一座。湖的南端被高山圍住，。北端才通往較開闊的河谷。往南，是達赫施泰因山塊。山上還有冰河，。也是世界遺產範圍的一部分。這座石灰岩高原底下，。藏著大大小小的洞穴。

這一帶有達赫施泰因冰洞、猛獁洞。與科本布呂勒洞。克里彭施泰因峰，。也在這片山塊上。往西是戈紹山脊，。一道陡峭的山稜。它是上奧地利邦與薩爾斯堡邦的界山。世界遺產名單上，。這裡登錄為「持續演變中的文化景觀」。

往北過了巴德伊施爾，。是另一座大湖，沃夫岡湖。薩爾茨卡默古特的湖泊，。一座接著一座。回到哈爾施塔特，。湖面、森林與冰河都在同一個畫面裡。這片風景是山、水與時間一起做出來的。

The Salzkammergut is Austria's lake district, and Hallstätter See is one of its lakes. Its southern end is closed in by high mountains; only the north opens into a wider valley. To the south rises the Dachstein massif. It still carries glaciers, and the massif is part of the World Heritage property. Beneath this limestone plateau lie caves large and small.

Here are the Dachstein Ice Cave, the Mammoth Cave and the Koppenbrüller Cave. The Krippenstein belongs to the same massif. To the west runs the Gosaukamm, a steep mountain ridge. It runs along the border of Upper Austria and Salzburg. On the World Heritage List it is a "continuing cultural landscape": nature and human use shaped together.

North beyond Bad Ischl lies another large lake, the Wolfgangsee. The Salzkammergut's lakes follow one after another. Back at Hallstatt, lake, forest and glacier share a single view. This landscape was made by mountains, water and time together.

地理 Geography

海底的鹽，山中的礦

Salt from a Sea, Mined from a Mountain

兩億三千萬年前的潟湖，怎麼變成阿爾卑斯山裡的鹽礦
How lagoons of 230 million years ago became salt inside the Alps

旁白全文 Full narration

為什麼阿爾卑斯山裡會有鹽？答案要回到兩億五千萬年前。兩億五千萬年前，這一帶還在水下。大約兩億三千萬年前，。古特提斯海的溫暖潟湖蒸發出鹽層。約一億兩千萬年前，。達赫施泰因的石灰岩高原疊在鹽層上方。約五千萬年前，板塊移動把這些地層。擠成阿爾卑斯山的鹽礦床。

鹽礦就在小鎮上方的高山谷裡。纜索鐵道從湖邊爬升約三百六十公尺，。才到得了礦區。小鎮本身在海拔五百一十一公尺，。擠在湖岸與山壁之間。湖的南端緊貼著達赫施泰因山塊。石灰岩被水溶蝕，。在山裡留下洞穴與冰洞。

往西，是戈紹山脊。山稜的形狀，。從空中看得最清楚。鹽在地下，石灰岩在上，。人住在兩者之間的湖岸。一座小鎮的歷史，。是從兩億多年前的一片海開始的。

Why is there salt inside the Alps? The answer goes back 250 million years. More than 250 million years ago, this area was under water. About 230 million years ago, salt formed in the warm lagoons of the ancient Tethys. Around 120 million years ago, the Dachstein limestone plateau built up over the salt. About 50 million years ago, moving plates pushed these layers into the alpine salt deposits.

The salt mine lies in the high valley above the town. A funicular climbs about 360 metres from the lakeside to reach the mine. The town itself lies at 511 metres, pressed between shore and rock wall. The southern end of the lake runs right up against the Dachstein massif. Water dissolves limestone, leaving caves and ice caves inside the mountain.

To the west lies the Gosaukamm. Seen from the air, the shape of its crest is easiest to read. Salt below, limestone above, and people living on the shore between them. A small town's history begins with a sea more than 200 million years ago.
