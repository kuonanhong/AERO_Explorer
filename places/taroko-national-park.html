歷史 History

古道、公路與國家公園

Old Roads, a Highway and a National Park

從1914年的警備道，到中橫通車與1986年成立國家公園
From the 1914 police road to the cross-island highway and the park of 1986

在 YouTube 觀看 Watch on YouTube在模擬器飛這段 Fly this tour

旁白全文 Full narration

太魯閣口的這座牌樓，標示著中部橫貫公路的東端入口。外來的道路，是怎麼一步步伸進這座峽谷的。牌樓建於1960年，為慶祝公路通車而立。現為花蓮縣歷史建築，彩繪橫梁在2026年初完成修復。峽谷原是太魯閣族人的家園。1914年，日本當局沿立霧溪開鑿軍用與警備道路，深入內山。從燕子口到慈母橋，山壁高處有一條錐麓古道。斷崖一段長約500公尺，路就鑿在岩壁上，僅容一人通行。1933年6月，合歡越嶺道路動工，隔年1月大致完工。1935年全線開通，從花蓮經天祥、合歡山通往霧社。

1934年1月，越嶺道路的完工典禮在今天的天祥舉行。這條路起初為軍事與警備而開，後來也成為採金道路。1937年，臺灣總督府指定次高太魯閣國立公園。範圍約27萬公頃，隨著日治結束而廢止。1956年7月7日，中部橫貫公路動工。路線的最高點，是海拔2565公尺的大禹嶺。築路的主力是退除役官兵，多半靠人力與炸藥開山。1960年5月9日，公路正式通車，取代了越嶺古道。長春祠，紀念築路期間殉職的兩百多位人員。祠堂曾被山崩毀壞，重建後依舊守在瀑布上方。

祠名來自終年不斷的長春瀑布。山壁、流水與祠堂，成為中橫公路上廣為人知的一景。峽谷也曾被當作資源開發，立霧溪上築壩引水發電。砂卡礑步道，原本就是興建發電廠時的施工道路。1986年11月28日，太魯閣國家公園成立，面積約9.2萬公頃。成立前後，立霧溪水力發電與崇德水泥開發計畫相繼喊停。布洛灣的山月吊橋，最早建於1914年。現在的橋長196公尺，距溪面153公尺，2020年8月啟用。2001到2007年間，燕子口另闢新隧道，舊路成為景觀道路。九曲洞的舊公路，也在1996年隧道通車後改為步道。

錐麓古道在2009年5月重新開放，進入前必須申請許可。昔日的警備道，如今是需要預約的歷史步道。2024年4月3日花蓮地震，重創園區的步道與中橫公路。修復分區進行，各處陸續重新開放，實際情形以最新公告為準。警備道、公路、國家公園，一層層歷史都刻在同一道峽谷裡。路會中斷，也會再修通，峽谷的故事還在繼續。

This archway at the gorge mouth marks the eastern entrance of the Central Cross-Island Highway. How did roads from outside push, step by step, into this gorge? It was built in 1960 to celebrate the opening of the highway. Now a Hualien County historic building, it had its painted beam restored in early 2026. The gorge was first of all the homeland of the Truku people. In 1914 the Japanese authorities cut military and police roads along the Liwu River into the interior. High on the walls between Yanzikou and Cimu Bridge runs the Zhuilu Old Road. Its cliff section, about 500 m long, is cut into the rock face and fits one walker at a time. In June 1933 work began on the Hehuan cross-ridge road; it was largely finished by January 1934. The full line opened in 1935, linking Hualien with Wushe via Tianxiang and Hehuanshan.

In January 1934 the road's completion ceremony was held at what is now Tianxiang. Opened for military and police use, the route later also served gold prospecting. In 1937 the Government-General designated Tsugitaka-Taroko National Park. It covered about 270,000 hectares and lapsed with the end of Japanese rule. On 7 July 1956 construction of the Central Cross-Island Highway began. Its highest point is Dayuling, at 2,565 m. Most of the builders were veterans, working largely by hand and with explosives. The highway opened on 9 May 1960 and replaced the old cross-ridge road. The Eternal Spring Shrine honours more than two hundred people who died building the road. Destroyed by landslides and rebuilt, it still stands above the waterfall.

Its name comes from the Changchun Falls, which flow all year round. Cliff, water and shrine form one of the highway's best-known views. The gorge was also developed as a resource: a dam on the Liwu diverts water for power. The Shakadang Trail began as a construction road for a power plant. Taroko National Park was established on 28 November 1986, covering about 92,000 hectares. Around that time a Liwu hydropower scheme and a cement project at Chongde were both halted. The Shanyue suspension bridge at Buluowan was first built in 1914. Today's bridge, 196 m long and 153 m above the river, opened in August 2020. Between 2001 and 2007 a new tunnel was built at Yanzikou, and the old road became a scenic route. At Jiuqudong the old highway became a walking trail after a bypass tunnel opened in 1996.

The Zhuilu Old Road reopened in May 2009, and a permit is required to enter. A former police road is now a historic trail that has to be booked. The Hualien earthquake of 3 April 2024 badly damaged the park's trails and the highway. Repairs go area by area and sites reopen in stages; current notices give the actual status. Police road, highway, national park: each layer of history is cut into the same gorge. Roads break and are mended again, and the gorge's story continues.

人文 Culture

太魯閣族的家園與地名

Truku Homeland, Truku Names

立霧、布洛灣、塔比多：留在地名裡的族人記憶
Liwu, Buluowan, Tupido: memory kept in place names

旁白全文 Full narration

太魯閣這個名字，來自世居此地的太魯閣族，Truku。峽谷裡的地名，還記得多少族人的生活。約兩百五十到三百年前，族人的祖先住在濁水溪上游。他們翻越中央山脈，往東進入立霧溪流域。遷徙的原因，可能是人口增加、耕地不足，或尋找新獵場。沿著溪谷與河階，部落一處處建立起來。天祥舊稱塔比多，在族語裡指的是山棕。現在的地名，是後來為紀念文天祥而改的。國家公園範圍內，已發現79處舊部落遺址。它們分布在立霧溪主流與支流的兩岸。

布洛灣這個地名，相傳是族語回音的意思。臺地上設有太魯閣族文化的展示空間，重現傳統竹屋。傳統衣料是自織的麻布，白底配上茶褐色條紋。紡織、藤編等手工藝，做工精巧細緻。砂卡礑溪是立霧溪的支流，步道舊名神秘谷。2001年，步道改用溪谷原有的地名：砂卡礑。步道途中的五間屋，曾是五戶族人的家。砂卡礑溪流域，過去還有數個部落。峽谷口上方的山上，有大禮、大同兩個部落。2004年起部落發展生態旅遊，訪客循步道上山。

立霧溪口附近的崇德，同樣是太魯閣族的聚落。立霧這個名字，源自族語Tkijig，原指這條溪與這處聚落。日治時期，這個詞被寫成日語讀音相近的漢字「立霧」。一個族語的詞，就這樣留在今天的地圖上。1914年的太魯閣戰爭之後，日本當局推行集團移住。山裡的族人被遷往今日的秀林、萬榮與卓溪。秀林鄉面積約1642平方公里，是全臺面積最大的鄉。鄉內居民多數是太魯閣族人。族人稱祖先傳下的規範為Gaya，約束行為與道德。狩獵有固定的季節，四到六月是休獵期。

過去，族人被歸類為泰雅族的一支。2004年1月，太魯閣族正名成功，成為臺灣第12個原住民族。2010年，國家公園與部落的共同管理會議首次召開。如何讓族人實質參與這片土地的管理，至今仍是課題。Tkijig、布洛灣、塔比多，地名替這片土地記住了族人的足跡。飛過峽谷，也是飛過太魯閣族人的家園。

The name Taroko comes from the Truku people, whose home this has long been. How much of their life do the gorge's place names still remember? Some 250 to 300 years ago their ancestors lived on the upper Zhuoshui River. They crossed the Central Mountain Range eastward into the Liwu River basin. Growing numbers, a shortage of farmland or the search for new hunting grounds may have moved them. Village after village was founded along the valleys and river terraces. Tianxiang was once Tupido, a Truku name referring to a palm, the Arenga. The present name came later, in memory of the Song-dynasty figure Wen Tianxiang. Seventy-nine former village sites have been identified within the national park. They are spread along both banks of the Liwu River and its tributaries.

Buluowan is said to mean 'echo' in the Truku language. On the terrace, exhibition spaces and rebuilt bamboo houses present Truku culture. Traditional cloth was home-woven: white, with tea-brown stripes. Weaving, rattan work and other crafts are noted for their fine workmanship. Shakadang Stream is a tributary of the Liwu; its trail was once called Mysterious Valley. In 2001 the trail took the valley's own name: Shakadang. Wujianwu, 'Five Houses', on the trail was once the home of five Indigenous households. The Shakadang valley once held several more villages. On the heights above the gorge mouth are the villages of Dali and Datong. Since 2004 they have developed eco-tourism, and visitors climb to them by trail.

Chongde, near the river mouth, is also a Truku settlement. 'Liwu' goes back to the Truku word Tkijig, which named both the river and this settlement. Under Japanese rule it was written with kanji read 'Takkiri', the origin of the name Liwu. A Truku word has stayed on the map ever since. After the Truku War of 1914, the Japanese authorities imposed group resettlement. People from the mountain villages were moved to today's Xiulin, Wanrong and Zhuoxi. Xiulin Township, about 1,642 square kilometres, is the largest township in Taiwan by area. Most of its residents are Truku. The rules handed down by the ancestors are called Gaya; they govern conduct and morals. Hunting keeps to set seasons, and April to June is a closed period.

The Truku were long classified as a branch of the Atayal. In January 2004 they gained recognition as Taiwan's twelfth Indigenous people. In 2010 the park's joint management committee with the communities met for the first time. How Truku people can take a real part in managing this land remains an open question. Tkijig, Buluowan, Tupido: place names keep the Truku presence on this land. To fly through the gorge is to fly through a Truku homeland.

自然 Nature

從海平面到三千公尺

From Sea Level to 3,000 Metres

一條溪谷串起的海拔落差與生命
Life along one valley's climb through the altitude zones

在 YouTube 觀看 Watch on YouTube在模擬器飛這段 Fly this tour

旁白全文 Full narration

立霧溪口，海拔零公尺。從這裡到園區最高的南湖大山，高差3742公尺，住著哪些生命。園區從亞熱帶的海岸，延伸到寒帶氣候的高山。已知的植物超過兩千種，動物超過一千五百種。低海拔的溪谷裡，闊葉林貼著大理岩壁生長。砂卡礑溪水色清澈，沿途匯成一個個深潭。燕子口岩壁上的孔洞，曾是燕子築巢的地方，地名由此而來。遊客增多之後，燕子已不如從前常見。以太魯閣或園內地名命名的植物，共有63種。其中22種，只生長在這座國家公園裡。

峽谷的岩壁陡峭少土，植物只能長在岩縫與窄小的平臺上。即使如此，岩壁上仍有稀有的峽谷植物。天祥一帶的闊葉林裡，有太魯閣櫟與太魯閣大戟。這裡也常有臺灣獼猴出沒，管理處提醒不接觸、不餵食。十三年的鳥類繫放調查，在園區記錄到213種鳥。繫放也讓人得知，有鳥在這裡與西伯利亞之間遷徙。海拔約1180公尺的蓮花池，調查記錄到臺灣野山羊、山羌與藍腹鷴。園區的蝙蝠共記錄到25種，被當作觀察氣候變遷的指標。沿著中橫公路往上，森林的樣貌隨海拔一段段改變。闊葉樹漸漸讓位給針葉樹。

園區長期研究臺灣水鹿。2009年起，研究人員為水鹿戴上衛星定位頸圈，追蹤牠們的行蹤。一部生態紀錄片在園區山林拍攝三年，拍到麝香貓與林鵰。拍攝期間，團隊也遇見了黑熊。武嶺，海拔3275公尺，是臺灣公路的最高點。到了這個高度，森林退去，稜線上是成片的高山草坡。合歡山，園區最西側的高山區。2007年起的三年間，管理處在這裡移植復育了36種高山植物。往東看，立霧溪的源頭就在合歡山與奇萊北峰之間。雨水從這裡出發，穿過所有海拔帶，流回海邊。

回到海岸，清水斷崖的山壁從海面直接升起。鳥類繫放調查的範圍，就從海平面一路涵蓋到三千公尺以上。園區內原本劃有33處礦區，2008年起禁止採礦。從溪口到稜線，每一段海拔都有各自的住民。

The mouth of the Liwu River, at sea level. From here to Nanhu Mountain, the park's highest summit, is 3,742 m. What lives in between? The park stretches from a subtropical coast to peaks with a frigid climate. More than 2,000 plant species and over 1,500 animal species are known here. In the low valleys, broadleaf forest clings to the marble walls. Shakadang Stream runs clear, collecting in one deep pool after another. Swallows once nested in the holes of the cliffs at Yanzikou, hence its name. With more visitors, the birds are no longer as common as they were. Sixty-three plant species are named after Taroko or places inside the park. Twenty-two of them grow only within this national park.

The gorge walls are steep with little soil; plants hold on in cracks and on narrow ledges. Even so, rare gorge plants grow on these cliffs. The broadleaf forest around Tianxiang holds Taroko oak and Taroko spurge. Formosan macaques are often seen here; the park asks people not to touch or feed them. Thirteen years of bird-banding surveys recorded 213 bird species in the park. Banding has also shown that some birds travel between here and Siberia. At Lianhua Pond, about 1,180 m up, surveys recorded Formosan serow, Reeves's muntjac and Swinhoe's pheasant. Twenty-five bat species are known in the park and are followed as indicators of climate change. Up the highway, the forest changes character with each rise in altitude. Broadleaf trees gradually give way to conifers.

The park has long studied the Formosan sambar deer. Since 2009 researchers have fitted sambar with GPS collars to follow their movements. An ecological documentary filmed here over three years captured a civet and the black eagle. During the shoot the crew also met a black bear. Wuling, 3,275 m, is the highest point on Taiwan's paved roads. At this height the forest falls away, and grassy alpine slopes cover the ridges. Hehuanshan forms the high-mountain area at the park's far western edge. Over three years from 2007, park staff transplanted 36 alpine plant species here for restoration. To the east, the Liwu River rises between Hehuanshan and Qilai North Peak. Rain that falls here passes through every altitude zone on its way back to the sea.

Back on the coast, the walls of the Qingshui Cliff rise straight from the sea. The bird-banding survey spans this whole range, from sea level to above 3,000 m. The park once contained 33 mining areas; mining has been banned since 2008. From river mouth to ridgeline, every band of altitude has residents of its own.

地理 Geography

切穿大理岩的立霧溪

The River That Cut Through Marble

從出海口溯到高山，看抬升與下切如何刻出峽谷
From river mouth to high peaks: how uplift and incision carved the gorge

在 YouTube 觀看 Watch on YouTube在模擬器飛這段 Fly this tour

旁白全文 Full narration

花蓮縣秀林鄉，立霧溪在這裡流入太平洋。一條55公里長的溪，如何切出一座大理岩峽谷。流域面積約616平方公里，全都在秀林鄉境內。源頭在三千公尺的高山，終點就是眼前的海。太魯閣口，寬闊的河床到這裡突然收窄。再往西，兩岸的岩壁陡然立起。岩壁的主角是大理岩，前身是兩億多年前淺海中的石灰岩。深埋之後受熱受壓，才變質成今天的大理岩。約四百萬年前，菲律賓海板塊與歐亞板塊相撞。碰撞把古老的岩層推擠抬高，中央山脈隨之隆起。

布洛灣，峽谷中的一塊平坦臺地。這一帶的地殼仍以每年約半公分的速度抬升。燕子口，整條峽谷最窄的地段之一。抬升與下切同時進行，緻密的大理岩便留下近乎垂直的岩壁。岩壁上成排的孔洞，是流水長期侵蝕大理岩的痕跡。從正上方看，溪谷只剩一道細縫。再往上游是九曲洞一帶，大理岩層的厚度估計超過一千公尺。溪谷連續轉彎，公路只能鑽進岩壁裡。峽谷裡不只有大理岩，還有片岩與片麻岩。這些變質岩，屬於臺灣目前已知最古老的岩層。

天祥，大沙溪與塔次基里溪在此匯流，合成立霧溪。匯流處的河階，是深谷裡難得的平地。往上游望去，山勢一路升高。海拔兩千公尺以上的山區，約占園區的一半。大禹嶺，中央山脈主稜上的隘口。主稜是分水嶺，東側的立霧溪流域約占園區三分之二。合歡山北峰，海拔3422公尺。園區內名列臺灣百岳的高峰，共有27座。回到海邊。清水斷崖綿延約21公里，平均高度約800公尺。大理岩與片麻岩構成的山壁，幾乎直接落入太平洋。

斷崖的最高點是清水山，海拔2408公尺。山與海之間幾乎沒有平地，公路只能貼著崖壁前進。陡峭的岩壁節理發達，原本就容易落石。2024年4月3日花蓮地震後，燕子口一帶出現大規模崩塌。抬升、下切、崩落，這座峽谷至今仍在改變。步道與公路的開放情形時有調整，以太魯閣國家公園管理處公告為準。

Xiulin Township, Hualien County: here the Liwu River reaches the Pacific. How did a river only 55 km long cut a gorge through solid marble? Its basin covers about 616 square kilometres, all within Xiulin Township. It starts some 3,000 m up in the high mountains and ends at this shore. At the gorge mouth the wide gravel bed suddenly narrows. Further west, rock walls rise sharply on both banks. The walls are marble that began as limestone in a shallow sea over 200 million years ago. Buried deep, heated and squeezed, it was transformed into the marble seen today. About four million years ago the Philippine Sea Plate collided with the Eurasian Plate. The collision squeezed these old rocks upward as the Central Mountain Range rose.

Buluowan is a level terrace set inside the gorge. The crust here is still rising, by roughly half a centimetre every year. Yanzikou, the Swallow Grotto, is among the narrowest stretches of the whole gorge. With uplift and downcutting at work together, the dense marble is left as near-vertical walls. Rows of holes in the cliff were worn into the marble by water over a long time. Seen from directly above, the valley is no more than a slit. Upstream lies Jiuqudong, where the marble is estimated to be over 1,000 m thick. The river twists through bend after bend, and the road has to tunnel into the rock. The gorge is not marble alone: there are schist and gneiss as well. These metamorphic rocks are among the oldest known in Taiwan.

At Tianxiang the Dasha and Tacijili streams meet to form the Liwu River. The river terrace at their confluence is rare level ground in this deep valley. Looking upstream, the ridges climb steadily. About half of the park lies above 2,000 m. Dayuling is a pass on the main crest of the Central Mountain Range. The crest is the divide; east of it, the Liwu basin makes up about two thirds of the park. Hehuanshan's North Peak rises to 3,422 m. Twenty-seven of Taiwan's 'Hundred Peaks' lie within the park. Back at the coast, the Qingshui Cliff runs for about 21 km and averages some 800 m in height. Walls of marble and gneiss drop almost straight into the Pacific.

The cliffs culminate in Mount Qingshui, 2,408 m. With almost no flat land between mountain and sea, the highway clings to the cliff face. These steep, heavily jointed walls have always shed rock. After the Hualien earthquake of 3 April 2024, large collapses struck the Yanzikou area. Uplift, incision, collapse: the gorge is still changing today. Trail and road openings change; rely on notices from Taroko National Park Headquarters.

實際飛行前 Before a real flight

太魯閣國家公園全區適用內政部公告的「太魯閣國家公園區域內禁止事項」，禁止擅自在指定地區以外操作遙控機具（含空拍機）；實際飛行須事先向太魯閣國家公園管理處申請許可，違者可處罰鍰。另須遵守民航局遙控無人機規定，行前請用民航局「Drone Map」App 查詢空域。

The whole park is covered by the Ministry of the Interior's "Prohibited Acts within Taroko National Park", which bans operating remote-controlled craft (drones included) outside designated areas without authorisation; real flights need prior permission from Taroko National Park Headquarters, and violations are fined. Civil Aeronautics Administration drone rules also apply: check the airspace in the CAA "Drone Map" app first.
